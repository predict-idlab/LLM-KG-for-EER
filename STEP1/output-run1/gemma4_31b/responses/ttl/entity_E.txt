*   **Current Status:** Detached building (1862) with an EPC score of 165.0 (B label).
*   **Critical Weaknesses:**

    *   **Windows:** All existing windows use **single glazing**, which is highly inefficient.
    *   **Ventilation:** Currently uses **System A** (natural ventilation), the least efficient type.
    *   **Heating:** Uses electric convectors (high operational cost) and there is a stated reason to change the emission system.

### 2. Proposed Renovation Path (Optimal Sequence)

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope (The "Quick Wins")
*Goal: Stop heat leakage using the most cost-effective methods.*
1.  **Insulate Wall Cavities (`reno:InsulateWallCavity`):** Since all walls are cavity walls and not insulated, this is the highest priority. It has a low nuisance level and the lowest unit price (22/m²).
2.  **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space (`reno:InsulateFloorAboveBasement`):** To prevent cold drafts from the crawl space, this is a low-cost (30/m²), low-nuisance upgrade.

#### Phase 2: Thermal Envelope (High Impact)
*Goal: Address the most significant remaining thermal bridges.*
3.  **Replace Glazing (`reno:ReplaceGlazing`):** The building currently has single glazing. Since the wooden frames are in **"Good State,"** it is more cost-effective to replace only the glass (e.g., to double or triple glazing) rather than replacing the entire window unit.

#### Phase 3: Ventilation & Air Quality
*Goal: Improve air exchange efficiency to prevent moisture and heat loss.*
4.  **Replace Ventilation System (`reno:ReplaceVentilationSystem`):** Upgrading from System A to a more modern system (like System C or D) will ensure that the newly insulated home remains healthy and does not suffer from condensation.

#### Phase 4: Heating Generation & Emission
*Goal: Transition from expensive electricity/old systems to high-efficiency technology.*
5.  **Install Air-Water Heat Pump (`reno:PlaceAirWaterHeatpump`):** Geothermal and heat grid options are unavailable for this site. An air-water heat pump is the most sustainable alternative to replace the current electric convectors.
6.  **Upgrade to Low-Temperature Emission (`reno:PlaceLowTemperatureEmission`):** Since the building "has reason to change the emission system," moving toward low-temperature emitters (like underfloor heating or larger radiators) will maximize the efficiency of the new heat pump.

#### Phase 5: Energy Production
*Goal: Offset operational costs with renewable energy.*
7.  **Install PV Panels on Roof (`reno:PlacePVPanelsOnRoof`):** The building is confirmed to have PV potential. This is the final step to lower the yearly energy bill and improve the EPC score further.
