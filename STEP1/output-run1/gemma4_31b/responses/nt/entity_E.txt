### **Proposed Renovation Path (Optimal Sequence)**

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Low-Hanging Fruit)**
*Focus: Stop heat loss using the most cost-effective methods available.*

1.  **Insulate Wall Cavities (`InsulateWallCavity`):** 
    *   **Reasoning:** All four walls are cavity walls and currently not insulated. This is the most cost-effective insulation method (Unit Price: 22) compared to interior or exterior options.
    *   **Benefit:** Immediate and significant reduction in heat loss through the walls.
2.  **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space (`InsulateFloorAboveBasement`):** 
    *   **Reasoning:** The building has a floor above a crawl space. Insulating this prevents cold drafts from the ground.
    *   **Benefit:** Increases thermal comfort and prevents heat from escaping through the floor.
3.  **Replace Glazing (`ReplaceGlazing`):** 
    *   **Reasoning:** Current windows use single glazing. Since the wooden frames are in "Good State," you can replace the glass with double or triple glazing without replacing the entire frame.
    *   **Benefit:** Drastically reduces heat loss through windows and improves acoustic insulation.

#### **Phase 2: Air Quality and Ventilation**
*Focus: Ensuring a healthy indoor climate after the building is sealed.*

4.  **Upgrade Ventilation System (`ReplaceVentilationSystem`):** 
    *   **Reasoning:** The current System A (natural supply) is inefficient. Upgrading to a more modern system (e.g., System D with heat recovery) prevents moisture buildup and stale air now that the walls and windows are tighter.
    *   **Benefit:** Maintains air quality while recovering heat from exhaust air.

#### **Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition**
*Focus: Replacing outdated generation systems with sustainable technology.*

5.  **Install Air-Water Heat Pump (`PlaceAirWaterHeatpump`):** 
    *   **Reasoning:** The owner has expressed a reason to change the emission system. Because the building already has floor heating and the envelope is now insulated, an Air-Water heat pump will operate at maximum efficiency.
    *   **Benefit:** Eliminates reliance on the old boiler and significantly lowers long-term operational energy costs.

#### **Phase 4: Renewable Energy Integration**
*Focus: Offsetting the electricity demand of the new heating system.*

6.  **Install PV Panels on Roof (`PlacePVPanelsOnRoof`):** 
    *   **Reasoning:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. 
    *   **Benefit:** Generates clean electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system, moving the building toward a net-zero energy balance and further improving the EPC score.
