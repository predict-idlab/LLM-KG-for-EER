### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Reducing Heat Loss)
*The goal is to create a tight thermal shell to lower the energy required for heating.*

1.  **Insulate Flat Roof (Roof Extension):** The roof extension is currently not insulated. Insulating this area is a high-priority, low-hanging fruit to prevent significant heat escape through the top of the building.
2.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The floor above the basement is not insulated and has no protection constraints. This will significantly improve thermal comfort and reduce the energy load.
3.  **Insulate Exterior Walls (Front and Rear):** Both the front and rear full-brick walls are not insulated. Since there is no historical protection, **Exterior Wall Insulation** is the most effective choice to eliminate thermal bridges and drastically improve the EPC score.

#### Phase 2: Ventilation and Air Quality
*Once the building is airtight, mechanical ventilation is required to maintain air quality and recover heat.*

4.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from the current System A (natural supply/mechanical exhaust) to a **Ventilation System D** (double-flow with heat recovery). This ensures that the heat kept inside by the new insulation is not lost through ventilation.

#### Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition
*With a reduced heat load, we can now install a smaller, more efficient heating system.*

5.  **Install Low-Temperature Emission Systems:** To maximize the efficiency of a heat pump, the current radiators should be transitioned to low-temperature emissions (e.g., floor heating or oversized radiators).
6.  **Install Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since geothermal drilling and heat grids are unavailable at this site, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most cost-effective and sustainable replacement for the current boiler.

#### Phase 4: Renewable Energy Integration
*The final step is to offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump.*

7.  **Place PV Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will reduce operational costs and further improve the EPC label toward the owner's goal.

| Sequence | Intervention | Logic |
