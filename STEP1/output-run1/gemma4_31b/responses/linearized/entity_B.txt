### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Reducing Demand)
The building has several critical "Not Insulated" areas. Addressing these first ensures that any future heating system is downsized and operates more efficiently.
1.  **Insulate Flat Roof (RoofExtension):** The flat roof is currently not insulated. This is a high-priority area for heat loss.
2.  **Insulate Exterior Walls (Wall_Front & Wall_Rear):** Both are full brick walls and not insulated. Since there is no historical protection, **External Wall Insulation** is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The floor is not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents heat loss to the basement and improves the thermal comfort of the living spaces.
4.  **Roof Main (Gable Roof) Review:** While currently "Moderately Insulated," a top-up of insulation should be considered if the budget allows, as this is the largest surface area for heat escape.

#### Phase 2: Systems & Ventilation Upgrade (Improving Efficiency)
Once the building is airtight and insulated, the heating and air exchange systems can be optimized.
5.  **Replace Ventilation System:** The current System A (natural supply/exhaust) is the least efficient. Upgrading to **Ventilation System D** (mechanical supply and exhaust) will significantly improve indoor air quality and reduce heat loss through uncontrolled ventilation.
6.  **Transition to Low-Temperature Emission:** Since the building currently uses standard radiators and the owner intends to change the emission system, installing **Low Temperature Emission** systems is a prerequisite for the efficient use of heat pumps.
7.  **Install Air-Water Heat Pump:** With geothermal potential being false and no heat grid available, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most cost-effective and sustainable alternative to the existing boiler.

#### Phase 3: Renewable Energy Integration (Closing the Loop)
The final step is to offset the remaining energy consumption with on-site generation.
8.  **Install PV Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels on the roof will lower operational costs and significantly improve the final EPC score.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence & Logic

| Sequence | Intervention | Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Roof/Walls/Floor** | Insulation | Prevents "over-specifying" the heating system; highest ROI on energy reduction. |
| **2. Ventilation** | System A $\rightarrow$ D | Ensures the insulated home remains healthy and avoids condensation. |
| **3. Emission System** | Low-Temp Radiators | Prepares the building for high-efficiency heat pump operation. |
| **4. Heat Generation** | Air-Water Heat Pump | Replaces the fossil-fuel boiler with a sustainable energy source. |
| **5. Renewables** | PV Panels | Reduces electricity bills and maximizes the energy score. |
