### Renovation Plan for the Building at example.com

#### **Context and Building Characteristics**
The building is an attached house with the following key characteristics:
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **Net Floor Area:** 212 m²
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC):** Score of 262 (Label C)
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** No
- **Potential for PV Panels:** Yes
- **Potential for Horizontal Geothermal Captation:** No
- **Potential for Vertical Geothermal Drilling:** No

#### **Current Issues and Priorities**
1. **Insulation Issues:**
   - **Roof Extension:** Not insulated
   - **Roof Main:** Moderately insulated
   - **Floor Above Basement:** Not insulated
   - **Wall Front and Rear:** Not insulated, with no historical protection

2. **Ventilation System:**
   - Currently a Ventilation System A (natural air supply and mechanical air exhaust)

3. **Heating System:**
   - Uses a radiator (Emission System)

4. **Windows:**
   - Living Area: Double glazing
   - Bedrooms: Double glazing, wooden frames in good condition

#### **Renovation Recommendations**
1. **Improve Insulation:**
   - **Insulate Roof Extension:** Replace or add insulation to the flat roof extension to improve thermal performance.
   - **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** Install insulation to improve comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Insulate Walls (Front and Rear):** Add exterior or interior insulation to improve thermal performance and comfort.

2. **Upgrade Ventilation System:**
   - **Replace Ventilation System:** Consider upgrading to a Ventilation System C or D for better air quality and efficiency.

3. **Consider Renewable Energy Options:**
   - **Install PV Panels:** Given the potential for PV panels, consider installing solar panels to reduce energy costs and environmental impact.
   - **Explore Geothermal Options:** Although the potential for geothermal drilling is low, explore other renewable energy options like heat pumps.

4. **Window Maintenance:**
   - **Maintain Windows:** Ensure that the windows (particularly the wooden frames) are well-maintained to prevent drafts and improve insulation.

#### **Action Plan and Prioritization**
1. **Short-Term Actions (Immediate to 6 Months):**
   - **Insulate Roof Extension and Floor Above Basement:** These are high-impact, relatively low-cost actions that can significantly improve comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Maintain Windows:** Ensure that the existing windows are in good condition to prevent heat loss.

2. **Medium-Term Actions (6 to 12 Months):**
   - **Insulate Walls:** This is a more involved project but will have a significant impact on energy efficiency and comfort.
   - **Upgrade Ventilation System:** Research and plan for upgrading the ventilation system to a more efficient model.

3. **Long-Term Actions (12+ Months):**
   - **Install PV Panels:** This requires more planning and investment but will provide long-term energy savings and environmental benefits.
   - **Explore Renewable Energy Options:** Consider other renewable energy options like heat pumps if geothermal drilling is not feasible.

#### **Cost Considerations**
- **Insulation Projects:** Costs will vary based on the type of insulation used (e.g., exterior vs. interior) and the area to be insulated.
- **Ventilation System Upgrade:** Costs will depend on the type of system chosen and the complexity of the installation.
- **PV Panels:** Initial investment will be higher but will provide long-term savings on energy bills.

#### **Additional Recommendations**
- **Monitor Energy Consumption:** Regularly monitor energy consumption to identify areas for further improvement.
- **Consider Energy-Efficient Heating:** Explore options for replacing or upgrading the existing radiator system to a more energy-efficient solution.

By following this plan, the building's energy efficiency can be significantly improved, leading to lower energy costs and a more comfortable living environment. The plan prioritizes actions based on cost, impact, and feasibility, ensuring a practical and achievable path to a more sustainable and efficient home.
