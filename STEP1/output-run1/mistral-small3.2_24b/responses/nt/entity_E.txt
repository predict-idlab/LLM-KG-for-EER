### Renovation Plan for the Building at example.com

#### 1. **Initial Assessment**
- **Building Type**: Detached building
- **Construction Year**: 1862
- **Current Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score**: 165 kWh/m²year
- **Potential for PV Installation**: Yes
- **Presence of Heat Grid**: No
- **Reason to Change Emission System**: Yes
- **Current Insulation Grades**:
  - Walls (Front, Left, Right, Rear): Not Insulated
  - Roof (Main): Well Insulated
  - Floor (Above Crawl Space): Not Specified

#### 2. **Immediate Actions**
1. **Replace Windows**
   - **Action**: Replace the current single-glazed windows with double or triple-glazed windows.
   - **Reason**: Single-glazed windows are inefficient and contribute significantly to heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Depends on the number and size of windows, but typically ranges from €800 to €1,500 per window.
   - **Ordering**: 46

2. **Insulate Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate the exterior walls (Front, Left, Right, Rear) with appropriate insulation material.
   - **Reason**: The current walls are not insulated, leading to significant heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Approximately €200 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 40

3. **Install PV Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the well-insulated roof.
   - **Reason**: The building has potential for PV installation and can benefit from renewable energy.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Approximately €275 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 85

4. **Upgrade Emission System**
   - **Action**: Replace the current emission system (Radiator or Electric Convector) with a more efficient system such as a heat pump or underfloor heating.
   - **Reason**: The current system is outdated and can be improved for better energy efficiency.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Varies significantly based on the type of system installed.
   - **Ordering**: 70

#### 3. **Medium-Term Actions**
1. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space to improve thermal comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Reason**: The current floor is not insulated, leading to heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Approximately €30 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 49

2. **Install Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Install a ventilation system (Type A or C) to improve indoor air quality.
   - **Reason**: Proper ventilation is essential for maintaining a healthy indoor environment.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Approximately €17 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 52

3. **Consider Connecting to Heat Grid (Future)**
   - **Action**: Explore the possibility of connecting to a heat grid in the future.
   - **Reason**: Although the current presence of a heat grid is no, future expansions might make it available.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Varies significantly based on the distance and connection requirements.

#### 4. **Long-Term Actions**
1. **Regular Maintenance and Monitoring**
   - **Action**: Schedule regular maintenance for all installed systems and monitor energy consumption.
   - **Reason**: Ensuring the longevity and efficiency of the installed systems.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Varies based on the type of maintenance required.

2. **Energy Audits**
   - **Action**: Conduct periodic energy audits to assess the building's energy performance and identify areas for further improvement.
   - **Reason**: Continuous improvement of energy efficiency over time.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Varies based on the scope and depth of the audit.

#### 5. **Additional Recommendations**
- **Smart Home Integration**: Consider integrating smart home devices for better control and monitoring of energy consumption.
- **Energy-Efficient Lighting**: Replace traditional lighting with LED or other energy-efficient options.
- **Water Heating**: Consider installing a solar water heater or a heat pump water heater for more efficient water heating.

This plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective. The actions are ordered based on their priority and potential impact on the building's energy performance. Regular monitoring and maintenance will ensure the longevity and efficiency of the installed systems.
