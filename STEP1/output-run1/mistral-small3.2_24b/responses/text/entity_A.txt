Based on the information provided, here's a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan focuses on improving energy efficiency in a cost-effective manner, prioritizing upgrades that offer the highest return on investment and long-term savings.

### 1. **Insulation Upgrades**
   - **Exterior Walls**: Install external wall insulation (EWI) to the solid brick walls. This will significantly reduce heat loss and improve the overall thermal performance of the house. EWI also has the added benefit of modernizing the appearance of the property.
   - **Pitched Roof**: Add insulation to the pitched roof. This is a critical area for heat loss, and insulating it will have a substantial impact on reducing heating costs.
   - **Flat-Roof Extension**: Top up the existing insulation in the flat-roof extension to match the insulation levels of the newly insulated pitched roof.
   - **Floor Above Basement**: Insulate the floor above the basement to prevent heat loss through the ground floor.

### 2. **Heating System Upgrade**
   - Replace the existing boiler and heat emission systems with a modern, high-efficiency condensing boiler. Consider installing individual thermostatic radiator valves (TRVs) to allow for more precise control of heating in different rooms.
   - Evaluate the feasibility of integrating a heat pump system, which can be more energy-efficient than traditional boilers, especially when combined with underfloor heating.

### 3. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
   - Replace the basic natural ventilation system with a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR). This will improve indoor air quality while reducing heat loss through ventilation.

### 4. **Windows and Doors**
   - Although the windows have double glazing, consider upgrading to triple-glazed windows if the budget allows. This will further reduce heat loss through the windows.
   - Ensure that all external doors are well-insulated and draught-proofed.

### 5. **Photovoltaic Panels**
   - Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable energy. This will reduce your reliance on the grid and lower your electricity bills. Given the potential for PV installation mentioned, this is a worthwhile investment.

### 6. **Smart Home Integration**
   - Install smart thermostats and heating controls to optimize the heating system's efficiency. This allows for more precise temperature control and can lead to significant energy savings.

### Optimal Sequence of Renovations
1. **Insulation Upgrades**: Start with insulating the exterior walls, pitched roof, and floor above the basement. This will create a well-insulated envelope, reducing the heating demand.
2. **Heating System Upgrade**: Replace the boiler and heat emission systems with a modern, high-efficiency system.
3. **Ventilation System Upgrade**: Install the MVHR system to ensure good indoor air quality and minimize heat loss.
4. **Windows and Doors**: Upgrade to triple-glazed windows and ensure doors are well-insulated.
5. **Photovoltaic Panels**: Install PV panels to generate renewable energy.
6. **Smart Home Integration**: Implement smart thermostats and heating controls to fine-tune the heating system's efficiency.

### Additional Considerations
- **Grants and Incentives**: Check for available grants and incentives for energy-efficient renovations in Ghent. These can significantly reduce the upfront costs of the renovations.
- **Professional Consultation**: Consult with energy efficiency experts or certified renovators to ensure that the proposed upgrades are correctly implemented and comply with local building regulations.

By following this plan, you can substantially improve the energy performance certificate of your house, reduce energy consumption, and achieve long-term cost savings.
