Based on the information provided, here’s a concise and actionable renovation plan for your detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan prioritizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness, focusing on practical upgrades that will reduce energy consumption and improve long-term operational performance.

### Renovation Plan:

#### 1. **Insulate the Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Install insulation in the cavity walls (front, rear, left side, and right side) using appropriate materials such as mineral wool or rigid foam insulation.
   - **Benefit**: This will significantly reduce heat loss through the walls, improving the overall energy performance of the house.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Insulating cavity walls is relatively cost-effective compared to solid wall insulation and offers a good return on investment.

#### 2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space using rigid insulation boards or other suitable materials.
   - **Benefit**: This will prevent heat loss through the floor, especially if the crawl space is not heated.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Insulating the floor is a straightforward and cost-effective measure.

#### 3. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - **Action**: Replace the existing single-glazed windows with double or triple-glazed windows, maintaining the wooden frames if they are in good condition.
   - **Benefit**: This will reduce heat loss through the windows and improve thermal comfort.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: While the upfront cost is higher, the long-term energy savings make it a worthwhile investment.

#### 4. **Upgrade the Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and heating emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors) with a modern, high-efficiency condensing boiler and a more efficient heating system, such as underfloor heating or low-temperature radiators.
   - **Benefit**: This will improve the efficiency of the heating system and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Upgrading the heating system is essential for improving energy performance and can be combined with other measures for better results.

#### 5. **Improve Natural Ventilation**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the basic natural ventilation system to a more efficient system, such as a heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system.
   - **Benefit**: This will improve indoor air quality and reduce heat loss through ventilation.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: While HRV systems have a higher upfront cost, they offer long-term energy savings and improved comfort.

#### 6. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**
   - **Action**: Assess the potential for installing photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable energy.
   - **Benefit**: This will reduce electricity costs and contribute to a lower carbon footprint.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: The cost of PV panels has decreased significantly, and they offer a good return on investment over time, especially with available subsidies and feed-in tariffs.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate the exterior walls and floor above the crawl space** to address the largest sources of heat loss.
2. **Replace the single-glazed windows** with double or triple glazing to further reduce heat loss.
3. **Upgrade the heating system** to ensure efficient heat distribution.
4. **Improve the ventilation system** to enhance indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
5. **Install photovoltaic panels** to generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.

- **Subsidies and Incentives**: Check for available subsidies and incentives for energy-efficient renovations in Ghent, Belgium, as these can significantly reduce the cost of the upgrades.
