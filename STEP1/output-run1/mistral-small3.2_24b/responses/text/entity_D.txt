Based on the information provided, here is a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan focuses on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, considering the current conditions and the goal of improving the energy performance certificate (EPC) score.

### 1. **Improve Roof Insulation**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the insulation of the main pitched roof to a higher standard (e.g., from 200 mm to 300 mm of mineral wool or equivalent).
   - **Rationale:** The main pitched roof has only moderate insulation, and improving it will significantly reduce heat loss.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** High. Roof insulation is one of the most cost-effective ways to improve energy efficiency.

### 2. **Enhance Ventilation System Efficiency**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the existing ventilation system D to a more energy-efficient model with heat recovery (e.g., a demand-controlled ventilation system).
   - **Rationale:** Heat recovery ventilation can reduce heat loss through ventilation by up to 90%, improving overall energy efficiency.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Moderate to high. The initial cost is higher, but long-term energy savings justify the investment.

### 3. **Optimize Heat Pump Performance**
   - **Action:** Conduct a professional assessment of the heat pump to ensure it is operating at peak efficiency. Consider upgrading the control system or adding smart thermostats for better temperature management.
   - **Rationale:** Ensuring the heat pump is running efficiently will maximize its performance and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Moderate. The cost of an assessment and potential upgrades is relatively low compared to the potential energy savings.

### 4. **Upgrade Windows (if necessary)**
   - **Action:** Assess the performance of the existing double-glazed windows. If they are not performing optimally, consider upgrading to triple-glazed windows or adding low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings.
   - **Rationale:** While the windows are in good condition, upgrading to triple glazing or Low-E coatings can further reduce heat loss through the windows.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Moderate. The cost of upgrading windows is higher, but the energy savings can be significant.

### 5. **Improve Air Sealing**
   - **Action:** Conduct an air tightness test to identify and seal any air leaks around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Rationale:** Reducing air infiltration will improve the overall energy efficiency of the house.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Low to moderate. The cost of air sealing is relatively low, and the energy savings can be significant.

### 6. **Maximize Photovoltaic (PV) Panel Output**
   - **Action:** Assess the performance of the existing PV panels and consider adding more panels or optimizing their orientation and tilt for maximum sunlight exposure.
   - **Rationale:** Increasing the PV panel output can further reduce the house's reliance on grid electricity.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Moderate to high. The cost of additional PV panels is high, but the long-term energy savings and potential feed-in tariffs can justify the investment.

### 7. **Monitor and Maintain Systems**
   - **Action:** Regularly monitor and maintain the heating, ventilation, and PV systems to ensure they continue to operate efficiently.
   - **Rationale:** Regular maintenance can prevent inefficiencies and extend the lifespan of the systems.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Low. The cost of maintenance is relatively low compared to the potential energy savings.

6. **Maximize PV Panel Output** (Moderate to high impact, moderate to high cost)
