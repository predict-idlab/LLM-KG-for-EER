### **Renovation Plan for the Attached Building**

#### **1. Insulate the Flat Roof (Roof Extension)**
- **Action**: Insulate the flat roof (Roof Extension) with an inverted roof structure.
- **Reason**: The flat roof is currently not insulated, and insulation will significantly improve energy efficiency.
- **Estimated Cost**: €65/m² (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: No (Flanders).
- **Priority**: High.

#### **2. Insulate the Gable Roof (Roof Main)**
- **Action**: Insulate the gable roof (Roof Main) with a vapor barrier on the warm side of the insulation.
- **Reason**: The gable roof is moderately insulated, and further insulation will enhance thermal comfort and energy efficiency.
- **Estimated Cost**: €60/m² (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: No (Flanders).
- **Priority**: Medium.

#### **3. Insulate the Floor Above Basement**
- **Action**: Insulate the floor above the basement.
- **Reason**: The floor above the basement is not insulated, which can lead to heat loss.
- **Estimated Cost**: €30/m² (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: No (Flanders).
- **Priority**: Medium.

#### **4. Insulate the Walls (Wall Front and Wall Rear)**
- **Action**: Insulate the walls (Wall Front and Wall Rear) on the exterior.
- **Reason**: The walls are not insulated, and exterior insulation will improve thermal comfort and energy efficiency.
- **Estimated Cost**: €200/m² (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: Only if the thickness of the total pack >26cm or the building line is overrun (Flanders).
- **Priority**: High.

#### **5. Replace the Ventilation System**
- **Action**: Replace the ventilation system (Ventilation System A) with a more efficient system, such as Ventilation System D.
- **Reason**: A more efficient ventilation system will improve indoor air quality and reduce energy consumption.
- **Estimated Cost**: €3,500 (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: No (Flanders).
- **Priority**: Medium.

#### **6. Replace the Emission System**
- **Action**: Replace the radiator (Emission System) with a low-temperature emission system, such as underfloor heating.
- **Reason**: A low-temperature emission system will improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
- **Estimated Cost**: €100/m² (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: No (Flanders).
- **Priority**: High.

#### **7. Replace the Generation System**
- **Action**: Replace the boiler (Generation System) with a heat pump boiler or an air-water heat pump.
- **Reason**: A heat pump boiler or an air-water heat pump will significantly reduce energy consumption and lower carbon emissions.
- **Estimated Cost**: €4,000 (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: No (Flanders).
- **Priority**: High.

#### **8. Install PV Panels**
- **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof (Roof Main).
- **Reason**: The building has potential for PV, and installing PV panels will generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.
- **Estimated Cost**: €275/m² (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: No (Flanders).
- **Priority**: Medium.

#### **9. Install Sun Shades**
- **Action**: Install sun shades on the windows (Windows Bedrooms and Windows Living Area).
- **Reason**: Sun shades will reduce solar gain in the summer, improving thermal comfort and reducing energy consumption.
- **Estimated Cost**: €750 (including labor and materials).
- **Permit Needed**: No (Flanders).
- **Priority**: Low.
