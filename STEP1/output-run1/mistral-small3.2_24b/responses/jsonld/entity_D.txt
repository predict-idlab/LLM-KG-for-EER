### Renovation Plan for the Building

#### **1. Replace the Windows (Priority: High)**
- **Intervention**: Replace the glazing of the windows in the living area and bedrooms.
- **Recommendation**: `https://w3id.org/reno#ReplaceGlazing`
- **Details**:
  - Replace single-glazing with double-glazing to improve insulation.
  - Ensure the frames are in good condition to fit new glazing.
  - The intervention is part of the renovation that took place in 2020.
- **Cost Estimate**:
  - Cost per m²: €200
  - Total cost: To be calculated based on the surface area of the windows.

#### **2. Insulate the Roof (Priority: Medium)**
- **Intervention**: Insulate the flat roof (extension).
- **Recommendation**: `https://w3id.org/reno#InsulateFlatRoof`
- **Details**:
  - Opt for an inverted roof structure to place the insulation on top of the roof covering.
  - Ensure proper condensation control with a vapor barrier.
- **Cost Estimate**:
  - Cost per m²: €50
  - Total cost: To be calculated based on the surface area of the roof.

#### **3. Upgrade the Ventilation System (Priority: Medium)**
- **Intervention**: Replace the ventilation system.
- **Recommendation**: `https://w3id.org/reno#ReplaceVentilationSystem`
- **Details**:
  - Replace the existing ventilation system D with a more efficient system.
  - Ensure proper ductwork installation for both supply and return.
- **Cost Estimate**:
  - Fixed cost: €3500
  - Cost per m²: €17
  - Total cost: To be calculated based on the floor area.

#### **4. Insulate Walls (Priority: Medium)**
- **Intervention**: Insulate the walls (front, rear, and left).
- **Recommendation**: `https://w3id.org/reno#InsulateWallExterior`
- **Details**:
  - Apply exterior insulation to improve thermal performance.
  - Ensure compatibility with the building line and council guidelines.
- **Cost Estimate**:
  - Cost per m²: €200
  - Total cost: To be calculated based on the surface area of the walls.

#### **5. Consider Renewable Energy Options (Priority: Low)**
- **Intervention**: Assess the potential for renewable energy systems.
- **Recommendation**: `https://w3id.org/reno#PlacePVPanelsOnRoof`
- **Details**:
  - Check the suitability of the roof for PV panel installation.
  - Ensure compliance with local regulations regarding PV panel installation.
- **Cost Estimate**:
  - Cost per m²: €275
  - Total cost: To be calculated based on the surface area of the roof.
