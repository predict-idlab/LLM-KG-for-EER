### Renovation Plan for the Semi-Detached Building

#### **1. Prioritize Based on Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness**
- **Insulate Roof (Extension and Main)**:
  - **Action**: Replace the current moderately insulated roof extension and uninsulated main gable roof.
  - **Rationale**: Roof insulation is critical for improving thermal performance and reducing heat loss.
  - **Recommendation**: Replace with high-performance insulation to achieve a well-insulated grade.
  - **Cost**: Moderate (€50 per m² for flat roof, €60 per m² for gable roof).
  - **Impact**: High impact on reducing energy consumption and improving thermal comfort.

- **Insulate Exterior Walls**:
  - **Action**: Insulate the exterior of the uninsulated full brick walls (front, rear, and right).
  - **Rationale**: Exterior wall insulation reduces heat loss through walls and improves overall building envelope performance.
  - **Recommendation**: Use high-performance insulation material suitable for exterior application.
  - **Cost**: Moderate to high (€200 per m²).
  - **Impact**: Significant improvement in energy efficiency and thermal comfort.

- **Insulate Floor Above Basement**:
  - **Action**: Insulate the uninsulated floor above the basement.
  - **Rationale**: Floor insulation reduces heat loss through the floor and improves overall thermal performance.
  - **Recommendation**: Use appropriate insulation material for floor application.
  - **Cost**: Low (€30 per m²).
  - **Impact**: Moderate improvement in energy efficiency and thermal comfort.

- **Replace Ventilation System**:
  - **Action**: Upgrade the current natural ventilation system (type A) to a more efficient system (type C or D).
  - **Rationale**: A more efficient ventilation system improves indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
  - **Recommendation**: Consider a system with heat recovery to maximize energy savings.
  - **Cost**: Moderate (€3500 fixed cost plus €17 per m²).
  - **Impact**: Moderate to high improvement in indoor air quality and energy efficiency.

#### **2. Optimize Based on Specific Building Characteristics**
- **Address Asbestos Removal**:
  - **Action**: Remove any asbestos-containing materials before starting any insulation work.
  - **Rationale**: Safety and regulatory compliance are crucial when dealing with asbestos.
  - **Recommendation**: Hire a licensed asbestos removal company for safe and compliant removal.
  - **Cost**: Varies depending on the extent of asbestos presence.
  - **Impact**: Essential for safety and compliance.

- **Consider PV Panel Installation**:
  - **Action**: Evaluate the potential for installing photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof.
  - **Rationale**: Solar energy can significantly reduce electricity costs and environmental impact.
  - **Recommendation**: Assess roof orientation, shading, and structural suitability for PV installation.
  - **Cost**: Moderate to high (€275 per m²).
  - **Impact**: High potential for long-term energy cost savings and sustainability.

#### **3. Additional Recommendations**
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)**:
  - **Action**: Ensure the EPC is up-to-date and reflects the improvements made.
  - **Rationale**: A good EPC rating can increase property value and attract potential buyers or tenants.
  - **Recommendation**: Work with a certified energy expert to update the EPC after renovations.
  - **Cost**: Varies depending on the service provider.
  - **Impact**: Essential for property valuation and marketability.

- **Monitor and Maintain**:
  - **Action**: Regularly monitor the performance of the renovated systems and maintain them as needed.
  - **Rationale**: Ongoing maintenance ensures the longevity and efficiency of the renovations.
  - **Recommendation**: Schedule periodic inspections and maintenance checks.
  - **Cost**: Variable.
  - **Impact**: Ensures sustained energy efficiency and performance.

### Summary of Prioritized Actions
1. **Insulate Roof (Extension and Main)** - High Impact, Moderate Cost
2. **Insulate Exterior Walls** - Significant Impact, Moderate to High Cost
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement** - Moderate Impact, Low Cost
4. **Replace Ventilation System** - Moderate to High Impact, Moderate Cost
5. **Address Asbestos Removal** - Essential for Safety and Compliance
6. **Consider PV Panel Installation** - High Potential for Long-Term Savings
7. **Update Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)** - Essential for Property Valuation
8. **Monitor and Maintain** - Ensures Long-Term Efficiency

This plan focuses on maximizing energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness while addressing the specific needs and characteristics of the semi-detached building. The sequence of actions is designed to prioritize high-impact improvements that will yield significant energy savings and enhance thermal comfort.
