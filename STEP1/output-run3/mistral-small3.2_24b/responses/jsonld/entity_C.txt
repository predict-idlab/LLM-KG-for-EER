### **Energy-Efficient Renovation Plan for the Attached Building**

#### **Key Observations and Recommendations**
1. **EPC Rating (D) - Poor Insulation**
   - The building has an **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) score of 50**, indicating significant room for improvement in energy efficiency.
   - The **floor above basement, walls (front and rear), and roof main** are **well-insulated**, but the **roof extension** is **moderately insulated**, and **windows** are **double-glazed** but may not be optimized for energy efficiency.

2. **Potential for PV Panels**
   - The **potential for PV panels is marked as false**, but this should be reassessed based on the building's orientation and roof space.

3. **Reason to Change Emission System**
   - The **emission system (electric convectors and floor heating) is outdated**, and there is a **reason to change it**.

4. **Historical Protection**
   - The walls have **no historical protection**, making them eligible for renovations.

5. **Renovation Motivation**
   - The owner's **primary motivation is to improve the EPC rating**.

### **Step-by-Step Renovation Plan**
#### **1. Insulate the Roof Extension**
   - **Recommendation:** Replace the **moderately insulated roof extension** with **well-insulated roofing**.
   - **Action:**
     - Use **high-performance insulation materials** (e.g., rigid foam or mineral wool).
     - Ensure proper **vapour barrier installation** to prevent condensation.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€50/m²

#### **2. Upgrade Window Glazing**
   - **Recommendation:** Replace **double-glazed windows** with **triple-glazed windows** or **high-performance double-glazed windows** with **low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings** and **argon gas filling**.
   - **Action:**
     - Choose **wooden or PVC frames** with **thermal breaks** for better insulation.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€200/m²

#### **3. Replace Emission System**
   - **Recommendation:** Upgrade the **electric convectors and floor heating** to a **more energy-efficient system** (e.g., **air-source heat pump** or **ground-source heat pump**).
   - **Action:**
     - Conduct a **heat loss assessment** to determine the optimal system.
     - Install a **programmable thermostat** for better temperature control.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€8000 (including installation)

#### **4. Assess PV Potential**
   - **Recommendation:** Re-evaluate the **potential for PV panels** based on the building’s **orientation, roof space, and local regulations**.
   - **Action:**
     - Consult a **solar energy expert** to assess feasibility.
     - If feasible, install **PV panels on the roof** to reduce electricity costs.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€275/m² (including installation)

#### **5. Improve Ventilation System**
   - **Recommendation:** Replace the **ventilation system (D)** with a **more efficient system** (e.g., **heat recovery ventilation system**).
   - **Action:**
     - Ensure proper **airflow and filtration** to improve indoor air quality.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€3500 (including installation)
