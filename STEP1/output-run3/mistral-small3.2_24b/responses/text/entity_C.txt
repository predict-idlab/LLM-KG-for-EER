Given the information provided, here's a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your attached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan focuses on improving energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, considering the most optimal sequence of upgrades:

### 1. **Upgrade Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and heat emission systems (underfloor heating and electric convectors) with a more efficient system.
   - **Recommendation**: Consider a **heat pump** (air-source or hybrid) combined with underfloor heating or low-temperature radiators. Heat pumps are highly efficient and can significantly reduce energy consumption.
   - **Benefit**: Improves heating efficiency and reduces energy costs. This is a priority as the current systems are in poor condition.

### 2. **Improve Roof Insulation**
   - **Action**: Enhance the insulation of the main pitched roof, which currently has a moderate level of insulation.
   - **Recommendation**: Add an additional layer of insulation (e.g., mineral wool or rigid foam boards) to bring it up to current energy efficiency standards.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss through the roof, improving overall energy performance.

### 3. **Optimize Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Ensure the existing ventilation system (D) is functioning efficiently and consider upgrading to a **heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system** if not already in place.
   - **Recommendation**: If the current system is a basic mechanical ventilation system, upgrading to an HRV system can recover heat from exhausted air, reducing heating demands.
   - **Benefit**: Improves indoor air quality and reduces heat loss through ventilation.

### 4. **Upgrade Windows (if necessary)**
   - **Action**: Although the windows were renewed in 2024 and are in good condition, consider upgrading to **triple-glazed windows** if further improvements are needed.
   - **Recommendation**: Triple glazing provides better insulation than double glazing, especially in colder climates like Belgium.
   - **Benefit**: Further reduces heat loss through windows, enhancing energy efficiency.

### 5. **Maximize Photovoltaic (PV) Panel Efficiency**
   - **Action**: Ensure the existing PV panels are operating at peak efficiency.
   - **Recommendation**: Regular maintenance and cleaning of the panels. Consider adding a **battery storage system** to store excess energy for use during peak times or when the panels are not generating electricity.
   - **Benefit**: Increases self-consumption of solar energy, reducing reliance on the grid.

### 6. **Smart Thermostat and Controls**
   - **Action**: Install a **smart thermostat** to optimize heating schedules and reduce energy waste.
   - **Recommendation**: Choose a programmable or learning thermostat that can adapt to your daily routines.
   - **Benefit**: Enhances comfort and reduces energy consumption by ensuring heating is used efficiently.

### 7. **Sealing and Draft Proofing**
   - **Action**: Check for and seal any air leaks around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Recommendation**: Use weatherstripping, seals, or draft excluders to minimize heat loss.
   - **Benefit**: Simple and cost-effective way to improve energy efficiency.

### **Optimal Sequence**:
1. Upgrade the heating system (priority due to poor condition).
2. Improve roof insulation.
3. Optimize or upgrade the ventilation system.
4. Consider upgrading windows to triple glazing if further improvements are needed.
5. Maximize PV panel efficiency and add battery storage if feasible.
6. Install a smart thermostat and perform sealing and draft proofing.

This plan focuses on practical, cost-effective upgrades that will significantly improve your home's energy performance certificate (EPC) score and long-term operational performance.
