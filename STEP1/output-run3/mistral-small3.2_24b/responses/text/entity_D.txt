### 1. **Upgrade Roof Insulation (Pitched Roof)**

### 2. **Enhance Ventilation System (System D)**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the existing ventilation system to a more energy-efficient model, such as a heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system.
   - **Benefit**: Improves indoor air quality and reduces heat loss through ventilation.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Moderate priority, but important for long-term comfort and efficiency.

### 3. **Optimize Heat Pump Performance**
   - **Action**: Conduct a professional audit of the heat pump system to ensure it is operating at peak efficiency. Consider smart thermostat integration for better temperature control.
   - **Benefit**: Ensures the heat pump is running efficiently, reducing energy consumption.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Low cost, high impact on operational efficiency.

### 4. **Seal Air Leakage Points**
   - **Action**: Perform a thorough inspection and sealing of any air leakage points around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces drafts and heat loss, improving overall energy efficiency.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Low cost, high impact on comfort and efficiency.

### 5. **Expand Photovoltaic (PV) System**
   - **Action**: Assess the feasibility of adding more PV panels to increase renewable energy generation.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces reliance on grid electricity, lowering energy bills and carbon footprint.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Moderate to high priority, depending on available roof space and incentives.

### 6. **Upgrade to Triple Glazing (If Budget Allows)**
   - **Action**: Consider replacing the existing double-glazed windows with triple-glazed windows for better insulation.
   - **Benefit**: Further reduces heat loss through windows, improving overall energy performance.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Higher cost, but significant long-term savings and comfort benefits.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. **Upgrade Roof Insulation** (High impact, foundational improvement)
2. **Enhance Ventilation System** (Improves air quality and efficiency)
3. **Optimize Heat Pump Performance** (Ensures efficient heating)
4. **Seal Air Leakage Points** (Low-cost, high-impact improvement)
5. **Expand PV System** (Increases renewable energy generation)
6. **Upgrade to Triple Glazing** (Optional, if budget allows)

This plan prioritizes upgrades that offer the highest energy savings and cost-effectiveness, ensuring a practical and impactful renovation path.
