Here's a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your home in Ghent, Belgium, focusing on energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness:

### 1. **Insulate the Flat-Roof Extension**
   - **Priority**: High
   - **Action**: Install insulation on the flat-roof extension to match the moderate level of insulation in the main pitched roof.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss through the roof, improving overall thermal performance.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: High, as it directly addresses a significant heat loss area.

### 2. **Insulate the Exterior Walls**
   - **Priority**: High
   - **Action**: Apply external wall insulation (EWI) to the solid brick walls at the front and rear of the house.
   - **Benefit**: Significantly reduces heat loss through the walls, improving the building's energy performance.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Moderate to high, considering the long-term energy savings and the avoidance of internal disruption.
   - **Note**: Ensure the chosen insulation system is compatible with the historic character of the building.

### 3. **Insulate the Floor Above the Basement**
   - **Priority**: Moderate
   - **Action**: Install floor insulation above the basement to minimize heat loss through the floor.
   - **Benefit**: Enhances comfort and reduces heating costs.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Moderate, as it is less impactful than wall or roof insulation but still beneficial.

### 4. **Replace the Heating System**
   - **Priority**: High
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and radiator system with a modern, energy-efficient heat pump or condensing boiler.
   - **Benefit**: Improves heating efficiency and reduces energy consumption.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: High, given the long-term energy savings and potential government incentives for renewable heating systems.
   - **Note**: Consider a heat pump if the building's insulation levels are significantly improved (steps 1-3).

### 5. **Upgrade the Ventilation System**
   - **Priority**: Moderate
   - **Action**: Install a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR) to improve air quality and reduce heat loss.
   - **Benefit**: Enhances indoor air quality and recovers heat from exhaust air, reducing heating demand.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Moderate, as it is more expensive than natural ventilation but offers long-term energy savings.

### 6. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**
   - **Priority**: Moderate
   - **Action**: Assess the suitability of the roof for photovoltaic (PV) panels and install them to generate renewable electricity.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces electricity costs and carbon footprint.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: High, considering the potential for feed-in tariffs or net metering in Belgium.

### 7. **Review and Upgrade Windows**
   - **Priority**: Low
   - **Action**: Although the current windows have double glazing, consider upgrading to triple glazing or secondary glazing if budget allows.
   - **Benefit**: Further reduces heat loss through windows.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Low to moderate, as the existing double glazing is already an improvement over single glazing.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate the flat-roof extension** (quick win with immediate impact).
2. **Insulate the exterior walls** (most significant impact on energy performance).
3. **Insulate the floor above the basement** (complements wall insulation).
4. **Replace the heating system** (ensure it is sized appropriately for the improved insulation).
5. **Upgrade the ventilation system** (enhances comfort and efficiency).
6. **Install photovoltaic panels** (generates renewable energy to offset consumption).
7. **Review and upgrade windows** (if budget and priorities allow).

This plan prioritizes actions that deliver the most significant energy savings and cost-effectiveness, ensuring a practical and phased approach to renovation.
