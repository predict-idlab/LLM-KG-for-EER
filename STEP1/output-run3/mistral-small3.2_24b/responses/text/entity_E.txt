Based on the information provided, here is a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan focuses on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, with a logical sequence of upgrades:

### 1. **Insulate the Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls at the front, rear, left side, and right side of the house.
   - **Why**: Cavity wall insulation is a cost-effective way to significantly reduce heat loss, especially in older homes.
   - **Notes**: Ensure the insulation material is compatible with the existing cavity walls and consider partial filling if full filling is not feasible.

### 2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space.
   - **Why**: This will prevent heat loss through the floor, which is a common issue in older homes.
   - **Notes**: Use rigid insulation boards or other suitable materials that can be installed directly on the floor.

### 3. **Replace the Windows**
   - **Action**: Replace the single-glazed windows with double or triple-glazed windows.
   - **Why**: New glazing will significantly reduce heat loss through the windows and improve overall comfort.
   - **Notes**: Since the wooden frames are in good condition, consider secondary glazing as a more cost-effective alternative if full replacement is not feasible.

### 4. **Upgrade the Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and heating emission systems with a modern, efficient system.
   - **Why**: A new boiler and heating system will improve energy efficiency and reduce operating costs.
   - **Notes**: Consider a condensing boiler or a heat pump, depending on the available space and budget. Ensure the new system is compatible with the existing radiators and underfloor heating.

### 5. **Improve Natural Ventilation**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the natural ventilation system to a more efficient one.
   - **Why**: Better ventilation will improve indoor air quality and reduce the risk of moisture-related issues.
   - **Notes**: Consider installing air vents with adjustable flaps or a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR) if budget allows.

### 6. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof.
   - **Why**: PV panels will generate renewable energy, reducing electricity costs and improving the energy performance certificate.
   - **Notes**: Ensure the roof structure can support the panels and consider battery storage if budget allows.

### 7. **Seal Air Leaks**
   - **Action**: Seal any air leaks around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Why**: Air sealing will prevent drafts and reduce heat loss.
   - **Notes**: Use weatherstripping, caulk, or other suitable sealing materials.

### 8. **Monitor and Maintain the Renovations**
   - **Action**: Regularly monitor the performance of the renovated systems and maintain them as needed.
   - **Why**: Proper maintenance will ensure the longevity and efficiency of the upgrades.
   - **Notes**: Schedule annual inspections of the heating system, insulation, and ventilation system.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. Insulate the exterior walls.
2. Insulate the floor above the crawl space.
3. Replace the windows or install secondary glazing.
4. Upgrade the heating system.
5. Improve natural ventilation.
6. Install photovoltaic panels.
7. Seal air leaks.
8. Monitor and maintain the renovations.

This plan prioritizes upgrades that provide the most significant energy savings and cost reductions, ensuring a logical and effective renovation process.
