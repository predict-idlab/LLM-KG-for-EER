### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### 1. **Assess Current Energy Performance**
   - **Current EPC Score:** 262 kWh/m²year
   - **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC):** The building's current EPC score indicates a significant opportunity for improvement. The score suggests that the building is not performing optimally in terms of energy efficiency.

#### 2. **Identify Key Areas for Improvement**
   - **Wall Insulation:** The rear and front walls are currently not insulated (`NotInsulated`). Improving insulation will significantly reduce heat loss.
   - **Roof Insulation:** The main roof is moderately insulated, but the extension roof is not insulated. Insulating the extension roof will enhance overall thermal performance.
   - **Floor Insulation:** The floor above the basement is not insulated. Insulating this floor will improve comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Window Glazing:** The windows in the living area and bedrooms have double glazing, which is good, but there may be opportunities to improve the framing or add additional insulation around the windows.

#### 3. **Recommended Renovation Actions**
   - **Insulate Walls:**
     - **Action:** Install external wall insulation on the rear and front walls.
     - **Material:** Use appropriate insulation material (e.g., mineral wool, rigid foam) suitable for external wall insulation.
     - **Benefit:** Reduces heat loss through walls, improving thermal comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Insulate Roof:**
     - **Action:** Insulate the extension roof.
     - **Material:** Use insulation material suitable for roof insulation (e.g., rigid foam, mineral wool).
     - **Benefit:** Reduces heat loss through the roof, improving overall thermal performance.
   - **Insulate Floor:**
     - **Action:** Insulate the floor above the basement.
     - **Material:** Use appropriate floor insulation material (e.g., rigid foam, mineral wool).
     - **Benefit:** Improves comfort and energy efficiency by reducing heat loss through the floor.
   - **Window and Frame Upgrades:**
     - **Action:** Ensure that the window frames are well-insulated and that the glazing is in good condition.
     - **Material:** Use high-quality sealing materials to prevent drafts and improve insulation around windows.
     - **Benefit:** Enhances the thermal performance of windows, reducing heat loss and improving comfort.

#### 4. **Additional Considerations**
   - **Ventilation System:** The building has a ventilation system type A, which works on the basis of natural air supply and mechanical air exhaust. Ensure that the ventilation system is operating efficiently and effectively.
   - **Heating System:** The building uses a radiator for space heating. Consider upgrading to a more energy-efficient heating system, such as a heat pump, if feasible and cost-effective.
   - **Solar Potential:** The building has potential for solar panel installation. Consider installing solar panels to generate renewable energy and reduce energy costs.

#### 5. **Implementation Timeline**
   - **Phase 1:** Assess and plan the renovation actions.
   - **Phase 2:** Execute the insulation and window upgrades.
   - **Phase 3:** Install additional energy-efficient measures, such as solar panels, if feasible.

#### 6. **Cost-Effectiveness and Long-Term Benefits**
   - **Cost:** The cost of the renovation will depend on the materials used and the complexity of the work. Ensure that the renovation is cost-effective by comparing quotes from different contractors.
   - **Long-Term Benefits:** The renovation will improve the building's energy efficiency, reduce energy costs, and enhance thermal comfort. It may also increase the building's market value.

#### 7. **Monitoring and Maintenance**
   - **Monitoring:** Regularly monitor the building's energy performance to ensure that the renovation has achieved the desired improvements.
   - **Maintenance:** Maintain the insulation and other energy-efficient measures to ensure their longevity and effectiveness.

By following this renovation plan, the building's energy efficiency can be significantly improved, leading to reduced energy costs and enhanced comfort for the residents.
