Based on the provided building data and the RENO ontology, here is the proposed renovation path. This plan follows the "fabric-first" principle: reducing energy demand through insulation before upgrading heating systems and adding renewable energy sources.

### **Building Analysis Summary**
*   **Current State:** A detached building (1862) with an EPC score of **165** (B label).
*   **Critical Weaknesses:** All four exterior walls are **not insulated** (cavity walls), and all windows use **single glazing**.
*   **Strengths:** The gable roof is already well-insulated, and the wooden window frames are in good condition.
*   **Constraints:** No potential for geothermal energy; no heat grid available.

---

### **Proposed Renovation Path**

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope (The "Quick Wins" & High Impact)**
*Goal: Minimize heat loss to reduce the required capacity of future heating systems.*

1.  **Insulate Wall Cavities (`InsulateWallCavity`):** 
    *   **Action:** Inject insulation into the four non-insulated cavity walls.
    *   **Justification:** This is the most cost-effective insulation method (lowest unit price in the ontology) and has a low nuisance level.
2.  **Upgrade Glazing (`ReplaceGlazing`):** 
    *   **Action:** Replace single glazing with double or triple glazing.
    *   **Justification:** Single glazing is a major source of heat loss. Since the wooden frames are in "Good State," replacing only the glass is more cost-effective than replacing entire window units.
3.  **Insulate Floor (`InsulateFloorAboveBasement`):** 
    *   **Action:** Insulate the floor above the crawl space.
    *   **Justification:** This prevents cold drafts from the ground, improving thermal comfort and efficiency.

#### **Phase 2: Ventilation & Systems Upgrade**
*Goal: Improve indoor air quality and transition to high-efficiency heating.*

4.  **Upgrade Ventilation (`ReplaceVentilationSystem`):** 
    *   **Action:** Transition from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more controlled system (e.g., System C or D).
    *   **Justification:** Once the building envelope is sealed (Phase 1), mechanical ventilation is necessary to prevent humidity buildup and ensure healthy air exchange.
5.  **Install Heat Pump (`PlaceAirWaterHeatpump`):** 
    *   **Action:** Replace the current boiler/electric convector setup with an air-to-water heat pump.
    *   **Justification:** Geothermal is not viable for this site. An air-water heat pump is the most efficient alternative for a building that already has radiators and floor heating.

#### **Phase 3: Renewable Energy Integration**
*Goal: Offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump.*

6.  **Install Solar Panels (`PlacePVPanelsOnRoof`):** 
    *   **Action:** Install PV panels on the gable roof.
    *   **Justification:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Generating your own electricity is the final step to maximize energy efficiency and reduce long-term operational costs.
