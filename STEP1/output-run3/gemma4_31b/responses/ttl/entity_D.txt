### Energy Efficiency Renovation Analysis

**Building Status Overview:**
The property is a modern semi-detached building (constructed in 2017) that is already performing at a high level of energy efficiency. With an **EPC score of 87.0**, it falls within the **A Label** category. It is already equipped with high-efficiency systems, including a **Heat Pump** for generation and a **Ventilation System D** (the most efficient mechanical ventilation type). Most of the building envelope (walls, floor above basement, and flat roof) is already "Well Insulated."

Given the owner's motivation to further improve the EPC, the focus shifts from "major overhaul" to "marginal gains."

---

### Proposed Renovation Path

Since the building is already highly efficient, the goal is to target the remaining thermal bridges and the only moderately insulated element to push the EPC score toward the A+ range.

#### Step 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)
**Action: Insulate Gable Roof**
*   **Reasoning:** The `RoofMain` is the only structural element currently rated as **Moderately Insulated**. In a building where walls and floors are already well-insulated, the roof becomes the primary source of heat loss (heat rises).
*   **Ontology Reference:** `reno:InsulateGableRoof`
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** Moderate. With a unit price of 60/m², this is the most cost-effective way to achieve a measurable drop in energy consumption.
*   **Point of Attention:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side to prevent condensation.

#### Step 2: Glazing Upgrade (Secondary Priority)
**Action: Replace Double Glazing with Triple Glazing**
*   **Reasoning:** The building currently uses `DoubleGlazing`. While efficient, upgrading to `TripleGlazing` further reduces U-values and improves thermal comfort. Since the `WoodenWindowFrame` is in "Good State" (value 8), it may be possible to replace only the glazing rather than the entire window unit.
*   **Ontology Reference:** `reno:ReplaceGlazing`
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** Lower than roof insulation, but provides a tangible boost to the EPC score.
*   **Point of Attention:** Verify that the existing wooden frames can support the additional weight of triple-pane glass.

---

### Excluded Upgrades & Justification

To remain cost-effective, the following were analyzed and rejected based on the provided data:
*   **Geothermal Heating:** Rejected. The site data explicitly states `potentialForHorizontalGeothermalCaptation false` and `potentialForVerticalGeothermalDrilling false`.
*   **Additional PV Panels:** Rejected. The building already has PV panels, and the data indicates `hasPotentialForPV false`.
*   **Wall/Floor Insulation:** Rejected. `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Rear`, and `FloorAboveBasement` are already `WellInsulated`.
*   **HVAC Replacement:** Rejected. The current Heat Pump and Ventilation System D are already industry-standard for high-efficiency homes.
