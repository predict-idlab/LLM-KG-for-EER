### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope (High Impact, Low-to-Moderate Cost)
*These steps prevent energy waste and are the most cost-effective ways to lower the EPC score.*

1.  **Insulate Gable Roof** (Ordering: 28)
    *   **Reasoning:** The main roof is currently not insulated. Heat rises, and this is the fastest way to reduce energy consumption.
    *   **Attention:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side to prevent condensation.
2.  **Insulate Exterior Walls** (Ordering: 40)
    *   **Reasoning:** All three outer walls (Front, Rear, Right) are full brick and not insulated. Exterior insulation is generally more efficient than interior insulation.
    *   **Attention:** Since there is no historical protection, this is the optimal choice to eliminate thermal bridges.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement** (Ordering: 49)
    *   **Reasoning:** The floor above the basement is not insulated, leading to significant heat loss into the lower level.

#### Phase 2: Ventilation & Air Quality (Moderate Impact)
*Improving ventilation is necessary once the house is "sealed" via insulation to prevent humidity issues.*

4.  **Replace Ventilation System** (Ordering: 52)
    *   **Reasoning:** The building currently uses Ventilation System A (natural supply/exhaust). Upgrading to System C or D will improve air quality and thermal efficiency.

#### Phase 3: Energy Generation & Emission (High Investment, Long-term Gain)
*Now that the building is insulated, we can install a smaller, more efficient heating system.*

5.  **Install Air-Water Heat Pump** (Ordering: 67)
    *   **Reasoning:** There is a stated reason to change the emission system, and the building has radiators and floor heating (compatible with low-temperature systems). Geothermal and Heat Grids are unavailable at this site.
