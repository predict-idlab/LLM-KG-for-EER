### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Low-Hanging Fruit)
The goal here is to reduce heat loss before upgrading heating systems, ensuring the new systems are sized correctly and operate efficiently.

1.  **Cavity Wall Insulation (`InsulateWallCavity`):**
    *   **Analysis:** All four exterior walls are cavity walls and currently "Not Insulated."
    *   **Reasoning:** This is the most cost-effective upgrade in the ontology (€22/m²) with a "low" nuisance level. Since there is no historical protection, this is the highest priority for immediate EPC improvement.
2.  **Floor Insulation (`InsulateFloorAboveBasement`):**
    *   **Analysis:** The building has a floor above a crawl space.
    *   **Reasoning:** At €30/m², this is a low-cost, low-nuisance intervention that prevents cold drafts from the ground.
3.  **Glazing Upgrade (`ReplaceGlazing`):**
    *   **Analysis:** All windows currently use "Single Glazing," but the wooden frames are in "Good State" (Value: 8).
    *   **Reasoning:** Rather than replacing entire windows (€800/m²), replacing only the glazing (€200/m²) is far more cost-effective while significantly reducing thermal bridging.

### Phase 2: Air Quality and Ventilation
As the building becomes more airtight through insulation and new glazing, ventilation must be upgraded to prevent moisture buildup and maintain health.

4.  **Ventilation System Upgrade (`ReplaceVentilationSystem`):**
    *   **Analysis:** The building currently uses Ventilation System A (natural supply/exhaust).
    *   **Reasoning:** Upgrading to a more modern system (e.g., System C or D) ensures consistent air exchange and complements the improved thermal envelope.

### Phase 3: Sustainable Heating Transition
With the heat loss minimized, we can now address the owner's desire to change the emission system.

5.  **Install Air-Water Heat Pump (`PlaceAirWaterHeatpump`):**
    *   **Analysis:** The building has a reason to change the emission system. Geothermal options are not viable (potential is `false`), and there is no heat grid available.
    *   **Reasoning:** An air-water heat pump is the most efficient alternative to the current boiler/electric convector setup, utilizing electricity to move heat rather than generating it from fuel.

### Phase 4: Renewable Energy Integration
The final step is to offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump.

6.  **Install Roof PV Panels (`PlacePVPanelsOnRoof`):**
    *   **Analysis:** The building is confirmed to have potential for PV.
    *   **Reasoning:** By placing panels on the roof, the owner can generate their own electricity to power the heat pump and other appliances, lowering long-term operational costs.
    *   **Point of Attention:** Per the ontology, ensure no asbestos is present in the roof structure before installation.
