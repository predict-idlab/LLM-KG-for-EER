*   **Sequence Logic:** By following the **Roof $\rightarrow$ Walls $\rightarrow$ Floor $\rightarrow$ Ventilation $\rightarrow$ Heat Pump $\rightarrow$ PV** sequence, we ensure the heat pump is sized correctly for a well-insulated house, preventing the installation of an oversized, more expensive unit.
