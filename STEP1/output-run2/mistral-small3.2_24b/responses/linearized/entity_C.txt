### **Renovation Plan for Attached Building (2023 Construction)**
**Goal:** Improve the Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) score from 50 to a higher label (e.g., C or B) while ensuring cost-effectiveness and minimal disruption.

---

#### **1. Insulate the Roof (RoofMain and RoofExtension)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof (RoofMain) and flat roof (RoofExtension).
   - **Why:** Roof insulation is critical for reducing heat loss, especially in gable and flat roofs with moderate insulation (RoofMain) and well-insulated (RoofExtension) grades.
   - **Recommendation:**
     - For the gable roof (RoofMain), use walkable insulation if needed (e.g., attic floor access).
     - Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation.
   - **Cost:** ~€50–€60/m² (RoofMain) and €50/m² (RoofExtension).

#### **2. Insulate the Walls (Wall_Front and Wall_Rear)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the front (Wall_Front) and rear (Wall_Rear) walls, both of which are well-insulated (FullBrickWall type).
   - **Why:** Wall insulation reduces heat loss and improves thermal comfort.
   - **Recommendation:**
     - If the walls are exterior-facing, consider exterior insulation (permits may be required).
     - For interior insulation, relocate sockets, pipes, and radiators against the walls.
   - **Cost:** ~€150–€200/m² (interior insulation).

#### **3. Upgrade Window Glazing (WindowsLivingArea and WindowsBedrooms)**
   - **Action:** Replace or upgrade the glazing of the windows in the living area and bedrooms.
   - **Why:** The current double glazing (WindowGlazingLivingArea and WindowGlazingBedrooms) can be improved to triple glazing for better thermal performance.
   - **Recommendation:**
     - Ensure the window frames (WoodenWindowFrame) are in good condition and can support the new glazing.
     - Check for thermal breaks in the frames to prevent heat loss.
   - **Cost:** ~€200–€300/m² (triple glazing).

#### **4. Replace the Emission System (EmissionSystem)**
   - **Action:** Replace the electric convector (EmissionSystem) with a more efficient system.
   - **Why:** Electric convectors are energy-intensive and less efficient than alternatives like heat pumps.
   - **Recommendation:**
     - Consider a low-temperature emission system (e.g., underfloor heating) or an air-water heat pump.
     - Ensure the new system is compatible with the existing floor heating (EmissionSystem).
   - **Cost:** ~€35–€100/m² (low-temperature emission system).

#### **5. Upgrade the Ventilation System (VentilationSystem)**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing ventilation system (VentilationSystemD) with a more efficient system (e.g., VentilationSystemA or B).
   - **Why:** Ventilation systems impact air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Recommendation:**
     - Choose a system that works on natural air supply and mechanical air exhaust (VentilationSystemA).
     - Ensure proper ductwork is installed.
   - **Cost:** ~€17–€35/m² (VentilationSystemA).

#### **6. Consider Solar Panels (PVPanels)**
   - **Action:** Install plug-and-play PV panels on the roof (PVPanels).
   - **Why:** Solar panels can reduce energy costs and improve sustainability.
   - **Recommendation:**
     - Check the roof’s orientation and shading to ensure optimal sunlight exposure.
     - Ensure the panels are securely installed and comply with local regulations.
   - **Cost:** ~€275/m² (PVPanelsOnRoof).

#### **7. Address Asbestos (If Applicable)**
   - **Action:** Remove asbestos from the roof (RoofMain) if present.
   - **Why:** Asbestos removal is necessary for safety and compliance.
   - **Recommendation:**
     - Hire a licensed asbestos removal company for non-bonded asbestos.
   - **Cost:** ~€40–€45/m² (RoofMain).
