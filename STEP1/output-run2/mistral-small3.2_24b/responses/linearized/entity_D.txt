### 1. **Insulate the Roof (RoofMain and RoofExtension)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof (RoofMain) and the flat roof (RoofExtension).

### 2. **Replace Single-Glazed Windows with Double-Glazed Windows**
   - **Action:** Replace the single-glazed windows in the bedrooms (WindowsBedrooms) with double-glazed windows.
   - **Reason:** Single-glazing has poor thermal properties, and replacing it with double-glazing will improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Materials:** Double-glazed windows with a suitable frame material (e.g., wood or aluminium).
   - **Cost:** Approximately €200 per window.
   - **Benefit:** Improved insulation and reduced heat loss through windows.

### 3. **Install Solar Panels (PVPanels)**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof.

### 4. **Upgrade the Ventilation System**

   - **Benefit:** Reduced energy consumption and lower heating costs.
