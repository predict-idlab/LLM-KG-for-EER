To create an energy-efficient and cost-effective renovation plan for the given building, we need to prioritize interventions that provide the highest energy savings and improve the building's Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) label. Here's a step-by-step renovation plan:

### **1. Insulate the Roof (RoofMain and RoofExtension)**
- **Action**: Insulate the gable roof (RoofMain) and the flat roof (RoofExtension).
- **Type**: InsulateGableRoof and InsulateFlatRoof.
- **Reasoning**:
  - The roofs have an insulation grade of "NotInsulated," which is a significant source of heat loss.
  - Insulating the roofs will improve thermal comfort and reduce heating costs.
  - Roof insulation typically offers a high return on investment due to its large surface area.
- **Priority**: High.
- **Ordering**: Roof insulation should be done first as it is a major source of heat loss and can significantly impact the EPC label.

### **2. Replace Double Glazing with Triple Glazing (WindowsLivingArea and WindowsBedrooms)**
- **Action**: Replace the existing double glazing (DoubleGlazing) with triple glazing (TripleGlazing) in the living area and bedrooms.
- **Type**: ReplaceGlazing.
- **Reasoning**:
  - Triple glazing provides better thermal insulation than double glazing, reducing heat loss through windows.
  - This will further improve the EPC label and enhance thermal comfort.
- **Priority**: Medium.
- **Ordering**: After roof insulation, as windows are another significant source of heat loss.

### **3. Insulate the Walls (Wall_Front and Wall_Rear)**
- **Action**: Insulate the front and rear walls (Wall_Front and Wall_Rear), which are currently "NotInsulated."
- **Type**: InsulateWallExterior or InsulateWallInterior, depending on feasibility and cost.
- **Reasoning**:
  - Wall insulation will further reduce heat loss and improve the building's thermal performance.
  - Exterior insulation is generally more effective but may require permits depending on local regulations.
- **Priority**: Medium.
- **Ordering**: After windows, as walls are another major source of heat loss.

### **4. Upgrade the Emission System (EmissionSystem)**
- **Action**: Replace the existing radiator (Radiator) with a low-temperature emission system or a heat pump boiler.
- **Type**: PlaceLowTemperatureEmission or PlaceHeatPumpBoiler.
- **Reasoning**:
  - A low-temperature emission system or heat pump boiler is more energy-efficient than a traditional radiator.
  - This will improve the heating system's efficiency and reduce energy consumption.
- **Priority**: Medium.
- **Ordering**: After insulating the building envelope (roof, walls, windows).

### **5. Install a Ventilation System (VentilationSystem)**
- **Action**: Install or upgrade the ventilation system to a more efficient type (e.g., VentilationSystemB or VentilationSystemC).
- **Type**: ReplaceVentilationSystem.
- **Reasoning**:
  - A more efficient ventilation system will improve indoor air quality and reduce energy losses.
  - This can also help meet the building's heating and cooling needs more efficiently.
- **Priority**: Low.
- **Ordering**: After upgrading the heating system.

### **6. Consider Additional Measures (if budget allows)**
- **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels if the building has potential for PV (hasPotentialForPV is true).
- **Type**: PlacePVPanelsOnRoof or PlacePVPanelsInGarden.
- **Reasoning**:
  - PV panels can generate renewable energy, reducing the building's reliance on grid electricity.
  - This can further improve the EPC label and reduce energy costs.
- **Priority**: Low.
- **Ordering**: After completing the core insulation and system upgrades.

### **Summary of Renovation Steps**:
1. **Insulate the Roof** (RoofMain and RoofExtension).
2. **Replace Double Glazing with Triple Glazing** (WindowsLivingArea and WindowsBedrooms).
3. **Insulate the Walls** (Wall_Front and Wall_Rear).
4. **Upgrade the Emission System** (EmissionSystem).
5. **Install a Ventilation System** (VentilationSystem).
6. **Consider Additional Measures** (e.g., PV panels).

This plan prioritizes interventions that provide the highest energy savings and improve the building's EPC label, ensuring a cost-effective and energy-efficient renovation.
