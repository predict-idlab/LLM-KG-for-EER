### Renovation Plan for the Building at http://example.com/Building

#### **Objective:**
Improve the energy efficiency of the building to achieve a better EPC label, while ensuring cost-effectiveness and practicality.

#### **Key Findings from the Ontology:**
1. **Current EPC Score:** 87 (C label)
2. **Insulation Grades:**
   - Walls (Front, Rear, Left): Well Insulated
   - Floor Above Basement: Well Insulated
   - Roofs (Main, Extension): Well Insulated (Main) and Moderately Insulated (Extension)
3. **Window Frames:** Wooden frames in good condition (State Value: 8).
4. **Ventilation System:** Ventilation System D (mechanical air supply and air exhaust).
5. **Generation System:** Heat Pump.
6. **PV Panels:** Present but no indication of performance.
7. **Emissions System:** Currently in place but no details on type or condition.

#### **Proposed Renovation Steps:**

1. **Improve Roof Insulation (Extension):**
   - **Action:** Insulate the flat roof extension to match the well-insulated standard of the main roof.
   - **Rationale:** The main roof is well insulated, but the extension is only moderately insulated. This discrepancy can be addressed to improve overall thermal performance.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€60/m² (based on ontology's "InsulateFlatRoof" suggestion).
   - **Priority:** Medium (Ordering: 25 in the ontology).

2. **Upgrade Window Glazing:**
   - **Action:** Replace existing double-glazing with triple-glazing for all windows (bedrooms and living area).
   - **Rationale:** Triple-glazing offers better thermal and acoustic insulation compared to double-glazing, which can significantly reduce heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€200/m² (based on ontology's "ReplaceGlazing" suggestion).
   - **Priority:** High (Ordering: 43 in the ontology).

3. **Install Additional PV Panels:**
   - **Action:** Add more PV panels on the roof if space permits, or consider ground-mounted panels if the roof is unsuitable.
   - **Rationale:** The building has potential for PV but currently has no indication of performance. Expanding the PV capacity can reduce energy costs and improve sustainability.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€275/m² for roof installation (based on ontology's "PlacePVPanelsOnRoof" suggestion).
   - **Priority:** Medium (Ordering: 85 in the ontology).

4. **Upgrade Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing Ventilation System D with a more efficient system (e.g., Ventilation System C).
   - **Rationale:** Ventilation System D may not be as efficient as other systems. Upgrading can improve air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Estimated Cost:** ~€3500 (based on ontology's "ReplaceVentilationSystem" suggestion).
   - **Priority:** Medium (Ordering: 52 in the ontology).

5. **Heat Pump Maintenance/Upgrade:**
   - **Action:** Ensure the heat pump is well-maintained or consider upgrading to a more efficient model if necessary.
   - **Rationale:** The heat pump is the primary heating source, and its efficiency directly impacts the building's energy performance.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Variable (depends on maintenance or upgrade needs).
   - **Priority:** High (Ordering: 61-79 in the ontology for various heat pump suggestions).

#### **Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Insulate Roof Extension** (Step 1)
2. **Upgrade Window Glazing** (Step 2)
3. **Install Additional PV Panels** (Step 3)
4. **Upgrade Ventilation System** (Step 4)
5. **Heat Pump Maintenance/Upgrade** (Step 5)

#### **Expected Outcomes:**
- **Improved EPC Label:** Aim for a B label or better.
- **Reduced Energy Costs:** Lower energy consumption due to better insulation and more efficient systems.
- **Enhanced Comfort:** Better thermal comfort and air quality.

#### **Notes:**
- **Historical Protection:** No elements are historically protected, so no restrictions on modifications.
- **Permits:** Check local regulations for any required permits, especially for structural changes.

This plan prioritizes actions that offer the most significant impact on energy efficiency while considering cost and practicality. The steps are sequenced to maximize benefits from each renovation phase.
