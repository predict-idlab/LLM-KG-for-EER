### Renovation Plan for the Detached Building at Address 9000, Belgium

#### Objective:
Improve the energy efficiency of the building to lower the energy consumption and costs while ensuring comfort and compliance with regulations.

#### Key Recommendations:

1. **Insulate Cavity Walls**
   - **Action:** Insulate the cavity walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right).
   - **Justification:** The current insulation grade is "Not Insulated," which indicates significant energy loss.
   - **Method:** Use injected insulation, ensuring the cavity is wide enough and free from contamination.
   - **Cost:** ~€22 per m².
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduce heat loss through walls, improving thermal comfort and lowering heating costs.

2. **Replace Single Glazing with Double Glazing**
   - **Action:** Replace the single glazing in all windows with double glazing.
   - **Justification:** Single glazing has poor thermal and acoustic properties, leading to higher energy consumption.
   - **Method:** Ensure the window frames are in good condition and have the right thickness for the new glazing.
   - **Cost:** ~€200 per m².
   - **Expected Impact:** Improve thermal performance of windows, reducing heat loss and enhancing comfort.

3. **Upgrade the Heating System**
   - **Action:** Replace the current air heating system (electric convectors, radiators) with a more efficient system.
   - **Justification:** Air heating systems are generally less efficient and may not provide uniform heating.
   - **Method:** Consider installing a heat pump or a modern boiler (e.g., a condensing boiler) for better efficiency.
   - **Cost:** Varies by system type (heat pump: ~€15,000–€25,000; boiler: ~€3,000–€8,000).
   - **Expected Impact:** Lower energy consumption and costs, improved comfort.

4. **Improve Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural air supply and exhaust) to Ventilation System B or C.
   - **Justification:** Better ventilation improves air quality and can reduce the risk of moisture-related issues.
   - **Method:** Install mechanical ventilation with heat recovery (if possible) to maintain efficiency.
   - **Cost:** ~€1,500–€4,000.
   - **Expected Impact:** Better indoor air quality and potential energy savings.

5. **Assess Roof Insulation**
   - **Action:** Evaluate the insulation of the gable roof.
   - **Justification:** The roof is currently well insulated, but ensuring no heat loss through the roof is critical.
   - **Method:** Check for any gaps or areas where insulation may have degraded.
   - **Cost:** Varies by repair needs.
   - **Expected Impact:** Ensure no heat loss through the roof, maintaining energy efficiency.

6. **Consider Solar Panels**
   - **Action:** Evaluate the feasibility of installing solar panels.
   - **Justification:** The building has potential for PV installation, which could generate renewable energy.
   - **Method:** Assess roof orientation, shading, and local regulations. Consider roof-mounted or ground-mounted options.
   - **Cost:** ~€275 per m².
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduce electricity costs and carbon footprint.

#### Prioritization:
1. Insulate cavity walls (high impact, relatively low cost).
2. Replace single glazing with double glazing (significant impact on comfort and energy efficiency).
3. Upgrade the heating system (critical for long-term energy savings and comfort).
4. Improve the ventilation system (important for air quality and moisture control).
5. Assess roof insulation (prevent heat loss through the roof).
6. Consider solar panels (long-term investment with environmental benefits).

#### Additional Considerations:
- **Permits:** Check local regulations for any required permits, especially for structural changes or solar panel installations.
- **Professional Assistance:** Consult with contractors or architects for expert advice, particularly for insulation and system upgrades.
- **Incentives:** Research available incentives or subsidies for energy-efficient renovations in Belgium.

This plan focuses on practical, cost-effective upgrades that will significantly improve the building's energy efficiency and comfort.
