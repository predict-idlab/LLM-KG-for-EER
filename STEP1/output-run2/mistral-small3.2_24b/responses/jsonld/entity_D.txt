### Renovation Plan for the Building

**1. Evaluate Current Energy Performance:**
   - The building has an EPC score of 87.
   - **Objective:** Improve the EPC score to achieve better energy efficiency.

**2. Assess Building Elements:**
   - **Roof:**
     - Main Roof (Gable Roof): Moderately Insulated
     - Extension Roof (Flat Roof): Well Insulated
   - **Walls:**
     - All walls are well insulated and do not have historical protection.
   - **Floors:**
     - Floor Above Basement: Well Insulated
   - **Windows:**
     - Windows in Living Area and Bedrooms: Framed with Wooden Frames and Double Glazing.
   - **Ventilation System:**
     - Ventilation System D is present.
   - **PV Panels:**
     - PV Panels are present.
   - **Heat Pump:**
     - Heat Pump is present as the main generation system.
   - **Emission System:**
     - Emission System is present.

**3. Identify Key Renovation Suggestions:**
   - **Improve Roof Insulation:**
     - The main roof (Gable Roof) is moderately insulated. Consider improving its insulation to enhance overall energy efficiency.
     - **Suggestion:** Insulate Gable Roof (Ordering: 28)
   - **Upgrade Windows:**
     - The windows have double glazing, which is good, but consider upgrading to triple glazing for better insulation.
     - **Suggestion:** Replace Windows (Ordering: 46)
   - **Optimize Ventilation System:**
     - The ventilation system is of type D, which is efficient but may require maintenance or upgrades to ensure optimal performance.
     - **Suggestion:** Replace Ventilation System (Ordering: 52)
   - **Consider Additional PV Panels:**
     - The building has potential for PV panels, which can further reduce energy consumption and costs.
     - **Suggestion:** Place PV Panels on Roof (Ordering: 85)
   - **Heat Pump Optimization:**
     - Ensure the heat pump is functioning optimally and consider regular maintenance to maintain efficiency.
     - **Suggestion:** Regular Maintenance of Heat Pump (Ordering: 61)

**4. Cost and Feasibility Analysis:**
   - **Insulate Gable Roof:**
     - Cost: €60 per m²
   - **Replace Windows:**
     - Cost: €800 per m²
   - **Replace Ventilation System:**
     - Cost: €3500 + €17 per m²
   - **Place PV Panels on Roof:**
     - Cost: €275 per m²
   - **Regular Maintenance of Heat Pump:**
     - Cost: €1500 (one-time)

**5. Execution Plan:**
   - **Phase 1: Roof Insulation and Window Replacement**
     - Start with insulating the gable roof and replacing windows to improve thermal comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Phase 2: Ventilation System Upgrade**
     - Upgrade the ventilation system to ensure better air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Phase 3: PV Panel Installation**
     - Install additional PV panels to reduce reliance on grid electricity and lower energy costs.
   - **Phase 4: Heat Pump Maintenance**
     - Schedule regular maintenance for the heat pump to ensure it operates at peak efficiency.

**6. Expected Outcomes:**
   - Improved EPC score leading to better energy efficiency and lower energy bills.
   - Enhanced thermal comfort within the building.
   - Reduced carbon footprint through the use of renewable energy sources.

**7. Next Steps:**
   - Consult with a certified energy expert to conduct a detailed energy audit and provide specific recommendations.
   - Obtain necessary permits and approvals for the planned renovations.
   - Schedule the renovations in a logical sequence to minimize disruption and maximize efficiency.

This plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective and practical. The recommendations are based on the current state of the building and the goals of improving the EPC score and overall energy performance.
