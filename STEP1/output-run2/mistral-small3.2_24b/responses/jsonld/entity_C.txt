### Renovation Plan for Example Building

#### **Current State Overview**
- **Building Type**: Attached building
- **Construction Year**: 2023
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score**: 50
- **Current Insulation Grades**:
  - **RoofMain**: Moderately Insulated
  - **RoofExtension**: Well Insulated
  - **Wall_Front**: Well Insulated
  - **Wall_Rear**: Well Insulated
  - **FloorAboveBasement**: Well Insulated
- **Potential for PV Panels**: No
- **Potential for Horizontal Geothermal Captation**: No
- **Potential for Vertical Geothermal Drilling**: No
- **Presence of Heat Grid**: No
- **Current Emission System**: Electric Convector and Floor Heating
- **Reason to Change Emission System**: Yes
- **Current Ventilation System**: Ventilation System D

#### **Renovation Goals**
1. **Improve the EPC Score**: Aim for an A+ label (<100 kWh/m²year) or at least an A label (100-200 kWh/m²year).
2. **Enhance Thermal Comfort**: Ensure all living spaces are well insulated and have efficient heating systems.
3. **Optimize Energy Efficiency**: Reduce energy consumption and consider sustainable energy sources.

#### **Recommended Renovation Steps**

1. **Insulate RoofMain**
   - **Action**: Insulate the gable roof.
   - **Type**: `InsulateGableRoof`
   - **Cost**: €60 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary in Flanders
   - **Order**: 28

2. **Replace Emission System**
   - **Action**: Place a heat pump boiler.
   - **Type**: `PlaceHeatPumpBoiler`
   - **Cost**: €4,000 (fixed price)
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary in Flanders
   - **Order**: 76

3. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Replace the current ventilation system with a more efficient one.
   - **Type**: `ReplaceVentilationSystem`
   - **Cost**: €3,500 (fixed price) + €17 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary in Flanders
   - **Order**: 52

4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the basement.
   - **Type**: `InsulateFloorAboveBasement`
   - **Cost**: €30 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary in Flanders
   - **Order**: 49

5. **Place PV Panels**
   - **Action**: Consider installing PV panels if the potential changes in the future.
   - **Type**: `PlacePVPanelsOnRoof`
   - **Cost**: €275 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary if the panels are in the same plane (Flanders)
   - **Order**: 85

6. **Replace Windows**
   - **Action**: Replace windows with double glazing to improve insulation.
   - **Type**: `ReplaceWindows`
   - **Cost**: €800 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level**: High
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary if the measurements are kept (Flanders)
   - **Order**: 46

#### **Optimal Sequence**
1. **Insulate RoofMain** (Order 28)
2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement** (Order 49)
3. **Replace Ventilation System** (Order 52)
4. **Replace Windows** (Order 46)
5. **Replace Emission System** (Order 76)
6. **Place PV Panels** (Order 85) (Future consideration)

#### **Estimated Total Cost**
- **Insulate RoofMain**: €60 × 159 m² = €9,540
- **Insulate Floor Above Basement**: €30 × 159 m² = €4,770
- **Replace Ventilation System**: €3,500 + (€17 × 159 m²) = €3,500 + €2,703 = €6,203
- **Replace Windows**: €800 × 159 m² = €127,200
- **Replace Emission System**: €4,000
- **Total**: €141,613

#### **Notes**
- **Permits**: Ensure all necessary permits are obtained based on local regulations.
- **Future Considerations**: If the potential for PV panels changes, reassess the installation of PV panels.
- **Energy Efficiency**: Continuously monitor the EPC score and energy consumption to ensure the renovation goals are met.

This plan prioritizes actions that maximize energy efficiency and thermal comfort while considering cost-effectiveness and permit requirements. The sequence ensures that the most impactful renovations are addressed first.
