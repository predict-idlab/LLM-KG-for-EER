Based on the information provided, here’s a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan prioritizes energy efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and practical upgrades while considering the optimal sequence of renovations.

### **Renovation Plan:**

#### **1. Insulate Exterior Walls (High Priority)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the uninsulated cavity walls (front, rear, left, and right sides) using cavity wall insulation.
   - **Why:** Exterior walls are a major source of heat loss, and insulating them will significantly improve the building’s thermal performance.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Cavity wall insulation is relatively affordable and provides high energy savings.

#### **2. Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**
   - **Action:** Insulate the floor above the crawl space if it is not already insulated.
   - **Why:** Uninsulated floors can lead to significant heat loss, especially in older homes with crawl spaces.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Floor insulation is a cost-effective upgrade with a good return on investment.

#### **3. Replace Single-Glazed Windows with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the single-glazed windows to double or triple glazing while keeping the wooden frames (if they are in good condition).
   - **Why:** Windows are another major source of heat loss, and upgrading to double or triple glazing will improve insulation and comfort.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Double glazing is more cost-effective than triple glazing but still provides significant energy savings.

#### **4. Replace Outdated Heating Systems**
   - **Action:** Replace the old boiler and outdated heat emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors) with a modern, high-efficiency heating system.
   - **Options:**
     - **Heat Pump:** A ground-source heat pump may not be suitable due to site constraints, but an air-source heat pump could be a viable option.
     - **Condensing Boiler:** If a heat pump is not feasible, a modern condensing boiler is a good alternative.
   - **Why:** Modern heating systems are more energy-efficient and can significantly reduce energy consumption.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** While the upfront cost is high, the long-term energy savings justify the investment.

#### **5. Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof to generate renewable energy.
   - **Why:** Belgium has good potential for solar energy, and PV panels can reduce electricity costs and improve the Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) score.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** PV panels have a relatively high upfront cost but offer long-term savings and may qualify for subsidies or tax incentives.

#### **6. Improve Natural Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the basic natural ventilation system to a more efficient mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR).
   - **Why:** MVHR systems improve indoor air quality while reducing heat loss through ventilation.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** While more expensive than natural ventilation, MVHR systems provide long-term energy savings.

#### **7. Seal Air Leaks**
   - **Action:** Conduct an air tightness test and seal any gaps or leaks around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Why:** Air leaks can significantly reduce the effectiveness of insulation and heating systems.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Sealing air leaks is a low-cost, high-impact upgrade.

### **Optimal Sequence of Renovations:**
1. **Insulate exterior walls** (highest priority for reducing heat loss).
2. **Insulate the floor above the crawl space** (if not already insulated).
3. **Replace single-glazed windows** with double or triple glazing.
4. **Replace outdated heating systems** with a modern, high-efficiency system.
5. **Install PV panels** to generate renewable energy.
6. **Upgrade the ventilation system** to MVHR.
7. **Seal air leaks** to improve overall air tightness.

### **Expected Outcomes:**
- **Improved EPC Score:** The combination of these upgrades will significantly reduce energy consumption and improve the EPC score.
- **Lower Energy Bills:** Reduced heat loss and more efficient heating systems will lead to long-term cost savings.
- **Increased Comfort:** Better insulation and modern heating systems will enhance indoor comfort.

This plan balances cost-effectiveness with long-term energy efficiency gains, ensuring that each upgrade maximizes the benefits of the previous steps.
