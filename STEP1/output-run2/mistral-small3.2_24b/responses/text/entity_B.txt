Based on the information provided, here’s a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your home in Ghent, Belgium. The plan prioritizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness while considering the optimal sequence of upgrades.

### **1. Insulate Uninsulated Areas (High Priority)**
**a. Exterior Walls (Front and Rear)**
- **Action:** Install internal or external wall insulation.
- **Why:** Solid brick walls are a major source of heat loss. Insulating them will significantly improve the energy performance of the house.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** External insulation is more expensive but provides better thermal performance and eliminates cold bridges. Internal insulation is cheaper but may reduce floor space.

**b. Flat-Roof Extension**
- **Action:** Insulate the flat roof with high-performance insulation (e.g., rigid foam boards or mineral wool).
- **Why:** Flat roofs are prone to heat loss, and insulating them will improve comfort and reduce heating costs.

**c. Floor Above the Basement**
- **Action:** Insulate the floor with rigid insulation boards or underfloor insulation.
- **Why:** Uninsulated floors contribute to heat loss, especially in older homes.

### **2. Upgrade Heating System (High Priority)**
- **Action:** Replace the old boiler and radiator system with a modern, high-efficiency condensing boiler or an air-source heat pump (if feasible).
- **Why:** A new boiler will improve efficiency and reduce heating costs. An air-source heat pump is more expensive but can provide long-term savings.
- **Additional Consideration:** If replacing the boiler, consider installing smart thermostats to optimize heating schedules.

### **3. Improve Natural Ventilation System (Medium Priority)**
- **Action:** Upgrade to a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR).
- **Why:** MVHR systems improve indoor air quality while recovering heat from exhaust air, reducing heating costs.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** More expensive upfront but provides long-term energy savings.

### **4. Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels (Medium Priority)**
- **Action:** Assess the roof’s suitability for PV panels and install a system sized to meet a significant portion of your electricity needs.
- **Why:** Belgium has incentives for solar panel installations, and they can reduce electricity costs and improve the home’s energy performance.

### **5. Minor Upgrades (Low Priority)**
- **Action:** Consider upgrading to triple-glazed windows if budget allows, though double-glazed windows are already decent.
- **Why:** Triple glazing provides additional insulation but may not be cost-effective unless combined with other upgrades.

### **Optimal Sequence of Renovations**
1. **Insulate the flat roof and floor above the basement** (quick wins with immediate impact).
2. **Insulate the exterior walls** (most significant impact on energy performance).
3. **Upgrade the heating system** (ensure the new system works efficiently with improved insulation).
4. **Install PV panels** (maximize solar potential after reducing heat loss).
5. **Upgrade ventilation system** (improve air quality and energy recovery).

### **Expected Outcomes**
- **Improved Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) score** (likely reduction to below 150, depending on upgrades).
- **Lower heating and electricity costs** (due to better insulation and renewable energy generation).
- **Increased comfort and sustainability** (better indoor air quality and reduced carbon footprint).

This plan balances immediate energy savings with long-term investment, ensuring a practical and cost-effective approach to renovating your home.
