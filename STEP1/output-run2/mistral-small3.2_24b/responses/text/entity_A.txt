Based on the information provided, here is a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan focuses on improving energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness, while considering the optimal sequence of renovations:

### 1. **Insulate the Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Install external wall insulation (EWI) on the solid brick walls.
   - **Benefits**: Significantly reduces heat loss through the walls, improves overall thermal performance, and enhances the building's appearance.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: High initial cost but provides long-term energy savings and increases property value.

### 2. **Insulate the Pitched Roof**
   - **Action**: Add insulation to the pitched roof, which is currently uninsulated.
   - **Benefits**: Roofs are a major source of heat loss; insulating the pitched roof will greatly improve the building's energy efficiency.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Moderate cost with substantial energy savings.

### 3. **Upgrade the Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and heating systems with a modern, high-efficiency condensing boiler and new radiators or underfloor heating.
   - **Benefits**: Improves heating efficiency, reduces energy consumption, and ensures reliable heating.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: High initial cost but essential for long-term energy savings and comfort.

### 4. **Insulate the Flat-Roof Extension**
   - **Action**: Enhance the existing moderate insulation in the flat-roof extension to meet higher standards.
   - **Benefits**: Further reduces heat loss and improves overall thermal performance.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Lower cost compared to insulating the exterior walls or pitched roof but still impactful.

### 5. **Insulate the Floor Above the Basement**
   - **Action**: Install insulation on the floor above the basement.
   - **Benefits**: Prevents heat loss through the floor and improves comfort in the living spaces above.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Moderate cost with noticeable energy savings.

### 6. **Improve Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the basic natural ventilation system to a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR).
   - **Benefits**: Enhances indoor air quality, reduces heat loss through ventilation, and recovers heat from exhaust air.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Higher initial cost but provides long-term energy savings and improved comfort.

### 7. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof to generate renewable energy.
   - **Benefits**: Reduces electricity costs and carbon footprint; may qualify for incentives or feed-in tariffs.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Moderate to high initial cost but offers long-term financial and environmental benefits.

### 8. **Consider Window Upgrades (Optional)**
   - **Action**: If the double-glazed windows are old or inefficient, consider upgrading to high-performance triple-glazed windows with low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings.
   - **Benefits**: Further reduces heat loss through windows and improves comfort.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Higher initial cost but provides long-term energy savings.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate the exterior walls** (highest impact on energy efficiency).
2. **Insulate the pitched roof** (next highest impact).
3. **Upgrade the heating system** (essential for overall efficiency).
4. **Insulate the flat-roof extension and floor above the basement** (moderate impact).
5. **Improve the ventilation system** (enhances indoor air quality and efficiency).
6. **Install PV panels** (generates renewable energy).

### Additional Recommendations:
- **Seek Professional Advice**: Consult with energy efficiency experts or certified contractors to ensure the renovations are correctly implemented.
- **Apply for Incentives**: Check for available grants, subsidies, or tax incentives for energy-efficient renovations in Belgium.
- **Monitor Progress**: Regularly assess the energy performance certificate (EPC) score after each renovation phase to track improvements.

This plan prioritizes the most impactful renovations first, ensuring that each step builds upon the previous one to maximize energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness.
