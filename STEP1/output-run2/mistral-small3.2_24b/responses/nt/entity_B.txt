### Renovation Plan for the Given Building

#### **1. Initial Assessment**
- **Building Type**: Attached building (likely a townhouse or semi-detached).
- **Construction Year**: 1832 (old, likely with poor insulation and energy efficiency).
- **Current EPC Score**: 262 kWh/m²year (F-label, indicating poor energy efficiency).
- **Potential for PV**: Yes, suitable for photovoltaic panels.
- **Heat Grid Presence**: No.
- **Potential for Geothermal**: No (both horizontal and vertical drilling are not feasible).

#### **2. Key Issues Identified**
- **Insulation**: Poor insulation in walls (rear and front), floor above basement, and roofs (main and extension).
- **Windows**: Double glazing in living area, but single glazing in bedrooms.
- **Ventilation**: System A (natural air supply and mechanical air exhaust).
- **Heating System**: Radiators (likely old and inefficient).

#### **3. Prioritized Renovation Steps**
**Phase 1: Immediate Improvements (High Impact, Low Cost)**
1. **Window Replacement**
   - **Action**: Replace single-glazed windows in bedrooms with double-glazed windows.
   - **Reason**: Single glazing is a major source of heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: €800 per window (assuming 5 windows: €4,000 total).
   - **Expected Savings**: Significant reduction in heat loss and improved comfort.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action**: Install insulation in the floor above the basement.
   - **Reason**: Uninsulated floors are a major source of heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: €30 per m² (assuming 50 m²: €1,500 total).
   - **Expected Savings**: Reduced heat loss through the floor.

3. **Wall Insulation (Interior)**
   - **Action**: Insulate interior walls (rear and front) with high-performance insulation.
   - **Reason**: Walls are currently not insulated, leading to significant heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: €150 per m² (assuming 100 m²: €15,000 total).
   - **Expected Savings**: Significant reduction in heat loss through walls.

**Phase 2: Medium-Term Improvements (Moderate Impact, Moderate Cost)**
4. **Roof Insulation**
   - **Action**: Insulate the main and extension roofs.
   - **Reason**: Uninsulated roofs are a major source of heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: €50 per m² (assuming 100 m²: €5,000 total).
   - **Expected Savings**: Reduced heat loss through the roof.

5. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
   - **Action**: Upgrade from Ventilation System A to System C (mechanical air supply and exhaust).
   - **Reason**: Improved air quality and efficiency.
   - **Estimated Cost**: €17 per m² (assuming 100 m²: €1,700 total).
   - **Expected Savings**: Better air quality and potential energy savings.

6. **Heating System Upgrade**
   - **Action**: Replace old radiators with a modern heat pump system.
   - **Reason**: Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than traditional radiators.
   - **Estimated Cost**: €35 per m² (assuming 100 m²: €3,500 total).
   - **Expected Savings**: Significant reduction in energy consumption for heating.

**Phase 3: Long-Term Improvements (High Impact, High Cost)**
7. **Photovoltaic Panels Installation**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic panels on the roof.
   - **Reason**: Utilize the building's potential for PV to generate renewable energy.
   - **Estimated Cost**: €275 per m² (assuming 50 m²: €13,750 total).
   - **Expected Savings**: Generation of renewable energy, reducing reliance on the grid.

#### **4. Expected Outcomes**
- **Energy Efficiency**: Significant improvement in energy efficiency, likely moving from an F-label to at least a C-label.
- **Comfort**: Improved thermal comfort due to better insulation and heating.
- **Cost Savings**: Reduced energy bills due to lower heating requirements and potential income from PV panels.

#### **5. Additional Recommendations**
- **Monitor and Maintain**: Regularly monitor the performance of the new systems and maintain them to ensure longevity.
- **Consider Future Upgrades**: Plan for future upgrades such as smart thermostats and additional renewable energy sources.

This plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, prioritizing actions that offer the highest return on investment. The steps are sequenced to ensure that each renovation builds on the previous one for optimal results.
