### Renovation Plan

**1. Insulate the Roof Main and Roof Extension**
- **Action**: Install insulation in the Roof Main and Roof Extension.
- **Priority**: High
- **Reasoning**: Both roofs are currently moderately insulated. Insulating them will significantly improve the energy efficiency of the building.
- **Suggestion**: Use the `InsulateFlatRoof` and `InsulateGableRoof` suggestions from the ontology.

**2. Replace Window Glazing in Living Area and Bedrooms**
- **Action**: Replace the glazing of the windows in the living area and bedrooms with double glazing.
- **Priority**: Medium
- **Reasoning**: The windows in both areas currently have single glazing, which has poor thermal and acoustic properties. Replacing them with double glazing will improve comfort and energy efficiency.
- **Suggestion**: Use the `ReplaceGlazing` suggestion from the ontology.

**3. Replace the Ventilation System**
- **Action**: Upgrade the current ventilation system to a more efficient model.
- **Priority**: Medium
- **Reasoning**: The current ventilation system is type D, which may not be the most efficient. Upgrading it can improve air quality and energy efficiency.
- **Suggestion**: Use the `ReplaceVentilationSystem` suggestion from the ontology.

**4. Consider Installing Solar Panels**
- **Action**: Evaluate the feasibility of installing solar panels on the building.
- **Priority**: Low
- **Reasoning**: The building has potential for solar panels, which can further reduce energy consumption and costs. This should be considered as a long-term investment.
- **Suggestion**: Use the `PlacePVPanelsOnRoof` suggestion from the ontology.

**5. Regular Maintenance of Existing Systems**
- **Action**: Ensure regular maintenance of the existing heating and ventilation systems.
- **Priority**: Ongoing
- **Reasoning**: Regular maintenance is crucial to keep the systems running efficiently and to prevent potential issues.
- **Suggestion**: Follow the maintenance guidelines provided in the ontology.

**6. Monitor Energy Consumption**
- **Action**: Regularly monitor the energy consumption of the building.
- **Priority**: Ongoing
- **Reasoning**: Monitoring energy consumption can help identify areas for further improvement and ensure that the renovations are having the desired effect.
- **Suggestion**: Use the energy monitoring suggestions from the ontology.

This plan prioritizes actions based on the potential impact on energy efficiency and comfort, while also considering long-term investments like solar panels. Regular maintenance and monitoring are emphasized to ensure sustained benefits from the renovations.
