### Building Analysis Summary
The building is a modern semi-detached house (constructed in 2017) with an already high level of energy efficiency. With an **EPC score of 87.0**, it currently falls within the **A Label** category. Most critical elements—including the walls, floor above the basement, and the flat roof extension—are already "Well Insulated." The building is also equipped with high-efficiency systems, including a heat pump and a Type D ventilation system.

Given that the owner's motivation is to further improve the EPC, the focus must shift from "major overhaul" to "marginal gains," as the building is already near peak performance.

---

### Proposed Renovation Path

Since the majority of the building envelope is already optimized, the renovation strategy focuses on the only remaining moderately insulated element and low-cost operational improvements.

#### Step 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)
*   **Action:** **Insulate Gable Roof** (`reno:InsulateGableRoof`)
*   **Justification:** The main gable roof is the only structural element currently rated as **"Moderately Insulated."** Upgrading this to "Well Insulated" will reduce thermal bridging and heat loss through the top of the building.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** This is a high-impact, relatively low-cost upgrade compared to window or wall replacements.
*   **Point of Attention:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation.
*   **Ordering:** This is the primary structural priority (Ontology Ordering: 28).

#### Step 2: Solar Heat Gain Management (Low Priority/Comfort)
*   **Action:** **Install Sun Shades** (`reno:PlaceSunShade`)
*   **Justification:** While the building is efficient in winter, modern well-insulated homes with double glazing can suffer from overheating in summer. External sunshades reduce the need for active cooling (which would consume electricity from the heat pump).
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** Low fixed cost with immediate impact on summer energy consumption.
*   **Point of Attention:** Prioritize outdoor blinds over indoor blinds for maximum efficiency.
*   **Ordering:** This is a finishing touch for operational efficiency (Ontology Ordering: 79).

---

### Analysis of Excluded Upgrades (Cost-Benefit Justification)

To remain **cost-effective**, the following common renovations are **not recommended** for this specific building:

*   **Wall/Floor Insulation:** All walls and the floor above the basement are already "Well Insulated." Further investment here would yield negligible energy savings.
*   **Window Replacement:** The current windows are double-glazed and in "Good State." While triple glazing would technically improve the EPC, the cost of replacing all windows (`reno:ReplaceWindows`) is extremely high relative to the marginal gain, given the building is already in the 'A' category.
*   **HVAC Upgrades:** The building already possesses a **Heat Pump** and **Ventilation System D** (the most advanced mechanical system). Replacing these would provide no meaningful efficiency increase.
*   **Geothermal Energy:** The site data explicitly indicates **no potential** for horizontal or vertical geothermal captation.
*   **PV Panel Expansion:** The building already has PV panels installed, and the building data indicates no further potential for PV expansion.
