### **Executive Summary: Renovation Strategy**
The building is a detached house from 1862 with a surprisingly good current EPC score (165), but it possesses significant "low-hanging fruit" for energy reduction, specifically in the walls and windows. Since the owner's motivation is to **Improve the EPC**, the focus is on reducing thermal transmission and transitioning to sustainable heating.

### **Phase 1: The Thermal Envelope (High Impact, Low-to-Moderate Cost)**
*Goal: Stop heat loss to reduce the load on future heating systems.*

1.  **Cavity Wall Insulation (`InsulateWallCavity`)**
    *   **Analysis:** All four walls are cavity walls and currently **not insulated**. This is the most cost-effective upgrade available.
    *   **Benefit:** Extremely low nuisance level and the lowest unit price (22/m²).
    *   **Action:** Inject insulation into the existing cavities.

2.  **Floor Insulation (`InsulateFloorAboveBasement`)**
    *   **Analysis:** The building has a floor above a basement.
    *   **Benefit:** Low nuisance and low cost (30/m²). This prevents "cold feet" and reduces heat loss to the crawl space.

3.  **Glazing Upgrade (`ReplaceGlazing`)**
    *   **Analysis:** All windows currently have **single glazing**, which is a major source of heat loss. However, the wooden frames are in **good state**.
    *   **Cost-Effective Choice:** Instead of replacing the entire window (`ReplaceWindows` at 800/m²), I propose replacing only the glazing (`ReplaceGlazing` at 200/m²).
    *   **Benefit:** Significant jump in thermal performance while preserving the existing structural frames.

### **Phase 2: Ventilation and Air Quality (Necessary Support)**
*Goal: Ensure healthy indoor air quality after the envelope is sealed.*

4.  **Ventilation System Upgrade (`ReplaceVentilationSystem`)**
    *   **Analysis:** The building currently uses **Ventilation System A** (natural supply/exhaust), which is inefficient and allows uncontrolled heat loss.
    *   **Action:** Upgrade to a more modern system (e.g., System C or D).
    *   **Note:** This should be done *after* the windows are upgraded to ensure the new system is integrated with the new glazing.

### **Phase 3: Sustainable Heating & Generation (Long-term Operational Performance)**
*Goal: Replace fossil-fuel/inefficient heating with a low-carbon alternative.*

5.  **Heating System Transition (`PlaceAirWaterHeatpump`)**
    *   **Analysis:** The building has a reason to change its emission system. Geothermal options are unavailable (per site data), and there is no heat grid.
    *   **Action:** Install an **Air-Water Heat Pump**. 
    *   **Reasoning:** This is the most efficient option given the site constraints. Because we insulated the walls and floors in Phase 1, the heat pump can operate at lower, more efficient temperatures.

6.  **Renewable Energy Integration (`PlacePVPanelsOnRoof`)**
    *   **Analysis:** The building has a gable roof that is already "well insulated" and has confirmed **potential for PV**.
    *   **Action:** Install solar PV panels.
    *   **Synergy:** The electricity generated will directly offset the operational costs of the new Air-Water Heat Pump, maximizing long-term cost-effectiveness.
