*   **Action:** **Insulate Gable Roof** (`InsulateGableRoof`).

*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** At **€60/m²**, this is significantly cheaper than a full roof replacement (€260/m²), which is unnecessary given the roof is in "Good" state.
*   **Point of Attention:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation.

#### **Step 2: Glazing Upgrade (Priority: Medium)**
*   **Action:** **Replace Glazing** (`ReplaceGlazing`).
*   **Justification:** Upgrading from double glazing to **triple glazing** will further lower the U-value of the windows.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** Because the wooden frames are in "Good" state and are structurally sound, replacing only the glass panes (**€200/m²**) is far more cost-effective than replacing the entire window unit (**€800/m²**).
*   **Point of Attention:** Verify that the existing wooden frames have the required thickness to support the heavier triple-pane glass.

#### **Step 3: Solar Heat Management (Priority: Low/Operational)**
*   **Action:** **Place Sunshade** (`PlaceSunShade`).
*   **Justification:** In highly insulated, modern homes (EPC < 100), "overheating" in summer often becomes a larger issue than "heating" in winter.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** This is a low-cost fixed investment (**€750**) that reduces the energy load on any cooling systems.
*   **Point of Attention:** Opt for outdoor blinds rather than indoor ones for maximum thermal efficiency.
