### Proposed Renovation Path

**Phase 1: Heating System Transition (Priority: High)**
Since the building envelope is already well-insulated and the ventilation system is efficient, the primary focus should be on upgrading the heating system to a more sustainable and efficient solution.

1. **Replace Boiler with Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - The current boiler is connected to underfloor heating and electric convectors, which are no longer in good condition.
   - An **air-water heat pump** is the most viable and efficient alternative, given the lack of district heating and geothermal potential.
   - Ensure the heat pump is sized appropriately for the building's reduced heat demand.

2. **Upgrade to Low-Temperature Emission System:**
   - Transition the underfloor heating to a low-temperature system to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the heat pump.
   - If electric convectors are still in use, consider replacing them with more efficient radiators or underfloor heating loops designed for low-temperature operation.

**Phase 2: Optimize Energy Generation and Usage (Priority: Medium)**
Your house already has photovoltaic (PV) panels installed, which is excellent for reducing electricity costs and carbon footprint. However, you can further optimize their usage.

3. **Integrate Smart Energy Management System:**
   - Install a **smart energy management system** to monitor and control energy usage, ensuring that the PV panels' output is maximized and prioritized for high-consumption appliances (e.g., heat pump, ventilation system).
   - This can also help balance electricity usage with PV generation to minimize grid dependency.

4. **Consider Battery Storage (Optional):**
   - If budget allows, adding a **battery storage system** can store excess solar energy for use during peak hours or at night, further reducing grid reliance.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence
**Air-Water Heat Pump** $\rightarrow$ **Low-Temperature Emission Upgrade** $\rightarrow$ **Smart Energy Management System** $\rightarrow$ **Battery Storage (Optional)**.

This plan focuses on upgrading the heating system to a more efficient and sustainable solution while optimizing the existing PV panels and energy management.
