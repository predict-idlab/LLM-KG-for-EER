### Renovation Plan:

Given the building data and the RENO ontology, the property is a semi-detached building from 2017 with an EPC score of 87 (B Label). The owner's primary motivation is to improve the EPC rating further.

To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the renovation must follow the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** sequence. Upgrading heating systems before the building envelope is sealed leads to oversized equipment and wasted energy.

### Proposed Renovation Path:

**Phase 1: Strengthening the Building Envelope (Priority: High)**
The building has already undergone significant upgrades, but there is still room for improvement in the thermal envelope.

1. **Insulate Gable Roof:** The `RoofMain` is currently moderately insulated. Upgrading it to well-insulated status will eliminate heat loss through the roof.
2. **Insulate Flat Roof:** The `RoofExtension` is currently well-insulated, but adding additional layers or improving the existing insulation could further reduce heat loss.
*Note: Windows are already double-glazed and frames are in a "Good State," so replacing them is not recommended at this stage to maintain cost-effectiveness.*

**Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)**
With a sealed envelope, the building's heat demand is now significantly lower, allowing for the installation of a more efficient, low-temperature system.
3. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since geothermal potential is "false" and there is no heat grid, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing system.
4. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** Before installing a heat pump, the current system should be transitioned to low-temperature emissions to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new generator.

**Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)**
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label.
5. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence
**Gable Roof Insulation** $\rightarrow$ **Flat Roof Insulation** $\rightarrow$ **Air-Water Heat Pump** $\rightarrow$ **Low-Temp Emission Upgrade** $\rightarrow$ **PV Panels**.

This plan ensures that the building's energy efficiency is maximized while maintaining cost-effectiveness and aligning with the owner's motivation to improve the EPC rating.
