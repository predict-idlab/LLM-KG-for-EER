### Renovation Plan:

**Objective:** Improve the Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) score of the property from 388 (D label) to a higher rating through cost-effective measures.

**Building Details:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached building
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **Location:** Belgium (Postal Code: 9000)
- **EPC Score:** 388 (D label)
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve the EPC rating
- **Potential for PV:** True
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** False
- **Reason to Change Emission System:** True

**Renovation Path:**

1. **Insulate Roof Main (Gable Roof)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the main roof which is currently not insulated.
   - **Rationale:** Gable roofs are more complex to insulate, but it is a priority to prevent heat loss through the top of the building.
   - **Expected Impact:** Significant improvement in thermal performance.

2. **Insulate Wall on Exterior (Front and Rear Walls)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the exterior walls which are full brick and not insulated.
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is more effective for full brick walls and helps eliminate thermal bridges.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduces heat loss through walls and improves overall energy efficiency.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof (Roof Extension)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the flat roof extension which is currently moderately insulated.
   - **Rationale:** Flat roofs are prone to heat loss, and improving insulation here will enhance energy efficiency.
   - **Expected Impact:** Improves thermal comfort and reduces energy consumption.

4. **Replace Ventilation System (Ventilation System A to Ventilation System D)**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the ventilation system from natural air supply and exhaust (System A) to mechanical air supply and exhaust (System D).
   - **Rationale:** A more efficient ventilation system ensures better air quality and reduces energy loss.
   - **Expected Impact:** Enhances indoor air quality and energy efficiency.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Rationale:** Air-water heat pumps are efficient and suitable for buildings without a heat grid.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduces reliance on fossil fuels and improves heating efficiency.

6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable electricity.
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV, and solar panels will offset electricity consumption.
   - **Expected Impact:** Generates clean energy and reduces electricity costs.

**Summary of Sequence and Rationale:**

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| 1        | Insulate Roof Main (Gable Roof) | Prevents heat loss through the top of the building. |
| 2        | Insulate Wall on Exterior (Front and Rear Walls) | Reduces heat loss through walls and improves overall energy efficiency. |
| 3        | Insulate Flat Roof (Roof Extension) | Enhances energy efficiency and thermal comfort. |
| 4        | Replace Ventilation System (System A to System D) | Ensures better air quality and reduces energy loss. |
| 5        | Place Air-Water Heat Pump | Reduces reliance on fossil fuels and improves heating efficiency. |
| 6        | Place PV-Panels on Roof | Generates clean energy and reduces electricity costs. |

This renovation plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency and improve the EPC score in a cost-effective manner, following the "Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate" sequence.
