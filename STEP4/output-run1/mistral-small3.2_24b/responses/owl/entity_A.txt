### Renovation Plan:

#### Context:
- **Building Type:** Semi-detached building from 1951.
- **Current EPC Score:** 388 (D Label).
- **Owner's Motivation:** Improve EPC rating.
- **Site Characteristics:** No potential for geothermal energy, no heat grid, potential for PV.
- **Current Building Envelope Issues:**
  - Non-insulated walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`).
  - Non-insulated `FloorAboveBasement`.
  - Moderately insulated flat roof (`RoofExtension`).
  - Not insulated gable roof (`RoofMain`).

#### Prioritized Renovation Path:
To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the renovation should follow the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** sequence. This ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system.

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (The "Fabric First" Stage)
The primary goal here is to stop heat leakage. Since the building has several uninsulated elements, this phase offers the highest return on investment.

1. **Insulate Gable Roof (`RoofMain`):**
   - The roof is currently not insulated. This is a priority as heat rises; insulating the gable roof is one of the most effective ways to lower energy consumption.
   - **Action:** `InsulateGableRoof` with a vapour barrier on the warm side and ensure the roof underlayment is air- and watertight.

2. **Insulate Flat Roof (`RoofExtension`):**
   - The roof is currently moderately insulated. Upgrading insulation to a higher level will further reduce heat loss.
   - **Action:** `InsulateFlatRoof` with an inverted roof structure, not a cold roof. Place the insulation on top of the roof covering.

3. **Insulate Wall On Exterior (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`):**
   - All three external walls are full brick and not insulated. **Insulating the wall on the exterior** is significantly more cost-effective than wall cavity insulation and provides a substantial increase in thermal retention.
   - **Action:** `InsulateWallExterior` with attention to local building guidelines and the building line.

4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - The floor above the basement is currently not insulated. Adding insulation here eliminates "cold feet" and reduces heat loss to the basement.
   - **Action:** `InsulateFloorAboveBasement`.

#### Phase 2: Window Performance
The window performance should be enhanced, since the double glazing leads to significant heat losses.

5. **Replace Glazing (`WindowsBedrooms`, `WindowsLivingArea`):**
   - All glazing should be replaced with triple glazing. Adding triple glazing would drastically improve the thermal performance.
   - **Action:** `ReplaceGlazing` with triple glazing.

#### Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition
With a tight thermal envelope, the building can now operate on lower temperatures, allowing for a transition away from the existing boiler.

6. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - Since the site has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most viable and efficient alternative to the current boiler.
   - **Action:** `PlaceAirWaterHeatpump`.

#### Phase 4: Air Quality and Ventilation
Once the building is sealed and insulated, the air exchange rate decreases, making a modern ventilation system necessary to prevent moisture buildup and maintain air quality.

7. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (such as System C or D). This ensures that the heat gained from the new insulation is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage.
   - **Action:** `ReplaceVentilationSystem`.

#### Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation (PV Integration)
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label.

8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Action:** `PlacePVPanelsOnRoof`.

#### Summary of Optimal Sequence
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Gable Roof** | Reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| **2** | **Insulate Flat Roof** | Further reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| **3** | **Insulate Walls on Exterior** | Reduces heat loss through the walls. |
| **4** | **Insulate Floor Above Basement** | Reduces heat loss to the basement. |
| **5** | **Replace Glazing** | Reduce the remaining thermal heat losses. |
| **6** | **Heat Pump** | Switches to a sustainable energy source tailored to the new lower heat demand. |
| **7** | **Ventilation Upgrade** | Maintains indoor air quality in a now-sealed envelope. |
| **8** | **PV Panels** | Offsets the electricity consumption of the heat pump. |

### Additional Notes:
- **Cost Considerations:** Prioritize actions based on their cost-effectiveness and impact on the EPC score.
- **Permits:** Ensure compliance with local building regulations and permits for each phase.
- **Professional Assistance:** Consider consulting with architects or contractors for expert advice and proper execution of the works.

This renovation plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, tailored to the specific building characteristics and the owner's motivations.
