   - **Bedrooms Windows:** Double Glazing, Wooden Frames

- **Primary Goal:** Improve EPC

---

### Proposed Renovation Path:

**Phase 1: Upgrade Insulation (High Priority)**
1. **Insulate Flat Roof (Roof Extension):**
   - **Action:** Insulate the flat roof to further improve insulation performance.
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof is currently well insulated, but upgrading it will enhance thermal performance and reduce heat loss.

**Phase 2: Enhance Window Performance (Medium Priority)**
2. **Replace Glazing (Living Area and Bedrooms):**
   - **Action:** Replace double glazing with triple glazing to reduce heat loss and improve thermal comfort.
   - **Rationale:** Upgrading from double to triple glazing will significantly improve the thermal performance of the windows, reducing heat loss and enhancing indoor comfort.

**Phase 3: Transition to High-Efficiency Heating (Medium Priority)**
3. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Rationale:** Given the absence of geothermal potential and heat grid, an air-water heat pump is the most sustainable and efficient heating solution. It will work well with the improved insulation and triple-glazed windows.

**Phase 4: Improve Air Quality and Ventilation (Medium Priority)**
4. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System D to a more efficient system (such as System C).
   - **Rationale:** Upgrading the ventilation system will ensure proper air exchange, maintain indoor air quality, and prevent moisture buildup in the well-insulated and sealed building.

**Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-term Priority)**
5. **Place PV Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic panels on the gable roof.
   - **Rationale:** Although the building has no potential for PV, installing solar panels will provide renewable electricity to power the heat pump and other electrical systems, further reducing the carbon footprint.

---

### Summary of Optimal Sequence:
**Insulate Flat Roof** $\rightarrow$ **Replace Glazing** $\rightarrow$ **Air-Water Heat Pump** $\rightarrow$ **Ventilation Upgrade** $\rightarrow$ **PV Panels**.

This plan ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before transitioning to high-efficiency heating systems, maximizing energy savings and improving the EPC score in a cost-effective manner.
