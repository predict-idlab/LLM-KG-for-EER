### **Energy-Efficient and Cost-Effective Renovation Plan**

#### **Building Overview**
- **Type:** Semi-detached (1963)
- **EPC Score:** 87 (A Label)
- **Current Insulation:**
  - **Well-insulated:** Walls (Front, Left, Rear), Floor Above Basement, Flat Roof
  - **Moderately insulated:** Gable Roof
- **Windows:** Double-glazed, wooden frames (Good State)
- **Heating System:** Heat Pump
- **Ventilation:** System D
- **PV Potential:** False
- **Heat Grid Availability:** False
- **Owner Motivation:** Improve EPC

---

### **Renovation Priorities & Strategy**
Since the building already has a good EPC (87), further improvements should focus on **maximizing efficiency** while **minimizing costs**. The key is to **optimize existing systems** rather than major structural changes.

#### **1. Improve Gable Roof Insulation**
- **Action:** `Insulate Gable Roof`
- **Rationale:** The gable roof is currently only moderately insulated. Upgrading to **well-insulated** status will reduce heat loss through the roof.
- **Cost:** ~€60/m²

#### **2. Upgrade Ventilation System**
- **Action:** `Replace Ventilation System`
- **Rationale:** A more efficient ventilation system (e.g., **System C**) can improve air quality while reducing energy losses.
- **Cost:** ~€3,500 (fixed) + €17/m²

#### **3. Optimize Heat Pump Performance**
- **Action:** `Place Emission System on Low Temperature`
- **Rationale:** Ensures the heat pump operates at peak efficiency, reducing energy consumption.
- **Cost:** ~€100/m²

#### **4. Consider PV Panels (If Feasible)**
- **Action:** `Place PV-Panels on Roof` (if PV potential changes)
- **Rationale:** If future solar potential becomes available, installing PV panels could further reduce energy costs.
- **Cost:** ~€275/m²

---

### **Optimal Renovation Sequence**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof** (Improve thermal envelope)
2. **Upgrade Ventilation System** (Enhance air quality & efficiency)
3. **Optimize Heat Pump Performance** (Reduce energy consumption)
4. **Consider PV Panels** (If future potential arises)
