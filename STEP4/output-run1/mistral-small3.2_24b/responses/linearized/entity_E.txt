### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (1862, EPC: 165)

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Detached building (1862)
- **EPC Score:** 165 (B Label)
- **Current Issues:**
  - All external walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Right`) are cavity walls with **no insulation**.
  - **Single-glazed windows** in both living area and bedrooms.
  - **FloorAboveCrawlSpace** is uninsulated.
  - **Presence of electric convectors** (high energy consumption).
  - **No ventilation system** (air quality concerns).

**Renovation Goals:**
1. **Improve thermal comfort** (reduce heat loss).
2. **Lower energy consumption** (reduce reliance on electric convectors).
3. **Upgrade to a more efficient heating system** (reduce carbon footprint).
4. **Ensure adequate ventilation** (prevent moisture buildup).

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First)**
*(Highest ROI—reduce heat loss first.)*
1. **Insulate Wall Cavity (Exterior Walls)**
   - Since all walls are cavity walls, **injecting insulation** is the most cost-effective method.
   - **Action:** Insulate all four walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Right`).
   - **Expected Benefit:** Significant reduction in heat loss through walls.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - The **uninsulated floor** allows cold air from the crawl space to seep into the living area.
   - **Action:** Insulate the floor to prevent heat loss downward.
   - **Expected Benefit:** Warmer floors and reduced heating demand.

3. **Replace Glazing (Double Glazing)**
   - The single-glazed windows are a major source of heat loss.
   - **Action:** Replace all single-glazed windows with **double-glazed units** (or triple-glazed if budget allows).
   - **Expected Benefit:** Reduced heat loss through windows.

#### **Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade**
*(Transition to a more efficient system after reducing heat loss.)*
4. **Replace Electric Convector with Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - The current **electric convectors** are inefficient and costly to run.
   - **Action:** Install an **air-water heat pump** (AWHP) with **low-temperature emissions**.
   - **Expected Benefit:** Lower energy bills and reduced carbon footprint.

#### **Phase 3: Ventilation & Air Quality**
*(Ensure proper air exchange in a now-sealed envelope.)*
5. **Install Ventilation System (Ventilation System B or C)**
   - The absence of a ventilation system may lead to moisture buildup.
   - **Action:** Install a **mechanical ventilation system** (e.g., System B or C) with heat recovery.
   - **Expected Benefit:** Improved indoor air quality and reduced condensation risks.

#### **Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Optional)**
*(Further reduce dependency on grid electricity.)*
6. **Install PV Panels (If Roof is Suitable)**
   - The building has **potential for PV panels**.
   - **Action:** Install **plug-and-play PV panels** on the roof (if structurally feasible).
   - **Expected Benefit:** Generates renewable electricity to power the heat pump and reduce energy costs.

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
| **Sequence** | **Action** | **Rationale** |
|-------------|------------|-------------|
| **1** | **Insulate Wall Cavity** (All 4 walls) | Reduces heat loss through walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Prevents cold air from seeping upward. |
| **3** | **Replace Glazing (Double Glazing)** | Reduces heat loss through windows. |
| **4** | **Replace Electric Convector with AWHP** | More efficient heating system. |
| **5** | **Install Ventilation System (B or C)** | Ensures proper air exchange. |
| **6** | **Install PV Panels (If Roof is Suitable)** | Generates renewable electricity. |

### **Cost-Effectiveness & Practicality**
- **Prioritize insulation** (highest ROI) before upgrading heating systems.
- **Replace glazing** before installing a heat pump (to avoid oversizing).
- **Consider ventilation** after sealing the envelope (to prevent moisture issues).
- **PV panels** are optional but recommended for long-term savings.

This plan ensures **maximum energy efficiency** while remaining **cost-effective**.
