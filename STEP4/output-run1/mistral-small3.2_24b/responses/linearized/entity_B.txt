### Renovation Plan

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** AttachedBuilding
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **Net Floor Area:** 212.0 m²
- **Current EPC Score:** 262 (C Label)
- **Potential for PV:** True
- **Heat Grid Availability:** False
- **Vertical Geothermal Drilling Potential:** False
- **Horizontal Geothermal Captation Potential:** False
- **Owner’s Motivation:** ToImproveEPC

**Key Issues:**
1. **Insulation:** Poor insulation on `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `FloorAboveBasement`, and `RoofExtension`.
2. **Heating System:** Radiators (likely inefficient).
3. **Ventilation System:** Type A (natural ventilation, less efficient).
4. **Windows:** Double-glazed with wooden frames in good condition.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

**Phase 1: Strengthen the Building Envelope (High Priority)**
1. **Insulate Wall on Exterior:**
   - **Action:** Apply exterior insulation to `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` (full brick, not insulated).
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is more effective for full brick walls and eliminates thermal bridges.
2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Add insulation to the floor above the basement.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts and reduces heat loss to the basement.
3. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** Heat rises rapidly through flat roofs; insulation here is critical.

**Phase 2: Transition to High-Efficiency Heating (Medium Priority)**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Rationale:** More efficient than the current boiler and suitable for the lower heat demand after insulation.
5. **Replace Emission System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.

**Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-Term Priority)**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install solar panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** Offsets the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system, further improving the EPC label.

**Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**
2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

**Rationale for Sequence:**
- **Insulation First:** Ensures the building envelope is sealed, reducing heat loss and lowering the heat demand.
- **Heat Pump Next:** With a sealed envelope, the heat pump operates more efficiently.
- **PV Panels Last:** Generates renewable energy to power the heat pump and ventilation system.

**Cost-Effectiveness and Practicality:**
- **Insulation:** Prioritizes areas with the highest heat loss.
- **Heat Pump:** More sustainable and efficient than the current boiler.
- **PV Panels:** Utilizes the building’s solar potential to offset energy costs.

This plan ensures a **fabric-first** approach, maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective.
