### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### **Building Overview**
- **Type**: Semi-detached building
- **Construction Year**: 1951
- **Current EPC Score**: 388 (D Label)
- **Potential for PV**: Yes
- **Heating System**: Air heating with radiators
- **Ventilation System**: Ventilation System A (natural)
- **Insulation Status**:
  - **Roof Main**: Not insulated
  - **Roof Extension**: Moderately insulated
  - **Walls (Front, Rear, Right)**: Not insulated
  - **Floor Above Basement**: Not insulated
- **Windows**:
  - **Living Area**: Double glazing, wooden frame
  - **Bedrooms**: Double glazing, wooden frame

#### **Renovation Objectives**
1. **Improve Energy Performance (EPC Score)**
2. **Maximize Energy Efficiency**
3. **Ensure Cost-Effectiveness**

#### **Proposed Renovation Path**

##### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First)**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
   - **Action**: Insulate the exterior walls (Front, Rear, Right) using exterior insulation.
   - **Rationale**: Exterior insulation is more effective for solid brick walls and eliminates thermal bridges. It also avoids reducing interior space.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Moderate
   - **Expected Energy Savings**: High

2. **Insulate Roof Main**
   - **Action**: Insulate the main roof (gable roof) with a vapor barrier.
   - **Rationale**: The main roof is currently not insulated, leading to significant heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Moderate
   - **Expected Energy Savings**: High

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the basement to prevent heat loss to the basement.
   - **Rationale**: The floor above the basement is currently not insulated, contributing to heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Low to Moderate
   - **Expected Energy Savings**: Medium

##### **Phase 2: Window and Ventilation Upgrades**
4. **Upgrade Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Replace Ventilation System A with Ventilation System C or D.
   - **Rationale**: A more efficient ventilation system ensures controlled air exchange, preventing moisture buildup and maintaining air quality in a sealed envelope.
   - **Estimated Cost**: Moderate
   - **Expected Energy Savings**: Medium

##### **Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: Install an Air-Water Heat Pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Rationale**: The site has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid, making an Air-Water Heat Pump the most viable and efficient alternative.
   - **Estimated Cost**: High
   - **Expected Energy Savings**: High

##### **Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable electricity.
   - **Rationale**: The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Estimated Cost**: High
   - **Expected Energy Savings**: High

#### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls** → **Insulate Roof Main** → **Insulate Floor Above Basement** → **Upgrade Ventilation System** → **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** → **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

#### **Expected Outcomes**
- **Improved EPC Score**: The renovation plan aims to significantly lower the EPC score, potentially moving from a D Label to a B or A Label.
- **Energy Efficiency**: The combination of insulation, efficient heating, and renewable energy generation will maximize energy efficiency.
- **Cost-Effectiveness**: The proposed sequence ensures that each renovation step is cost-effective and provides a high return on investment.

#### **Additional Considerations**
- **Permits**: Ensure that all necessary permits are obtained for the renovation work.
- **Professional Assistance**: Consult with architects, contractors, or craftsmen for expert advice and proper execution of the works.
- **Long-Term Maintenance**: Plan for regular maintenance of the new systems to ensure their longevity and efficiency.

This renovation plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, tailored to the specific needs and characteristics of the building.
