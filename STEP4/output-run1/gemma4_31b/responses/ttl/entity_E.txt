### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
The goal is to eliminate the most significant sources of heat loss. Because the building uses cavity walls, there is a highly cost-effective opportunity for insulation.

1.  **Insulate Wall Cavities:** All four external walls (Front, Left, Rear, Right) are identified as `CavityWall` and are currently `NotInsulated`. **Insulating the wall cavity** is the most cost-effective insulation method available, offering a high return on investment with low nuisance.
2.  **Replace Glazing:** The bedrooms and living area currently have `SingleGlazing`. This is a major thermal bridge. Since the wooden frames are in a `GoodState` (value 8), **replacing only the glazing** (upgrading to double or triple glazing) is more cost-effective than replacing the entire window unit.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space:** The floor above the crawl space is currently uninsulated. Adding insulation here prevents heat loss to the ground and eliminates cold drafts.

#### Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)
Once the building is sealed, the total heat load is reduced, allowing for a smaller, more efficient heating system.

4.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** The current system is a mix of radiators and electric convectors. To maximize the efficiency (COP) of a modern heat pump, the emission system should be upgraded to **low-temperature emissions** first.
5.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** With no potential for geothermal energy or a heat grid, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing boiler.

#### Phase 3: Ventilation and Air Quality (Priority: Medium)
Upgrading the envelope (walls and windows) reduces natural air infiltration. To prevent moisture buildup and maintain indoor air quality, the ventilation must be modernized.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more controlled system (such as System C or D) to ensure that heat is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and ventilation system.

7.  **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the new energy-efficient installations and further lower the EPC score.
