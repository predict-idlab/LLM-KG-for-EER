### Proposed Renovation Path

1.  **Insulate Gable Roof:** While the flat roof is well-insulated, the main gable roof is only **"Moderately Insulated."** Upgrading this to a "Well Insulated" state is the most logical first step to minimize the remaining thermal bridges.
2.  **Upgrade to Triple Glazing:** The windows currently have double glazing. For a building of this modern standard, transitioning to **triple glazing** is the most effective way to further reduce the EPC score and increase thermal comfort near window surfaces.

#### Phase 2: Transitioning to Sustainable Heating
The current heating setup relies on a boiler and electric convectors. Electric convectors are inefficient and lead to higher operational costs.

3.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since there is no potential for geothermal energy or a heat grid, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the optimal choice. It will replace the boiler and the inefficient electric convectors, leveraging the already high insulation levels to operate at maximum efficiency.
4.  **Optimize for Low-Temperature Emission:** To maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump, the existing floor heating and emission systems should be verified and tuned for **low-temperature operation**.

#### Phase 3: Energy Synergy
5.  **PV System Optimization:** The building already possesses PV panels. With the installation of a heat pump, the electricity demand will increase. The owner should ensure the existing PV system is optimized to offset the power consumption of the heat pump and the Ventilation System D.
