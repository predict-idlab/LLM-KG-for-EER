Insulating a building from 1832 significantly reduces natural air infiltration. To prevent humidity buildup and ensure a healthy indoor climate, the ventilation must be modernized.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more controlled system (such as System C or D). This ensures that the energy gains from the new insulation are not offset by uncontrolled air leakage.
