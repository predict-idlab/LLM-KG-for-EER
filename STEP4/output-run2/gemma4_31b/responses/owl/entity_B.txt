1.  **Insulate Flat Roof:** The `RoofExtension` is currently not insulated. Since heat rises, insulating this section is a high-priority, high-impact intervention.

#### Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)
Once the envelope is sealed, the total heat demand of the house will drop, allowing for the installation of a high-efficiency, low-temperature system.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more controlled system (such as System C or D) to ensure that the energy gains from insulation are not lost through uncontrolled drafts.
