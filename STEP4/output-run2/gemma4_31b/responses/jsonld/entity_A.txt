4.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** To maximize the efficiency (COP) of a modern heat pump, the current radiators and heating elements should be transitioned to a low-temperature emission system.

As the building is now better insulated and sealed, the natural air exchange rate will decrease.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (such as System C or D) to prevent moisture buildup and ensure a healthy indoor climate without losing excessive heat.
