### Renovation Plan for Attached Building (Construction Year: 1832)

**Current Status:**
- **EPC Score:** 262 (C Label)
- **Building Type:** Attached (19th Century)
- **Envelope Issues:** Non-insulated walls, floor, and roof extension
- **Windows:** Double-glazed (Good State)
- **Heating System:** Radiators with a boiler
- **Ventilation:** System A (Natural)
- **PV Potential:** Yes
- **Motivation:** Improve EPC

**Renovation Strategy:**
The goal is to reduce heat loss, upgrade the heating system, and add renewable energy generation. The sequence should prioritize **thermal envelope improvements** before transitioning to sustainable heat sources.

---

### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
   - **Action:** Exterior wall insulation (FullBrickWall)
   - **Why:** Eliminates thermal bridges, reduces heat loss.
   - **Method:** Apply rigid foam or mineral wool panels.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action:** Add insulation layer above basement.
   - **Why:** Prevents cold drafts, improves comfort.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof (RoofExtension)**
   - **Action:** Inverted roof insulation (warm roof).
   - **Why:** Heat rises rapidly through flat roofs; insulation here is critical.

4. **Insulate Gable Roof (RoofMain)**
   - **Action:** Add insulation between rafters.
   - **Why:** RoofMain is already moderately insulated, but further improvement is needed.

---

### **Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade (Medium Priority)**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Why:** More energy-efficient than boilers, compatible with low-temperature emissions.

6. **Place Low-Temperature Emission System**
   - **Action:** Transition radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Why:** Maximizes heat pump efficiency.

---

### **Phase 3: Ventilation & Air Quality (Medium Priority)**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from System A to System D.
   - **Why:** Mechanical ventilation ensures air quality in a sealed envelope.

---

### **Phase 4: Renewable Energy Generation (Long-Term Priority)**
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install solar panels.
   - **Why:** Offsets electricity use of heat pump and ventilation.

---

### **Optimal Sequence**
1. **Exterior Wall Insulation**
2. **Floor Insulation**
3. **Roof Insulation (Flat & Gable)**
4. **Heat Pump Installation**
5. **Low-Temperature Emission Upgrade**
6. **Ventilation Upgrade**
7. **PV Panels**

**Rationale:**
- **Seal First, Heat Second:** Insulation reduces heat demand, allowing for a smaller, more efficient heat pump.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Addressing the building envelope first lowers the overall energy load, reducing the size (and cost) of heating upgrades.
- **Sustainability:** PV panels are added last to power the new systems with renewable energy.

This plan balances **thermal comfort, energy efficiency, and cost-effectiveness** while adhering to the "fabric-first" principle.
