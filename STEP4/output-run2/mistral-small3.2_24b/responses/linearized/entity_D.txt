### Renovation Plan:

#### **Current Building Status:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached building from 2017.
- **EPC Score:** 87 (A+ label).
- **Key Elements:**
  - **RoofMain (Gable Roof):** Moderately insulated.
  - **RoofExtension (Flat Roof):** Well insulated.
  - **Walls (Front, Rear, Left):** All well insulated.
  - **FloorAboveBasement:** Well insulated.
  - **Windows:**
    - Living Area: Double-glazed with wooden frames (Good State).
    - Bedrooms: Double-glazed with wooden frames (Good State).
  - **Ventilation System:** Type D.
  - **Heating System:** Heat Pump.
  - **PV Panels Installed:** Yes.

#### **Owner's Motivation:**
- **ToImproveEPC:** The owner aims to further improve the EPC label.

#### **Renovation Recommendations:**
1. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from **Ventilation System D** to **Ventilation System C**.
   - **Rationale:** System C is more energy-efficient and better suited for modern, well-insulated homes.

2. **Replace Glazing (Living Area & Bedrooms):**
   - **Action:** Replace **Double Glazing** with **Triple Glazing** for all windows.
   - **Rationale:** Triple glazing further reduces heat loss, improving thermal comfort and lowering energy consumption.

3. **Insulate Gable Roof:**
   - **Action:** Improve insulation of **RoofMain** from "Moderately Insulated" to "Well Insulated".
   - **Rationale:** Enhances thermal performance and reduces heat loss through the gable roof.

4. **Place Sun Shade (Optional but Recommended):**
   - **Action:** Install **sun shades** on south-facing windows.
   - **Rationale:** Reduces solar heat gain in summer, lowering cooling needs and improving comfort.

#### **Optimal Sequence:**
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Replace Ventilation System (D → C)** | Reduces energy consumption and improves air quality. |
| **2** | **Replace Glazing (Double → Triple)** | Enhances thermal performance and reduces heat loss. |
| **3** | **Insulate Gable Roof** | Improves insulation and lowers energy demand. |
| **4** | **Place Sun Shade (Optional)** | Reduces solar heat gain and improves summer comfort. |

#### **Cost-Effectiveness & Energy Efficiency:**
- **Cost-Effective:** The proposed upgrades focus on high-impact, cost-effective measures that maximize energy savings.
- **Energy Efficiency:** Each step is designed to reduce energy consumption while improving thermal comfort and sustainability.

This plan ensures that the building remains at the forefront of energy efficiency while maintaining cost-effectiveness.
