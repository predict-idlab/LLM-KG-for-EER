### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached Building (1951, EPC 388)

**Primary Motivation:** Improve EPC (Energy Performance Certificate) score.

**Key Observations:**
1. **Building Envelope:** Several non-insulated elements (walls, roof, floor) leading to heat loss.
2. **Windows:** Double-glazed but with wooden frames in good condition.
3. **Heating System:** Current system (radiators + boiler) is inefficient for a well-insulated building.
4. **Potential for PV:** The building has confirmed potential for photovoltaic (PV) panels.
5. **Ventilation:** Current system (Type A) is basic and not optimized for an insulated building.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric-First Approach)**
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls:**
   - Walls (Front, Rear, Right) are full brick and uninsulated.
   - **Action:** Exterior wall insulation (EWI) is preferred over interior insulation to avoid thermal bridging and reduce condensation risks.
   - **Cost:** ~€150-200 per m² (depending on thickness and materials).

2. **Insulate Gable Roof (RoofMain):**
   - Currently uninsulated, leading to heat loss through the roof.
   - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof (RoofMain) to prevent heat loss.
   - **Cost:** ~€60 per m².

3. **Insulate Flat Roof (RoofExtension):**
   - Currently uninsulated, leading to heat loss through the roof.
   - **Action:** Insulate the flat roof (RoofExtension) to prevent heat loss.
   - **Cost:** ~€50 per m².

4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement (FloorAboveBasement):**
   - Currently uninsulated, leading to heat loss through the floor.
   - **Action:** Insulate the floor above the basement to prevent heat loss.
   - **Cost:** ~€30 per m².

**Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade**
5. **Replace Boiler with Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - Current boiler is inefficient for a well-insulated building.
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to reduce energy consumption and improve efficiency.
   - **Cost:** ~€8,000-10,000 (including installation).

**Phase 3: Ventilation System Upgrade**
6. **Upgrade Ventilation System to Type C or D:**
   - Current ventilation system (Type A) is not optimized for an insulated building.
   - **Action:** Install a more efficient ventilation system (Type C or D) to improve air quality and reduce energy loss.
   - **Cost:** ~€3,500-5,000 (including installation).

**Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
7. **Install PV Panels on Roof:**
   - The building has confirmed potential for PV panels.
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof to generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.
   - **Cost:** ~€275 per m² (including installation).

**Phase 5: Additional Measures (Optional)**
8. **Replace Glazing (if necessary):**
   - Current windows are double-glazed, but if they are old or in poor condition, consider replacing them with triple-glazed windows to further improve insulation.
   - **Cost:** ~€800 per window (including installation).

**Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Exterior Wall Insulation**
2. **Roof Insulation (Gable and Flat)**
3. **Floor Insulation (Above Basement)**
4. **Air-Water Heat Pump Installation**
5. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
6. **PV Panels Installation**

**Estimated Total Cost:** €25,000-35,000 (depending on materials, labor, and additional measures).

**Expected EPC Improvement:** From D Label (388) to B Label (100-200) or better, depending on the extent of the renovation and additional measures.

**Notes:**
- The proposed renovation path follows the **"Insulate → Ventilate → Generate"** sequence to maximize energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness.
- The sequence ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system.
- The use of the RENO ontology ensures that the proposed measures are grounded in the latest standards and best practices for energy-efficient renovations.
