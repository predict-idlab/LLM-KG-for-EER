### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (1862)

**Current EPC Score:** 165 (B Label)
**Primary Motivation:** Improve EPC
**Key Challenges:**
- **Thermal Envelope:** All exterior walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`, `Wall_Left`) and `FloorAboveCrawlSpace` are not insulated.
- **Windows:** All windows have single glazing.
- **Heating System:** Electric convectors and floor heating, which are inefficient for a pre-war building.

**Proposed Renovation Path (Fabric-First Approach):**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior** (Priority: High)
   - **Action:** Insulate `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`, and `Wall_Left` on the exterior.
   - **Why?** Since these are cavity walls, exterior insulation avoids structural issues and improves thermal performance.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Moderate (depends on insulation material and labor).

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** (Priority: High)
   - **Action:** Add insulation to `FloorAboveCrawlSpace`.
   - **Why?** Crawl spaces are often damp and poorly insulated, leading to significant heat loss. Insulating this floor will improve comfort and reduce energy demand.

**Phase 2: Window Performance Upgrade**
3. **Replace Glazing** (Priority: Medium)
   - **Action:** Replace all single glazing with **double glazing** (or triple if budget allows).
   - **Why?** Single glazing is a major source of heat loss. Upgrading to double/triple glazing will significantly improve thermal efficiency.
   - **Note:** Since the window frames (`WoodenWindowFrame`) are in good condition, only the glazing needs replacement.

**Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** (Priority: Medium)
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Why?** Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than boilers, especially in well-insulated buildings. Since geothermal potential is low and there is no heat grid, an air-water system is the best alternative.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** (Priority: Medium)
   - **Action:** Transition from radiators to low-temperature emissions (e.g., underfloor heating or low-temperature radiators).
   - **Why?** Low-temperature systems work best with heat pumps, maximizing efficiency.

**Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Optional but Recommended)**
6. **Place PV Panels on Roof** (Priority: Low)
   - **Action:** Install solar panels on the `RoofMain` (gable roof).
   - **Why?** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Generating renewable electricity will further improve the EPC label and reduce energy costs.

**Phase 5: Ventilation Upgrade (If Needed)**
7. **Replace Ventilation System** (Priority: Low)
   - **Action:** Upgrade from `VentilationSystemA` (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., `VentilationSystemC` or `VentilationSystemD`).
   - **Why?** Once the building is sealed, controlled ventilation is necessary to prevent moisture buildup.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence
1. **Exterior Wall Insulation**
2. **Floor Insulation**
3. **Glazing Replacement**
4. **Air-Water Heat Pump**
5. **Low-Temperature Emission Upgrade**
6. **PV Panels (Optional)**
7. **Ventilation Upgrade (If Needed)**

This plan prioritizes cost-effective, high-impact upgrades first, ensuring that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before transitioning to a more efficient heating system. The sequence follows the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** principle, which is widely accepted in energy-efficient renovations.
