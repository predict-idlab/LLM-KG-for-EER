Based on the analysis of the provided building-related information, here is a renovation plan that maximizes **energy efficiency** while remaining **cost-effective**:

### **Priority 1: Insulate the Building Envelope**
1. **Insulate Wall Cavity (Priority: High)**
   - **Affected Elements:** `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`
   - **Action:** Inject insulation into the cavity of these walls to eliminate thermal bridges.
   - **Rationale:** Cavity wall insulation is a cost-effective way to improve the thermal performance of the building envelope, especially for pre-1970s buildings. This action reduces heat loss through the walls, which are currently not insulated.

2. **Insulate Attic Floor (Priority: High)**
   - **Affected Elements:** `AtticFloor`
   - **Action:** Add insulation to the attic floor to prevent heat loss to the attic.
   - **Rationale:** Insulating the attic floor is a relatively simple and cost-effective way to improve energy efficiency, especially in older buildings where attics are often uninsulated.

### **Priority 2: Improve Window Performance**
3. **Replace Glazing (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Affected Elements:** `WindowGlazingLivingArea`, `WindowGlazingBedrooms`
   - **Action:** Replace single glazing with double or triple glazing to reduce heat loss through windows.
   - **Rationale:** Upgrading from single to double or triple glazing significantly improves the thermal performance of windows, reducing heat loss and improving comfort.

### **Priority 3: Upgrade Heating and Emission Systems**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Affected Elements:** `EmissionSystem`
   - **Action:** Replace the existing boiler with an air-water heat pump.
   - **Rationale:** Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than traditional boilers, especially when combined with a well-insulated building envelope.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Affected Elements:** `EmissionSystem`
   - **Action:** Transition the existing radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Operating the heat pump at lower temperatures increases its Coefficient of Performance (COP), making it more efficient.

### **Priority 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof (Priority: Long-term)**
   - **Affected Elements:** `RoofMain`
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable electricity.
   - **Rationale:** PV panels can offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and other appliances, further improving the building's energy efficiency and sustainability.

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
The proposed sequence of actions is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective. The sequence is as follows:

1. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
2. **Insulate Attic Floor**
3. **Replace Glazing**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
