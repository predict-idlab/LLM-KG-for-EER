### Renovation Plan:

**Building Overview:**
The building is a semi-detached house from 2017 with an EPC score of 87 (A label). The owner's primary motivation is to improve the EPC rating. The building has potential for PV, but no heat grid or geothermal potential.

**Renovation Path:**
To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the renovation should follow a "fabric-first" approach. This ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system.

**Proposed Renovation Sequence:**

1. **Insulate Exterior Walls:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the exterior walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Left`).
   - **Rationale:** All three external walls are full brick and not insulated. Insulating them on the exterior is more cost-effective than cavity insulation and significantly improves thermal retention.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** The floor above the basement is currently not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents cold drafts from the basement.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof is currently not insulated. Insulating it is a priority as heat rises rapidly through flat sections.

4. **Replace Glazing:**
   - **Action:** Replace the glazing of all windows, as they currently have double glazing but could benefit from triple glazing for better thermal performance.
   - **Rationale:** Enhancing window performance reduces remaining thermal heat losses.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an Air-Water Heat Pump.
   - **Rationale:** Since there is no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most viable and efficient alternative to the current heating system.

6. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the ventilation system from System D to a more efficient system (such as System C).
   - **Rationale:** Ensures that the heat gained from the new insulation is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage.

7. **Place PV Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.

**Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
The renovation plan follows the sequence of **Exterior Wall Insulation** → **Floor Insulation** → **Flat Roof Insulation** → **Air-Water Heat Pump Installation** → **Ventilation System Upgrade** → **PV Panel Installation**. This sequence ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized first, followed by the installation of efficient heating and renewable energy systems.
