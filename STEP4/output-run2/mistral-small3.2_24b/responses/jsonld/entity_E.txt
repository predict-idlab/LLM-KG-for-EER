### Renovation Plan for the Detached Building
The property is a detached building constructed in 1862 with an EPC score of 165 (D Label). The owner's primary motivation is to improve the EPC rating. The renovation should follow the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** sequence to ensure the most cost-effective approach.

#### **Phase 1: Strengthening the Building Envelope (High Priority)**
Since the building has multiple uninsulated elements, addressing these first is the most cost-effective way to lower the energy score.

1. **Insulate Cavity Walls (Exterior)**
   - **Walls**: `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Rear`, and `Wall_Right` are cavity walls and not insulated.
   - **Action**: **Insulate the cavity** of each wall. Since they are cavity walls, this method is more cost-effective than exterior insulation. The process involves drilling small holes in the outer masonry, injecting insulation, and sealing the holes.
   - **Benefit**: Eliminates thermal bridges and reduces heat loss.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - **Element**: `FloorAboveCrawlSpace` is currently not insulated.
   - **Action**: **Insulate the floor** to prevent cold air from rising into the living area.
   - **Benefit**: Eliminates drafts and improves overall thermal comfort.

#### **Phase 2: Window Performance Upgrade (Medium Priority)**
The building's windows have single glazing, which leads to significant heat loss.

3. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Windows**: All windows in `Bedrooms` and `LivingArea` have single glazing.
   - **Action**: **Replace the glazing** with double glazing. This will improve the thermal performance of the windows without replacing the entire frame.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss through windows and improves thermal comfort.

#### **Phase 3: Heating & Emission System Transition (Medium Priority)**
With a sealed envelope, the building's heat demand is now significantly lower, allowing for the installation of a more efficient, low-temperature system.

4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Current System**: `Generation_System` is a boiler.
   - **Action**: Install an **Air-Water Heat Pump** as a sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing boiler.
   - **Benefit**: Lowers the building's carbon footprint and reduces energy costs.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Current Emission System**: `EmissionSystem` includes radiators.
   - **Action**: **Transition the radiators to low-temperature emissions** to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.
   - **Benefit**: Ensures the heat pump operates efficiently and reduces energy consumption.

#### **Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-term Priority)**
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label.

6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Potential**: The building has confirmed potential for PV.
   - **Action**: **Install solar panels** on the roof.
   - **Benefit**: Provides renewable electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system, reducing reliance on the grid.

### **Optimal Sequence of Actions**
1. **Insulate Cavity Walls** (Highest priority)
2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
3. **Replace Glazing** (Medium priority)
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** (Medium priority)
5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof** (Long-term priority)

### **Summary Table**
| **Sequence** | **Action** | **Rationale** |
|-------------|------------|-------------|
| **1** | **Insulate Cavity Walls** | Reduces heat loss through uninsulated cavity walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Eliminates cold drafts from the crawl space. |
| **3** | **Replace Glazing** | Improves thermal performance of single-glazed windows. |
| **4** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | Replaces the boiler with a more efficient, sustainable heating system. |
| **5** | **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** | Optimizes the heat pump's performance. |

This plan ensures a **cost-effective**, **energy-efficient**, and **sustainable** renovation while aligning with the owner's motivation to improve the EPC rating.
