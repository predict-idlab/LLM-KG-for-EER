### Renovation Plan for Entity:
**Construction Year:** 1951
**Type:** Semi-Detached Building
**EPC Score:** 388 (D Label)
**Current Heating System:** Boiler + Radiators
**Current Ventilation:** System A (natural air supply and air exhaust)
**PV Potential:** Yes
**Heat Grid Availability:** No
**Geothermal Potential:** No

### Proposed Renovation Path:

#### Phase 1: Strengthen the Building Envelope (Priority: High)
1. **Insulate Roof Main (Gable Roof):**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofMain` (currently not insulated).
   - **Reason:** Reduce heat loss through the roof, which is a major source of thermal bridging.
   - **Rationale:** Since the roof is not insulated, this is a priority to prevent heat loss.

2. **Insulate Walls on Exterior:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, and `Wall_Right` (all full brick and not insulated).
   - **Reason:** These walls are currently not insulated, leading to significant heat loss. Exterior insulation is more effective for these walls.
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is preferred for full brick walls to eliminate thermal bridges.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `FloorAboveBasement` (currently not insulated).
   - **Reason:** Prevent cold drafts from the basement and reduce heat loss through the floor.
   - **Rationale:** The floor is a major source of heat loss, especially in older homes.

#### Phase 2: Window Performance (Priority: Medium)
4. **Replace Glazing (if necessary):**
   - **Action:** Evaluate the condition of the windows and replace glazing if it is single-glazed or in poor condition.
   - **Reason:** Ensure that the windows are energy-efficient and reduce heat loss.
   - **Rationale:** While the windows are currently double-glazed, their condition should be assessed to ensure they meet energy efficiency standards.

#### Phase 3: Heating & Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an Air-Water Heat Pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Reason:** Transition to a more efficient and sustainable heating system.
   - **Rationale:** With a sealed envelope, the building's heat demand is lower, making a heat pump more efficient.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Transition the current radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Reason:** Maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.
   - **Rationale:** Low-temperature emissions are more efficient with heat pumps.

#### Phase 4: Air Quality & Ventilation (Priority: Medium)
7. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A to a more efficient system (such as System C or D).
   - **Reason:** Ensure proper air exchange and maintain indoor air quality.
   - **Rationale:** A modern ventilation system is necessary to prevent moisture buildup and maintain air quality in a sealed envelope.

#### Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install solar panels on the roof to generate renewable electricity.
   - **Reason:** Offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV, making this a viable option.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence:

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulation (Roof $\rightarrow$ Walls $\rightarrow$ Floor)** | Reduces the total energy demand of the house. |
| **2** | **Glazing Replacement (if necessary)** | Reduce the remaining thermal heat losses. |
| **3** | **Heat Pump** | Switches to a sustainable energy source tailored to the new lower heat demand. |
| **4** | **Low-Temp Emission Upgrade** | Maximizes the efficiency of the heat pump. |
| **5** | **Ventilation Upgrade** | Maintains indoor air quality in a now-sealed envelope. |
| **6** | **PV Panels** | Generates renewable electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system. |

This plan ensures that the renovation is cost-effective, maximizes energy efficiency, and aligns with the entity's characteristics and motivations.
