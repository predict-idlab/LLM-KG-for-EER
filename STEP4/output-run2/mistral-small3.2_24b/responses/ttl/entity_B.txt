### Renovation Plan for Attached Building (Construction Year: 1832)

#### **Current Status:**
- **EPC Score:** 262.0 (C Label)
- **Building Type:** Attached (shared walls)
- **Construction Year:** 1832 (historic)
- **Key Issues:**
  - Non-insulated walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`)
  - Non-insulated flat roof (`RoofExtension`)
  - Moderately insulated gable roof (`RoofMain`)
  - Single glazing (though double glazing is mentioned, the frames are wooden and in good condition)
  - Ventilation System A (natural, inefficient)

#### **Renovation Strategy:**
The goal is to improve the EPC score while balancing cost-effectiveness and energy efficiency. The recommended sequence follows the **"Insulate → Ventilate → Generate"** principle.

---

### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`)**
   - **Action:** `InsulateWallExterior` (Ordering: 40)
   - **Cost:** €200/m²
   - **Rationale:** Full brick walls with no insulation are major sources of heat loss. Exterior insulation avoids reducing interior space and eliminates thermal bridges.

2. **Insulate Flat Roof (`RoofExtension`)**
   - **Action:** `InsulateFlatRoof` (Ordering: 25)
   - **Cost:** €50/m²
   - **Rationale:** Flat roofs lose significant heat; insulation here is cost-effective and prevents condensation issues.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement (`FloorAboveBasement`)**
   - **Action:** `InsulateFloorAboveBasement` (Ordering: 49)
   - **Cost:** €30/m²
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the basement and improves overall comfort.

---

### **Phase 2: Window Performance (Medium Priority)**
4. **Replace Glazing (if needed)**
   - **Action:** `ReplaceGlazing` (Ordering: 43)
   - **Cost:** €200/m²
   - **Rationale:** If the double glazing is old or damaged, replacement improves thermal performance. However, since the glazing type is already `DoubleGlazing`, this may not be necessary.

---

### **Phase 3: Heating & Emission System Transition (Medium Priority)**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** `PlaceAirWaterHeatpump` (Ordering: 67)
   - **Cost:** €8000 (fixed) + €35/m²
   - **Rationale:** Replaces the existing boiler with a more efficient, low-carbon alternative. Since geothermal potential is low, an air-source heat pump is ideal.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action:** `PlaceLowTemperatureEmission` (Ordering: 70)
   - **Cost:** €100/m²
   - **Rationale:** Ensures the heat pump operates at maximum efficiency by adapting the emission system to lower temperatures.

---

### **Phase 4: Ventilation Upgrade (Low Priority)**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** `ReplaceVentilationSystem` (Ordering: 52)
   - **Cost:** €3500 (fixed) + €17/m²
   - **Rationale:** Upgrading from System A (natural) to System C or D improves air quality and reduces moisture buildup in a now-sealed envelope.

---

### **Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-Term)**
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** `PlacePVPanelsOnRoof` (Ordering: 85)
   - **Cost:** €275/m²
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Solar panels will offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.

---

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
| Sequence | Action                          | Rationale                                                                                     |
|----------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1        | Insulate Exterior Walls          | Eliminates thermal bridges and reduces heat loss.                                              |
| 2        | Insulate Flat Roof               | Prevents heat loss through the roof and avoids condensation issues.                          |
| 3        | Insulate Floor Above Basement    | Prevents cold drafts from the basement.                                                     |
| 4        | Replace Glazing (if needed)      | Improves thermal performance if the existing glazing is damaged or inefficient.             |
| 5        | Place Air-Water Heat Pump        | Replaces the boiler with a more efficient, low-carbon heating system.                       |
| 6        | Place Low-Temperature Emission   | Maximizes the efficiency of the heat pump by adapting the emission system.                  |
| 7        | Replace Ventilation System       | Improves air quality and reduces moisture buildup in a sealed envelope.                     |
| 8        | Place PV-Panels on Roof          | Offsets electricity consumption and further improves the EPC label.                          |

This plan ensures a cost-effective, phased approach to improving energy efficiency while maximizing long-term savings.
