### Renovation Plan for Attached Building (EPC: 50.0)

Given the building's high EPC score (A label), the focus should be on **maintaining efficiency** and **further optimizing performance** while ensuring **cost-effectiveness**. The current state indicates good insulation and modern systems, so the plan will prioritize **minimizing disruption** and **maximizing long-term savings**.

---

#### **Phase 1: Fine-Tuning Insulation (Low Disruption, High Impact)**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - The `RoofMain` is moderately insulated. Upgrading to **well-insulated** status will further reduce heat loss.
   - *Cost:* €60/m² (low nuisance level).
   - *Note:* Ensure vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation.

2. **Insulate Flat Roof (if possible)**
   - The `RoofExtension` is already well-insulated, but consider **inverted roof structure** for better thermal performance.
   - *Cost:* €50/m² (high nuisance level, but long-term benefits).

---

#### **Phase 2: Heating System Optimization**
3. **Place Low-Temperature Emission System**
   - The current `EmissionSystem` (electric convector + floor heating) can be optimized for **lower temperature setpoints**, improving efficiency.
   - *Cost:* €100/m² (moderate nuisance level).

4. **Replace Ventilation System (if needed)**
   - The `VentilationSystemD` is already mechanical, but verify if **heat recovery** is in place to minimize energy loss.
   - *Cost:* €3,500 (fixed) + €17/m² (if ductwork changes are needed).

### **Optimal Sequence & Rationale**
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | **Insulate Gable Roof** | Reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| 2 | **Optimize Emission System** | Ensures heating system works efficiently with the existing insulation. |

### **Key Considerations**
- **Permits:** No major permits required for insulation or emission upgrades.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Prioritize actions with the **highest ROI** (e.g., roof insulation before PV).
- **Disruption:** Minimize nuisance by staggering high-disruption tasks (e.g., roof work in summer).

This plan ensures **long-term energy efficiency** while keeping costs **controlled**.
