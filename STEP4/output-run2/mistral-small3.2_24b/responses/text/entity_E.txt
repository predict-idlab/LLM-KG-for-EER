### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Highest Priority)**
Since the house has **uninsulated cavity walls**, **single-glazed windows**, and an **uninsulated floor above the crawl space**, these should be addressed first to reduce heat loss.

1. **Insulate Cavity Walls (Exterior or Interior)**

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - If the floor is uninsulated, adding **rigid insulation boards** or **suspended floor insulation** will prevent heat loss to the crawl space.
   - *Rationale*: Eliminates drafts and improves comfort.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof (If Applicable)**
   - If there is a flat-roof extension (not mentioned in your description), insulating it should be a priority.

### **Phase 2: Window Upgrades (Medium Priority)**
4. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - Since the windows have **single glazing**, upgrading to **double or triple glazing** (while keeping the existing wooden frames if in good condition) will significantly reduce heat loss.
   - *Rationale*: Windows are a major source of heat loss, and double/triple glazing improves insulation.

### **Phase 3: Heating System Upgrade (Medium Priority)**
5. **Replace Boiler with an Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - Since **geothermal is not feasible** and **district heating is unavailable**, an **air-water heat pump** is the most efficient and sustainable replacement for the old boiler.
   - *Rationale*: Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than traditional boilers, especially after improving insulation.

6. **Upgrade Heating Emission Systems**
   - Replace old radiators, underfloor heating, and electric convectors with **low-temperature emission systems** (e.g., modern radiators or underfloor heating designed for heat pumps).
   - *Rationale*: Ensures the heat pump operates efficiently.

7. **Replace Natural Ventilation with a Mechanical System (System C or D)**

8. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels on the Roof**
