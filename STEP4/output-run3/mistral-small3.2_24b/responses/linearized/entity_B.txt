### Renovation Plan for Attached Building (1832)

#### Key Objectives:
1. **Improve EPC Label** (Current: 262 - C Label)
2. **Maximize Energy Efficiency**
3. **Ensure Cost-Effectiveness**

#### Proposed Renovation Path:

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)**
1. **Insulate Wall Exterior** (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear)
   - *Action:* Insulate the exterior of both full brick walls to eliminate thermal bridges.
   - *Rationale:* The walls are not insulated and have no historical protection, making exterior insulation feasible and effective.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - *Action:* Add insulation to the floor above the basement to prevent heat loss to the unconditioned space.
   - *Rationale:* The floor is currently not insulated, and insulation will reduce heat loss significantly.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof** (RoofExtension)
   - *Action:* Insulate the flat roof to prevent heat loss through the roof.
   - *Rationale:* The roof is currently not insulated, and insulation will reduce heat loss significantly.

**Phase 2: Heating System Transition (Priority: Medium)**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - *Action:* Install an air-water heat pump as the primary heating source.
   - *Rationale:* The building has no geothermal potential and no heat grid, making an air-water heat pump the most sustainable and efficient option.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - *Action:* Transition the current radiators to low-temperature emissions to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.
   - *Rationale:* Low-temperature emissions improve the efficiency of the heat pump.

**Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - *Action:* Install solar panels on the roof to generate renewable electricity.
   - *Rationale:* The building has confirmed potential for PV, and solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.

**Phase 4: Ventilation System Upgrade (Priority: Medium)**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - *Action:* Upgrade from Ventilation System A to a more efficient system (e.g., System C or D).
   - *Rationale:* The current ventilation system is not efficient, and an upgrade will improve indoor air quality and reduce energy loss.

#### Summary of Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate Wall Exterior**
2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**

#### Cost-Effectiveness and Energy Efficiency:
- **Cost-Effective:** The proposed plan prioritizes high-impact, low-cost measures first, such as insulation and ventilation upgrades.
- **Energy Efficient:** The plan ensures that the building envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system.

This renovation plan is tailored to the specific needs and characteristics of the building, ensuring that the EPC label is improved while maximizing energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness.
