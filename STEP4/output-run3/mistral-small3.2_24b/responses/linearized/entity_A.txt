### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached Building (1951, EPC 388)

**Objective:** Improve energy efficiency and lower EPC score from 388 to an optimal level while remaining cost-effective.

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
1. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**
   - **Action:** Insulate `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Right` (full brick walls, currently uninsulated).
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation eliminates thermal bridges and is more effective than interior insulation for full brick walls.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Moderate (depends on cladding type).

2. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Action:** Insulate `RoofExtension` (currently moderately insulated).
   - **Rationale:** Flat roofs lose significant heat; upgrading insulation will reduce heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Low to moderate.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action:** Insulate `FloorAboveBasement` (currently uninsulated).
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold air infiltration from the basement.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Low.

#### **Phase 2: Window Upgrades**
4. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action:** Replace glazing in `WindowsBedrooms` and `WindowsLivingArea` (currently double-glazed, but frames are wooden and in good condition).
   - **Rationale:** Upgrading to triple glazing will further reduce heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Moderate.

#### **Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump as the primary heating source.
   - **Rationale:** Replaces the existing boiler with a more efficient, sustainable system.
   - **Estimated Cost:** High.

6. **Place Low-Temperature Emission System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Moderate.

#### **Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on `RoofMain` (gable roof).
   - **Rationale:** Generates renewable electricity to offset the heat pump's consumption.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Moderate.

#### **Phase 5: Ventilation System Upgrade**
8. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from `VentilationSystemA` (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., `VentilationSystemC` or `VentilationSystemD`).
   - **Rationale:** Ensures proper air exchange in a now-sealed envelope.
   - **Estimated Cost:** Low to moderate.

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
2. **Insulate Flat Roof**
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
4. **Replace Glazing**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
6. **Place Low-Temperature Emission System**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
8. **Replace Ventilation System**

### **Key Considerations**
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Prioritize actions with the highest return on investment (e.g., insulation before glazing upgrades).
- **Sequencing:** Follow the "Insulate → Ventilate → Generate" principle to avoid oversizing HVAC systems.
- **Permits:** Check local regulations for permits (e.g., roof underlayment, PV panel installation).

This plan ensures a systematic, cost-effective approach to improving energy efficiency and lowering the EPC score.
