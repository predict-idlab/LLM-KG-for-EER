### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (EPC: 165)

Given the building's characteristics—detached, constructed in 1862, with an EPC score of 165 (B Label)—the renovation plan will focus on improving energy efficiency while maintaining cost-effectiveness. The strategy follows the "fabric-first" approach, prioritizing thermal envelope upgrades before addressing heating systems.

#### Key Observations:
1. **Building Type**: Detached (high heat loss through external walls).
2. **Construction Year**: 1862 (likely poor insulation standards).
3. **Current EPC**: 165 (B Label).
4. **Walls**: Four cavity walls, uninsulated (high priority for improvement).
5. **Roof**: Well-insulated (no immediate action needed).
6. **Windows**: Single-glazed (poor thermal performance).
7. **Heating System**: Electric convector and radiators (inefficient).
8. **Ventilation**: Natural (System A, inadequate for modern standards).
9. **PV Potential**: Confirmed (opportunity for renewable energy).

---

### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)
1. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
   - **Action**: Inject insulation into the cavities of all four external walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`).
   - **Rationale**: Cavity wall insulation is cost-effective and significantly reduces heat loss. Ensure the cavities are clean and wide enough for proper installation.
   - **Estimated Cost**: ~€22/m² (based on RENO ontology).

2. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action**: Upgrade all windows from single to double glazing.
   - **Rationale**: Single glazing contributes to high heat loss. Double glazing improves thermal performance without altering the window frames.
   - **Estimated Cost**: ~€200/m² (based on RENO ontology).

3. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Add insulation to the floor above the crawl space.
   - **Rationale**: Prevents heat loss to unconditioned spaces below.
   - **Estimated Cost**: ~€30/m² (based on RENO ontology).

#### Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade (Medium Priority)
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler and electric convector.
   - **Rationale**: Heat pumps are more efficient and sustainable, especially after improving the thermal envelope.
   - **Estimated Cost**: ~€8,000 (fixed) + €35/m² (based on RENO ontology).

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action**: Modify the radiators or underfloor heating to operate at lower temperatures.
   - **Rationale**: Optimizes the heat pump's efficiency.
   - **Estimated Cost**: ~€100/m² (based on RENO ontology).

#### Phase 3: Ventilation Upgrade (Medium Priority)
6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to System C or D (mechanical).
   - **Rationale**: Ensures proper air exchange and reduces moisture buildup in a now-sealed envelope.
   - **Estimated Cost**: ~€3,500 (fixed) + €17/m² (based on RENO ontology).

#### Phase 4: Renewable Energy Integration (Long-Term)
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the well-insulated gable roof.
   - **Rationale**: Generates renewable electricity to power the heat pump and reduce grid dependence.
   - **Estimated Cost**: ~€275/m² (based on RENO ontology).
