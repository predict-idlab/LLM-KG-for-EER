### Renovation Plan for Attached Building (Construction Year: 1832)

#### Building Characteristics:
- **Type:** Attached Building
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **Current EPC Score:** 262.0 (C Label)
- **Current Insulation:**
  - **Roof Extension (Flat Roof):** Not Insulated
  - **Roof Main (Gable Roof):** Moderately Insulated
  - **Walls (Front and Rear):** Not Insulated
  - **Floor Above Basement:** Not Insulated
- **Windows:** Double Glazing, Wooden Frames (Good State)
- **Ventilation System:** Type A (Natural)
- **Heating System:** Radiators
- **Potential for PV:** True
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** False

#### Owner's Motivation:
- **Primary Goal:** Improve EPC (Energy Performance Certificate) rating

---

### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric-First Approach)
1. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofExtension` (Flat Roof) to prevent heat loss through the roof.
   - **Rationale:** Flat roofs are particularly prone to heat loss, and insulation here will significantly reduce energy consumption.

2. **Insulate Exterior Walls:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** Since these walls are full brick and not insulated, exterior insulation is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges and improve thermal performance.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** Insulating the floor above the basement prevents cold drafts from the basement and improves overall comfort.

#### Phase 2: Window Performance
4. **Maintain Current Windows:**
   - **Action:** No action required for windows at this stage.
   - **Rationale:** The windows already have double glazing and wooden frames in good condition, so replacing them is not cost-effective at this stage.

#### Phase 3: Heating System Transition
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an Air-Water Heat Pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Rationale:** With a sealed envelope, the building's heat demand is lower, making a heat pump more efficient and sustainable.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** This maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump, ensuring optimal efficiency.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the `RoofMain` (Gable Roof).
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV, and installing solar panels will provide renewable electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system.

#### Phase 5: Ventilation System Upgrade
8. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., System C or D).
   - **Rationale:** A modern ventilation system ensures proper air exchange in a now-sealed envelope, preventing moisture buildup and maintaining indoor air quality.
