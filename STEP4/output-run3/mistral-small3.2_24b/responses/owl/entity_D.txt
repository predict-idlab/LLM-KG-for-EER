### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached Building (2017)
The property is a semi-detached building from 2017 with an EPC score of 87 (A Label). The owner's primary motivation is to improve the EPC rating.

#### **Current Energy Efficiency Status**
- The building is well-insulated (`WellInsulated` for most elements).
- Windows are double-glazed (`DoubleGlazing`).
- The heating system is a heat pump (`HeatPump`).
- Ventilation is System D (`VentilationSystemD`).
- There is no potential for PV panels (`false`).
- No reasons to change the emission system (`false`).

#### **Renovation Recommendations**
Since the building already has a high EPC score, the focus should be on **maintaining and optimizing the current setup** rather than major renovations.

1. **Regular Maintenance of Heat Pump and Ventilation System**
   - Schedule annual inspections and maintenance for the heat pump and ventilation system to ensure optimal performance.
   - Clean or replace filters in the ventilation system regularly to maintain air quality.

2. **Window Maintenance**
   - Check the condition of the double-glazed windows. If any damage or drafts are detected, repair or replace the affected units.
   - Ensure that all windows are properly sealed to prevent air leakage.

3. **Energy-Efficient Lighting and Appliances**
   - Replace any remaining incandescent bulbs with LED lighting.
   - Upgrade to energy-efficient appliances if any are outdated.

4. **Smart Thermostat Installation**
   - Install a smart thermostat to optimize heating schedules and reduce energy consumption.

5. **Monitor and Optimize Heat Pump Performance**
   - Regularly check the performance of the heat pump and adjust settings as needed to ensure it operates efficiently.

6. **Inspect and Maintain Insulation**
   - Periodically inspect the insulation of walls, roof, and floor to ensure it remains in good condition.
   - Address any areas where insulation may have degraded or become damaged.

7. **Review and Optimize Energy Consumption Patterns**
   - Analyze energy consumption patterns to identify areas for further efficiency improvements.
   - Consider installing a home energy management system to monitor and control energy use.

#### **Summary of Actionable Steps**
| Action | Rationale |
|---|---|
| **Maintain Heat Pump & Ventilation System** | Ensures optimal performance and efficiency. |
| **Window Maintenance** | Prevents air leakage and maintains insulation. |
| **Energy-Efficient Lighting & Appliances** | Reduces energy consumption. |
| **Install Smart Thermostat** | Optimizes heating schedules and reduces energy use. |
| **Monitor Heat Pump Performance** | Ensures the heat pump operates efficiently. |
| **Inspect and Maintain Insulation** | Maintains the integrity of the building's thermal envelope. |
| **Review and Optimize Energy Consumption Patterns** | Identifies further efficiency opportunities. |

By following this plan, the building's high EPC score can be maintained and potentially further improved, ensuring continued energy efficiency and cost savings.
