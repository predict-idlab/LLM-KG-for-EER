### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached House in Ghent, Belgium (Built 1951)

#### **Current EPC Score:** 388
#### **Key Goals:** Improve energy efficiency, reduce energy consumption, and enhance long-term operational performance.

---

### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric-First Approach)**
**Objective:** Reduce heat loss by insulating key thermal bridges.

1. **Insulate Pitched Roof**
   - The main roof is a pitched roof without insulation.
   - Insulating the pitched roof will significantly reduce heat loss, as heat rises.
   - Recommended: Add insulation between and over the rafters.

2. **Insulate Exterior Walls (Interior Insulation)**
   - The exterior walls are solid brick and uninsulated.
   - Interior insulation is cost-effective and avoids structural changes.
   - Recommended: Use high-performance insulation (e.g., mineral wool or rigid foam).

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - The floor above the basement is uninsulated.
   - Insulating this floor reduces heat loss to the basement and improves comfort.
   - Recommended: Add rigid insulation boards or use a floating floor system.

---

### **Phase 2: Window Performance**
**Objective:** Further reduce heat loss through windows.

4. **Upgrade to High-Performance Double or Triple Glazing (if budget allows)**
   - The windows currently have double glazing, but upgrading to high-performance double or triple glazing can further improve thermal efficiency.
   - Recommended: Consider low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings and argon-filled glazing for better insulation.

---

### **Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
**Objective:** Replace the old boiler with a more efficient and sustainable heating system.

5. **Install an Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - Since the site is not suitable for geothermal energy and there is no district heating, an air-water heat pump is the most viable alternative.
   - Recommended: Pair the heat pump with low-temperature emission systems (e.g., underfloor heating or low-temperature radiators) for optimal performance.

---

### **Phase 4: Ventilation Upgrade**
**Objective:** Ensure proper air quality and reduce heat loss through uncontrolled ventilation.

6. **Upgrade to a Mechanical Ventilation System with Heat Recovery (MVHR)**
   - The current natural ventilation system is inefficient and can lead to heat loss.
   - Recommended: Install a System C or D ventilation system to maintain indoor air quality while recovering heat from exhausted air.

---

### **Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation**
**Objective:** Generate renewable energy to offset the heat pump’s electricity consumption.

7. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - The house has potential for PV panel installation.
   - Recommended: Install PV panels on the roof to generate electricity for the heat pump and other household needs.

---

### **Optimal Renovation Sequence**
| **Sequence** | **Action** | **Rationale** |
|-------------|-----------|--------------|
| 1 | Insulate Pitched Roof | Reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| 2 | Insulate Exterior Walls (Interior) | Improves thermal efficiency of solid brick walls. |
| 3 | Insulate Floor Above Basement | Reduces heat loss to the basement. |
| 4 | Upgrade Glazing (if budget allows) | Enhances window insulation. |
| 5 | Install Air-Water Heat Pump | Replaces the old boiler with a more efficient system. |
| 6 | Upgrade Ventilation to MVHR | Ensures proper air quality and heat recovery. |
| 7 | Install PV Panels | Generates renewable energy to power the heat pump. |

---

### **Summary**
This renovation plan follows a **fabric-first approach**, ensuring that the building’s thermal envelope is optimized before upgrading the heating system. The sequence maximizes energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective. By insulating key thermal bridges, upgrading the heating system, and installing PV panels, the EPC score will improve significantly, leading to long-term energy savings and enhanced comfort.
