### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (1862)

**Current State:**
- **EPC Score:** 165 (B Label)
- **Construction Year:** 1862
- **Building Type:** Detached
- **Insulation Grade:**
  - All walls: Not Insulated (Cavity Walls)
  - Floor Above Crawl Space: Not Specified (Assumed Uninsulated)
  - Roof: Well Insulated
- **Windows:**
  - Glazing: Single Glazing (Poor Thermal Performance)
  - Frame Material: Wood (State: Good)
- **Heating System:** Boiler
- **Ventilation System:** Natural (System A)
- **PV Potential:** True
- **Heat Grid Availability:** False

**Owner's Motivation:** Improve EPC

---

### Proposed Renovation Path (Cost-Effective & Energy Efficiency Focused)

**Phase 1: Building Envelope Optimization (High Priority)**
1. **Insulate Cavity Walls (Exterior)**
   - *Action:* Inject insulation into cavity walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right).
   - *Rationale:* Eliminates thermal bridges and reduces heat loss through walls. Exterior insulation is more cost-effective for cavity walls.
   - *Estimated Impact:* ~10-20% reduction in heat loss.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - *Action:* Add insulation to the floor to prevent heat loss to the crawl space.
   - *Rationale:* Prevents cold drafts and improves thermal comfort on the ground floor.
   - *Estimated Impact:* ~5-10% reduction in heat loss.

3. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - *Action:* Replace all windows with double or triple glazing.
   - *Rationale:* Single glazing is a significant source of heat loss. Upgrading to double/triple glazing improves thermal performance.
   - *Estimated Impact:* ~15-25% reduction in heat loss.

**Phase 2: Heating System Transition (Medium Priority)**
4. **Replace Boiler with Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - *Action:* Install an air-water heat pump.
   - *Rationale:* More energy-efficient than a boiler and suitable for the building’s lower heat demand after insulation improvements.
   - *Estimated Impact:* ~20-30% reduction in energy consumption.

5. **Upgrade Ventilation System**
   - *Action:* Replace natural ventilation (System A) with a mechanical system (System C or D).
   - *Rationale:* Ensures proper air exchange in a sealed building envelope.
   - *Estimated Impact:* Improves indoor air quality and thermal comfort.

**Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-Term Priority)**
6. **Install PV Panels on Roof**
   - *Action:* Install photovoltaic panels on the roof.
   - *Rationale:* Generates renewable electricity to offset the heat pump’s energy consumption.
   - *Estimated Impact:* Reduces reliance on the grid and lowers energy costs.

---

### Summary of Optimal Sequence

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Cavity Walls (Exterior)** | Eliminates thermal bridges and reduces heat loss. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Prevents cold drafts and improves thermal comfort. |
| **3** | **Replace Single Glazing with Double/Triple Glazing** | Improves thermal performance of windows. |
| **4** | **Replace Boiler with Air-Water Heat Pump** | More energy-efficient heating. |
| **5** | **Upgrade Ventilation System** | Ensures proper air exchange in a sealed envelope. |
| **6** | **Install PV Panels on Roof** | Generates renewable electricity. |

**Note:** The proposed plan follows the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** sequence to ensure that the building’s thermal envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system.
