2.  **Insulate Walls on Exterior:** Both `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` are full brick and not insulated. For full brick walls, exterior insulation is the most effective way to eliminate thermal bridges and protect the structure.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The `FloorAboveBasement` is currently not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents heat loss to the basement and improves ground-floor thermal comfort.

#### Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)

The final phase ensures the building remains healthy and energy-independent.
6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrading from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more controlled system (such as System C or D) is essential after insulating the house to prevent moisture buildup and ensure fresh air circulation.
