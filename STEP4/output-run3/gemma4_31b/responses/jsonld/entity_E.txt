### Proposed Renovation Path

Injecting insulation into these cavities is one of the most cost-effective upgrades available, offering a high return on investment with low nuisance.

Since the wooden window frames are in a **"Good State,"** it is more cost-effective to **replace the glazing** (upgrading to double or triple glazing) rather than replacing the entire window units.

As the house becomes more airtight through insulation and glazing upgrades, mechanical ventilation becomes necessary to prevent moisture buildup and ensure healthy indoor air quality.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from **Ventilation System A** (natural) to a more efficient system (such as System C or D) to maintain air quality without losing the heat gained from the new insulation.
