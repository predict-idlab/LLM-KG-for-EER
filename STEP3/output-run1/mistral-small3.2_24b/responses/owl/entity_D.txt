### Renovation Plan for the Building

#### Step 1: Assess the Building's Current State
The building is a semi-detached structure constructed in 2017. It has several key elements:
- **Roofs**: Two roofs (gable and flat) with moderate to well insulation.
- **Walls**: All walls are full brick and well insulated.
- **Windows**: Windows in the living area and bedrooms have wooden frames with double glazing.
- **Ventilation System**: Type D (mechanical air supply and exhaust).
- **Generation System**: Heat pump.
- **PV Panels**: Present but with no potential for additional installation.
- **EPC Score**: 87 (indicating good energy performance but room for improvement).

#### Step 2: Identify Priority Renovations
Given the EPC score of 87 and the owner's motivation to improve it, the following renovations are suggested:

1. **Insulate the Flat Roof**
   - **Reasoning**: The flat roof is well insulated but could benefit from additional insulation to further improve energy efficiency.
   - **Action**: Insulate the flat roof according to the suggested guidelines.
   - **Cost**: €50 per m².

2. **Replace the Gable Roof**
   - **Reasoning**: The gable roof is moderately insulated. Replacing it with a better-insulated roof could significantly improve the building's energy performance.
   - **Action**: Replace the gable roof with a more energy-efficient option.
   - **Cost**: €260 per m².

3. **Insulate the Attic Floor**
   - **Reasoning**: The attic floor is well insulated, but ensuring proper insulation can prevent heat loss and improve overall energy efficiency.
   - **Action**: Insulate the attic floor according to the suggested guidelines.
   - **Cost**: €60 per m².

4. **Replace the Ventilation System**
   - **Reasoning**: The current ventilation system (Type D) is efficient but could be upgraded to a more advanced system for better air quality and energy savings.
   - **Action**: Replace the ventilation system with a more efficient model.
   - **Cost**: €3,500 (fixed) + €17 per m².

5. **Place a Heat Pump Boiler**
   - **Reasoning**: The current generation system is a heat pump, but adding a heat pump boiler can further enhance heating efficiency.
   - **Action**: Install a heat pump boiler.
   - **Cost**: €4,000 (fixed).

6. **Place PV Panels on the Roof**
   - **Reasoning**: The building has PV panels but no potential for additional installation. However, ensuring the existing panels are optimally placed can improve energy generation.
   - **Action**: Review the placement of existing PV panels and optimize if necessary.
   - **Cost**: €275 per m².

7. **Place Sunshades**
   - **Reasoning**: Sunshades can help regulate the building's temperature by blocking excessive sunlight, reducing the need for cooling.
   - **Action**: Install sunshades on relevant windows.
   - **Cost**: €750 (fixed).

#### Step 3: Optimal Sequence of Renovations
To maximize the benefits of each renovation step, the following sequence is recommended:
1. **Insulate the Flat Roof**: This ensures a solid foundation for further improvements.
2. **Replace the Gable Roof**: Enhances overall insulation and structural integrity.
3. **Insulate the Attic Floor**: Complements the roof improvements.
4. **Replace the Ventilation System**: Ensures proper air quality and efficiency.
5. **Place a Heat Pump Boiler**: Optimizes heating efficiency.
6. **Place PV Panels on the Roof**: Maximizes energy generation.
7. **Place Sunshades**: Regulates temperature and reduces cooling needs.

#### Step 4: Cost Estimation and Budgeting
- **Total Estimated Cost**: Approximately €15,525 (assuming 50 m² for roof and attic floor insulation, and considering fixed costs for other actions).
- **Potential Savings**: Improved EPC score and reduced energy consumption can lead to long-term savings on energy bills.

#### Step 5: Monitoring and Maintenance
- Regularly monitor the performance of the new systems and insulation to ensure they are functioning optimally.
- Schedule periodic maintenance for the ventilation system and heat pump boiler to prolong their lifespan and efficiency.

This plan provides a structured approach to improving the building's energy efficiency and overall comfort while considering cost-effectiveness and practicality.
