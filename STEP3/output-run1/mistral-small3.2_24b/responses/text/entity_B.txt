Based on the information provided, here's a step-by-step renovation plan that prioritizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness for your 1832 attached house in Ghent, Belgium:

### Step 1: Air Sealing and Insulation
1. **Exterior Walls Insulation**: Since the front and rear walls are uninsulated solid brick walls, consider internal wall insulation (IWI) to minimize disruption and maintain the exterior aesthetic. This will significantly reduce heat loss.
2. **Flat-Roof Extension Insulation**: Insulate the uninsulated flat roof extension to match the moderate insulation level of the main pitched roof. Use high-performance insulation materials suitable for flat roofs.
3. **Floor Insulation**: Insulate the floor above the basement to prevent heat loss through the ground. Use rigid insulation boards with a high R-value.

### Step 2: Heating System Upgrade
1. **Replace Boiler and Radiators**: Install a modern, condensing boiler with a high efficiency rating (e.g., ErP A-rated). Replace old radiators with new, low-water-content radiators or consider underfloor heating for better heat distribution.
2. **Heat Pump Consideration**: While the site is not suitable for geothermal, consider an air-source heat pump (ASHP) if the climate and budget allow. ASHPs can be more efficient than boilers, especially when combined with low-temperature heating systems like underfloor heating.

### Step 3: Ventilation System Upgrade
1. **Install Mechanical Ventilation with Heat Recovery (MVHR)**: Replace the basic natural ventilation system with an MVHR system to improve indoor air quality and reduce heat loss. This is particularly important after insulating the walls and roof.

### Step 4: Window Upgrades (if necessary)
1. **Window Replacement**: Although the windows have double glazing, consider upgrading to triple glazing or high-performance double glazing with low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings and argon or krypton gas fill. Ensure the wooden frames are well-sealed to prevent air leakage.

### Step 5: Photovoltaic Panels Installation
1. **Solar Panel Installation**: Given the potential for photovoltaic panels, install a solar panel system on the roof to generate renewable energy. This can significantly reduce electricity costs and improve the energy performance certificate (EPC) score.

### Step 6: Smart Controls and Monitoring
1. **Install Smart Thermostats and Controls**: Implement smart thermostats and zoning controls to optimize heating and reduce energy waste. This can be integrated with the new boiler or heat pump for better efficiency.

### Step 7: Final EPC Assessment
1. **Post-Renovation EPC Assessment**: After completing the renovations, conduct a new EPC assessment to evaluate the improved energy performance and identify any remaining areas for improvement.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulation and Air Sealing**: Start with insulating the walls, roof, and floor to minimize heat loss.
2. **Heating System Upgrade**: Replace the boiler and radiators to ensure efficient heat distribution.
3. **Ventilation Upgrade**: Install MVHR to maintain indoor air quality and reduce heat loss.
4. **Window Upgrades**: Upgrade windows if necessary to further improve insulation.
5. **Photovoltaic Panels**: Install solar panels to generate renewable energy.
6. **Smart Controls**: Implement smart controls to optimize energy usage.

This plan prioritizes cost-effective upgrades that will have the most significant impact on energy efficiency and long-term operational performance.
