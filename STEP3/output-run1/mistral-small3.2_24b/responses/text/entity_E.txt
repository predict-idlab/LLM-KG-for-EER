To improve the energy efficiency of your 1862 detached house in Ghent, Belgium, while keeping the renovations cost-effective, I'll outline a step-by-step renovation plan. The goal is to maximize energy savings and improve your Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) score.

### Step-by-Step Renovation Plan:

#### 1. **Insulate the Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls at the front, rear, left side, and right side of the house.
   - **Why**: Cavity wall insulation is one of the most cost-effective ways to improve thermal performance, especially in older houses with uninsulated walls.
   - **Materials**: Use mineral wool or foam insulation, which are suitable for cavity walls.
   - **Note**: Ensure the insulation is installed properly to avoid moisture issues, which can be a concern in older buildings.

#### 2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space if it is not already insulated.
   - **Why**: Insulating the floor will reduce heat loss through the floor, especially if the crawl space is not heated.
   - **Materials**: Use rigid insulation boards or blown-in insulation, depending on the accessibility of the crawl space.

#### 3. **Upgrade the Windows**
   - **Action**: Replace the single-glazed windows with double-glazed or triple-glazed windows.
   - **Why**: Windows are a significant source of heat loss, and upgrading to double or triple glazing will greatly improve the thermal performance of the house.
   - **Note**: Since the wooden frames are in good condition, consider retrofitting them with new glazing to preserve the aesthetic while improving insulation.

#### 4. **Replace the Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and heating emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors) with a more efficient system.
   - **Options**:
     - **Heat Pump**: A ground-source or air-source heat pump could be a good option, depending on the available space and budget.
     - **Condensing Boiler**: If a heat pump is not feasible, a modern condensing boiler is more efficient than older models.
     - **Underfloor Heating**: If replacing the heating emission systems, consider underfloor heating as it is more efficient and provides more even heat distribution.
   - **Why**: Upgrading the heating system will reduce energy consumption and improve comfort.

#### 5. **Improve Ventilation**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the natural ventilation system to a more efficient mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR).
   - **Why**: MVHR systems recover heat from the exhaust air and use it to pre-heat the incoming fresh air, reducing heat loss and improving indoor air quality.

#### 6. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate electricity.
   - **Why**: PV panels can significantly reduce your electricity bills and contribute to a lower carbon footprint. Given that the site is suitable for PV installation, this is a worthwhile investment.

#### 7. **Seal Air Leaks**
   - **Action**: Seal any air leaks around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Why**: Air leakage can account for a significant portion of heat loss, and sealing these leaks will improve the overall energy efficiency of the house.

#### 8. **Monitor and Maintain**
   - **Action**: Regularly monitor the energy performance of the house and maintain the new systems to ensure they continue to operate efficiently.
   - **Why**: Ongoing maintenance and monitoring will help identify any issues early and ensure that the renovations continue to provide the expected benefits.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate the exterior walls and floor above the crawl space** (to address the largest sources of heat loss).
2. **Upgrade the windows** (to further reduce heat loss through the building envelope).
3. **Replace the heating system** (to ensure efficient heat distribution).
4. **Improve ventilation** (to ensure good indoor air quality while minimizing heat loss).
5. **Install photovoltaic panels** (to generate renewable energy).
6. **Seal air leaks** (to fine-tune the energy efficiency of the house).

This sequence ensures that each step builds on the previous one, maximizing the benefits of the renovations and improving the overall energy performance of the house.
