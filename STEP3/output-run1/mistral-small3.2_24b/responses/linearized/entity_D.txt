### **Step-by-Step Renovation Plan**

#### **1. Assess the Building**
- **Current State**:
  - Semi-detached building (173 m² net floor area) with a gable roof (moderately insulated) and a flat roof (well insulated).
  - Walls (front, rear, left) are well insulated and not historically protected.
  - Windows in living area and bedrooms have wooden frames with double glazing.
  - Heat pump is the generation system.
  - No heat grid, no potential for geothermal drilling or PV panels.
  - EPC energy score: 87.

- **Goals**:
  - Improve EPC label (current: E label).

#### **2. Prioritize High-Impact, Cost-Effective Renovations**
- **Insulate Gable Roof**:
  - **Why**: Moderately insulated gable roof contributes to higher energy consumption.
  - **Action**: Upgrade insulation to well-insulated (ordering: 28).
  - **Cost**: €60/m² (PER-M2).

- **Replace Glazing**:
  - **Why**: Double glazing is less efficient than triple glazing. Replacing glazing improves thermal performance.
  - **Action**: Upgrade to triple glazing (ordering: 43).
  - **Cost**: €200/m² (PER-M2).

- **Insulate Wall on Exterior**:
  - **Why**: Exterior insulation improves thermal comfort and reduces heat loss.
  - **Action**: Insulate walls (ordering: 40).
  - **Cost**: €200/m² (PER-M2).

- **Replace Ventilation System**:
  - **Why**: Current ventilation system (type D) may not be efficient. Upgrading to a more efficient system (e.g., type C) can improve air quality and reduce energy consumption.
  - **Action**: Replace ventilation system (ordering: 52).
  - **Cost**: €3,500 (fixed price).

#### **3. Secondary Renovations (Optional)**
- **Insulate Attic Floor**:
  - **Why**: Attic floor is well insulated, but walkable insulation may be preferred for usability.
  - **Action**: Insulate attic floor (ordering: 31).
  - **Cost**: €60/m² (PER-M2).

- **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**:
  - **Why**: Upgrading the heat pump can improve efficiency and reduce energy costs.
  - **Action**: Place air-water heat pump (ordering: 67).
  - **Cost**: €35/m² (PER-M2).

#### **4. Permits and Regulations**
- **Check Permits**: Ensure compliance with local regulations (e.g., Flanders) for insulation, glazing, and ventilation system replacements.
- **Asbestos Removal**: If asbestos is present, hire a licensed removal company.

#### **5. Execution Plan**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof** (immediate priority).
2. **Replace Glazing** (next priority).
3. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**.
4. **Replace Ventilation System**.
5. **Optional**: Insulate attic floor and upgrade heat pump.

#### **6. Expected Outcomes**
- **Improved EPC Label**: From E to D or higher.
- **Reduced Energy Consumption**: Lower energy bills and better thermal comfort.
- **Increased Property Value**: Higher market value due to improved energy efficiency.

#### **7. Tools & Materials**
- **Insulation Materials**: High-performance insulation for walls and roof.
- **Glazing**: Triple-glazed windows.
- **Ventilation System**: Efficient mechanical ventilation system.

#### **8. Budget Estimate**
- **Total Cost**: ~€15,000–€20,000 (depending on surface areas and additional upgrades).

This plan maximizes energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, with a clear sequence to optimize benefits.
