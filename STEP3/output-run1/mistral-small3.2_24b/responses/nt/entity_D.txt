### Step 1: Analyze Building Structure and Current State
The building is a semi-detached house with a gable roof (RoofMain) and a flat roof extension (RoofExtension). It has three main walls: Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, and Wall_Left. The building's owner wants to improve the EPC (Energy Performance Certificate) of the dwelling.

### Step 2: Identify Key Areas for Improvement
1. **Roof Insulation**: The RoofMain is moderately insulated, and the RoofExtension is well insulated. Insulating the RoofMain could significantly improve the EPC.
2. **Wall Insulation**: All walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Left) are well insulated. No immediate need for wall insulation.
3. **Window Glazing**: The windows in the living area and bedrooms have double glazing (WindowGlazingLivingArea and WindowGlazingBedrooms). Replacing these with triple glazing could further improve energy efficiency.
4. **Floor Insulation**: The FloorAboveBasement is well insulated. No immediate need for floor insulation.
5. **Ventilation System**: The building has a VentilationSystemD, which is a high-efficiency system. No need for ventilation system replacement.

### Step 3: Prioritize Renovations Based on EPC Improvement
1. **Replace Glazing**: Replace double glazing with triple glazing in the living area and bedrooms. This is a high-impact renovation that directly improves the building's energy efficiency.
2. **Insulate RoofMain**: Insulate the gable roof to improve its insulation grade from moderately insulated to well insulated.
3. **Replace RoofUnderlayment**: Replace the roof underlayment to ensure it is completely air- and watertight, especially important for the flat roof extension.
