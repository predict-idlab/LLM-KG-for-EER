### Renovation Plan for the Semi-Detached Building

#### **1. Initial Assessment**
The building is a semi-detached house constructed in 1951, with the following key characteristics:
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score**: 388 kWh/m²year (F label).
- **Net Floor Area**: 149 m².
- **Heating System**: Radiator (air heating) and ventilation system type A.
- **Insulation**:
  - **Walls**: Not insulated.
  - **Roofs**: Not insulated.
  - **Floor Above Basement**: Not insulated.
- **Windows**: Double glazing.
- **Owner's Motivation**: To improve the EPC.
- **Potential for PV**: Yes.
- **Heat Grid**: Not present.
- **Resident**: Present.

#### **2. Recommended Renovations**
Based on the building's characteristics and the ontology's suggestion list, the following renovations are recommended to improve the building's energy efficiency:

##### **A. Roof Insulation**
- **Insulate Gable Roof (RoofMain)**:
  - **Action**: Insulate the gable roof with a thickness of 240 mm.
  - **Expected Energy Savings**: ~30%.
  - **Estimated Cost**: ~€12,000 (€50/m² x 240 m²).
  - **Permit Needed**: No.

- **Insulate Flat Roof (RoofExtension)**:
  - **Action**: Insulate the flat roof with a thickness of 240 mm.
  - **Expected Energy Savings**: ~25%.
  - **Estimated Cost**: ~€12,000 (€50/m² x 240 m²).
  - **Permit Needed**: No.

##### **B. Wall Insulation**
- **Insulate Exterior Walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right)**:
  - **Action**: Insulate the exterior walls with a thickness of 140 mm.
  - **Expected Energy Savings**: ~20%.
  - **Estimated Cost**: ~€11,800 (€200/m² x 59 m²).
  - **Permit Needed**: Yes, if the total pack thickness exceeds 26 cm.

##### **C. Floor Insulation**
- **Insulate Floor Above Basement (FloorAboveBasement)**:
  - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the basement with a thickness of 100 mm.
  - **Expected Energy Savings**: ~10%.
  - **Estimated Cost**: ~€4,900 (€30/m² x 163 m²).
  - **Permit Needed**: No.

##### **D. Window and Glazing Upgrades**
- **Replace Glazing (WindowsLivingArea, WindowsBedrooms)**:
  - **Action**: Replace the double glazing with triple glazing.
  - **Expected Energy Savings**: ~15%.
  - **Estimated Cost**: ~€10,000 (€200/m² x 50 m²).
  - **Permit Needed**: No.

##### **E. Heating System Upgrade**
- **Replace Emission System (EmissionSystem)**:
  - **Action**: Replace the radiator with a heat pump boiler.
  - **Expected Energy Savings**: ~35%.
  - **Estimated Cost**: ~€4,000.
  - **Permit Needed**: No.

##### **F. Additional Recommendations**
- **Install PV Panels**:
  - **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof with a capacity of 6 kWp.
  - **Expected Energy Savings**: ~20%.
  - **Estimated Cost**: ~€6,000.
  - **Permit Needed**: No.

- **Improve Ventilation System**:
  - **Action**: Upgrade the ventilation system from type A to type C.
  - **Expected Energy Savings**: ~5%.
  - **Estimated Cost**: ~€1,700 (€17/m² x 100 m²).
  - **Permit Needed**: No.

#### **3. Implementation Timeline**
The renovations should be carried out in the following order to maximize efficiency and minimize disruption:
1. **Roof Insulation** (RoofMain and RoofExtension).
2. **Wall Insulation** (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right).
3. **Floor Insulation** (FloorAboveBasement).
4. **Window and Glazing Upgrades** (WindowsLivingArea, WindowsBedrooms).
5. **Heating System Upgrade** (EmissionSystem).
6. **Install PV Panels**.
7. **Improve Ventilation System**.

#### **4. Expected Outcomes**
- **Energy Savings**: ~50-60% reduction in energy consumption.
- **Cost Savings**: ~€3,000-€4,000 annually on energy bills.
- **EPC Improvement**: Expected to improve from F to B or C.

#### **5. Additional Considerations**
- **Financial Assistance**: Check for available grants or subsidies for energy-efficient renovations.
- **Professional Assistance**: Consult with an energy expert to ensure the renovations are carried out correctly and safely.
- **Resident Comfort**: Ensure that the resident is informed and prepared for the renovations to minimize disruption.

This plan provides a structured and actionable approach to renovating the building to improve its energy efficiency, comfort, and value.
