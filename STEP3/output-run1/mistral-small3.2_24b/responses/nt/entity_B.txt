### Renovation Plan

**Objective:**
Improve the energy efficiency of the building while considering cost-effectiveness and practicality.

**Current State:**
- **Building Type:** Attached
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score:** 262 kWh/m²/year (E label)
- **Insulation:**
  - **Wall Front:** Not insulated
  - **Wall Rear:** Not insulated
  - **Roof Main:** Moderately insulated
  - **Roof Extension:** Not insulated
  - **Floor Above Basement:** Not insulated
  - **Windows Living Area:** Double glazing, wooden frame
  - **Windows Bedrooms:** Double glazing, wooden frame
- **Ventilation System:** Type A
- **Emission System:** Radiator
- **Generation System:** Boiler
- **Potential for PV Panels:** Yes
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** No
- **Potential for Geothermal Drilling:** No
- **Potential for Horizontal Geothermal Captation:** No

**Renovation Priorities and Suggestions:**

1. **Insulate Roof Extension**
   - **Reason:** The roof extension is not insulated, which is a significant source of heat loss.
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate the flat roof with a warm roof setup.
   - **Ordering:** 25

2. **Insulate Wall Rear**
   - **Reason:** The rear wall is not insulated, contributing to heat loss.
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate the wall on the exterior to avoid reducing living space.
   - **Ordering:** 40

3. **Insulate Wall Front**
   - **Reason:** The front wall is not insulated, leading to heat loss.
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate the wall on the exterior to avoid reducing living space.
   - **Ordering:** 40

4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Reason:** The floor above the basement is not insulated, leading to heat loss.
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate the floor to improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Ordering:** 49

5. **Insulate Attic Floor**
   - **Reason:** The attic floor is not insulated, leading to heat loss.
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate the attic floor to improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Ordering:** 31

6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Reason:** The current ventilation system (Type A) may not be efficient.
   - **Suggestion:** Consider upgrading to a more efficient ventilation system.
   - **Ordering:** 52

7. **Place PV Panels on Roof**
   - **Reason:** The building has potential for PV panels.
   - **Suggestion:** Install PV panels on the roof to generate renewable energy.
   - **Ordering:** 85

8. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Reason:** The main roof is moderately insulated.
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate the gable roof to further improve insulation.
   - **Ordering:** 28

9. **Place Air-Water Heatpump**
   - **Reason:** The current heating system may be inefficient.
   - **Suggestion:** Install an air-water heatpump to improve heating efficiency.
   - **Ordering:** 67

**Cost-Effectiveness and Practicality:**
- Prioritize insulation of non-insulated elements to maximize energy savings.
- Consider the ordering of renovation suggestions to ensure that preliminary work is completed before subsequent steps.
- Evaluate the feasibility of installing PV panels and upgrading the heating system based on local regulations and budget constraints.

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Improved energy efficiency, leading to lower energy consumption and costs.
- Enhanced thermal comfort within the building.
- Potential reduction in the EPC label, indicating better energy performance.

This plan provides a structured approach to renovating the building for improved energy efficiency, considering both practical and cost-effective measures.
