### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### **1. Initial Assessment**
- **Building Type:** Detached building constructed in 1862.
- **Current Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score:** 165.0 (C label).
- **Potential for PV Panels:** Yes.
- **Heat Grid Availability:** No.
- **Current Heating System:** Electric convector, floor heating, radiator.
- **Current Ventilation System:** Natural air supply and exhaust (Ventilation System A).
- **Insulation Status:**
  - **Roof (Gable Roof):** Well insulated.
  - **Walls (Cavity Walls):** Not insulated.
  - **Windows:** Single glazing, wooden frames in good condition.

#### **2. Prioritized Renovation Actions**

**Phase 1: Insulation Upgrades**
1. **Insulate Cavity Walls**
   - **Action:** Insulate the cavity walls on the interior or exterior.
   - **Rationale:** Uninsulated walls are a significant source of heat loss.
   - **Cost:** Moderate (interior) to high (exterior).
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate (interior) to high (exterior).

2. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing single glazing with double or triple glazing.
   - **Rationale:** Single glazing has poor thermal performance.
   - **Cost:** High.
   - **Nuisance Level:** High.

3. **Insulate Attic Floor**
   - **Action:** Install insulation on the attic floor.
   - **Rationale:** Attic floors can be a source of heat loss, especially if the roof is not well insulated.
   - **Cost:** Low.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low.

**Phase 2: Heating and Ventilation Upgrades**
4. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade to a mechanical ventilation system (e.g., Ventilation System D).
   - **Rationale:** Improved air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Cost:** Moderate.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate.

5. **Install a Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Consider installing an air-water heat pump or a ground-source heat pump.
   - **Rationale:** More energy-efficient than electric convectors and radiators.
   - **Cost:** High.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate.

**Phase 3: Renewable Energy Integration**
6. **Install PV Panels on the Roof**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** Utilize the building's potential for solar energy.
   - **Cost:** Moderate to high.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low.

#### **3. Sequence of Actions**
1. **Insulate Cavity Walls** → **Replace Single Glazing** → **Insulate Attic Floor** → **Replace Ventilation System** → **Install Heat Pump** → **Install PV Panels**.

#### **4. Additional Considerations**
- **Permits:** Check local regulations for permits, especially for exterior wall insulation and roof modifications.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Prioritize actions with the highest cost-to-benefit ratio, such as insulating walls and replacing glazing.
- **Long-Term Benefits:** Focus on actions that improve the EPC score and reduce long-term energy costs.

This plan ensures a systematic approach to improving energy efficiency while balancing cost and practicality.
