### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### **1. Insulation Upgrades**
- **Insulate Gable Roof**
  - **Action**: Replace the current moderately insulated gable roof with a better-insulated option.
  - **Reason**: Improves thermal performance and reduces heat loss.
  - **Cost**: 60 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 28

- **Insulate Attic Floor**
  - **Action**: Add insulation to the attic floor.
  - **Reason**: Prevents heat loss through the attic.
  - **Cost**: 60 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 31

- **Insulate Wall Cavity**
  - **Action**: Inject insulation into the wall cavities.
  - **Reason**: Enhances wall insulation without significant structural changes.
  - **Cost**: 22 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 34

#### **2. Roof and Ventilation System**
- **Replace Gable Roof (if necessary)**
  - **Action**: Consider replacing the gable roof if it's in poor condition.
  - **Reason**: Ensures long-term durability and better insulation.
  - **Cost**: 260 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 13

- **Replace Ventilation System**
  - **Action**: Upgrade to a more efficient ventilation system.
  - **Reason**: Improves air quality and energy efficiency.
  - **Cost**: 3500 € (fixed) + 17 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 52

#### **3. Window Upgrades**
- **Replace Glazing**
  - **Action**: Upgrade to triple glazing if possible.
  - **Reason**: Reduces heat loss through windows.
  - **Cost**: 200 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 43

- **Replace Windows**
  - **Action**: Replace old windows with new, energy-efficient ones.
  - **Reason**: Improves insulation and reduces drafts.
  - **Cost**: 800 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 46

#### **4. Emission System**
- **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
  - **Action**: Install a low-temperature emission system.
  - **Reason**: More efficient heating with lower energy consumption.
  - **Cost**: 100 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 70

#### **5. Renewable Energy**
- **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
  - **Action**: Install photovoltaic panels on the roof.
  - **Reason**: Generates renewable energy, reducing reliance on the grid.
  - **Cost**: 275 €/m²
  - **Ordering**: 85

#### **6. Additional Recommendations**
- **Place Sunshade**
  - **Action**: Install sunshades to reduce solar heat gain.
  - **Reason**: Improves comfort and reduces cooling needs.
  - **Cost**: 750 € (fixed)
  - **Ordering**: 79

### **Optimal Sequence**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
2. **Insulate Attic Floor**
3. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
4. **Replace Glazing**
5. **Replace Windows**
6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
9. **Place Sunshade**

This plan ensures that the most impactful and cost-effective upgrades are prioritized, maximizing energy efficiency while minimizing costs.
