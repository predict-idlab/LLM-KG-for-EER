The building is a very modern attached house (constructed in 2023) with an already excellent energy performance certificate (EPC score: 50.0, which falls under the **A Label**). Most elements—including the walls, floor above basement, and flat roof—are already "Well Insulated." The ventilation system is already at the highest standard (System D).

### **Proposed Renovation Plan**

To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the renovations should follow the "envelope-first" principle: improve the building's shell to reduce heat loss before upgrading the heating system.

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
*Goal: Reduce heat loss to the lowest possible level to allow for a smaller, more efficient heating system.*

1.  **Insulate Gable Roof:** The main roof is currently only "Moderately Insulated." Upgrading this to "Well Insulated" is the most immediate gain for the building shell.
2.  **Replace Glazing:** The windows currently have double glazing and the wooden frames are in "Good State" (Value: 8). To be cost-effective, **replace only the glazing** (upgrading to triple glazing) rather than replacing the entire window unit. This leverages the existing high-quality frames.

#### **Phase 2: Emission System Alignment**
*Goal: Prepare the home for high-efficiency heat generation.*

3.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** Since the building already has floor heating, optimizing the emission system for low temperatures is a crucial prerequisite for the efficiency of a heat pump. This ensures the system doesn't have to work harder than necessary to maintain comfort.

#### **Phase 3: Sustainable Heat Generation**
*Goal: Replace fossil-fuel or inefficient electric heating with a high-performance renewable source.*

4.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** The building has a reason to change its emission system and currently uses electric convectors (which are energy-intensive). Since geothermal options are not viable for this site, an air-water heat pump is the most efficient choice. It integrates perfectly with the existing floor heating and the newly optimized low-temperature emission system.
