### **Proposed Renovation Path**

The sequence follows the "Envelope First, Systems Second" principle to ensure that the new heating system is sized correctly for the building's actual heat loss.

*   **Step 1: Insulate Gable Roof**

    *   **Point of Attention:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side to prevent condensation.
*   **Step 2: Upgrade Glazing**
    *   **Action:** `replace glazing` (Upgrade from Double to Triple Glazing)
    *   **Reasoning:** The building currently has double glazing. Moving to triple glazing will further reduce thermal transmission, supporting the owner's goal of maximizing the EPC label.

#### **Phase 2: System Upgrade (Efficient Generation & Emission)**
*   **Step 3: Install Air-Water Heat Pump**
    *   **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`
    *   **Reasoning:** The building has a "reason to change the emission system" and currently relies on electric convectors. Because the house already features **floor heating**, an air-water heat pump is the most compatible and efficient choice, as it can operate at lower flow temperatures.
*   **Step 4: Install Heat Pump Boiler**
    *   **Action:** `place heatpumpboiler`
    *   **Reasoning:** To replace traditional electric water heating with a high-efficiency system that extracts heat from the air for domestic hot water.

#### **Phase 3: Operational Performance & Comfort**
*   **Step 5: Install Sunshades**
    *   **Action:** `place sunshade`
    *   **Reasoning:** To prevent overheating during summer months, reducing the need for active cooling and improving long-term operational energy performance.
