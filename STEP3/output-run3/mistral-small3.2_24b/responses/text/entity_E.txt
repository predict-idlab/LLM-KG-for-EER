Based on the information provided, here is a step-by-step renovation plan to improve the energy efficiency of your home in Ghent, Belgium, while keeping costs in mind. The plan prioritizes cost-effective upgrades and focuses on practical improvements that will yield the most significant energy savings.

### Step 1: Assess the Existing Condition of the Floor Above the Crawl Space
- **Action**: Determine whether the floor above the crawl space is insulated. If not, this is a critical area to address as it can lead to significant heat loss.
- **Why First**: Insulating the floor above the crawl space is a relatively straightforward and cost-effective improvement that can yield immediate energy savings.

### Step 2: Upgrade Windows to Double or Triple Glazing
- **Action**: Replace single-glazed windows with double or triple-glazed windows. Since the existing wooden frames are in good condition, consider retrofitting them with new glazing to preserve the aesthetic while improving insulation.
- **Why Next**: Windows are a major source of heat loss. Upgrading them will significantly improve the overall energy performance of the house.
- **Note**: Ensure that the new glazing meets the latest energy efficiency standards.

### Step 3: Insulate the Cavity Walls
- **Action**: Insulate the exterior cavity walls. This can be done by injecting insulation material into the cavity, which is a common and effective method for cavity walls.
- **Why Next**: Wall insulation is crucial for reducing heat loss. Since the walls are cavity walls, injecting insulation is a practical and cost-effective solution.
- **Note**: Ensure that the insulation material is suitable for the specific type of cavity wall in your house.

### Step 4: Replace the Heating System
- **Action**: Replace the old heating system with a modern, energy-efficient system. Given the options available, a heat pump is a highly efficient choice. If a heat pump is not feasible, consider a modern boiler with a high energy efficiency rating.
- **Why Next**: Upgrading the heating system will not only improve comfort but also reduce energy consumption significantly.
- **Note**: Ensure that the new system is compatible with the existing radiators, underfloor heating, and air heating systems, or plan to upgrade these components as well.

### Step 5: Install a Mechanical Ventilation System with Heat Recovery (MVHR)
- **Action**: Replace the basic natural ventilation system with a mechanical ventilation system that includes heat recovery. This system will improve indoor air quality while recovering heat from the exhaust air.
- **Why Next**: MVHR systems are highly efficient and can reduce the need for additional heating by recovering heat that would otherwise be lost.
- **Note**: Ensure that the system is properly sized for your home to maximize efficiency.

### Step 6: Install Photovoltaic Panels
- **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate electricity. This will reduce your reliance on the grid and lower your energy bills.
- **Why Next**: PV panels are a cost-effective way to generate renewable energy, especially in a region with good solar potential.
- **Note**: Consider battery storage options to maximize the use of the generated electricity.

### Step 7: Optimize the Building Envelope
- **Action**: Conduct a thorough energy audit to identify any remaining areas of heat loss, such as around doors, vents, and other openings. Seal these areas with appropriate insulation materials.
- **Why Next**: Ensuring a well-sealed building envelope is crucial for maintaining the improvements made in the previous steps.
- **Note**: Use high-quality sealing materials to ensure durability and effectiveness.

### Step 8: Monitor and Maintain the System
- **Action**: Regularly monitor the performance of the new systems and maintain them according to the manufacturer's recommendations. This will ensure that they continue to operate at peak efficiency.
- **Why Next**: Ongoing maintenance is essential for sustaining the energy savings achieved through the renovations.

### Summary of the Renovation Plan:
1. Assess and insulate the floor above the crawl space.
2. Upgrade windows to double or triple glazing.
3. Insulate the exterior cavity walls.
4. Replace the heating system with a modern, energy-efficient system.
5. Install a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery.
6. Install photovoltaic panels.
7. Optimize the building envelope by sealing any remaining gaps.
8. Monitor and maintain the systems regularly.

By following this plan, you will significantly improve the energy performance of your home, reduce energy consumption, and lower your operational costs.
