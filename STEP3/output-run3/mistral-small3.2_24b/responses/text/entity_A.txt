Based on the information provided, here’s a step-by-step renovation plan to improve the energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness of your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium:

### **1. Insulation Upgrades**
**Priority:** High
**Action:**
- **Exterior Walls:** Install external wall insulation (EWI) to improve thermal performance. This is more cost-effective and less disruptive than internal insulation.
- **Pitched Roof:** Add insulation to the pitched roof, as it is currently uninsulated. Use high-performance insulation materials like mineral wool or rigid foam boards.
- **Flat-Roof Extension:** Although it has moderate insulation, consider topping up with additional insulation to match the performance of the pitched roof.
- **Floor Above Basement:** Insulate the floor to reduce heat loss to the basement. Use rigid insulation boards or underfloor insulation.

**Benefits:**
- Significant reduction in heat loss.
- Improved comfort and reduced heating costs.

### **2. Heating System Replacement**
**Priority:** High
**Action:**
- Replace the old boiler with a modern, high-efficiency condensing boiler.
- Consider switching to a heat pump (air-source or hybrid) if feasible, as they are more energy-efficient.
- Upgrade the heat emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, and air heating) to more efficient models.

**Benefits:**
- Lower energy consumption and reduced heating costs.
- Improved heating performance and comfort.

### **3. Ventilation System Upgrade**
**Priority:** Medium
**Action:**
- Replace the basic natural ventilation system with a **mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR)**. This system recovers heat from exhausted air and preheats incoming fresh air, reducing heat loss.

**Benefits:**
- Improved indoor air quality.
- Reduced heat loss through ventilation.

### **4. Window Upgrades**
**Priority:** Medium
**Action:**
- Although the windows have double glazing, consider upgrading to **triple-glazed windows** or adding secondary glazing to further reduce heat loss.
- Ensure proper sealing around window frames to prevent drafts.

**Benefits:**
- Reduced heat loss through windows.
- Improved thermal comfort.

### **5. Photovoltaic (PV) Panels Installation**
**Priority:** Medium
**Action:**
- Install photovoltaic panels on the roof to generate renewable energy. Given the potential for PV installation mentioned, this can significantly reduce electricity costs and carbon footprint.

**Benefits:**
- Lower electricity bills.
- Contribution to renewable energy goals.

### **6. Smart Thermostat and Controls**
**Priority:** Low
**Action:**
- Install a **smart thermostat** to optimize heating schedules and reduce energy waste.

**Benefits:**
- Better control over heating systems.
- Reduced energy consumption.

### **Optimal Sequence of Renovation Steps:**
1. **Insulation Upgrades** (start with exterior walls and roof, then floor).
2. **Heating System Replacement** (install new boiler or heat pump).
3. **Ventilation System Upgrade** (install MVHR).
4. **Window Upgrades** (if budget allows).
5. **Photovoltaic Panels Installation** (once other upgrades are complete).
6. **Smart Thermostat and Controls** (final touch).

### **Cost-Effectiveness Considerations:**
- Prioritize insulation and heating system upgrades first, as they offer the highest energy savings.
- Consider government grants or subsidies for energy-efficient renovations in Belgium, which can help offset costs.

By following this plan, you can significantly improve the energy performance of your home, reduce energy bills, and enhance comfort.
