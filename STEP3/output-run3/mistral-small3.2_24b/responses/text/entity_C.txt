   - **Action**: Replace the existing ventilation system (D) with a more efficient heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system.
   - **Rationale**: HRV systems can recover heat from the exhaust air and use it to preheat the incoming fresh air, reducing the heating load.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: While HRV systems have a higher upfront cost, they can lead to significant energy savings over time.

### 4. **Optimize Photovoltaic (PV) System**
   - **Action**: Assess the performance of the existing PV panels and consider expanding the system or adding a battery storage system.
   - **Rationale**: Expanding the PV system or adding battery storage can increase the self-consumption of the generated electricity, further reducing energy costs.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: The payback period for PV systems can be relatively short, especially with government incentives.

### 5. **Seal Air Leakages**
   - **Action**: Conduct an air tightness test to identify and seal any air leakages around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Rationale**: Air leakages can significantly reduce the overall energy efficiency of the house.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Sealing air leakages is a low-cost measure that can have a high impact on energy efficiency.

### 6. **Smart Thermostats and Controls**
   - **Action**: Install smart thermostats and controls to optimize the heating and ventilation systems.
   - **Rationale**: Smart controls can help manage energy usage more efficiently by adjusting settings based on occupancy and weather conditions.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: Smart thermostats are relatively inexpensive and can lead to significant energy savings.

2. **Improve Roof Insulation**: Follow up with roof insulation to reduce heat loss.
