The renovation plan is structured based on the specific needs of the building and its components. The goal is to improve the energy performance of the building (EPC value) from 165 to below 100, while also considering the potential for solar panel installation and the possibility to change the emission system.

### Step 1: Insulate the Walls
The building has four cavity walls that are not insulated. Insulating these walls will significantly reduce heat loss and improve the overall energy efficiency of the building.
- **Action**: Insulate wall on exterior for Wall_Front, Wall_Left, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right.
- **Order**: 40
- **Unit Price**: €200/m²
- **Permit Needed**: Only if thickness of total pack >26cm or the building line is overrun (Flanders)
- **Multi-phase**: No

### Step 2: Replace the Roof
The roof is a gable roof that is well insulated. However, replacing the roof will further improve the energy performance and can be an opportunity to install solar panels if desired.
- **Action**: Replace gable roof
- **Order**: 13
- **Unit Price**: €260/m²
- **Permit Needed**: Only if roof framework is renewed (Flanders)
- **Multi-phase**: No

### Step 3: Replace the Windows
The windows in the living area and bedrooms have single glazing, which is not energy efficient. Replacing these windows with double or triple glazing will further improve the energy performance.
- **Action**: Replace windows
- **Order**: 46
- **Unit Price**: €800/m²
- **Permit Needed**: Not necessary if the measurements are kept (Flanders)
- **Multi-phase**: No

### Step 4: Insulate the Attic Floor
The attic floor is not insulated, which can lead to significant heat loss. Insulating the attic floor will improve the overall energy efficiency of the building.
- **Action**: Insulate attic floor
- **Order**: 31
- **Unit Price**: €60/m²
- **Permit Needed**: Not necessary (Flanders)
- **Multi-phase**: No

### Step 5: Replace the Emission System
The emission system is an electric convector, which is not the most energy-efficient option. Replacing it with a more efficient system, such as a heat pump, will further improve the energy performance.
