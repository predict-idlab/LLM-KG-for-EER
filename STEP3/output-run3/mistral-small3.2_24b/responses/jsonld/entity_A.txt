### Renovation Plan for the Given Building

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached house
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **Net Floor Area:** 149 m²
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score:** 388 kWh/m²year (D label)
- **Current Insulation:**
  - Roof (Main Gable): Not Insulated
  - Roof (Extension Flat): Moderately Insulated
  - Walls: Not Insulated
  - Floor Above Basement: Not Insulated
- **Heating System:**
  - Emission System: Air Heating, Floor Heating, Radiators
  - Generation System: Boiler
  - Ventilation System: Natural Air Supply and Air Exhaust (Type A)
- **Windows:**
  - Wooden Frames with Double Glazing
- **Potential for Renewable Energy:**
  - Potential for PV Panels: Yes
  - Potential for Horizontal Geothermal Captation: No
  - Potential for Vertical Geothermal Drilling: No
- **Heat Grid Availability:** No
- **Historical Protection:** No

**Owner's Motivation:**
- Improve the EPC score

**Renovation Plan:**

1. **Roof Insulation:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the main gable roof (currently not insulated).
   - **Suggestion:** `insulate gable roof`
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Price:** ~€60/m²
   - **Permit Needed:** No
   - **Note:** Pay attention to condensation problems and install a vapour barrier on the warm side of the insulation.

2. **Wall Insulation:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the exterior walls (front, rear, right) which are currently not insulated.
   - **Suggestion:** `insulate wall on exterior`
   - **Nuisance Level:** High
   - **Price:** ~€200/m²
   - **Permit Needed:** Only if the thickness of the total package exceeds 26 cm or the building line is overrun.
   - **Note:** Consider the position of the building line and the council's guidelines for possible cladding options.

3. **Floor Insulation:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the floor above the basement which is currently not insulated.
   - **Suggestion:** `insulate floor above basement`
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Price:** ~€30/m²
   - **Permit Needed:** No

4. **Ventilation System Upgrade:**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing ventilation system (Type A) with a more efficient system (Type C or D).
   - **Suggestion:** `replace ventilation system`
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Price:** ~€3500 + €17/m²
   - **Permit Needed:** No (if above-ground parts are at least 2m away from the parcel boundary)
   - **Note:** For Type C, ensure grilles are installed in window frames.

5. **Heating System Upgrade:**
   - **Action:** Consider replacing the current emission system with a heat pump (air-water or geothermal) depending on feasibility and budget.
   - **Suggestion:** `place air-water heatpump` or `place vertical geothermal heatpump`
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Price:** ~€8000 + €35/m² (air-water) or €15000 + €50/m² (geothermal)
   - **Permit Needed:** Reporting or permit requirement depends on depth of drilling, soil characteristics, and groundwater (for geothermal).

6. **Renewable Energy Integration:**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof to leverage the potential for renewable energy.
   - **Suggestion:** `place PV-panels on roof`
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Price:** ~€275/m²
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary if the panels are in the same plane.
   - **Note:** Ensure the roof is properly oriented and captures enough sunlight. Check for asbestos before installation.

**Additional Recommendations:**
- **Sunshades:** Consider installing outdoor sunshades to improve energy efficiency during hot seasons. (`place sunshade`)
- **Air Conditioning:** If needed, consider installing air conditioning units. (`place airconditioning`)

**Optimization Sequence:**
1. Insulate the roof (gable and extension).
2. Insulate the exterior walls.
3. Insulate the floor above the basement.
4. Upgrade the ventilation system.
5. Upgrade the heating system (consider heat pumps).
6. Install PV panels on the roof.
7. Additional recommendations (sunshades, air conditioning).

**Expected Outcome:**
- Improved EPC score, potentially moving from a D label to a B or A label.
- Reduced energy consumption and improved thermal comfort.
- Potential for long-term energy cost savings and increased property value.

This plan prioritizes actions that provide the most significant impact on energy efficiency and EPC improvement while considering the building's specific characteristics and the owner's motivation.
