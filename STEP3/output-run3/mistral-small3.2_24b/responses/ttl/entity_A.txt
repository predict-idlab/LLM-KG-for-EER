### 3. **Detailed Renovation Steps**
   - **Step 1: Insulate Attic Floor**
     - **Action**: Install insulation on the attic floor.
     - **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss through the roof.
     - **Considerations**: Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation.

   - **Step 2: Insulate Wall Cavity**
     - **Action**: Inject insulation into the wall cavities.
     - **Benefit**: Improves thermal performance of external walls.
     - **Considerations**: Check for suitability (e.g., width of cavity, contamination).

   - **Step 3: Replace Glazing**
     - **Action**: Upgrade windows to double or triple glazing.
     - **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss through windows.
     - **Considerations**: Ensure window frames are in good condition and have the right thickness.

   - **Step 4: Insulate Wall on Interior**
     - **Action**: Install internal wall insulation.
     - **Benefit**: Improves thermal comfort and reduces energy consumption.
     - **Considerations**: Relocate sockets, pipes, and radiators against the outer walls.

   - **Step 5: Replace Ventilation System**
     - **Action**: Upgrade to a mechanical ventilation system (e.g., system D).
     - **Benefit**: Improves air quality and reduces condensation.
     - **Considerations**: Requires ductwork for both supply and return.

   - **Step 6: Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
     - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump for space heating and hot water.
     - **Benefit**: Reduces reliance on fossil fuels and lowers energy bills.
     - **Considerations**: May require a multi-phase electricity connection.

   - **Step 7: Place PV Panels on Roof**
     - **Action**: Install photovoltaic panels on the roof.
     - **Benefit**: Generates renewable energy, reducing electricity costs.
     - **Considerations**: Check roof orientation, potential shading, and asbestos removal if necessary.

### 4. **Expected Outcomes**
   - **Energy Efficiency**: Significant reduction in energy consumption, leading to a better EPC score.
   - **Cost Savings**: Lower energy bills due to improved insulation and renewable energy generation.
   - **Comfort**: Enhanced thermal comfort and indoor air quality.

### 5. **Additional Recommendations**
   - **Monitor Progress**: Regularly assess the impact of each renovation step on energy consumption and comfort.
   - **Future Considerations**: Plan for further upgrades like connecting to a heat grid if it becomes available.

This plan ensures a systematic approach to improving energy efficiency while maximizing cost-effectiveness.
