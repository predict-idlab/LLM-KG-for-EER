### Renovation Plan for the Attached Building

Given the building's current state and the goal to improve the Energy Performance Certificate (EPC), the following renovation plan is proposed. The plan focuses on energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness, prioritizing actions that provide the most significant impact on reducing energy consumption and improving thermal comfort.

#### 1. **Insulate the Gable Roof (RoofMain)**
   - **Action**: Insulate the gable roof.
   - **Justification**: The gable roof is currently moderately insulated. Improving its insulation will enhance thermal performance and reduce heat loss.
   - **Cost**: €60 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 28.

#### 2. **Insulate the Attic Floor**
   - **Action**: Insulate the attic floor.
   - **Justification**: Insulating the attic floor will prevent heat loss through the roof and improve overall energy efficiency.
   - **Cost**: €60 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 31.

#### 3. **Insulate the Walls on the Interior**
   - **Action**: Insulate the walls on the interior.
   - **Justification**: The walls are currently well insulated, but interior insulation can further improve thermal comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Cost**: €150 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 37.

#### 4. **Replace the Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Replace the existing ventilation system (VentilationSystemD) with a more efficient system.
   - **Justification**: Upgrading the ventilation system can improve air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Cost**: €3,500 (fixed) + €17 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 52.

#### 5. **Place an Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Justification**: This will provide a more efficient heating solution, reducing energy consumption and costs.
   - **Cost**: €8,000 (fixed) + €35 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 67.

#### 6. **Place an Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action**: Install a low-temperature emission system.
   - **Justification**: This will work efficiently with the air-water heat pump, further improving energy efficiency.
   - **Cost**: €100 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 70.

#### 7. **Place PV Panels on the Roof**
   - **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Justification**: Generating solar energy will reduce reliance on grid electricity and lower energy costs.
   - **Cost**: €275 per m².
   - **Ordering**: 85.

### Summary of Actions:
1. **Insulate the Gable Roof**
2. **Insulate the Attic Floor**
3. **Insulate the Walls on the Interior**
4. **Replace the Ventilation System**
5. **Place an Air-Water Heat Pump**
6. **Place an Emission System on Low Temperature**
7. **Place PV Panels on the Roof**

This plan prioritizes actions that will have the most significant impact on improving the building's energy efficiency and reducing energy consumption, ultimately leading to a better EPC rating. The sequence of actions is designed to maximize the benefits of each renovation step, ensuring a cost-effective and practical approach.
