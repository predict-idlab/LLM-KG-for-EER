### Step-by-Step Analysis and Renovation Plan

#### 1. **Building Overview**
- **Type**: Detached building
- **Construction Year**: 1862 (old building, likely poor insulation)
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)**: 165.0 (poor energy efficiency)
- **Potential for PV**: Yes
- **Heat Grid Availability**: No
- **Emission System**: Needs change (electric convector, floor heating, radiator)

#### 2. **Key Areas for Improvement**
- **Roof**: Well-insulated but potential for further optimization.
- **Walls**: Not insulated (cavity walls, no historical protection).
- **Windows**: Single glazing, wooden frames (likely poor insulation).
- **Ventilation System**: Type A (natural air supply and exhaust).
- **Emission System**: Needs upgrade.

#### 3. **Renovation Priorities**
Given the poor EPC and lack of insulation, the focus should be on improving thermal performance and reducing energy consumption.

#### 4. **Proposed Renovation Plan**

**Phase 1: Roof and Wall Insulation**
- **Insulate Gable Roof**: Improve insulation further if possible.
  - **Action**: Insulate gable roof (low nuisance, no permit needed).
- **Insulate Wall Cavity**: Fill the cavity walls with insulation.
  - **Action**: Insulate wall cavity (low nuisance, no permit needed).
- **Insulate Walls on Interior**: If cavity insulation is not feasible, consider internal insulation.
  - **Action**: Insulate walls on interior (moderate nuisance, no permit needed).

**Phase 2: Window Upgrades**
- **Replace Glazing**: Upgrade to double or triple glazing to improve thermal performance.
  - **Action**: Replace glazing (high nuisance, no permit needed).
- **Replace Windows**: If frames are old, replace entire windows with modern, energy-efficient ones.
  - **Action**: Replace windows (high nuisance, no permit needed).

**Phase 3: Floor Insulation**
- **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**: Improve thermal comfort and reduce heat loss.
  - **Action**: Insulate floor above basement (low nuisance, no permit needed).

**Phase 4: Ventilation and Heating System Upgrades**
- **Replace Ventilation System**: Upgrade to a more efficient system (e.g., Type D).
  - **Action**: Replace ventilation system (moderate nuisance, permit may be needed).
- **Place Heat Pump Boiler**: Upgrade the heating system to a more efficient heat pump boiler.
  - **Action**: Place heat pump boiler (moderate nuisance, no permit needed).

**Phase 5: Renewable Energy Integration**
- **Place PV-Panels on Roof**: Utilize the potential for PV to generate renewable energy.
  - **Action**: Place PV-panels on roof (low nuisance, permit may be needed).

#### 5. **Optimal Sequence**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
2. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
3. **Insulate Walls on Interior**
4. **Replace Glazing**
5. **Replace Windows**
6. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**
8. **Place Heat Pump Boiler**
9. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

#### 6. **Cost-Effectiveness and Energy Efficiency**
- **Insulation**: High impact on reducing heat loss and improving thermal comfort.
- **Window Upgrades**: Significant improvement in thermal performance.
- **Heat Pump Boiler**: More efficient heating system.
- **PV-Panels**: Reduce reliance on grid electricity.

This plan prioritizes actions that provide the highest energy savings and cost-effectiveness while considering the building's constraints and the owner's motivation to improve the EPC.
