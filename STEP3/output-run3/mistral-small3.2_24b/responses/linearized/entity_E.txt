### Renovation Plan for Detached Building

#### Step 1: Initial Assessment
- **Building Type**: Detached, constructed in 1862.
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)**: Energy score of 165 (poor insulation).
- **Insulation Grades**: Walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right) are Not Insulated; Roof is Well Insulated.
- **Windows**: Single-glazed with wooden frames in GoodState.
- **Heating System**: FloorHeating, Radiator, ElectricConvector, AirHeating.
- **Ventilation**: Type A (natural air supply and exhaust).
- **Potential for PV**: Yes.
- **Heat Grid**: Not available.
- **Geothermal Potential**: None.

#### Step 2: Prioritize Energy-Efficient Upgrades
1. **Insulate Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate walls on the interior (moderate nuisance, no permit needed in Flanders).
   - **Reason**: Walls are currently Not Insulated, contributing significantly to heat loss.
   - **Cost**: ~€150/m² (interior insulation).

2. **Improve Window Insulation**
   - **Action**: Replace glazing with double glazing (high nuisance, no permit needed).
   - **Reason**: Single glazing has poor thermal performance; double glazing will reduce heat loss.
   - **Cost**: ~€200/m² (glazing replacement).

3. **Insulate Attic Floor**
   - **Action**: Insulate attic floor (low nuisance, no permit needed).
   - **Reason**: Ensures consistent insulation across the building envelope.
   - **Cost**: ~€60/m² (attic floor insulation).

4. **Upgrade Heating System**
   - **Action**: Place air-water heat pump with cooling (moderate nuisance, depends on system).
   - **Reason**: Current heating system (ElectricConvector, AirHeating) is inefficient.
   - **Cost**: ~€35/m² (air-water heat pump).

5. **Solar Panel Installation**
   - **Action**: Place PV panels on roof (low nuisance, no permit needed if in same plane).
   - **Reason**: Building has potential for PV, and solar energy will reduce electricity costs.
   - **Cost**: ~€275/m² (PV panels on roof).

#### Step 3: Optimal Sequence
1. **Insulate Walls** (Interior) → **Replace Glazing** → **Insulate Attic Floor** → **Upgrade Heating System** → **Install PV Panels**.
   - **Rationale**: Insulation upgrades first to reduce heat loss, followed by heating system upgrade for efficiency, and finally solar panels for renewable energy.

#### Step 4: Additional Recommendations
- **Ventilation Upgrade**: Consider replacing the ventilation system to a more efficient type (e.g., Type C) if budget allows.
- **Roof Underlayment**: Ensure roof underlayment is air- and watertight to prevent condensation issues.
