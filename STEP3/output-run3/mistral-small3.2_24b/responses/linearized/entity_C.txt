To create a cost-effective and energy-efficient renovation plan for the given building, we need to analyze the current state of the building and identify the most impactful and practical upgrades. Here's a step-by-step plan:

### 1. **Assess Current Energy Performance**
- The building has an Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) with an energy score of 50.
- The building is an attached structure with a net floor area of 159.0 m².
- The construction year is 2023, indicating relatively modern construction standards.

### 2. **Identify Key Areas for Improvement**
- **Insulation**: The walls (Front and Rear) are well-insulated, but the roof (Main and Extension) has moderate insulation.
- **Windows**: The windows in the bedrooms and living area have double glazing, which is good but could be upgraded to triple glazing for better insulation.
- **Heating System**: The current heating system consists of floor heating and electric convectors, which are not the most energy-efficient options.
- **Ventilation**: The building has a ventilation system type D, which is mechanical for both supply and exhaust.
- **Roofing**: The roof extension is a flat roof, and the main roof is a gable roof, both in good condition.

### 3. **Prioritize Renovations Based on Impact and Cost-Effectiveness**
#### **High Priority (Immediate Impact and Cost-Effective)**
1. **Insulate Attic Floor**:
   - **Reason**: The attic floor is a significant area for heat loss. Insulating it will improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Action**: Install insulation with a vapour barrier on the warm side.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €60 per m².

2. **Upgrade Window Glazing to Triple Glazing**:
   - **Reason**: Triple glazing provides better insulation than double glazing, reducing heat loss through windows.
   - **Action**: Replace the existing double-glazed windows with triple-glazed ones.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €200 per window.

3. **Replace Ventilation System**:
   - **Reason**: A more efficient ventilation system can improve air quality and reduce energy loss.
   - **Action**: Upgrade to a more efficient ventilation system type (e.g., type C).
   - **Cost**: Approximately €3500.

#### **Medium Priority (Moderate Impact and Cost)**
4. **Insulate Gable Roof**:
   - **Reason**: The gable roof has moderate insulation. Improving it will further reduce heat loss.
   - **Action**: Add additional insulation layers, ensuring a vapour barrier is installed.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €60 per m².

5. **Insulate Flat Roof**:
   - **Reason**: The flat roof extension also has moderate insulation. Improving it will enhance overall building insulation.
   - **Action**: Add additional insulation layers, ensuring a vapour barrier is installed.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €50 per m².

#### **Low Priority (Long-Term Benefits)**
6. **Replace Heating System**:
   - **Reason**: The current heating system (floor heating and electric convectors) is not energy-efficient. Replacing it with a heat pump or another efficient system will significantly reduce energy consumption.
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump with cooling capability.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €9000.

7. **Place PV Panels on Roof**:
   - **Reason**: Installing solar panels can generate renewable energy, reducing dependency on the grid.
   - **Action**: Assess the roof's suitability for PV panels and install them.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €275 per panel.

### 4. **Optimal Sequence of Renovations**
1. **Insulate Attic Floor**: Quick and cost-effective way to improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
2. **Upgrade Window Glazing to Triple Glazing**: Enhances insulation and reduces heat loss through windows.
3. **Replace Ventilation System**: Improves air quality and reduces energy loss.
4. **Insulate Gable Roof and Flat Roof**: Further reduces heat loss and improves overall insulation.
5. **Replace Heating System**: Long-term benefit with significant energy savings.
6. **Place PV Panels on Roof**: Generates renewable energy, reducing dependency on the grid.

### 5. **Final Renovation Plan**
1. **Insulate Attic Floor**:
   - **Action**: Install insulation with a vapour barrier on the warm side.
   - **Cost**: €60 per m².

2. **Upgrade Window Glazing to Triple Glazing**:
   - **Action**: Replace existing double-glazed windows with triple-glazed ones.
   - **Cost**: €200 per window.

3. **Replace Ventilation System**:
   - **Action**: Upgrade to a more efficient ventilation system type (e.g., type C).
   - **Cost**: €3500.

4. **Insulate Gable Roof and Flat Roof**:
   - **Action**: Add additional insulation layers, ensuring a vapour barrier is installed.
   - **Cost**: €60 per m² for gable roof, €50 per m² for flat roof.

5. **Replace Heating System**:
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump with cooling capability.
   - **Cost**: €9000.

This plan prioritizes immediate and cost-effective upgrades while considering long-term benefits. It ensures a systematic approach to improving the building's energy efficiency and overall comfort.
