### **Executive Summary: Building Analysis**
*   **Current State:** The building is a detached house from 1862 with a surprisingly good EPC score (165), but it possesses several critical energy leaks: **single-glazed windows** and **uninsulated cavity walls**.
*   **Primary Goal:** Improve the EPC label and long-term operational performance.
*   **Constraints:** Geothermal options are not viable for this site, and there is no available heat grid.

---

### **Proposed Renovation Path**

#### **Phase 1: The Thermal Envelope (Immediate ROI)**
The goal here is to stop heat loss. Upgrading the envelope first ensures that any new heating system installed later is downsized and more efficient.

1.  **Insulate Wall Cavities:** 
    *   **Action:** `insulate wall cavity`
    *   **Justification:** All four walls (Front, Left, Right, Rear) are cavity walls and currently **Not Insulated**. Cavity wall insulation is one of the most cost-effective ways to reduce heat loss without altering the building's facade (crucial for a house from 1862).
2.  **Upgrade Window Glazing:** 
    *   **Action:** `replace glazing`
    *   **Justification:** Both the living area and bedrooms currently have **Single Glazing**, which is highly inefficient. Since the wooden frames are in a **Good State** (value 8), replacing only the glazing (rather than the entire window) is the most cost-effective way to improve thermal performance.
3.  **Insulate the Floor:** 
    *   **Action:** `insulate floor above basement`
    *   **Justification:** The building has a floor above a crawl space. Insulating this prevents cold air from entering the living areas from below.

#### **Phase 2: System Modernization (Operational Efficiency)**
With the heat loss minimized, we can now address the inefficient heating and ventilation systems.

4.  **Upgrade Heating Generation:** 
    *   **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`
    *   **Justification:** The current system uses inefficient electric convectors and an old boiler. Since geothermal is not possible and no heat grid exists, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the optimal sustainable alternative to replace the boiler.
5.  **Optimize Emission Systems:** 
    *   **Action:** `place emission system on low temperature`
    *   **Justification:** To maximize the efficiency of the new heat pump, the existing radiators and floor heating should be optimized for low-temperature operation.
6.  **Ventilation Upgrade:** 
    *   **Action:** `replace ventilation system`
    *   **Justification:** The building currently uses **Ventilation System A** (natural supply). Upgrading to a more modern system (e.g., System B or D) will improve air quality and reduce heat loss caused by uncontrolled ventilation.

#### **Phase 3: Renewable Energy (Sustainability)**
The final step is to transition from reducing consumption to generating clean energy.

7.  **Install Solar Energy:** 
    *   **Action:** `place PV-panels on roof`
    *   **Justification:** The building data explicitly confirms `hasPotentialForPV = true`. Installing PV panels will significantly lower the electricity costs associated with the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label.
