### Renovation Plan:

**Objective:** Improve the energy efficiency of the building to lower energy costs and increase thermal comfort, while adhering to the given constraints and leveraging the potential for renewable energy sources.

**Priority Actions:**
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls:**
   - **Why:** The building has cavity walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Left, Wall_Right, Wall_Rear) that are currently not insulated (InsulationGrade: NotInsulated). Insulating these walls will significantly reduce heat loss and improve thermal comfort.
   - **How:** Use external wall insulation with a U-value of ≤ 0.28 W/m²K. Ensure the insulation is compatible with the historical protection status of the walls (which is false for all walls).
   - **Cost:** Approximate cost can be calculated based on the surface area of the walls and the unit price of the insulation material.
   - **Nuisance Level:** High (due to scaffolding and potential disruption to the exterior appearance).

2. **Replace the Existing Ventilation System:**
   - **Why:** The current ventilation system (VentilationSystemA) is outdated and may not meet the required air quality standards. Upgrading to a VentilationSystemC will improve indoor air quality and comfort.
   - **How:** Install a new ventilation system with a heat recovery unit to minimize heat loss. Ensure the new system is compatible with the existing ductwork and complies with local regulations.
   - **Cost:** The cost will depend on the size of the building and the complexity of the installation.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate (due to potential disruption during installation).
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary if the above-ground parts are at least 2 m away from the parcel boundary (Flanders).

3. **Upgrade the Emission System:**

   - **Cost:** The cost will depend on the type and size of the new emission system.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate (due to potential disruption during installation).

4. **Insulate the Roof:**
