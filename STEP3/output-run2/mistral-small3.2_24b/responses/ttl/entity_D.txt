### Renovation Plan

Given the building's characteristics and the owner's motivation, the renovation plan should focus on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective. The plan will follow a logical sequence to ensure that each step builds on the previous one.

#### Step 1: Roof Improvements
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**:
   - **Action**: Improve insulation of the gable roof from moderately insulated to well insulated.
   - **Justification**: The gable roof is currently moderately insulated, and improving its insulation will contribute to a better EPC.
   - **Cost**: €60 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.

2. **Place Roof Underlayment**:
   - **Action**: Ensure the gable roof has a complete and airtight roof underlayment.
   - **Justification**: This step is crucial for preventing water damage and improving thermal performance.
   - **Cost**: €65 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.

#### Step 2: Window Improvements
3. **Replace Glazing**:
   - **Action**: Replace the current double glazing with triple glazing to further improve thermal performance.
   - **Justification**: Triple glazing provides better insulation than double glazing, which will help in improving the EPC.
   - **Cost**: €200 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: High.

#### Step 3: Ventilation System
4. **Replace Ventilation System**:
   - **Action**: Consider upgrading the ventilation system to a more efficient model if necessary.
   - **Justification**: The current system is type D, which is efficient, but newer models might offer better performance.
   - **Cost**: Fixed cost of €3,500 plus €17 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.

#### Step 4: Additional Measures
5. **Place Sunshade**:
   - **Action**: Install sunshades to reduce solar gain and improve thermal comfort.
   - **Justification**: Sunshades can help in reducing the need for cooling in summer and improving overall energy efficiency.
   - **Cost**: Fixed cost of €750.
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
