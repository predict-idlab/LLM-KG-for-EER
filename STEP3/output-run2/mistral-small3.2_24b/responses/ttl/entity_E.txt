### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

**Objective**: Improve the energy performance of the building while ensuring cost-effectiveness and practicality.

#### **1. Insulate Uninsulated Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Left, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right) which are currently uninsulated.
   - **Method**: Use cavity wall insulation, which is cost-effective and has a low nuisance level.
   - **Justification**: Walls account for a significant portion of heat loss. Insulating them will improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Priority**: High (Ordering: 34)

#### **2. Replace Single Glazing with Double Glazing**
   - **Action**: Replace the single glazing (WindowGlazingBedrooms, WindowGlazingLivingArea) with double glazing.
   - **Method**: Ensure the window frames (WoodenWindowFrame) are in good condition to fit new glazing.
   - **Justification**: Double glazing significantly improves insulation, reducing heat loss through windows.
   - **Priority**: High (Ordering: 43)

#### **3. Insulate the Attic Floor**
   - **Action**: Insulate the attic floor (if applicable) to prevent heat loss through the roof.
   - **Method**: Use appropriate insulation materials and ensure a vapor barrier is installed on the warm side.
   - **Justification**: Attic floors are common sources of heat loss. Insulation will enhance energy efficiency.
   - **Priority**: Medium (Ordering: 31)

#### **4. Replace the Roof Underlayment**
   - **Action**: Ensure the roof underlayment is air- and watertight to prevent heat loss and moisture issues.
   - **Method**: Install or replace the roof underlayment with high-quality materials.
   - **Justification**: A proper roof underlayment improves insulation and prevents structural damage.
   - **Priority**: Low (Ordering: 16)

#### **5. Upgrade the Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Replace the existing ventilation system (VentilationSystemA) with a more efficient system (e.g., VentilationSystemB or C).
   - **Method**: Install mechanical ventilation with heat recovery to improve air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Justification**: Efficient ventilation reduces energy loss and improves indoor air quality.
   - **Priority**: Medium (Ordering: 52)

#### **6. Install Solar Panels**
   - **Action**: Place PV-panels on the roof (RoofMain) if the orientation and sunlight exposure are favorable.
   - **Method**: Check the 'Zonnekaart' to ensure optimal placement and avoid shading.
   - **Justification**: Solar panels reduce energy costs and reliance on grid electricity.
   - **Priority**: High (Ordering: 85)

#### **7. Consider a Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: Evaluate the feasibility of installing an air-water heat pump or a geothermal heat pump.
   - **Method**: Assess the site's potential for geothermal drilling and the building's compatibility with heat pumps.
   - **Justification**: Heat pumps are energy-efficient and can significantly reduce heating costs.
   - **Priority**: Medium (Ordering: 67 or 64)

#### **8. Place Sunshades**
   - **Action**: Install sunshades on windows to reduce solar heat gain in summer.
   - **Method**: Use outdoor blinds for better efficiency.
   - **Justification**: Sunshades reduce the need for cooling and improve comfort.
   - **Priority**: Low (Ordering: 79)

### **Implementation Sequence**
1. **Insulate Walls** (High Priority)
2. **Replace Glazing** (High Priority)
3. **Insulate Attic Floor** (Medium Priority)
4. **Replace Roof Underlayment** (Low Priority)
5. **Upgrade Ventilation System** (Medium Priority)
6. **Install Solar Panels** (High Priority)
7. **Consider Heat Pump** (Medium Priority)
8. **Place Sunshades** (Low Priority)

### **Additional Considerations**
- **Permits**: Check local regulations for any necessary permits, especially for structural changes.
- **Budget**: Prioritize actions based on cost-effectiveness and immediate energy savings.
- **Professional Assistance**: Consult with architects or contractors for complex tasks like insulation and ventilation upgrades.

This plan ensures a systematic approach to improving energy efficiency while balancing cost and practicality.
