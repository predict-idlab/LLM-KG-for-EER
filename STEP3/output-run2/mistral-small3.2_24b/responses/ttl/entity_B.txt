### Renovation Plan for the Attached Building (1832)

#### **1. Initial Assessment**
- **Building Type**: Attached (shared walls with adjacent buildings).
- **Construction Year**: 1832 (old building, likely poor insulation).
- **Current EPC Score**: 262.0 (E label, poor energy performance).
- **Owner's Motivation**: Improve EPC.
- **Potential for PV**: Yes.
- **Heat Grid Availability**: No.
- **Ventilation System**: Natural (VentilationSystemA).

#### **2. Key Priorities**
- **Insulation**: Walls, roof, and floor are not insulated or poorly insulated.
- **Windows**: Replace or upgrade glazing.
- **Roof**: Replace or insulate (asbestos not mentioned, but check).
- **Heating System**: Consider upgrading to a more efficient system.

#### **3. Step-by-Step Renovation Plan**

**Phase 1: Roof and Exterior Work**
1. **Replace Gable Roof (RoofMain)**
   - **Action**: Replace the gable roof if it's in poor condition or has asbestos.
   - **Ordering**: 13 (high priority).
   - **Nuisance**: High (major renovation).
   - **Cost**: €260/m².

2. **Insulate Gable Roof (RoofMain)**
   - **Action**: Insulate the gable roof if it's in good condition.
   - **Ordering**: 28 (moderate priority).
   - **Nuisance**: Low.
   - **Cost**: €60/m².
   - **Note**: Ensure vapour barrier is installed.

3. **Replace Flat Roof (RoofExtension)**
   - **Action**: Replace the flat roof if it's in poor condition or has asbestos.
   - **Ordering**: 10 (high priority).
   - **Nuisance**: High.
   - **Cost**: €120/m².

4. **Insulate Flat Roof (RoofExtension)**
   - **Action**: Insulate the flat roof if it's in good condition.
   - **Ordering**: 25 (moderate priority).
   - **Nuisance**: High.
   - **Cost**: €50/m².
   - **Note**: Opt for an inverted roof structure.

**Phase 2: Wall and Floor Insulation**
5. **Insulate Wall on Interior (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear)**

   - **Ordering**: 37 (moderate priority).
   - **Nuisance**: Moderate.

6. **Insulate Floor Above Basement (FloorAboveBasement)**

   - **Ordering**: 49 (moderate priority).
   - **Nuisance**: Low.
   - **Cost**: €30/m².

**Phase 3: Windows and Ventilation**
7. **Replace Windows (WindowsBedrooms, WindowsLivingArea)**

   - **Ordering**: 46 (moderate priority).
   - **Nuisance**: High.
   - **Cost**: €800/m².

8. **Replace Glazing (WindowGlazingBedrooms, WindowGlazingLivingArea)**
   - **Action**: Upgrade to triple glazing if possible.
   - **Ordering**: 43 (moderate priority).
   - **Nuisance**: High.
   - **Cost**: €200/m².

9. **Replace Ventilation System (VentilationSystem)**
   - **Action**: Upgrade to a mechanical ventilation system (e.g., VentilationSystemD).
   - **Ordering**: 52 (moderate priority).
   - **Nuisance**: Moderate.
   - **Cost**: €3500 (fixed) + €17/m².

**Phase 4: Heating and Renewable Energy**
10. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump (Generation_System)**
    - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump for efficient heating.
    - **Ordering**: 67 (moderate priority).
    - **Nuisance**: Moderate.
    - **Cost**: €8000 (fixed) + €35/m².

11. **Place PV Panels on Roof (RoofMain)**
    - **Action**: Install PV panels on the gable roof.
    - **Ordering**: 85 (low priority).
    - **Nuisance**: Low.
    - **Cost**: €275/m².
    - **Note**: Check roof orientation and shading.

**Phase 5: Final Touches**
12. **Place Sunshade (WindowsBedrooms, WindowsLivingArea)**
    - **Action**: Install sunshades to reduce solar gain in summer.
    - **Ordering**: 79 (low priority).
    - **Nuisance**: Low.
    - **Cost**: €750 (fixed).

#### **4. Expected Outcomes**
- **Improved EPC**: Significant reduction in energy consumption and better thermal comfort.
- **Cost-Effectiveness**: Prioritize high-impact, low-cost actions first (e.g., insulation, roof replacement).
- **Long-Term Savings**: Lower energy bills and potential government incentives for renewable energy installations.

#### **5. Notes**
- **Asbestos Check**: Ensure asbestos removal if present in roofs or walls.
- **Permits**: Check local regulations for permits, especially for exterior work.
- **Phasing**: Work from top to bottom (roof → walls → floor) to minimize disruption.

This plan ensures a structured, cost-effective approach to improving the building's energy efficiency while maximizing long-term benefits.
