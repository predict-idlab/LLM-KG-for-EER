### Proposed Renovation Plan:

#### Phase 1: Roof and Wall Insulation
1. **Insulate Gable Roof (RoofMain)**
   - **Action**: Install insulation in the gable roof.
   - **Cost**: €60/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Rationale**: The gable roof is currently uninsulated, representing a significant opportunity for energy savings.

2. **Insulate Flat Roof (RoofExtension)**
   - **Action**: Upgrade insulation in the flat roof (opt for an inverted roof structure).
   - **Cost**: €50/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: High.
   - **Rationale**: Improve insulation where it is already moderately insulated.

3. **Insulate Walls on Exterior (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right)**
   - **Action**: Apply exterior wall insulation.
   - **Cost**: €200/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: High.
   - **Rationale**: Walls are currently uninsulated, and exterior insulation avoids reducing interior space.

#### Phase 2: Floor and Ventilation Upgrades
4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the basement.
   - **Cost**: €30/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Rationale**: The floor is uninsulated, and insulation will reduce heat loss to the basement.

5. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Upgrade from Type A to Type B or C (mechanical supply and natural exhaust).
   - **Cost**: €3500 (fixed) + €17/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.
   - **Rationale**: Improve air quality and energy efficiency.

#### Phase 3: Renewable Energy Integration
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Cost**: €275/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Rationale**: The building has potential for PV, and the roof is a suitable location.

#### Phase 4: Optional Upgrades (Based on Budget and Motivation)
7. **Replace Glazing (If Budget Allows)**
   - **Action**: Upgrade from double to triple glazing.
   - **Cost**: €200/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: High.
   - **Rationale**: Further improve thermal performance of windows.

8. **Place Sunshade**
   - **Action**: Install outdoor blinds or sunshades.
   - **Cost**: €750 (fixed).
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Rationale**: Reduce solar heat gain in summer.

### Summary of Key Recommendations:
1. **Immediate Actions**:
   - Insulate the gable roof (highest priority).
   - Insulate the exterior walls (significant impact).
   - Upgrade the ventilation system.

2. **Medium-Term Actions**:
   - Install PV panels on the roof.

3. **Optional Actions**:
   - Replace glazing with triple glazing.

This plan prioritizes high-impact, cost-effective upgrades to significantly improve the building's energy efficiency and EPC rating.
