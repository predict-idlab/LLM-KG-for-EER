### Step-by-Step Renovation Plan

#### **1. Initial Assessment**
- **Building Type**: Attached building (shares walls with neighboring properties).
- **Construction Year**: 1832 (historic, potential for preservation constraints).
- **Current Energy Performance**: EPC label E (energy score 262 kWh/m²/year).
- **Owner's Motivation**: Improve EPC (energy efficiency).
- **Current Insulation Grades**:
  - RoofMain: Moderately Insulated
  - RoofExtension: Not Insulated
  - Wall_Rear: Not Insulated
  - Wall_Front: Not Insulated
  - FloorAboveBasement: Not Insulated
- **Ventilation System**: Type A (natural air supply and exhaust).
- **Windows**: Double-glazed, wooden frames in good condition.
- **Potential for PV**: Yes (but site constraints: no vertical geothermal drilling, no horizontal geothermal captation).

#### **2. Prioritize Renovations**
- **Highest Impact, Lowest Nuisance**:
  - **Insulate Attic Floor** (Ordering: 31, Nuisance: Low, Price: €60/m²).
  - **Insulate Wall Cavity** (Ordering: 34, Nuisance: Low, Price: €22/m²).
  - **Insulate Floor Above Basement** (Ordering: 49, Nuisance: Low, Price: €30/m²).
- **Moderate Impact, Higher Nuisance**:
  - **Replace Ventilation System** (to Type B or D for better efficiency, Ordering: 52, Nuisance: Moderate).
  - **Insulate Wall on Interior** (Ordering: 37, Nuisance: Moderate, Price: €150/m²).
- **Low Impact, High Nuisance**:
  - **Replace Flat Roof** or **Replace Gable Roof** (high nuisance due to structural work).

#### **3. Step-by-Step Plan**
**Phase 1: Immediate Actions (Low Nuisance, High Impact)**
1. **Insulate Attic Floor** (€60/m²):
   - Focus on vapour barrier to prevent condensation.
   - Walkable insulation preferred if the attic is accessible.
2. **Insulate Wall Cavity** (€22/m²):
   - Check cavity width and contamination before injecting insulation.
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement** (€30/m²):
   - Use appropriate insulation material for floors (e.g., rigid foam boards).

**Phase 2: System Upgrades (Moderate Nuisance)**
4. **Replace Ventilation System** (Type B or D):
   - Ensure ductwork is compatible with new system.
   - Install grilles in window frames if switching to Type C.
5. **Insulate Wall on Interior** (€150/m²):
   - Relocate sockets, pipes, and radiators against outer walls.
   - Use breathable insulation to avoid moisture issues.

**Phase 3: Structural Upgrades (High Nuisance)**
6. **Replace Roofs** (if budget allows):
   - Opt for warm roof setup with proper drainage (2% gradient).
   - Consider inverted roof structure for flat roofs.
7. **Replace Windows** (if glazing is damaged or frames are weak):
   - Ensure frames meet thickness requirements for double glazing.

**Phase 4: Optional Add-Ons**
8. **Connect to Heat Grid** (if available):
   - Check compatibility with existing emission system.
9. **Install PV Panels** (if roof orientation and shading are favorable):
   - Use plug-and-play panels for simplicity.

#### **4. Cost and Timeline Estimation**
- **Total Estimated Cost**:
  - Insulation: €200–€400/m² (depending on materials and complexity).
  - Ventilation System: €3,500 (fixed) + potential additional costs.
  - Roof Replacement: €120–€260/m² (depending on type).
- **Timeline**:
  - Phase 1: 2–4 weeks (parallel tasks).
  - Phase 2: 4–6 weeks (sequential tasks).
  - Phase 3: 6–12 weeks (structural work).

#### **5. Key Considerations**
- **Permits**: Check local regulations for historic buildings (e.g., Flanders).
- **Asbestos**: Test for asbestos in roofs and walls before removal.
- **Energy Expert**: Consult for EPC certification after renovations.
