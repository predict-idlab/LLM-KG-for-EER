### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### **1. Insulate Wall Exterior**
   - **Action**: Insulate the exterior walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right) to improve thermal performance.
   - **Reason**: All walls are currently not insulated (NotInsulated), and exterior insulation is more effective in reducing heat loss.
   - **Cost**: High nuisance level but long-term savings.
   - **Ordering**: 40

#### **2. Insulate Attic Floor**
   - **Action**: Insulate the attic floor to reduce heat loss through the roof.
   - **Reason**: RoofMain has an insulation grade of NotInsulated, and insulating the attic floor is a cost-effective measure.
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Ordering**: 31

#### **3. Insulate Wall Cavity**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls if applicable.
   - **Reason**: Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, and Wall_Right are FullBrickWalls, which may have cavities suitable for insulation.
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Ordering**: 34

#### **4. Replace Windows**
   - **Action**: Replace the windows (WindowsLivingArea, WindowsBedrooms) with double-glazed windows if not already in good condition.
   - **Reason**: Current windows have double glazing, but replacing them ensures better thermal performance and airtightness.
   - **Nuisance Level**: High.
   - **Ordering**: 46

#### **5. Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Action**: Insulate the flat roof (RoofExtension) which is currently moderately insulated.
   - **Reason**: Improving insulation will enhance energy efficiency.
   - **Nuisance Level**: High.
   - **Ordering**: 25

#### **6. Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Replace the ventilation system (VentilationSystem) to a more efficient model.
   - **Reason**: Current system is type A, which may not be as energy-efficient as newer models.
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.
   - **Ordering**: 52

#### **7. Connect to Heat Grid**
   - **Action**: Connect to the heat grid if available.
   - **Reason**: Current emission system (Radiator, AirHeating, FloorHeating) could benefit from a more centralized and efficient heat source.
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Ordering**: 55

#### **8. Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump for space heating and hot water.
   - **Reason**: Current heating systems (Radiator, AirHeating, FloorHeating) could be supplemented or replaced with a more energy-efficient heat pump.
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.
   - **Ordering**: 67

#### **9. Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install PV-panels on the roof (RoofMain, RoofExtension).
   - **Reason**: The building has potential for PV (true), and roof installation is efficient.
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Ordering**: 85

### **Implementation Sequence**
1. **Insulate Wall Exterior** (High impact, long-term savings)
2. **Insulate Attic Floor** (Low nuisance, immediate benefits)
3. **Insulate Wall Cavity** (Low nuisance, cost-effective)
4. **Replace Windows** (High impact, improves comfort and efficiency)
5. **Insulate Flat Roof** (Improves thermal performance)
6. **Replace Ventilation System** (Enhances air quality and efficiency)
7. **Connect to Heat Grid** (If feasible, improves heating efficiency)
8. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** (Modernizes heating system)
9. **Place PV-Panels on Roof** (Generates renewable energy)

This plan prioritizes actions that provide the most energy savings and long-term benefits while considering cost-effectiveness and nuisance levels. The sequence ensures that each step builds on the previous improvements for maximum efficiency.
