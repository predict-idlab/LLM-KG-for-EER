### Analysis of the Building and Requirements

1. **Building Characteristics**:
   - **Type**: Detached building
   - **Construction Year**: 1862
   - **Net Floor Area**: 136.0 m²
   - **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score**: 165 kWh/m²year (indicates poor energy efficiency)
   - **Potential for PV**: Yes
   - **Reason to Change Emission System**: Yes
   - **Presence of Heat Grid**: No
   - **Historical Protection**: No
   - **Insulation Grades**:
     - Walls: Not Insulated
     - Roof: Well Insulated

2. **Current Systems**:
   - **Emission System**: Electric Convector, Floor Heating, Radiator, Air Heating
   - **Ventilation System**: Natural Air Supply and Air Exhaust (Ventilation System A)
   - **Generation System**: Boiler

3. **Current State of Building Elements**:
   - **Windows**: Single Glazing (poor thermal and acoustic performance)
   - **Walls**: Cavity Walls (not insulated)
   - **Roof**: Gable Roof (well insulated)

4. **Owner's Motivation**:
   - **Primary Goal**: To improve the EPC of the dwelling.

### Recommended Renovation Steps

1. **Improve Insulation**:
   - **Insulate Walls**: Since the walls are cavity walls and not insulated, insulating the cavity is a practical and effective measure. This will significantly reduce heat loss through the walls.
     - **Action**: Insulate Wall Cavity (Ordering: 34)
   - **Insulate Attic Floor**: Although the roof is well insulated, the attic floor might not be. Insulating the attic floor can prevent heat loss through the ceiling.
     - **Action**: Insulate Attic Floor (Ordering: 31)

2. **Upgrade Windows**:
   - **Replace Glazing**: The current windows have single glazing, which is inefficient. Replacing the glazing with double or triple glazing will improve thermal performance.
     - **Action**: Replace Glazing (Ordering: 43)
   - **Replace Windows**: If the window frames are in good condition, replacing only the glazing might suffice. However, if the frames are old or damaged, replacing the entire windows might be necessary.
     - **Action**: Replace Windows (Ordering: 46)

3. **Upgrade Emission System**:
   - **Place Low-Temperature Emission System**: Upgrading to a low-temperature emission system can improve energy efficiency and comfort.
     - **Action**: Place Emission System on Low Temperature (Ordering: 70)

4. **Consider Renewable Energy**:
   - **Place PV Panels**: Given the potential for PV and the absence of a heat grid, installing PV panels can reduce dependency on non-renewable energy sources.
     - **Action**: Place PV Panels on Roof (Ordering: 85)

5. **Additional Considerations**:
   - **Ventilation System**: The current ventilation system is natural. Depending on the specific needs and comfort requirements, upgrading to a mechanical ventilation system (e.g., Ventilation System B or C) might be beneficial.
     - **Action**: Replace Ventilation System (Ordering: 52)
   - **Sunshade**: Installing sunshades can help regulate indoor temperature by reducing solar gain in summer.
     - **Action**: Place Sunshade (Ordering: 79)

### Final Renovation Plan

1. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
   - **Action**: Inject insulation material into the wall cavities.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss through walls, improving overall energy efficiency.

2. **Insulate Attic Floor**
   - **Action**: Install insulation material on the attic floor.
   - **Benefit**: Prevents heat loss through the ceiling, enhancing thermal comfort.

3. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action**: Upgrade single glazing to double or triple glazing.
   - **Benefit**: Improves thermal performance of windows, reducing heat loss.

4. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action**: Install a low-temperature emission system.
   - **Benefit**: Enhances energy efficiency and comfort by using lower temperature settings.

5. **Place PV Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic panels on the roof.
   - **Benefit**: Generates renewable energy, reducing dependency on non-renewable sources.

6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Upgrade to a mechanical ventilation system (e.g., Ventilation System B or C).
   - **Benefit**: Improves indoor air quality and comfort.

7. **Place Sunshade**
   - **Action**: Install sunshades on windows.
   - **Benefit**: Regulates indoor temperature by reducing solar gain in summer.

This plan addresses the primary goal of improving the EPC of the dwelling while considering cost-effectiveness and practical implementation. The actions are prioritized based on their potential impact on energy efficiency and comfort.
