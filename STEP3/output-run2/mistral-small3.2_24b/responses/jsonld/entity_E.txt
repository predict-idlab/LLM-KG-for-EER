### Renovation Plan for the Detached House in Belgium

#### **1. Replace Gable Roof**
- **Reasoning**: The current gable roof is well insulated, but replacing it can improve its energy efficiency and durability. This action is important to ensure long-term energy savings and structural integrity.
- **Action**: Remove the existing roof and replace it with a new one. Ensure proper insulation and roof underlayment installation.
- **Ordering**: 13

#### **2. Insulate Wall Cavity**
- **Reasoning**: The walls (front, right, rear, left) are currently not insulated. Insulating the cavity walls will significantly improve the thermal performance of the building, reducing heat loss and energy consumption.
- **Action**: Inject insulation material into the wall cavities. Ensure the cavity is suitable for insulation by checking for contamination and ensuring proper vapor permeability.
- **Ordering**: 34

#### **3. Replace Windows**
- **Reasoning**: The current windows have single glazing, which is inefficient. Replacing them with double or triple glazing will improve thermal insulation and reduce energy loss.
- **Action**: Remove the existing windows and install new ones with double or triple glazing. Ensure the window frames are in good condition and properly sealed.
- **Ordering**: 46

#### **4. Replace Ventilation System**
- **Reasoning**: The current ventilation system is of type A, which is less efficient compared to modern systems like D. Upgrading the ventilation system will improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
- **Action**: Install a new ventilation system (type D). Ensure proper ductwork and grilles are installed in the window frames.
- **Ordering**: 52

#### **5. Place Air-Water Heatpump**
- **Reasoning**: The current heating system (electric convector) is inefficient. An air-water heatpump will provide more efficient heating and reduce energy consumption.
- **Action**: Install an air-water heatpump. Ensure proper placement and connection to the existing heating system.
- **Ordering**: 67

#### **6. Place PV Panels on Roof**
- **Reasoning**: The building has potential for PV panels, which can generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.
- **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof. Ensure proper orientation and shading analysis to maximize energy production.
- **Ordering**: 85

#### **7. Place Sunshade**
- **Reasoning**: Installing outdoor blinds or sunshades can reduce solar heat gain in the summer, improving comfort and reducing cooling needs.
- **Action**: Install sunshades on the windows. Ensure they are properly sized and positioned for effective shading.
- **Ordering**: 79
