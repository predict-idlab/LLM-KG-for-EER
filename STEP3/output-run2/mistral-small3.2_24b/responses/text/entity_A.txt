Based on the information provided, here is a step-by-step renovation plan tailored to your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan prioritizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness while considering the optimal sequence of upgrades:

### 1. **Insulate the Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Install external wall insulation (EWI) on the solid brick walls.
   - **Benefits**: This is one of the most impactful upgrades for reducing heat loss and improving the energy performance certificate (EPC) score.
   - **Considerations**: External insulation is less disruptive than internal insulation and can improve the appearance of the house. Ensure the chosen insulation material is suitable for the local climate.

### 2. **Insulate the Pitched Roof**
   - **Action**: Add insulation to the pitched roof, which is currently uninsulated.
   - **Benefits**: Roof insulation is highly effective in reducing heat loss, especially in a pitched roof where heat rises.
   - **Considerations**: Use high-performance insulation materials like mineral wool or rigid foam boards. Ensure proper ventilation to prevent condensation.

### 3. **Upgrade the Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the existing boiler and outdated heat emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, and air heating) with a modern, energy-efficient system.
   - **Options**:
     - **Heat Pump**: Consider an air-source heat pump, which is suitable for your climate and can significantly reduce energy consumption.
     - **Condensing Boiler**: If a heat pump is not feasible, install a high-efficiency condensing boiler.
   - **Benefits**: A modern heating system will improve comfort and reduce energy bills.

### 4. **Insulate the Flat-Roof Extension**
   - **Action**: Enhance the existing moderate insulation in the flat-roof extension to match the performance of the pitched roof insulation.
   - **Benefits**: Ensures consistent insulation levels across the entire roof area.

### 5. **Insulate the Floor Above the Basement**
   - **Action**: Install insulation on the floor above the basement to reduce heat loss through the ground.
   - **Benefits**: This is a cost-effective way to improve the overall thermal performance of the house.

### 6. **Improve Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the basic natural ventilation system to a more efficient mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR).
   - **Benefits**: MVHR systems improve indoor air quality and reduce heat loss by recovering heat from exhausted air.

### 7. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - **Action**: Assess the suitability of your roof for PV panel installation and consider installing them to generate renewable energy.
   - **Benefits**: Reduces reliance on grid electricity and lowers energy costs. In Belgium, there may be incentives or feed-in tariffs for solar energy.

### 8. **Window Upgrades (Optional)**
   - **Action**: Although your windows have double glazing, consider upgrading to triple glazing or high-performance double glazing if your budget allows.
   - **Benefits**: Further reduces heat loss through windows.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate the exterior walls** (most impactful upgrade).
2. **Insulate the pitched roof** (next most impactful upgrade).
3. **Upgrade the heating system** (improves comfort and efficiency).
4. **Insulate the flat-roof extension and floor above the basement** (ensures consistent insulation).
5. **Improve the ventilation system** (enhances indoor air quality and efficiency).
6. **Install PV panels** (generates renewable energy).

### Additional Considerations:
- **Budgeting**: Prioritize upgrades based on your budget, starting with the most cost-effective and impactful measures.
- **Professional Consultation**: Consult with energy experts or contractors to ensure the chosen materials and systems are suitable for your specific needs and local building regulations.

This plan should significantly improve your home's energy performance, comfort, and long-term operational efficiency.
