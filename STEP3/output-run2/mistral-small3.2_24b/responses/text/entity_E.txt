To improve the energy performance of your 1862 detached house in Ghent, Belgium, we'll focus on practical, cost-effective upgrades that maximize energy efficiency. Here's a step-by-step renovation plan:

### 1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls at the front, rear, left, and right sides of the house.
   - **Why First**: Exterior walls account for a significant portion of heat loss. Insulating them will have a substantial impact on reducing energy consumption.
   - **Options**:
     - **Cavity Wall Insulation**: Fill the existing cavity with insulating material. This is cost-effective and minimally invasive.
     - **Internal or External Insulation**: If cavity insulation isn't feasible, consider internal or external wall insulation. External insulation may be more disruptive but can also improve the aesthetic and weatherproofing of the house.

### 2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space to prevent heat loss through the floor.
   - **Options**:
     - **Underfloor Insulation**: Install rigid insulation boards beneath the floorboards. This can be done without major disruption.
     - **Crawl Space Insulation**: If accessible, insulate the walls and ceiling of the crawl space to reduce heat loss.

### 3. **Upgrade Windows**
   - **Action**: Replace single-glazed windows with double or triple-glazed windows.
   - **Why**: Windows are a major source of heat loss. Upgrading to double or triple glazing will significantly improve insulation.
   - **Options**:
     - **Double Glazing**: A cost-effective upgrade that provides a good balance between cost and insulation.
     - **Triple Glazing**: More expensive but offers even better insulation, which may be worthwhile given the age and construction of the house.

### 4. **Replace Heating Systems**
   - **Action**: Replace the old heating systems (radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors) with more efficient alternatives.
   - **Options**:
     - **Heat Pump**: Consider an air-source heat pump, which is efficient and can work well with underfloor heating or low-temperature radiators.
     - **Modern Boiler**: If a heat pump isn't feasible, install a high-efficiency condensing boiler.
     - **Underfloor Heating**: If replacing underfloor heating, ensure it's designed for low-temperature operation to work efficiently with a heat pump or modern boiler.

### 5. **Improve Ventilation**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the natural ventilation system to a more efficient mechanical ventilation system.
   - **Options**:
     - **Heat Recovery Ventilation (HRV)**: Install an HRV system to recover heat from exhausted air, improving indoor air quality and reducing heat loss.
     - **Balanced Ventilation**: Ensure the system is properly balanced to prevent drafts and moisture issues.

### 6. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels to generate renewable energy.
   - **Why**: Given the potential for PV installation, this can significantly reduce your electricity costs and carbon footprint.
   - **Options**:
     - **Roof-Mounted PV Panels**: Install panels on the pitched roof, which is already well-insulated.
     - **Battery Storage**: Consider adding a battery storage system to store excess energy for use during peak times or outages.

### 7. **Additional Considerations**
   - **Smart Thermostat**: Install a smart thermostat to optimize heating schedules and reduce energy waste.
   - **Draft Proofing**: Seal any drafts around windows, doors, and other openings to further reduce heat loss.

### Optimal Sequence
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**: This will have the most significant impact on reducing heat loss.
2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**: Address another major source of heat loss.
3. **Upgrade Windows**: Improve insulation and reduce drafts.
4. **Replace Heating Systems**: Ensure efficient heat distribution.
5. **Improve Ventilation**: Enhance indoor air quality and reduce heat loss.
6. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**: Generate renewable energy to further reduce costs.

### Cost-Effectiveness
- Prioritize upgrades with the highest return on investment, such as insulation and heating system replacement.
- Consider financing options or government incentives for energy-efficient renovations, which can help offset costs.

By following this plan, you can significantly improve the energy performance certificate of your home while keeping costs manageable.
