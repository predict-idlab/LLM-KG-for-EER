### **1. Insulation Upgrades (Prioritize High-Impact Areas)**
- **Exterior Walls (Front & Rear):** Install external wall insulation (EWI) on the solid brick walls. *Note:* The main pitched roof already has moderate insulation, so no immediate action is needed unless further upgrades are planned later.

### **2. Heating System Replacement**
- Replace the old boiler and radiator system with a **modern condensing boiler** combined with **low-temperature radiators or underfloor heating**. This will improve efficiency and reduce fuel consumption.

- Replace the basic natural ventilation system with a **mechanical heat recovery ventilation (MHRV) system**. This will improve air quality while reducing heat loss by recovering warmth from exhaust air.
- Ensure the system is properly sized and installed to maximize efficiency.

- The existing double-glazed windows are in good condition, so no immediate replacement is necessary. However, if drafts or inefficiencies are noticed, consider **secondary glazing** as a cost-effective alternative to full window replacement.

### **5. Photovoltaic (PV) Panel Installation**
- Assess the roof’s suitability for PV panels and consider installing a system to generate renewable electricity. This will reduce energy costs and improve the home’s sustainability.

### **6. Smart Thermostat & Controls**
- Install a **programmable or smart thermostat** to optimize heating schedules and reduce energy waste.

1. **Insulate the exterior walls, flat roof, and floor above the basement** (highest impact on reducing heat loss).
