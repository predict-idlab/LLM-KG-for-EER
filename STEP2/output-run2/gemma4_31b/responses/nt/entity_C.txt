### Proposed Renovation Path

To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the renovations should follow the "Envelope First" principle:

#### Phase 1: Envelope Optimization (Thermal Shell)
Before upgrading the heating system, we ensure the building retains as much heat as possible.
1.  **Insulate Gable Roof**: The main roof is currently "Moderately Insulated." Upgrading this to "Well Insulated" is the most practical envelope improvement remaining, as the walls and floor are already well-insulated.
2.  **Replace Glazing (Double $\rightarrow$ Triple)**: The windows currently feature double glazing. Upgrading to **Triple Glazing** will further reduce thermal bridging and lower the energy demand for the new heating system.

#### Phase 2: High-Efficiency System Transition
With the envelope optimized, we address the owner's desire to change the emission system.
3.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**: Since geothermal options are unavailable and the building already has **Floor Heating** (which is a low-temperature emission system), an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most cost-effective and energy-efficient choice. It will replace the current electric convectors/boiler, significantly reducing operational energy costs.
