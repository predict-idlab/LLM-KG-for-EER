Based on the information provided, here's a concise and actionable renovation plan for your 1832 attached house in Ghent, Belgium, focusing on energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness:

### **1. Insulate the Exterior Walls (Front and Rear)**
- **Action**: Install external wall insulation (EWI) on the solid brick walls.
- **Why First**: Walls account for a significant portion of heat loss. Insulating them first maximizes the impact of subsequent upgrades.
- **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss, improves thermal comfort, and prepares the facade for future upgrades like photovoltaic panels.

### **2. Insulate the Flat-Roof Extension**
- **Action**: Add insulation to the flat roof, ensuring proper ventilation to prevent condensation.
- **Why Next**: Roofs are major sources of heat loss, and insulating the flat roof will further reduce energy consumption.

### **3. Replace the Heating System**
- **Action**: Upgrade to a high-efficiency condensing boiler or an air-source heat pump (ASHP), depending on budget and long-term savings.
- **Why**: The current system is inefficient, and replacing it with a modern, energy-efficient solution will significantly cut heating costs.
- **Note**: If opting for an ASHP, ensure the house is well-insulated first to maximize efficiency.

### **4. Improve Ventilation**
- **Action**: Install a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR) to replace the basic natural ventilation.
- **Why**: Ensures fresh air while minimizing heat loss, improving indoor air quality and energy efficiency.

### **5. Insulate the Floor Above the Basement**
- **Action**: Add insulation to the floor above the basement to reduce heat loss through the ground.
- **Why**: While less impactful than wall or roof insulation, it still contributes to overall energy savings.

### **6. Upgrade Windows (If Needed)**
- **Action**: Replace existing double-glazed windows with high-performance triple-glazed windows if budget allows.
- **Why**: While your current windows are in good condition, upgrading to triple glazing can further reduce heat loss.
- **Note**: If budget is tight, prioritize other upgrades first.

### **7. Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
- **Action**: Assess roof suitability and install PV panels to generate renewable energy.
- **Why**: Belgium has incentives for solar power, and PV panels can offset electricity costs.

### **8. Consider Smart Thermostats and Controls**
- **Action**: Install programmable thermostats and smart controls for heating and ventilation.
- **Why**: Optimizes energy use by adjusting heating based on occupancy and preferences.

### **Optimal Sequence Summary**:
1. Insulate exterior walls (front and rear).
2. Insulate the flat roof.
3. Replace the heating system (boiler or ASHP).
4. Upgrade ventilation to MVHR.
5. Insulate the floor above the basement.
6. Upgrade windows (if budget allows).
7. Install photovoltaic panels.
8. Add smart controls for heating and ventilation.

This plan ensures that each upgrade builds on the previous one, maximizing energy efficiency and cost savings.
