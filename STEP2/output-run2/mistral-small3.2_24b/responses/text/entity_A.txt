Based on the information provided, here’s a concise and actionable renovation plan for your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium, focusing on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective. The plan is prioritized to ensure each step builds on the previous one for optimal benefits.

---

### **1. Insulate the Exterior Walls**
- **Action:** Install external wall insulation (EWI) on the solid brick walls.
- **Why First:** Walls are the largest source of heat loss in uninsulated homes. EWI also protects the brickwork and can be combined with other upgrades.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** High upfront cost but provides long-term energy savings and improves the building’s thermal performance significantly.

---

### **2. Insulate the Pitched Roof**
- **Action:** Add insulation to the pitched roof (e.g., loft insulation or between rafters if accessible).
- **Why Next:** Roof insulation is highly effective at reducing heat loss, especially in older homes with uninsulated roofs.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Relatively affordable and easy to install, with quick payback through energy savings.

---

### **3. Upgrade the Heating System**
- **Action:** Replace the old boiler with a modern, high-efficiency condensing boiler and update the heat emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, and air heating).
- **Why Next:** A new boiler will work more efficiently after insulation upgrades, reducing fuel consumption.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Medium upfront cost but essential for long-term energy savings.

---

### **4. Improve Ventilation**
- **Action:** Install a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR).
- **Why Next:** Ensures good indoor air quality while recovering heat from exhaust air, reducing heating demands.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Higher upfront cost but reduces heat loss and improves comfort.

---

### **5. Insulate the Flat-Roof Extension (if possible)**
- **Action:** Top up insulation in the flat-roof extension to match or exceed current standards.
- **Why Next:** Ensures consistent thermal performance across the entire roof area.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Lower cost than insulating walls but still impactful.

---

### **6. Insulate the Floor Above the Basement**
- **Action:** Add insulation to the floor above the basement (e.g., rigid insulation boards or underfloor insulation).
- **Why Next:** Reduces heat loss through the floor, especially if the basement is unheated.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Moderate cost but effective at reducing drafts and heat loss.

---

### **7. Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
- **Action:** Assess the roof’s suitability for PV panels and install them to generate renewable electricity.
- **Why Last:** PV panels work best when the building’s energy demand is minimized (after insulation and heating upgrades).
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Higher upfront cost but provides long-term savings on electricity bills and potential feed-in tariffs.

---

### **Additional Recommendations:**
- **Windows:** Although your windows have double glazing, consider upgrading to triple glazing if budget allows, especially for north-facing windows.
- **Smart Thermostats:** Install a smart thermostat to optimize heating schedules and further reduce energy use.
- **Air Sealing:** Ensure proper air sealing around windows, doors, and other gaps to prevent drafts.

---

### **Optimal Sequence Summary:**
1. Insulate exterior walls → 2. Insulate pitched roof → 3. Upgrade heating system → 4. Improve ventilation → 5. Insulate flat-roof extension → 6. Insulate floor above basement → 7. Install PV panels.

This plan ensures that each upgrade maximizes the benefits of the previous steps, leading to a more energy-efficient and cost-effective home.
