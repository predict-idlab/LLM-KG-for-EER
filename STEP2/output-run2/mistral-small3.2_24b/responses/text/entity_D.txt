Given the information about your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium, here is a concise and actionable renovation plan focused on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective. The plan prioritizes upgrades that provide the highest return on investment and long-term operational performance.

### 1. **Improve Roof Insulation**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the insulation of the main pitched roof to match the high standards of the flat-roof extension.
   - **Rationale**: The pitched roof has moderate insulation, and improving it will significantly reduce heat loss through the roof, which is a major source of energy loss in homes.
   - **Expected Benefit**: Enhanced thermal performance, leading to lower heating costs and improved energy efficiency.

### 2. **Optimize Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Ensure the ventilation system (D) is operating at peak efficiency. This may involve cleaning or replacing filters, checking ducts for leaks, and verifying that the system is properly balanced.
   - **Rationale**: Efficient ventilation is crucial for maintaining indoor air quality and reducing energy loss. A well-maintained system can also help in reducing the load on the heating system.
   - **Expected Benefit**: Improved indoor air quality and reduced energy consumption.

### 3. **Upgrade to Triple Glazing**
   - **Action**: Replace the existing double-glazed windows with triple-glazed windows.
   - **Rationale**: Triple glazing provides better insulation than double glazing, reducing heat loss through windows. Given that the frames are in good condition, this upgrade can be done without replacing the entire window structure.
   - **Expected Benefit**: Further reduction in heat loss and improved comfort, especially in colder months.

### 4. **Enhance Heat Pump Efficiency**
   - **Action**: Conduct a professional audit of the heat pump system to ensure it is operating at peak efficiency. This may include cleaning or servicing the unit, checking the refrigerant levels, and verifying the system settings.
   - **Rationale**: A well-maintained heat pump operates more efficiently, reducing energy consumption and lowering heating costs.
   - **Expected Benefit**: Optimized performance of the existing heating system, leading to energy savings.

### 5. **Install Smart Thermostats and Controls**
   - **Action**: Install smart thermostats and programmable controls for the heating and ventilation systems.
   - **Rationale**: Smart controls allow for more precise management of heating and ventilation, ensuring that energy is used efficiently and only when needed.
   - **Expected Benefit**: Reduced energy waste and lower heating costs.

### 6. **Seal Air Leaks**
   - **Action**: Conduct an energy audit to identify and seal any air leaks around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Rationale**: Air leaks can significantly reduce the effectiveness of insulation and heating systems. Sealing them can improve overall energy efficiency.
   - **Expected Benefit**: Reduced drafts and improved comfort, along with lower energy consumption.

### 7. **Expand Photovoltaic Panel Capacity**
   - **Action**: Assess the feasibility of adding more photovoltaic panels to increase the house's self-sufficiency in electricity generation.
   - **Rationale**: Given that the house already has photovoltaic panels, expanding the capacity can further reduce reliance on the grid and lower electricity costs.
   - **Expected Benefit**: Increased renewable energy generation and reduced energy bills.

### 8. **Consider Energy-Efficient Lighting**
   - **Action**: Replace any remaining incandescent or halogen bulbs with LED lighting.
   - **Rationale**: LED lighting is more energy-efficient and has a longer lifespan, reducing both energy consumption and maintenance costs.
   - **Expected Benefit**: Lower electricity usage and long-term cost savings.

### Implementation Sequence:
1. **Improve Roof Insulation**
2. **Optimize Ventilation System**
3. **Upgrade to Triple Glazing**
4. **Enhance Heat Pump Efficiency**
5. **Install Smart Thermostats and Controls**
6. **Seal Air Leaks**
7. **Expand Photovoltaic Panel Capacity**
8. **Consider Energy-Efficient Lighting**

This plan focuses on practical upgrades that will significantly improve the energy performance of your home while remaining cost-effective. Each step builds on the previous one to maximize the benefits of the renovations.
