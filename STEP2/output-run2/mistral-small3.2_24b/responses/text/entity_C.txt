### Renovation Plan:

1. **Replace Heating System:**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing boiler and heat emission systems (underfloor heating and electric convectors) with a high-efficiency heat pump. Given the existing underfloor heating, a ground-source heat pump could be a suitable option, but an air-source heat pump might be more practical and cost-effective.
   - **Benefits:** Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than boilers, reducing energy consumption and improving the energy performance certificate (EPC) score.

2. **Improve Roof Insulation:**
   - **Action:** Enhance the insulation of the main pitched roof to match the high standards of the flat-roof extension. Consider using materials with high insulation values, such as rigid foam boards or mineral wool.
   - **Benefits:** Better insulation reduces heat loss through the roof, improving overall energy efficiency.

3. **Optimize Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the existing ventilation system (System D) to a more efficient heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system. Ensure the system is properly balanced and maintained.
   - **Benefits:** HRV systems recover heat from exhaust air, reducing the need for additional heating and improving indoor air quality.

4. **Seal Air Leakages:**
   - **Action:** Conduct an energy audit to identify and seal any air leakages around windows, doors, and other openings. Use weatherstripping, caulking, or foam sealant as needed.
   - **Benefits:** Reduces drafts and heat loss, improving overall energy efficiency.

5. **Enhance Window Performance (if necessary):**
   - **Action:** Although the windows are in good condition with double glazing, consider upgrading to triple glazing if the budget allows. This is particularly beneficial for windows facing north or those exposed to harsh weather conditions.
   - **Benefits:** Triple glazing provides better insulation, reducing heat loss through windows.

6. **Optimize Photovoltaic (PV) System:**
   - **Action:** Review the existing PV system to ensure it is operating at peak efficiency. Consider adding a battery storage system to store excess energy for use during peak demand times.
   - **Benefits:** Maximizes the use of renewable energy, reducing reliance on the grid and lowering energy bills.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. Replace the heating system with a heat pump.
2. Improve roof insulation.
3. Upgrade the ventilation system to HRV.
4. Seal air leakages.
5. Enhance window performance (if budget allows).
6. Optimize the PV system with battery storage.
