Based on the information provided, here is a concise and actionable renovation plan for your detached house in Ghent, Belgium, focusing on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective:

### 1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls (front, rear, left side, and right side) using cavity wall insulation. This is a cost-effective way to significantly reduce heat loss.
   - **Benefit**: Improves thermal performance and reduces heating costs.
   - **Note**: Ensure the insulation material is compatible with the existing cavity walls.

### 2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space. If it is not already insulated, adding insulation will prevent heat loss through the floor.
   - **Benefit**: Enhances comfort and reduces energy consumption.
   - **Note**: Use appropriate insulation materials suitable for floors, such as rigid foam boards or mineral wool.

### 3. **Replace Windows with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - **Action**: Replace the single-glazed windows with double or triple-glazed windows. Since the wooden frames are in good condition, consider retrofitting them with new glazing to preserve the aesthetic while improving insulation.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss through windows and improves overall insulation.
   - **Note**: Choose energy-efficient glazing with a low U-value.

### 4. **Upgrade the Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and heating emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors) with a modern, energy-efficient system. Consider a heat pump, which is suitable for Belgium's climate and can significantly reduce energy consumption.
   - **Benefit**: Improves heating efficiency and reduces energy bills.
   - **Note**: Ensure the new system is compatible with the existing underfloor heating if you wish to retain it.

### 5. **Improve Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the basic natural ventilation system to a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR). This system recovers heat from the exhaust air and uses it to warm the incoming fresh air.
   - **Benefit**: Enhances indoor air quality and reduces heat loss through ventilation.
   - **Note**: Ensure proper installation to maximize efficiency.

### 6. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable energy. Given the potential for PV installation, this can significantly reduce electricity costs and improve the energy performance certificate.
   - **Benefit**: Lowers electricity bills and increases the house's energy independence.
   - **Note**: Check local regulations and incentives for PV installation.

### 7. **Seal Air Leakages**
   - **Action**: Identify and seal any air leakages around windows, doors, and other openings. Use weatherstripping, caulking, or sealant to prevent drafts.
   - **Benefit**: Reduces heat loss and improves overall insulation.
   - **Note**: Regularly inspect and maintain seals to ensure effectiveness.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**: Start with the most significant heat loss areas.
2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**: Address floor insulation next.
3. **Replace Windows**: Improve window insulation to complement wall and floor insulation.
4. **Upgrade the Heating System**: Install a new, efficient heating system to work with the improved insulation.
5. **Improve Ventilation System**: Enhance air quality and heat recovery.
6. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**: Generate renewable energy to further reduce energy costs.
7. **Seal Air Leakages**: Final step to ensure all potential heat loss points are addressed.

This plan prioritizes cost-effective upgrades that will have the most significant impact on energy efficiency and long-term operational performance.
