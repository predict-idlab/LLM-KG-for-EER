### Renovation Plan for the Semi-Detached Building

#### **1. Insulate Attic Floor (Ordering: 31)**
   - **Objective**: Improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Action**: Insulate the attic floor using walkable insulation if possible.
   - **Cost**: €60/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Point of Attention**:
     - Install a vapour barrier on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation.
     - Choose insulation that is walkable if the attic floor is used frequently.
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary in Flanders.

#### **2. Replace Glazing (Ordering: 43)**
   - **Objective**: Enhance thermal performance of windows.
   - **Action**: Replace the glazing of the windows in the bedrooms and living area.
   - **Cost**: €200/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: High.
   - **Point of Attention**:
     - Ensure window frames are in good condition and have the right thickness for the new glazing.
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary in Flanders if measurements are kept.

#### **3. Connect to Heat Grid (Ordering: 55)**
   - **Objective**: Transition to a more sustainable heating solution.
   - **Action**: Connect the building to the local heat grid.
   - **Cost**: €7000 (fixed).
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Point of Attention**:
     - Check if the emission system corresponds to the supply and return temperatures of the heat grid.
     - Contact the municipality or grid operator for more information.
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary in Flanders.

#### **4. Place Air-Water Heat Pump with Cooling (Ordering: 73)**
   - **Objective**: Upgrade the heating system for better efficiency and cooling capability.
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump with cooling functionality.
   - **Cost**: €9000 (fixed) + €35/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.
   - **Point of Attention**:
     - Ensure the above-ground parts are at least 2m away from the parcel boundary.
   - **Permit Needed**: Not necessary if the above-ground parts are at least 2m away from the parcel boundary (Flanders).

### **Summary**
1. **Insulate Attic Floor** - Improves thermal comfort and reduces energy consumption.
2. **Replace Glazing** - Enhances the thermal performance of windows.
3. **Connect to Heat Grid** - Transition to a more sustainable heating solution.
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump with Cooling** - Upgrades the heating system for better efficiency and cooling capability.

This plan maximizes energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, prioritizing actions that provide the most significant benefits first.
