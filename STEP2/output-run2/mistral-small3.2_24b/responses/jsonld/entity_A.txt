### Renovation Plan for the Semi-Detached House

**Objective:** Improve energy efficiency and comfort while considering cost-effectiveness.

#### **Immediate Actions (High Priority)**
1. **Insulate Walls (Interior)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the interior walls (front, rear, right) with an insulation grade of at least "Moderately Insulated."
   - **Cost:** ~€150 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Rationale:** The building has no insulation on the walls, and interior insulation is a cost-effective solution that does not require external permits.

2. **Replace Roof Covering (Flat & Gable)**
   - **Action:** Replace the flat and gable roofs with new insulation-compatible coverings.
   - **Cost:** ~€65 per m² (flat roof) and ~€260 per m² (gable roof)
   - **Nuisance Level:** High
   - **Rationale:** The roofs are currently uninsulated or moderately insulated. Replacing them will improve thermal performance and may allow for additional upgrades like PV panels.

3. **Insulate Attic Floor**
   - **Action:** Insulate the attic floor to prevent heat loss.
   - **Cost:** ~€60 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Rationale:** The attic floor is currently uninsulated, and this is a relatively simple and low-cost intervention.

#### **Medium-Term Actions (Moderate Priority)**
4. **Upgrade Window Glazing**
   - **Action:** Replace the single glazing in the bedrooms with double glazing.
   - **Cost:** ~€200 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level:** High
   - **Rationale:** The current single glazing has poor thermal and acoustic properties. Double glazing will improve comfort and energy efficiency.

5. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the ventilation system to a more efficient model (e.g., Ventilation System C or D).
   - **Cost:** ~€3500 (fixed) + €17 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Rationale:** The current system may be outdated or inefficient. A more modern system will improve air quality and reduce energy consumption.

#### **Long-Term Actions (Low Priority)**
6. **Install Renewable Energy Systems**
   - **Action:** Consider installing PV panels on the roof or in the garden.
   - **Cost:** ~€275 per m²
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Rationale:** The building has potential for PV installation, which can significantly reduce energy costs and carbon footprint.

7. **Connect to Heat Grid (If Available)**
   - **Action:** Explore the possibility of connecting to a heat grid if one becomes available in the future.
   - **Cost:** ~€7000
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Rationale:** A heat grid can provide a more sustainable and efficient heating solution.

#### **Additional Considerations**
- **Historical Protection:** Ensure that any renovations comply with local regulations, especially for elements with historical protection.
- **Asbestos Removal:** If asbestos is present in the roofs, arrange for safe removal before any insulation work.
- **Owner’s Motivation:** The owner’s primary goal is to improve the EPC rating. Prioritize actions that have the most significant impact on energy performance.

This plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while balancing cost and disruption. It starts with the most impactful and cost-effective interventions and progresses to more advanced solutions.
