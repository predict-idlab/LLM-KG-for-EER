### Renovation Plan for the Building

#### **Step 1: Assess the Current State**
- **Building Type**: Attached building.
- **Construction Year**: 2023.
- **EPC Score**: 50.
- **Potential for PV**: No.
- **Presence of Heat Grid**: No.
- **Reason to Change Emission System**: Yes.
- **Resident**: Present.
- **Site Characteristics**: No potential for horizontal or vertical geothermal systems.

#### **Step 2: Identify Key Areas for Improvement**
1. **Roofing**:
   - **Main Roof (Gable Roof)**: Moderately insulated, good state.
   - **Extension Roof (Flat Roof)**: Well insulated, good state.
   - **Suggestions**:
     - **Insulate Gable Roof**: Improve insulation to reduce heat loss.
     - **Insulate Flat Roof**: Already well insulated, but consider additional measures if needed.
     - **Place Roof Underlayment**: Ensure complete air- and watertightness.
     - **Remove Asbestos from Gable Roof**: If present, remove asbestos for safety and compliance.

2. **Windows**:
   - **Living Area Windows**: Double glazing, good state.
   - **Bedroom Windows**: Double glazing, good state.
   - **Suggestions**:
     - **Replace Glazing**: Consider upgrading to triple glazing for better thermal performance.
     - **Replace Windows**: If frames are in good condition, replace glazing; otherwise, consider replacing entire windows.

3. **Walls**:
   - **Front Wall**: Full brick wall, well insulated, no historical protection.
   - **Rear Wall**: Full brick wall, well insulated, no historical protection.
   - **Suggestions**:
     - **Insulate Wall on Exterior**: If exterior insulation is feasible, consider adding it to improve thermal performance.
     - **Insulate Wall on Interior**: If exterior insulation is not feasible, consider interior insulation.

4. **Floors**:
   - **Floor Above Basement**: Well insulated, no protection.
   - **Suggestions**:
     - **Insulate Floor Above Basement**: Ensure adequate insulation to prevent heat loss.

5. **Ventilation System**:
   - **Type**: Ventilation System D (mechanical air supply and exhaust).
   - **Suggestions**:
     - **Replace Ventilation System**: Consider upgrading to a more efficient system if needed.

6. **Emission System**:
   - **Type**: Electric convector and floor heating.
   - **Suggestions**:
     - **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**: Optimize the emission system for better efficiency.
     - **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**: Consider installing an air-water heat pump for more efficient heating.

7. **PV Panels**:
   - **Current State**: No PV panels installed.
   - **Suggestions**:
     - **Place PV-Panels on Roof**: If feasible, install PV panels to generate renewable energy.
     - **Place PV-Panels in Garden**: If roof installation is not feasible, consider garden installation.

#### **Step 3: Prioritize Actions**
1. **High Priority**:
   - **Insulate Gable Roof**: Improve insulation to reduce heat loss.
   - **Replace Glazing**: Upgrade to triple glazing for better thermal performance.
   - **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**: Optimize the emission system.

2. **Medium Priority**:
   - **Insulate Wall on Exterior**: If feasible, add exterior insulation.
   - **Replace Ventilation System**: Upgrade to a more efficient system if needed.
   - **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**: Consider installing for more efficient heating.

3. **Low Priority**:
   - **Place Roof Underlayment**: Ensure complete air- and watertightness.
   - **Place PV-Panels on Roof/Garden**: Install PV panels if feasible.

#### **Step 4: Implementation Timeline**
- **Phase 1 (Immediate Actions)**:
  - Insulate gable roof.
  - Replace glazing with triple glazing.
  - Optimize emission system.

- **Phase 2 (Medium-Term Actions)**:
  - Insulate exterior walls.
  - Upgrade ventilation system.
  - Install air-water heat pump.

- **Phase 3 (Long-Term Actions)**:
  - Install PV panels on the roof or in the garden.

#### **Step 5: Monitoring and Evaluation**
- **Regular Inspections**: Conduct regular inspections to ensure the effectiveness of the installed systems.
- **Energy Audits**: Perform periodic energy audits to assess the impact of the renovations on energy consumption and costs.
- **Feedback Loop**: Gather feedback from residents to identify any issues or areas for further improvement.

This plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective and practical. The steps are ordered to prioritize actions that provide the most significant improvements first, ensuring a structured and efficient renovation process.
