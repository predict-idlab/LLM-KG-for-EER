### Energy-Efficient and Cost-Effective Renovation Plan

**Objective:** Improve energy efficiency while maintaining cost-effectiveness for the semi-detached building constructed in 2017.

#### **1. Roof and Insulation Upgrades**
- **Replace Gable Roof (RoofMain):**
  - **Action:** Replace the gable roof to improve insulation and energy efficiency.
  - **Reason:** The current roof is moderately insulated (reno:ModeratelyInsulated).
  - **Cost:** €260/m² (high nuisance level, permit required if roof framework is renewed).

- **Insulate Gable Roof:**
  - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof to enhance thermal performance.
  - **Reason:** The roof is currently moderately insulated.
  - **Cost:** €60/m² (low nuisance level, no permit required).

- **Insulate Attic Floor:**
  - **Action:** Insulate the attic floor to reduce heat loss.
  - **Reason:** The attic floor is not mentioned in the current state, assuming it is not insulated.
  - **Cost:** €60/m² (low nuisance level, no permit required).

#### **2. Window and Glazing Upgrades**
- **Replace Glazing:**
  - **Action:** Replace the existing double glazing with triple glazing to improve insulation.
  - **Reason:** The current windows have double glazing (reno:DoubleGlazing), which can be upgraded for better performance.
  - **Cost:** €200/m² (high nuisance level, no permit required).

- **Replace Windows:**
  - **Action:** Replace the wooden window frames with more energy-efficient frames (e.g., PVC or aluminum with thermal breaks).
  - **Reason:** Wooden frames (reno:Wood) may not provide optimal insulation.
  - **Cost:** €800/m² (high nuisance level, no permit required if measurements are kept).

#### **3. Ventilation System Upgrade**
- **Replace Ventilation System:**
  - **Action:** Upgrade the existing ventilation system (reno:VentilationSystemD) to a more efficient model.
  - **Reason:** Improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
  - **Cost:** €3,500 (fixed) + €17/m² (moderate nuisance level, no permit required).

#### **4. Emission System Upgrade**
- **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
  - **Action:** Install a low-temperature emission system to work efficiently with heat pumps or other low-temperature heat sources.
  - **Reason:** Enhances compatibility with renewable energy sources.
  - **Cost:** €100/m² (moderate nuisance level, no permit required).

#### **5. Renewable Energy Integration**
- **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
  - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable energy.
  - **Reason:** The roof is well-insulated and suitable for PV installation.
  - **Cost:** €275/m² (low nuisance level, permit not required if panels are in the same plane).

#### **6. Additional Recommendations**
- **Place Roof Underlayment:**
  - **Action:** Install a roof underlayment to ensure air- and watertightness.
  - **Reason:** Critical for long-term roof performance and energy efficiency.
  - **Cost:** €65/m² (low nuisance level, no permit required).

### **Optimal Sequence of Renovations**
1. **Replace Gable Roof** (high impact on insulation and energy efficiency).
2. **Insulate Gable Roof and Attic Floor** (complementary to roof replacement).
3. **Replace Glazing and Windows** (improves thermal performance of windows).
4. **Replace Ventilation System** (ensures proper air quality and energy efficiency).
5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** (optimizes heating system).
6. **Install PV-Panels on Roof** (generates renewable energy).
7. **Place Roof Underlayment** (final step to ensure roof integrity).
