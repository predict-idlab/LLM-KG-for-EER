### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

**Objective:** Improve the energy efficiency of the building while maintaining cost-effectiveness.

**Current State:**
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score:** 50
- **Net Floor Area:** 159.0 m²
- **Building Type:** Attached, with a gable roof (RoofMain) and a flat roof extension (RoofExtension)
- **Insulation Grades:**
  - **Wall Front:** Well Insulated
  - **Wall Rear:** Well Insulated
  - **RoofMain:** Moderately Insulated
  - **RoofExtension:** Well Insulated
  - **FloorAboveBasement:** Well Insulated
- **Heating System:** FloorHeating and ElectricConvector
- **Windows:**
  - **Living Area:** Double Glazing (changed during a renovation intervention)
  - **Bedrooms:** Double Glazing
- **Ventilation System:** VentilationSystemD
- **PV Panels:** Present
- **Potential for Geothermal Drilling:** None
- **Heat Grid Availability:** None
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve the EPC

**Recommended Renovations:**

1. **Insulate Gable Roof (RoofMain):**
   - **Action:** Upgrade insulation to Well Insulated.
   - **Reasoning:** The current insulation is Moderately Insulated, which can be improved for better energy efficiency.
   - **Cost:** Moderate (unit price: 60 EUR/m², nuisance level: low).

2. **Replace Glazing (WindowsLivingArea and WindowsBedrooms):**
   - **Action:** Replace existing double glazing with higher-performance double or triple glazing.
   - **Reasoning:** Upgrading glazing can significantly improve thermal performance and reduce heat loss.
   - **Cost:** High (unit price: 200 EUR/m², nuisance level: high).

3. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from VentilationSystemD to a more efficient system like VentilationSystemA or VentilationSystemB.
   - **Reasoning:** Improved ventilation can enhance indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Cost:** Moderate (unit price: 17 EUR/m², nuisance level: moderate).

4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to replace the current ElectricConvector.
   - **Reasoning:** Heat pumps are more energy-efficient for heating and can reduce energy consumption.
   - **Cost:** Moderate (unit price: 35 EUR/m², nuisance level: moderate).

5. **Insulate Attic Floor:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the attic floor if not already done.
   - **Reasoning:** Attic insulation can prevent heat loss through the roof and improve overall energy efficiency.
   - **Cost:** Low (unit price: 60 EUR/m², nuisance level: low).

6. **Place PV Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Expand the existing PV panel installation or optimize the current setup.
   - **Reasoning:** Solar panels can generate renewable energy, reducing reliance on grid electricity.
   - **Cost:** Moderate (unit price: 275 EUR/m², nuisance level: low).

**Implementation Sequence:**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof** (to improve overall building envelope performance).
2. **Replace Glazing** (to enhance window performance).
3. **Replace Ventilation System** (to improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency).
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** (to upgrade the heating system).
5. **Insulate Attic Floor** (to complete the building envelope improvements).
6. **Place PV Panels on Roof** (to maximize renewable energy generation).

**Expected Outcomes:**
- **Improved EPC Score:** The combined upgrades should significantly improve the energy efficiency of the building, leading to a better EPC score.
- **Reduced Energy Consumption:** Enhanced insulation, efficient heating, and renewable energy generation will lower energy usage.
- **Cost Savings:** Lower energy bills and potential incentives for renewable energy installations.

**Note:** Always consult with local regulations and professionals to ensure compliance and optimal implementation of the renovation plan.
