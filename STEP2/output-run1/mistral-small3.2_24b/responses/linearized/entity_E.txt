### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### **1. Insulate the Attic Floor**
- **Action**: Insulate the attic floor to reduce heat loss.
- **Why**: The attic floor is often a significant source of heat loss in older homes. Insulating it will improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
- **Cost**: €60 per m².
- **Nuisance Level**: Low.

#### **2. Insulate the Gable Roof**
- **Action**: Insulate the gable roof to further reduce heat loss.
- **Why**: The gable roof is currently well-insulated, but adding additional insulation can enhance energy efficiency.
- **Cost**: €60 per m².
- **Nuisance Level**: Low.

#### **3. Replace Glazing on Windows**
- **Action**: Replace the single glazing on all windows with double glazing.
- **Why**: Single glazing has poor thermal and acoustic properties. Double glazing will improve insulation and reduce heat loss.
- **Cost**: €200 per m².
- **Nuisance Level**: High.

#### **4. Replace the Roof Underlayment**
- **Action**: Place a roof underlayment to ensure air- and watertightness.
- **Why**: This will prevent condensation problems and enhance the roof's durability.
- **Cost**: €65 per m².
- **Nuisance Level**: Low.

#### **5. Insulate the Wall Cavity**
- **Action**: Inject insulation into the cavity walls.
- **Why**: Cavity walls can be effectively insulated to improve thermal performance without significant structural changes.
- **Cost**: €22 per m².
- **Nuisance Level**: Low.

#### **6. Replace the Ventilation System**
- **Action**: Upgrade the ventilation system to a more efficient model.
- **Why**: An efficient ventilation system can improve air quality and reduce energy consumption.
- **Cost**: €17 per m².
- **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.

#### **7. Place PV Panels on the Roof**
- **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof.
- **Why**: Solar panels can generate renewable energy, reducing reliance on grid electricity and lowering energy costs.
- **Cost**: €275 per m².
- **Nuisance Level**: Low.

#### **8. Place a Heat Pump Boiler**
- **Action**: Install a heat pump boiler for more efficient heating.
- **Why**: Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than traditional boilers, reducing energy consumption and costs.
- **Cost**: €4,000 (fixed price).
- **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.

### **Optimized Sequence of Actions**
1. **Insulate the Attic Floor**
2. **Insulate the Gable Roof**
3. **Replace Glazing on Windows**
4. **Replace the Roof Underlayment**
5. **Insulate the Wall Cavity**
6. **Replace the Ventilation System**
7. **Place PV Panels on the Roof**
8. **Place a Heat Pump Boiler**

### **Rationale for Sequence**
- **Attic and Roof Insulation First**: These actions address the most significant sources of heat loss and are relatively non-disruptive.
- **Window Glazing and Roof Underlayment**: These improvements enhance the building's thermal envelope and are essential before installing more complex systems.
- **Wall Cavity Insulation and Ventilation System**: These steps further improve energy efficiency and indoor air quality.
- **PV Panels and Heat Pump Boiler**: These are the final steps, providing renewable energy generation and efficient heating solutions.

This plan ensures a systematic approach to maximizing energy efficiency while minimizing disruption and cost.
