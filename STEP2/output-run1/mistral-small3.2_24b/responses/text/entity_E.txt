Based on the information provided, here's a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan prioritizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness while considering the optimal sequence of upgrades:

### 1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls at the front, rear, left side, and right side of the house.
   - **Why**: Cavity wall insulation is a cost-effective way to reduce heat loss through the walls, which are currently uninsulated.
   - **Note**: Ensure the insulation material is suitable for cavity walls and complies with local building regulations.

### 2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space if it is not already insulated.
   - **Why**: Insulating the floor will prevent heat loss through the ground, improving overall energy efficiency.
   - **Note**: Consider using rigid insulation boards or other suitable materials for floor insulation.

### 3. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the windows with wooden frames to double or triple glazing.
   - **Why**: Single glazing is a significant source of heat loss. Double or triple glazing will improve insulation and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Note**: Ensure the new glazing is compatible with the existing wooden frames to maintain the aesthetic appeal of the house.

### 4. **Upgrade the Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and heat emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors) with a more efficient system.
   - **Options**:
     - **Heat Pump**: Consider an air-source heat pump, which is suitable for your location and can provide both heating and cooling.
     - **Condensing Boiler**: If a heat pump is not feasible, a modern condensing boiler can be more efficient than the existing system.
   - **Why**: Upgrading the heating system will improve energy efficiency and reduce long-term operational costs.
   - **Note**: Ensure the new system is compatible with the existing infrastructure and complies with local regulations.

### 5. **Install a Mechanical Ventilation System with Heat Recovery (MVHR)**
   - **Action**: Replace the basic natural ventilation system with an MVHR system.
   - **Why**: MVHR systems recover heat from exhaust air and use it to preheat incoming fresh air, reducing heat loss and improving indoor air quality.
   - **Note**: Ensure the system is properly sized and installed to maximize efficiency.

### 6. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - **Action**: Assess the potential for installing PV panels on the roof.
   - **Why**: PV panels can generate renewable energy, reducing your reliance on the grid and lowering energy bills.
   - **Note**: Consult with a professional to determine the optimal placement and size of the PV system.

### 7. **Improve Natural Lighting and Daylighting**
   - **Action**: Consider adding skylights or larger windows in strategic locations to maximize natural light.
   - **Why**: Natural lighting reduces the need for artificial lighting during the day, saving energy.
   - **Note**: Ensure any additions comply with local building regulations and do not compromise the structural integrity of the house.

### 8. **Monitor and Optimize Energy Use**
   - **Action**: Install a smart thermostat and energy monitoring system.
   - **Why**: Smart thermostats can optimize heating schedules, and energy monitoring systems can help identify areas for further energy savings.
   - **Note**: Ensure the systems are compatible with your new heating system and other smart home devices.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. Insulate exterior walls.
2. Insulate the floor above the crawl space.
3. Replace single glazing with double or triple glazing.
4. Upgrade the heating system.
5. Install an MVHR system.
6. Install PV panels.
7. Improve natural lighting and daylighting.
8. Monitor and optimize energy use.

By following this plan, you can significantly improve the energy performance certificate of your house while maintaining cost-effectiveness.
