Given the information provided, here’s a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your home in Ghent, Belgium. The plan focuses on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, considering the existing conditions and constraints.

### Priority 1: Upgrade Heating System
1. **Replace Underfloor Heating and Electric Convectors**
   - **Action**: Install a modern, high-efficiency heat pump (air-source or hybrid) combined with underfloor heating in key areas. This will significantly improve heating efficiency and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Benefit**: Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than boilers, especially when combined with underfloor heating, which operates at lower temperatures.
   - **Cost**: Moderate to high, but eligible for subsidies in Belgium.

2. **Optimize Ventilation System D**
   - **Action**: Ensure the ventilation system is properly maintained and upgraded to a heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system if not already present. This will reduce heat loss through ventilation.
   - **Benefit**: HRV systems can recover up to 90% of the heat from exhaust air, improving overall energy efficiency.

### Priority 2: Improve Roof Insulation
1. **Enhance Insulation of the Main Pitched Roof**
   - **Action**: Add additional insulation to the pitched roof to bring it up to the current standard (e.g., 30-40 cm of mineral wool or equivalent).
   - **Benefit**: Roofs are a major source of heat loss, and improving insulation will significantly reduce heating demand.
   - **Cost**: Moderate, but with long-term energy savings.

### Priority 3: Smart Controls and Monitoring
1. **Install Smart Thermostats and Zoning Controls**
   - **Action**: Implement smart thermostats and zoning controls to optimize heating based on occupancy and time of day.
   - **Benefit**: Precise temperature control reduces energy waste and improves comfort.
   - **Cost**: Low to moderate, with potential subsidies.

2. **Monitor Energy Consumption**
   - **Action**: Install energy monitoring systems to track consumption and identify areas for further improvement.
   - **Benefit**: Data-driven insights can help optimize energy use and maintenance.

### Priority 4: Additional Considerations
1. **Photovoltaic Panels**
   - **Action**: Ensure the existing photovoltaic panels are well-maintained and consider expanding the system if feasible.
   - **Benefit**: Maximizes renewable energy generation and reduces reliance on the grid.

2. **Window Upgrades (if needed)**
   - **Action**: Although the windows are in good condition, consider upgrading to triple glazing if significant heat loss is observed.
   - **Benefit**: Further reduces heat loss through windows.
   - **Cost**: High, but may be justified by long-term energy savings.

### Optimal Sequence
1. **Upgrade Heating System**: Start with the heat pump installation to immediately improve energy efficiency.
2. **Improve Roof Insulation**: Follow up with roof insulation to reduce heat loss.
3. **Optimize Ventilation**: Ensure the HRV system is in place to complement the heating upgrades.
4. **Smart Controls**: Implement smart thermostats and monitoring systems to fine-tune energy use.
5. **Additional Upgrades**: Consider window upgrades and photovoltaic expansion if needed.

### Subsidies and Incentives
- **Check for Subsidies**: Belgium offers various subsidies for energy-efficient renovations. Ensure to apply for available grants to reduce costs.
