Given the specifics of your 1832 attached house in Ghent, Belgium, here’s a cost-effective, step-by-step renovation plan to improve energy efficiency while prioritizing practical upgrades:

### **1. Improve Insulation**
**Priority:** High
**Focus Areas:**
- **Exterior Walls (Front & Rear):** Install external wall insulation (EWI) on the solid brick walls. This minimizes heat loss and avoids disrupting the interior. Use a high-performance insulation material like mineral wool or rigid foam.
- **Flat-Roof Extension:** Add insulation to the uninsulated flat roof. Use a suitable material (e.g., polyisocyanurate foam) to ensure thermal performance.
- **Floor Above Basement:** Insulate this area to reduce heat loss to the basement. Use a thin but effective insulation material to avoid reducing headroom.

**Why First?** Insulation is the most cost-effective way to reduce heat loss and improve the EPC score.

### **2. Upgrade Heating System**
**Priority:** High
**Options:**
- Replace the old boiler with a **condensing boiler** (higher efficiency than conventional boilers).
- Consider a **heat pump** (air-source or hybrid) if budget allows, as they are more energy-efficient in the long run.
- Upgrade radiators to **low-temperature or underfloor heating** to work better with modern boilers/heat pumps.

**Why Next?** A more efficient heating system complements insulation upgrades.

### **3. Optimize Ventilation**
**Priority:** Medium
**Action:**
- Replace the basic natural ventilation with a **heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system**. This reduces heat loss while maintaining air quality.

**Why?** Proper ventilation prevents drafts and improves indoor air quality without wasting energy.

### **4. Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
**Priority:** Medium-Low
**Action:**
- Assess the roof’s suitability for PV panels (south-facing, minimal shading).
- Install a **PV system** to generate electricity, reducing reliance on the grid.

**Why Later?** PV panels are beneficial but depend on initial insulation and heating upgrades for maximum impact.

### **5. Window Upgrades (If Needed)**
**Priority:** Low
**Action:**
- Since your windows have double glazing and are in good condition, **no immediate upgrade is needed**. However, if drafts are an issue, consider **secondary glazing** or **weatherstripping** as a low-cost alternative.

### **6. Smart Controls & Monitoring**
**Priority:** Low
**Action:**
- Install a **smart thermostat** to optimize heating schedules and reduce energy waste.

### **Optimal Renovation Sequence:**
1. **Insulation** (walls, flat roof, floor above basement).
2. **Heating system upgrade** (boiler/heat pump + radiators).
3. **Ventilation upgrade** (HRV system).
4. **Photovoltaic panels** (if feasible).
5. **Minor window improvements** (if needed).

### **Expected Benefits:**
- **Lower energy bills** due to reduced heat loss and efficient heating.
- **Higher EPC score**, potentially improving property value.
- **Long-term cost savings** from lower energy consumption.
