Based on the information provided, here's a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium, focusing on energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness:

### 1. **Insulation Upgrades (Prioritize High-Impact Areas)**
   - **Exterior Walls**: Install external wall insulation (EWI) to the solid brick walls. This will significantly improve thermal performance and reduce heat loss. EWI also avoids reducing internal floor space and can be combined with a fresh exterior finish.
   - **Pitched Roof**: Add insulation to the pitched roof, as roofs are major sources of heat loss. Use high-performance insulation materials like mineral wool or rigid foam boards.
   - **Floor Above Basement**: Insulate the floor above the basement to prevent heat loss through the ground. Use rigid insulation boards or underfloor insulation.

### 2. **Replace and Upgrade Heating Systems**
   - **Boiler**: Replace the old boiler with a modern, high-efficiency condensing boiler. Consider a combination boiler if space is limited.
   - **Heating Systems**: Replace the old radiators, underfloor heating, and air heating systems with modern, efficient radiators or underfloor heating systems. Ensure they are properly sized for each room.
   - **Smart Thermostat**: Install a smart thermostat to optimize heating schedules and reduce energy waste.

### 3. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
   - **Mechanical Ventilation with Heat Recovery (MVHR)**: Replace the basic natural ventilation system with an MVHR system. This will improve indoor air quality while recovering heat from exhaust air, reducing heating demands.

### 4. **Windows and Doors**
   - Although your windows have double glazing, consider upgrading to triple glazing or high-performance double glazing with low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings and argon or krypton gas fills to further reduce heat loss.

### 5. **Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - **Install PV Panels**: Given the potential for PV panels, install a system on the roof to generate renewable electricity. This will reduce your reliance on grid electricity and lower your energy bills.

### 6. **Additional Measures (If Budget Allows)**
   - **Draft Proofing**: Seal gaps around windows, doors, and other openings to prevent drafts and heat loss.
   - **Thermal Bridging**: Address thermal bridges (e.g., around windows, doors, and where walls meet the roof) to minimize heat loss.

### Optimal Renovation Sequence:
1. **Insulation Upgrades**: Start with exterior walls, followed by the pitched roof and floor above the basement. This will address the largest sources of heat loss first.
2. **Heating System Replacement**: Replace the boiler and heating systems to ensure efficient heat distribution.
3. **Ventilation Upgrade**: Install the MVHR system to complement the improved insulation and heating systems.
4. **Windows and Doors**: Upgrade windows and doors to further enhance thermal performance.
5. **PV Panels**: Install PV panels to generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.

### Cost-Effectiveness and Energy Efficiency:
- Prioritize insulation and heating system upgrades as they offer the highest return on investment in terms of energy savings.
- Consider government grants or subsidies for energy-efficient renovations, which can significantly reduce costs.
- Ensure that each renovation step builds on the previous one to maximize benefits (e.g., insulation reduces heating demands, allowing for a smaller, more cost-effective heating system).

This plan will significantly improve your home's energy performance certificate (EPC) score, reduce energy consumption, and lower long-term operational costs.
