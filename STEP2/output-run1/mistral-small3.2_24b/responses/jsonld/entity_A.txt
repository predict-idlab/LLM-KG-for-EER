### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached House

**Objective**: Improve the EPC label of the dwelling, enhancing energy efficiency while being cost-effective.

**Key Focus Areas**:
1. **Insulation Improvements**:
   - **Replace Gable Roof**: High priority due to poor insulation (Not Insulated) and poor state (GoodState with value 8).
   - **Insulate Flat Roof**: Moderately insulated, but potential for improvement.
   - **Insulate Wall Cavity**: Walls are not insulated (Not Insulated).

2. **Window and Glazing Upgrades**:
   - **Replace Windows**: Windows are moderately efficient (Double Glazing), but replacing them with higher efficiency glazing (e.g., Triple Glazing) can significantly improve thermal performance.
   - **Replace Glazing**: Consider upgrading to Triple Glazing for better insulation.

3. **Heating System Upgrades**:
   - **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**: Replace the existing boiler with a more efficient and environmentally friendly system.
   - **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**: Modernize the emission system for better energy efficiency.

4. **Additional Recommendations**:
   - **Place PV-Panels on Roof**: Utilize the potential for PV installation to generate renewable energy.
   - **Place Sunshade**: Reduce solar heat gain in summer, improving comfort and energy efficiency.

**Proposed Sequence of Actions**:
1. **Replace Gable Roof** (High Priority):
   - **Action**: Replace the gable roof with a new, well-insulated roof.
   - **Benefits**: Improves thermal performance, reduces heat loss, and enhances comfort.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €260 per m².

2. **Insulate Flat Roof**:
   - **Action**: Insulate the flat roof to improve its insulation grade.
   - **Benefits**: Enhances energy efficiency and reduces heat loss.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €50 per m².

3. **Insulate Wall Cavity**:
   - **Action**: Inject insulation into the wall cavities.
   - **Benefits**: Reduces heat loss through walls, improving overall energy efficiency.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €22 per m².

4. **Replace Windows**:
   - **Action**: Replace existing windows with new, high-efficiency windows (e.g., Triple Glazing).
   - **Benefits**: Improves thermal performance, reduces heat loss, and enhances comfort.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €800 per m².

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**:
   - **Action**: Install a new air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Benefits**: More energy-efficient and environmentally friendly heating system.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €8,000 (fixed) + €35 per m².

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**:
   - **Action**: Modernize the emission system to operate at lower temperatures.
   - **Benefits**: Improves energy efficiency and reduces energy consumption.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €100 per m².

7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**:
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic panels on the roof to generate renewable energy.
   - **Benefits**: Reduces reliance on grid electricity, lowers energy bills, and reduces carbon footprint.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €275 per m².

8. **Place Sunshade**:
   - **Action**: Install sunshades to reduce solar heat gain in summer.
   - **Benefits**: Improves comfort and reduces the need for cooling.
   - **Cost**: Approximately €750 (fixed).

**Expected Outcomes**:
- **Improved EPC Label**: From current EPC score of 388 (likely D label) to a higher label (e.g., C or B label).
- **Enhanced Comfort**: Better insulation and heating system will improve thermal comfort.
- **Lower Energy Bills**: Reduced energy consumption due to better insulation and more efficient heating system.
- **Environmental Benefits**: Reduced carbon footprint through renewable energy generation and improved energy efficiency.

**Note**: The sequence of actions is designed to maximize energy efficiency improvements while considering cost-effectiveness. Prioritize actions that offer the highest impact on energy efficiency and comfort.
