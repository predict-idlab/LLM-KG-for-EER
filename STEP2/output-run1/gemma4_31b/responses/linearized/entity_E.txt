1.  **Insulate Wall Cavity:** All four walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right) are identified as **Cavity Walls** and are currently **Not Insulated**. Cavity wall insulation is one of the most cost-effective upgrades as it requires no structural changes to the facade.

Since the wooden window frames are in **Good State**, you should **replace the glazing** (e.g., to double or triple glazing) rather than replacing the entire window unit, significantly reducing costs.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The building has a floor above a crawl space. Insulating this area prevents cold air from rising into the living spaces.

### Phase 2: Ventilation and Air Quality
Once the house is "sealed" with better insulation and glazing, air exchange must be managed to prevent condensation and maintain air quality.

4.  **Replace Ventilation System:** The current **Ventilation System A** (natural supply/exhaust) is the most basic. Upgrading to a more modern system (System B, C, or D) will improve thermal comfort and reduce the energy loss associated with uncontrolled ventilation.

5.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** Before installing a new heat pump, the current mixed emission system (radiators, electric convectors) should be transitioned to low-temperature emitters to maximize the efficiency of the next step.
