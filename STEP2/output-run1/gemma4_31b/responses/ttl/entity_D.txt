*   **Cost/Nuisance:** This is a low-nuisance intervention with a moderate unit price (60/m²), making it highly cost-effective.

*   **Action:** **Replace Glazing** (Upgrade from Double to Triple Glazing)

*   **Constraint:** This is a higher-cost intervention (200/m²) and has a high nuisance level. Given that the building is already very efficient, this should only be pursued if the budget allows after the roof is addressed.
