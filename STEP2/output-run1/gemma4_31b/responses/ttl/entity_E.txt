These steps reduce energy loss and are the most cost-effective per square meter.

1.  **Insulate Wall Cavities:** The front, left, rear, and right walls are all cavity walls and are currently **not insulated**. 
    *   **Action:** `insulate wall cavity`
    *   **Reasoning:** This is the most cost-effective insulation method (22/m²) with low nuisance. Since there is no historical protection, this is the highest priority for reducing the EPC score.
2.  **Insulate Floor:** The building has a floor above a crawl space.
    *   **Action:** `insulate floor above basement`
    *   **Reasoning:** Low cost (30/m²) and low nuisance; it prevents cold drafts from the crawl space.
3.  **Upgrade Glazing:** All windows currently have **single glazing**, which is a major source of heat loss.
    *   **Action:** `replace glazing`
    *   **Reasoning:** Since the wooden window frames are in **good state**, replacing only the glazing (rather than the whole window) is more cost-effective and sustainable.
