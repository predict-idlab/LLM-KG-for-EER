The building is a very modern **Attached Building (constructed in 2023)** with an already strong energy profile (**EPC score of 50.0**, which corresponds to an **A label**). Most elements—including the walls, flat roof, and floor above the basement—are already "Well Insulated." 

However, there are two primary areas for improvement to maximize energy efficiency and satisfy the owner's motivation to further improve the EPC:
1.  **The Thermal Envelope:** The main gable roof is only "Moderately Insulated."
2.  **The Heating System:** There is an explicit flag (`hasReasonToChangeEmissionSystem true`) and the presence of electric convectors, which are generally inefficient compared to modern heat pump technology.

### **Proposed Renovation Path**

To maximize the benefit of each step, the renovation should follow the **"Fabric First"** principle: improve the insulation envelope before upgrading the technical heating systems to ensure the new system is sized correctly and operates at peak efficiency.

#### **Step 1: Envelope Optimization (High Impact, Moderate Cost)**
*   **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
*   **Reasoning:** The `RoofMain` (Gable Roof) is the only significant part of the building envelope that is not "Well Insulated." Since heat rises, improving the roof insulation is the most cost-effective way to reduce heat loss.
*   **Practical Note:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side to prevent condensation, as per the ontology's point of attention.

#### **Step 2: Heating System Upgrade (High Efficiency, Higher Cost)**
*   **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`
*   **Reasoning:** The building currently uses electric convectors and a generic boiler. Given that the building already has **Floor Heating** (a low-temperature emission system), an air-water heat pump is the most compatible and energy-efficient choice. 
*   **Constraint Check:** Geothermal options were ruled out (`potentialForHorizontalGeothermalCaptation` and `potentialForVerticalGeothermalDrilling` are both `false`), and there is no available heat grid.
*   **Benefit:** This replaces inefficient electric resistance heating with a high-COP (Coefficient of Performance) system, significantly lowering long-term operational costs and improving the EPC score.

#### **Step 3: Emission System Refinement (Optional/Supporting)**
*   **Action:** `place emission system on low temperature`
*   **Reasoning:** While floor heating is present, ensuring that the remaining areas (previously served by electric convectors) are transitioned to low-temperature emitters will maximize the efficiency of the new air-water heat pump.
