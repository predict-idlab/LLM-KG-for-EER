    *   *Action:* `insulate floor above basement`.

4.  **Upgrade Ventilation:** To maintain air quality after sealing the building with insulation and to improve heat recovery.

The owner has a stated reason to change the emission system, and the current boiler is outdated. Since geothermal and heat grids are not available for this site, an air-source solution is the most viable path.

5.  **Low-Temperature Emission Transition:** Heat pumps are most efficient when paired with low-temperature emitters (e.g., underfloor heating or oversized radiators) rather than traditional old radiators.
    *   *Action:* `place emission system on low temperature`.
6.  **Install Heat Pump:** Once the envelope is insulated and emitters are upgraded, the boiler can be replaced.

7.  **Solar Energy Installation:** The building is flagged as having potential for PV.

| Sequence | Category | Action | Logic |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulation** | Flat Roof $\rightarrow$ Floor $\rightarrow$ Walls | Stops heat leaks first; prevents "over-sizing" the new heater. |
