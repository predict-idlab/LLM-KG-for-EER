*Focus: High energy savings with low-to-moderate cost and nuisance.*

5.  **Optimize Emission System:** Consider placing the emission system on **low temperature** to maximize the efficiency (COP) of the newly installed heat pump.

*Focus: Reducing operational costs and carbon footprint.*

6.  **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building is a detached house with confirmed **potential for PV**. Since the roof is already **Well Insulated**, it is an ideal candidate for solar panels to offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump.
