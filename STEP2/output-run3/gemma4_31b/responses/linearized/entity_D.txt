### Building Analysis
The building is a modern semi-detached house (constructed in 2017) with an already very high energy performance (EPC score of 87, which falls within the **A label** category). Most building elements are already "Well Insulated," including the walls (front, rear, and left), the floor above the basement, and the flat roof extension. The heating system is already optimized with a heat pump and PV panels.

The primary motivation of the owner is **To Improve EPC**. Given the current state, the marginal gains for energy efficiency are small, but there is one clear area for improvement.

### Proposed Renovation Path

To maximize energy efficiency and further lower the EPC score while remaining cost-effective, the following sequence is proposed:

#### 1. Priority Upgrade: Roof Insulation
*   **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
*   **Reasoning:** The `RoofMain` (Gable Roof) is currently the only major structural element rated as **Moderately Insulated**, whereas all other shells are "Well Insulated." Upgrading this to a "Well Insulated" state is the most impactful step to reduce thermal loss and improve the EPC score.
*   **Practical Note:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation problems.

#### 2. Secondary Upgrade: Glazing Optimization
*   **Action:** `replace glazing`
*   **Reasoning:** The current windows use double glazing and the wooden frames are in a "Good State" (value 8). To further improve the EPC toward an A+ label without the high cost of replacing entire window units, upgrading the glass to **Triple Glazing** is the most cost-effective path.
*   **Practical Note:** Since the frames are in good condition, only the glazing needs replacement, provided the frame thickness can accommodate the triple-pane glass.

### Summary of the Plan

| Sequence | Renovation Action | Target Element | Impact | Cost-Effectiveness |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Gable Roof** | `RoofMain` | High (closes the main thermal gap) | High |
| **2** | **Replace Glazing** | `WindowsBedrooms` / `WindowsLivingArea` | Moderate (reduces radiation loss) | Moderate (glazing only) |

**Excluded Actions:**
*   **Wall/Floor Insulation:** Not required; already "Well Insulated."
*   **Emission/Generation Systems:** Not required; heat pump and PV panels are already installed.
*   **Ventilation:** While `replace ventilation system` is possible, the current EPC is already so low that the investment cost would likely outweigh the energy savings.
