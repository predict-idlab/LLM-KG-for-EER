### Proposed Renovation Path

To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, I propose the following sequence of interventions, ordered by their logical dependency and the ontology's priority ranking.

#### 1. Thermal Envelope Optimization: Gable Roof
*   **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
*   **Justification:** While other areas are "Well Insulated," the main gable roof is only **"Moderately Insulated."** This is the single largest opportunity to reduce thermal bridging and heat loss.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** At **€60/m²**, this is a low-cost intervention with a high impact on the overall energy score.
*   **Point of Attention:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side to prevent condensation.

#### 2. Glazing Upgrade
*   **Action:** `replace glazing`
*   **Justification:** The building currently uses double glazing. Upgrading to triple glazing will further lower the U-value of the windows. 
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** Because the existing wooden window frames are in **"Good State"** (Value: 8), it is significantly more cost-effective to replace only the glazing (**€200/m²**) rather than replacing the entire window unit (**€800/m²**).
*   **Point of Attention:** Verify that the existing wooden frames have the necessary thickness to support heavier triple-pane glass.

#### 3. Operational Efficiency: Solar Protection
*   **Action:** `place sunshade`
*   **Justification:** To prevent overheating during summer months and reduce the energy load on the cooling system, external sunshades should be installed.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** This is a low-cost fixed investment (**€750**) that improves long-term operational performance and thermal comfort.
*   **Point of Attention:** Prioritize outdoor blinds over indoor blinds for maximum efficiency.
