Based on the provided building data and the RENO ontology, the property is a very modern attached building (constructed in 2023) with an already excellent energy performance score (EPC: 50). Most of the building envelope (walls, flat roof, and floor) is already "Well Insulated."

To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the focus should shift from the building envelope (which is nearly optimized) to the **thermal efficiency of the roof** and the **modernization of the heating system**, as the owner has explicitly indicated a reason to change the emission system.

### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Envelope Optimization (Fabric First)
Before upgrading heating systems, we must address the only remaining thermal weakness in the building shell to ensure the new system operates at peak efficiency.
*   **Action: Insulate Gable Roof**
    *   **Reasoning:** While the flat roof is well-insulated, the main gable roof is only "Moderately Insulated." Upgrading this to "Well Insulated" will reduce heat loss through the top of the house.
    *   **Practical Note:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation.
    *   **Cost-Effectiveness:** High. This is a relatively low-cost intervention compared to wall or window replacements.

#### Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Upgrade
The building currently relies on electric convectors, which are generally less efficient than modern heat pump technology. Since there is a stated reason to change the emission system and no potential for geothermal or heat grid connection, air-source technology is the optimal path.

1.  **Action: Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
    *   **Reasoning:** Heat pumps are significantly more efficient when delivering heat at lower temperatures. This step prepares the building for the new generation system.
2.  **Action: Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
    *   **Reasoning:** Replacing electric convectors with an air-water heat pump will drastically reduce the primary energy consumption for space heating.
3.  **Action: Place Heat Pump Boiler**
    *   **Reasoning:** To complete the transition away from inefficient electric heating, a heat pump boiler should be installed to handle domestic hot water efficiently.

### Why other suggestions were excluded:
*   **Walls/Windows/Floor:** These are already "Well Insulated" or were recently renovated (2023). Replacing them would provide negligible energy gains and would not be cost-effective.
*   **PV Panels:** The building already has PV panels and the data indicates there is no further potential for additional installations.
*   **Geothermal/Heat Grid:** The site data explicitly states there is no potential for vertical/horizontal geothermal drilling and no presence of a heat grid.
