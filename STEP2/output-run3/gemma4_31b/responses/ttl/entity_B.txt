### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
*Goal: Stop heat leakage and reduce the overall energy demand of the building.*

1.  **Insulate Flat Roof:** The `RoofExtension` is currently not insulated. This is a priority as heat rises and flat roofs are significant sources of energy loss.
2.  **Insulate Exterior Walls:** Both `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` are full brick and not insulated. Since there is no historical protection, **insulating the walls on the exterior** is the most energy-efficient choice to eliminate thermal bridges.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The `FloorAboveBasement` is not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents cold air from entering the living spaces from below.

### Phase 2: Air Quality & Ventilation (Priority: Medium)
*Goal: Ensure healthy indoor air quality after sealing the building envelope.*

4.  **Replace Ventilation System:** The building currently uses `VentilationSystemA` (natural). Once the envelope is insulated, natural ventilation is often insufficient and can lead to condensation. Upgrading to a mechanical system (e.g., System C or D) is essential for maintaining efficiency and health.

### Phase 3: Heating System Upgrade (Priority: Medium-High)
*Goal: Transition from fossil fuels to sustainable, low-carbon heating.*

5.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** The building currently uses radiators. To maximize the efficiency of a heat pump, the emission system should be upgraded to low-temperature emitters (e.g., underfloor heating or oversized radiators).
6.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since geothermal options are not viable for this site and there is no heat grid, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most cost-effective and efficient replacement for the current boiler.

### Phase 4: Renewable Energy Integration (Priority: Long-term)
*Goal: Offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump.*

7.  **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has high potential for PV. Installing solar panels on the roof will significantly lower operational costs and improve the EPC label further.
