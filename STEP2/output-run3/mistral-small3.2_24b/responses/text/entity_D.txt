   - **Action:** Consider upgrading the existing ventilation system to a more energy-efficient model, such as a heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system if it is not already in place.

   - **Action:** Replace the double-glazed windows with triple-glazed windows or add low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings to the existing double-glazed windows.
   - **Reason:** While the windows are in good condition, upgrading to triple glazing or Low-E coatings can further reduce heat loss through the windows.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness:** Moderate, but the long-term energy savings can justify the cost.
