Given the information provided, here’s a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your detached house in Ghent, Belgium, focusing on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective:

### 1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls at the front, rear, left side, and right side of the house.
   - **Rationale**: Uninsulated cavity walls are a significant source of heat loss. Insulating them will greatly improve the overall energy performance of the house.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: This is a high-impact, cost-effective measure, especially if done in combination with other exterior works.

### 2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space if it is not already insulated.
   - **Rationale**: Crawl spaces can be a source of cold drafts and heat loss. Insulating this floor will improve comfort and reduce heating costs.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: This is a relatively low-cost measure with a good return on investment.

### 3. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - **Action**: Replace the single-glazed windows with double or triple-glazed windows, keeping the existing wooden frames if they are in good condition.
   - **Rationale**: Single glazing is a major source of heat loss. Upgrading to double or triple glazing will significantly improve insulation and reduce heating costs.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: While this is a more expensive measure, it is a long-term investment that will pay off in energy savings.

### 4. **Upgrade the Heating System**
   - **Action**: Replace the old boiler and heating emission systems (radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors) with a modern, energy-efficient system.
   - **Options**:
     - **Heat Pump**: Consider an air-source heat pump, which is suitable for your location and can be combined with underfloor heating or low-temperature radiators.
     - **Condensing Boiler**: If a heat pump is not feasible, a modern condensing boiler is a more efficient alternative to your current boiler.
   - **Rationale**: Upgrading to a more efficient heating system will reduce energy consumption and improve comfort.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: This is a significant investment, but it will lead to long-term energy savings.

### 5. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic panels on the roof to generate renewable energy.
   - **Rationale**: Given the potential for PV panels, this will reduce your reliance on grid electricity and lower your energy bills.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: The initial cost can be high, but there are often subsidies and incentives available for renewable energy installations in Belgium.

### 6. **Improve Natural Ventilation**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the basic natural ventilation system to a more efficient system, such as a heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system.
   - **Rationale**: An HRV system can improve indoor air quality while recovering heat from exhausted air, reducing heating costs.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: This is a more expensive measure, but it can lead to significant energy savings and improved comfort.

### 7. **Seal Air Leaks**
   - **Action**: Seal any air leaks around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Rationale**: Air leaks can account for a significant portion of heat loss. Sealing them will improve the overall energy efficiency of the house.
   - **Cost-Effectiveness**: This is a low-cost measure with immediate benefits.

### **Optimal Sequence of Renovations**
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**: Start with this as it is a high-impact measure and will improve the overall thermal performance of the house.
2. **Insulate the Floor Above the Crawl Space**: This is a relatively quick and low-cost measure that can be done in conjunction with other insulation works.
3. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**: This should be done after insulating the walls to ensure the best possible insulation performance.
4. **Upgrade the Heating System**: This should be done after improving the insulation and windows to ensure the new heating system operates as efficiently as possible.
5. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**: This can be done at any stage, but it is often more practical to do it after other major renovations are complete.
6. **Improve Natural Ventilation**: This can be done in conjunction with other renovations or after they are complete.
7. **Seal Air Leaks**: This should be done throughout the renovation process to ensure all potential air leaks are addressed.

### **Conclusion**
By following this renovation plan, you will significantly improve the energy performance of your house, reduce energy consumption, and lower your long-term operational costs. The proposed measures are practical, cost-effective, and tailored to the specific needs of your detached house in Ghent, Belgium.
