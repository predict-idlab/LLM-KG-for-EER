Given the information provided, here’s a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your home in Ghent, Belgium, focusing on energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness:

### 1. **Replace the Heating System**
   - **Action:** Replace the boiler and heat emission systems (underfloor heating and electric convectors) with a more efficient system.
   - **Options:**
     - **Heat Pump:** Consider an air-source heat pump (ASHP) or a hybrid system (boiler + heat pump) if the heat pump alone cannot meet the demand.
     - **Modern Boiler:** If a heat pump is not feasible, opt for a high-efficiency condensing boiler.
   - **Benefits:** Improved heating efficiency, reduced energy consumption, and better alignment with your photovoltaic panels.
   - **Priority:** High

### 2. **Improve Roof Insulation**
   - **Action:** Enhance the insulation of the main pitched roof, which currently has a moderate level of insulation.
   - **Options:**
     - Add an extra layer of insulation (e.g., mineral wool or rigid foam boards) between the rafters.
     - Consider adding a vapor barrier if necessary.
   - **Benefits:** Reduced heat loss through the roof, improved overall insulation performance.
   - **Priority:** Medium

### 3. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
   - **Action:** Ensure the existing ventilation system (Ventilation System D) is well-maintained and optimized for energy efficiency.
   - **Options:**
     - Regular cleaning and maintenance of the system.
     - Consider upgrading to a heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system if the current system is not efficient.
   - **Benefits:** Improved air quality and reduced energy loss through ventilation.
   - **Priority:** Low-Medium

### 4. **Window Upgrades (If Needed)**
   - **Action:** Although the windows are in good condition and have double glazing, consider upgrading to triple glazing if budget allows.
   - **Benefits:** Further reduction in heat loss through windows.
   - **Priority:** Low

### 5. **Thermal Bridging and Air Sealing**
   - **Action:** Identify and seal any thermal bridges and air leaks, especially around windows, doors, and where the walls meet the roof and floor.
   - **Options:**
     - Use expanding foam, weatherstripping, or specialized sealants.
   - **Benefits:** Reduced drafts and heat loss.
   - **Priority:** Medium

### 6. **Smart Thermostats and Controls**
   - **Action:** Install smart thermostats and programmable controls for the heating system.
   - **Benefits:** Optimized heating schedules, reduced energy waste.
   - **Priority:** Low

1. Replace the heating system (High Priority).
2. Improve roof insulation (Medium Priority).
3. Upgrade ventilation system (Low-Medium Priority).
4. Seal thermal bridges and air leaks (Medium Priority).
5. Consider window upgrades (Low Priority).
6. Install smart thermostats (Low Priority).
