### Renovation Plan for the Detached Building

#### Objective:
To improve the energy performance of the detached building, reduce energy consumption, and enhance thermal comfort, while considering cost-effectiveness and practicality.

#### Current State:
- **Building Type**: Detached
- **Construction Year**: 1862
- **Net Floor Area**: 136.0 m²
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC)**: Energy Score of 165 kWh/m²year
- **Current Heating System**: Electric Convector (Floor Heating)
- **Ventilation System**: Ventilation System A
- **Insulation**: Mostly Not Insulated (Cavity Walls, Attic Floor, and Floor Above Crawl Space)
- **Windows**: Single Glazing with Wooden Frames
- **Roof**: Gable Roof (Well Insulated)
- **Potential for PV Panels**: True
- **Presence of Heat Grid**: False
- **Historical Protection**: None
- **Potential for Geothermal Drilling**: Not Applicable

#### Renovation Priorities and Recommendations:

1. **Replace Gable Roof (Priority: High)**
   - **Reason**: The gable roof is well insulated, but replacing it can improve its energy efficiency further and ensure durability.
   - **Action**: Replace the existing gable roof with a new, more efficient roofing material.
   - **Point of Attention**: Ensure proper installation to prevent condensation issues and install a vapour barrier on the warm side of the insulation.

2. **Insulate Attic Floor (Priority: High)**
   - **Reason**: The attic floor is not insulated, leading to significant heat loss.
   - **Action**: Insulate the attic floor using walkable insulation to ensure durability and effectiveness.
   - **Point of Attention**: Install a vapour barrier to prevent condensation problems.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space (Priority: High)**
   - **Reason**: The floor above the crawl space is not insulated, resulting in heat loss and discomfort.
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space to improve thermal comfort and energy efficiency.

4. **Insulate Cavity Walls (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Reason**: The cavity walls are not insulated, leading to heat loss.
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls by injecting insulation material.
   - **Point of Attention**: Ensure the cavity is wide enough and free from contamination before injecting insulation.

5. **Replace Windows (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Reason**: The current windows have single glazing and wooden frames, which are not energy-efficient.
   - **Action**: Replace the existing windows with double or triple glazed windows with thermally broken frames.
   - **Point of Attention**: Ensure the window frames are strong enough to support the new glass and check that the frames have the right thickness for the new glazing.

6. **Replace Ventilation System (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Reason**: The current ventilation system (Type A) may not be efficient.
   - **Action**: Replace the existing ventilation system with a more efficient system (e.g., Type C) that meets current standards.
   - **Point of Attention**: Ensure the new system is compatible with the building's layout and meets local regulations.

7. **Install PV Panels (Priority: Low)**
   - **Reason**: The building has potential for PV panels, which can generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.
   - **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof or in the garden, depending on the available space and sunlight exposure.
   - **Point of Attention**: Check the building's orientation and ensure the panels are properly secured and connected to the electrical system.

8. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump (Priority: Low)**
   - **Reason**: An air-water heat pump can provide more efficient heating and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing electric convector.
   - **Point of Attention**: Ensure the heat pump is properly sized for the building and meets local regulations.

#### Implementation Sequence:
1. **Roof Replacement and Insulation**: Start with the roof replacement and attic floor insulation to address the most significant heat loss areas.
2. **Wall Insulation and Window Replacement**: Proceed with cavity wall insulation and window replacement to improve overall energy efficiency.
3. **Ventilation System Upgrade**: Replace the ventilation system to ensure proper air circulation and indoor air quality.
4. **Heating System Upgrade**: Install the air-water heat pump to replace the existing heating system.
5. **PV Panel Installation**: Finally, install PV panels to generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.

#### Cost-Effectiveness:
- Prioritize renovations that offer the most significant energy savings and cost benefits.
- Consider the long-term operational performance and potential energy cost reductions when selecting renovation options.
- Ensure that the chosen renovations are compatible with the building's current state and local regulations.

#### Conclusion:
By following this renovation plan, the detached building can achieve improved energy efficiency, reduced energy consumption, and enhanced thermal comfort. The plan is tailored to the building's specific characteristics and grounded in the provided ontology and suggestion list.
