### Renovation Plan for Example Building

#### **Objective:**
Improve the energy efficiency of the building to reduce energy consumption and costs, while enhancing thermal comfort and sustainability.

#### **Current Building Characteristics:**
- **Type:** Attached Building
- **Construction Year:** 2023
- **Net Floor Area:** 159.0 m²
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score:** 50 (E Label)
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Label:** E Label
- **Resident:** Present
- **Potential for PV:** No
- **Potential for Horizontal Geothermal Captation:** No
- **Potential for Vertical Geothermal Drilling:** No
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** No
- **Reason to Change Emission System:** Yes
- **Heat Grid Connection:** No
- **Insulation Grades:**
  - Roof Main: Moderately Insulated
  - Roof Extension: Well Insulated
  - Wall Front: Well Insulated
  - Wall Rear: Well Insulated
  - Floor Above Basement: Well Insulated

#### **Renovation Suggestions:**
1. **Replace Flat Roof**
   - **Action:** Replace the flat roof with a more energy-efficient option.
   - **Reason:** The current flat roof (Roof Extension) is well insulated but replacing it could further improve energy efficiency.
   - **Nuisance Level:** High
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary (Flanders)
   - **Ordering:** 10

2. **Replace Gable Roof**
   - **Action:** Replace the gable roof (Roof Main) with a more energy-efficient option.
   - **Reason:** The current gable roof is moderately insulated and replacing it could improve energy efficiency.
   - **Nuisance Level:** High
   - **Permit Needed:** Only if roof framework is renewed (Flanders)
   - **Ordering:** 13

3. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Action:** Improve the insulation of the gable roof.
   - **Reason:** The current gable roof is moderately insulated and further insulation could enhance thermal comfort.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary (Flanders)
   - **Ordering:** 28

4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action:** Improve the insulation of the floor above the basement.
   - **Reason:** The current floor above the basement is well insulated but further insulation could enhance thermal comfort.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary (Flanders)
   - **Ordering:** 49

5. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing ventilation system (Ventilation System D) with a more efficient system.
   - **Reason:** Upgrading the ventilation system could improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary if the above-ground parts are at least 2m away from the parcel boundary (Flanders)
   - **Ordering:** 52

6. **Place Air-Water Heatpump**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Reason:** Replacing the current emission system (Electric Convector) with an air-water heat pump could reduce energy consumption.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary if the above-ground parts are at least 2m away from the parcel boundary (Flanders)
   - **Ordering:** 67

7. **Place Sunshade**
   - **Action:** Install sunshades to reduce solar heat gain.
   - **Reason:** Sunshades can help reduce cooling needs in summer, improving energy efficiency.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary (Flanders)
   - **Ordering:** 79

8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof.
   - **Reason:** PV panels can generate renewable energy, reducing the building's reliance on the grid.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Permit Needed:** Not necessary if the panels are in the same plane (Flanders)
   - **Ordering:** 85

#### **Implementation Sequence:**
1. **Initial Assessment and Planning**
   - Conduct a detailed energy audit to identify specific areas for improvement.
   - Develop a detailed cost-benefit analysis for each suggested renovation.

2. **Permits and Approvals**
   - Obtain necessary permits for renovations requiring approval.

3. **Renovation Execution**
   - Follow the suggested ordering to maximize the benefits of each renovation step.

4. **Post-Renovation Evaluation**
   - Conduct a post-renovation energy audit to assess the improvements.
   - Monitor energy consumption and costs to ensure the expected benefits are achieved.

#### **Expected Benefits:**
- **Energy Efficiency:** Improved insulation and replacement of outdated systems will reduce energy consumption.
- **Cost Savings:** Lower energy bills due to reduced energy consumption.
- **Thermal Comfort:** Enhanced insulation and upgraded heating systems will improve indoor comfort.
- **Sustainability:** Installation of renewable energy sources like PV panels will reduce the building's carbon footprint.

By following this renovation plan, the building can achieve significant improvements in energy efficiency, cost savings, and sustainability.
