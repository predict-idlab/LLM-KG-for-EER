### Renovation Plan for Building at http://example.com/Building

#### **Objective:**
Improve the Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) score of the dwelling.

#### **Current State:**
- **Building:** Attached, constructed in 1832, located in Belgium (Postal Code: 9000).
- **EPC Score:** 262 kWh/m²year (D label).
- **Key Elements:**
  - **Roof Main (Gable Roof):** Moderately insulated.
  - **Roof Extension (Flat Roof):** Not insulated.
  - **Walls (Front and Rear):** Full brick walls, not insulated.
  - **Windows:**
    - Bedrooms: Double glazing, wooden frames.
    - Living Area: Double glazing, wooden frames.
  - **Emission System:** Radiator.
  - **Generation System:** Boiler.
  - **Ventilation System:** Type A (natural air supply and air exhaust).
  - **Potential for PV Panels:** Yes.
  - **Potential for Geothermal Drilling:** No.
  - **Heat Grid Availability:** No.

#### **Renovation Actions:**

1. **Insulate Flat Roof (Replace Flat Roof)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the flat roof extension.
   - **Reason:** The flat roof is currently not insulated, contributing to higher energy consumption.
   - **Estimated Cost:** €50 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level:** High.
   - **Permit Required:** Only if the thickness of the total pack > 26 cm.

2. **Insulate Gable Roof (Insulate Gable Roof)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the main gable roof.
   - **Reason:** The gable roof is moderately insulated but could benefit from further insulation to reduce heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost:** €60 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low.
   - **Permit Required:** Not necessary.

3. **Insulate Wall on Exterior (Insulate Wall on Exterior)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the exterior walls (front and rear).
   - **Reason:** Both walls are not insulated, leading to significant heat loss.
   - **Estimated Cost:** €200 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level:** High.
   - **Permit Required:** Only if the thickness of the total pack > 26 cm or the building line is overrun.

4. **Replace Glazing (Replace Glazing)**
   - **Action:** Replace the glazing in all windows with more energy-efficient options (e.g., triple glazing).
   - **Reason:** Current double glazing, while better than single glazing, could be upgraded for better thermal performance.
   - **Estimated Cost:** €200 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level:** High.
   - **Permit Required:** Not necessary.

5. **Place PV Panels on Roof (Place PV-panels on roof)**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof.
   - **Reason:** The building has potential for PV panels, and the roof is suitable for installation.
   - **Estimated Cost:** €275 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low.
   - **Permit Required:** Not necessary if the panels are in the same plane.

6. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump (Place air-water heatpump)**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump for space heating.
   - **Reason:** The current emission system (radiator) could be replaced with a more energy-efficient system.
   - **Estimated Cost:** €8000 fixed price + €35 per m².
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate.
   - **Permit Required:** Not necessary if the above-ground parts are at least 2m away from the parcel boundary.

7. **Place Sun Shade (Place sunshade)**
   - **Action:** Install sun shades or blinds.
   - **Reason:** To reduce solar heat gain and improve thermal comfort.
   - **Estimated Cost:** €750 fixed price.
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low.
   - **Permit Required:** Not necessary.

#### **Expected Benefits:**
- **Energy Efficiency:** Significant reduction in energy consumption and improvement in the EPC score.
- **Comfort:** Enhanced thermal comfort through better insulation and improved heating system.
- **Sustainability:** Reduced carbon footprint through renewable energy generation (PV panels) and efficient use of energy.

#### **Priority Order:**
1. Insulate Flat Roof (High Impact, High Nuisance).
2. Insulate Gable Roof (Moderate Impact, Low Nuisance).
3. Insulate Wall on Exterior (High Impact, High Nuisance).
4. Replace Glazing (Moderate Impact, High Nuisance).
5. Place PV Panels on Roof (Moderate Impact, Low Nuisance).
6. Place Air-Water Heat Pump (Moderate Impact, Moderate Nuisance).
7. Place Sun Shade (Low Impact, Low Nuisance).

#### **Next Steps:**
- **Consultation:** Engage with a certified energy expert to assess the feasibility and cost of the proposed actions.
- **Permits:** Check local regulations and obtain necessary permits for high-impact actions.
- **Execution:** Plan and execute the renovations in the prioritized order.

This plan aims to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective and actionable within the given constraints.
