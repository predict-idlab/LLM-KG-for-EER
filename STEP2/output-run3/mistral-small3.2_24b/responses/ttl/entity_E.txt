### Energy-Efficient and Cost-Effective Renovation Plan

#### **1. Insulate Walls (Cavity & Interior)**
- **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Left, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right) using cavity insulation.
- **Rationale**: The walls are currently not insulated (reno:NotInsulated), which is a significant source of heat loss. Cavity wall insulation is cost-effective and has a low nuisance level.
- **Order**: High priority (ordering 34).

#### **2. Replace Single-Glazed Windows with Double or Triple Glazing**
- **Action**: Replace the single-glazed windows (ex:WindowsBedrooms, ex:WindowsLivingArea) with double or triple glazing.
- **Rationale**: Single glazing has poor thermal performance. Upgrading to double or triple glazing will improve insulation and reduce heat loss.
- **Order**: Medium priority (ordering 43 for glazing, 46 for windows).

#### **3. Insulate the Attic Floor**
- **Action**: Insulate the attic floor (ex:FloorAboveCrawlSpace) to reduce heat loss through the roof.
- **Rationale**: The attic floor is currently not insulated, and insulating it will improve overall energy efficiency.
- **Order**: High priority (ordering 31).

#### **4. Upgrade the Ventilation System**
- **Action**: Replace the existing natural ventilation system (VentilationSystemA) with a more efficient system (e.g., VentilationSystemB or VentilationSystemD).
- **Rationale**: A more efficient ventilation system will improve air quality and energy efficiency.
- **Order**: Medium priority (ordering 52).

#### **5. Install Solar Panels (PV-Panels) on the Roof**
- **Action**: Place PV-panels on the roof (ex:RoofMain) if the roof is suitably oriented and unshaded.
- **Rationale**: Solar panels can generate renewable energy, reducing reliance on grid electricity.
- **Order**: Low priority (ordering 85).

#### **6. Consider a Heat Pump**
- **Action**: Evaluate the feasibility of installing an air-water heat pump or a ground-source heat pump (vertical or horizontal) to replace the existing emission system (ex:EmissionSystem).
- **Rationale**: Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than conventional heating systems.
- **Order**: Medium priority (ordering 67 for air-water heat pump, 61 for vertical geothermal heat pump).

#### **7. Place a Sunshade**
- **Action**: Install a sunshade to reduce solar heat gain in summer.
- **Rationale**: Sunshades can help regulate indoor temperatures and reduce cooling costs.
- **Order**: Low priority (ordering 79).

### **Implementation Sequence**
1. **Insulate Walls** (Cavity & Interior)
2. **Replace Windows** (Glazing & Frames)
3. **Insulate Attic Floor**
4. **Upgrade Ventilation System**
5. **Install Solar Panels** (if feasible)
6. **Consider Heat Pump**
7. **Place Sunshade**

### **Additional Notes**
- **Asbestos**: Ensure that any asbestos-containing materials are safely removed before insulation or roof work.
- **Permits**: Check local regulations for permits, especially for external wall insulation and roof work.
- **Budget**: Prioritize actions based on cost-effectiveness and energy savings.

This plan focuses on maximizing energy efficiency while being mindful of cost and practicality. The sequence ensures that each step builds on the previous one for optimal results.
