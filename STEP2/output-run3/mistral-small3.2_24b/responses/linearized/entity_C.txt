### **Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency & Cost-Effectiveness**

#### **1. Assess Current State & Prioritize Upgrades**
- **Current EPC Score**: 50 (poor energy performance)
- **Primary Goal**: Improve EPC to meet higher standards (e.g., B-label)
- **Key Weaknesses**:
  - **Roof Insulation**: Moderately insulated (RoofMain)
  - **Windows**: Double-glazed (acceptable but can be upgraded)
  - **Ventilation System**: Type D (inefficient)
  - **Heating System**: Electric convector + floor heating (energy-intensive)

#### **2. Proposed Upgrades (Ordered by Priority)**
| **Upgrade**                     | **Justification**                                                                 | **Cost (Estimate)**       |
|----------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------|
| **Insulate Flat Roof**           | RoofExtension is well-insulated, but RoofMain is only moderately insulated.          | €50/m²                    |
| **Insulate Gable Roof**          | RoofMain is moderately insulated; upgrading to well-insulated improves thermal comfort. | €60/m²                    |
| **Replace Glazing**              | Upgrade double-glazed windows to triple-glazed for better insulation.                | €200/m²                   |
| **Replace Ventilation System**    | VentilationSystemD is inefficient; upgrade to a more efficient system (e.g., A or B). | €17/m² + €3,500 fixed cost |
| **Install Air-Water Heatpump**   | Replace electric convector with a heatpump for space heating and water heating.      | €8,000                    |
| **Install PV Panels on Roof**    | Generate renewable energy to offset consumption.                                     | €275/m²                   |
| **Place Sunshade**               | Reduce solar heat gain in summer.                                                     | €750 fixed cost            |

#### **3. Step-by-Step Implementation**
1. **Insulate Roofs (Flat & Gable)**
   - Hire a contractor to insulate RoofMain and RoofExtension.
   - Ensure proper vapour barrier installation to prevent condensation.
   - **Estimated Time**: 2–3 weeks.

2. **Upgrade Windows (Replace Glazing)**
   - Replace double-glazed windows with triple-glazed ones for improved insulation.
   - **Estimated Time**: 1–2 weeks per room.

3. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - Upgrade to a more efficient system (e.g., VentilationSystemA or B).
   - **Estimated Time**: 1–2 weeks.

4. **Install Air-Water Heatpump**
   - Replace electric convector with a heatpump for space heating and water heating.
   - **Estimated Time**: 1–2 weeks.

5. **Install PV Panels on Roof**
   - Assess roof orientation and install solar panels for renewable energy generation.
   - **Estimated Time**: 1 week.

6. **Place Sunshade**
   - Install external blinds or shades to reduce solar heat gain in summer.
   - **Estimated Time**: 1 day.

#### **4. Expected Benefits**
- **Energy Efficiency**: Improved insulation, efficient heating, and renewable energy generation.
- **Cost Savings**: Reduced electricity bills and lower operational costs.
- **Comfort**: Better thermal comfort and indoor air quality.

#### **5. Long-Term Maintenance**
- Regularly maintain insulation, windows, and the ventilation system.
- Monitor PV panel performance and clean them periodically.

This plan ensures a **cost-effective**, **energy-efficient** renovation while improving the EPC score significantly.
