### Renovation Plan for the Attached Building

**Objective:** Improve the energy efficiency of the attached building by implementing cost-effective and practical upgrades.

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Attached building
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **Net Floor Area:** 212 m²
- **Current EPC Score:** 262 kWh/m²year (C label)
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Heat Grid Availability:** No
- **Resident:** Present
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve EPC

**Key Areas for Renovation:**

1. **Roofing:**
   - **Roof Extension (Flat Roof):**
     - **Current State:** Not insulated
     - **Suggestion:** Insulate the flat roof (ordering: 25)
     - **Reasoning:** Insulating the roof will significantly reduce heat loss and improve overall energy efficiency.
   - **Roof Main (Gable Roof):**
     - **Current State:** Moderately insulated
     - **Suggestion:** No immediate action required, but consider future insulation upgrades if further efficiency improvements are needed.

2. **Walls:**
   - **Wall Front and Wall Rear (Full Brick Walls):**
     - **Current State:** Not insulated
     - **Suggestion:** Insulate the walls on the interior (ordering: 37)
     - **Reasoning:** Insulating the walls will reduce heat loss through the building envelope and improve thermal comfort.

3. **Windows:**
   - **Windows in Bedrooms and Living Area:**
     - **Current State:** Double glazing with wooden frames in good condition
     - **Suggestion:** No immediate action required, but consider replacing the glazing with triple glazing for further efficiency improvements if budget allows.

4. **Floor:**
   - **Floor Above Basement:**
     - **Current State:** Not insulated
     - **Suggestion:** Insulate the floor above the basement (ordering: 49)
     - **Reasoning:** Insulating the floor will prevent heat loss to the basement and improve overall energy efficiency.

5. **Ventilation System:**
   - **Current State:** Ventilation System A (natural air supply and air exhaust)
   - **Suggestion:** Consider upgrading to a more efficient ventilation system (e.g., Ventilation System B or C) if further efficiency improvements are needed.

6. **Emission System:**
   - **Current State:** Radiator
   - **Suggestion:** Replace with a more efficient emission system, such as floor heating or a low-temperature emission system (ordering: 70)
   - **Reasoning:** Upgrading the emission system will improve heat distribution and reduce energy consumption.

7. **Heating System:**
   - **Current State:** Boiler (type not specified)
   - **Suggestion:** Consider replacing the boiler with a more efficient heat pump (e.g., air-water heat pump or vertical geothermal heat pump) if further efficiency improvements are needed.

8. **Renewable Energy:**
   - **Potential for PV:** Yes
   - **Suggestion:** Install PV panels on the roof (ordering: 85)
   - **Reasoning:** Utilizing renewable energy will reduce reliance on conventional energy sources and lower energy costs.

**Priority Order of Renovations:**
1. Insulate the flat roof (Roof Extension)
2. Insulate the walls on the interior (Wall Front and Wall Rear)
3. Insulate the floor above the basement (Floor Above Basement)
4. Upgrade the emission system (e.g., floor heating or low-temperature emission system)
5. Install PV panels on the roof
6. Consider upgrading the ventilation system (if needed)
7. Consider replacing the boiler with a more efficient heat pump (if needed)

**Budget Considerations:**
- Prioritize renovations based on cost-effectiveness and energy savings.
- Consider the potential return on investment for each renovation action.

**Next Steps:**
- Obtain detailed cost estimates for each suggested renovation.
- Consult with a professional to assess the feasibility and potential energy savings of each renovation.
- Develop a phased renovation plan to minimize disruption to the resident.

This plan provides a structured approach to improving the energy efficiency of the attached building while considering practical and cost-effective solutions.
