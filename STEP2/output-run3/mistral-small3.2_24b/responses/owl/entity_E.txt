### Renovation Plan

**Objective:** Improve the energy performance of the building to achieve a better EPC label.

**Current Status:**
- **Building Type:** Detached
- **Construction Year:** 1862
- **Current EPC Score:** 165 (F label)
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Heat Grid Availability:** No
- **Insulation Grade:**
  - Roof: Well insulated
  - Walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right): Not insulated
  - Floor Above Crawl Space: Not protected
- **Ventilation System:** Type A (natural air supply and exhaust)
- **Heating System:** Air heating (electric convector, floor heating, radiator)
- **Windows:**
  - Bedrooms: Single glazing, wooden frame in good condition
  - Living Area: Single glazing, wooden frame in good condition

**Renovation Steps:**

1. **Insulate Walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right):**
   - **Action:** Insulate wall cavity
   - **Reasoning:** The walls are currently not insulated, which significantly contributes to the poor energy performance.
   - **Ordering:** 34 (based on the suggestion list)

2. **Replace Glazing:**
   - **Action:** Replace glazing
   - **Reasoning:** Single glazing has poor thermal and acoustic properties. Replacing it with double or triple glazing will improve insulation.
   - **Ordering:** 43

3. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Replace ventilation system
   - **Reasoning:** Upgrading to a more efficient ventilation system (e.g., Type B or C) will improve air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Ordering:** 52

4. **Install PV Panels:**
   - **Action:** Place PV-panels on roof
   - **Reasoning:** The building has potential for PV installation, which can reduce energy consumption and costs.
   - **Ordering:** 85

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Place air-water heatpump
   - **Reasoning:** Replacing the current electric heating system with a more efficient heat pump will reduce energy consumption and improve thermal comfort.
   - **Ordering:** 67

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Place emission system on low temperature
   - **Reasoning:** This will ensure that the heating system operates more efficiently.
   - **Ordering:** 70

**Sequence and Dependencies:**
- The insulation of walls should be done first to improve the thermal envelope of the building.
- Replacing the glazing should follow to further enhance insulation.
- The ventilation system upgrade should be done before installing the PV panels to ensure compatibility.
- The heat pump installation should be done after ensuring the building envelope is well-insulated.

**Additional Considerations:**
- **Permits:** Check local regulations for any necessary permits, especially for structural changes.
- **Historical Protection:** Ensure that no elements are historically protected before making changes.
- **Cost and Budget:** Estimate costs for each step and ensure they fit within the renovation budget.

This plan aims to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective and practical. The steps are ordered to ensure that foundational improvements (insulation, glazing) are completed before more advanced systems (PV panels, heat pump) are installed.
