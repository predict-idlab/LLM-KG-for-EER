### Renovation Plan

**Objective:** Improve the energy performance of the semi-detached building by implementing cost-effective and energy-efficient upgrades.

#### **1. Insulate the Roof**
- **Suggestion:** Insulate the gable roof
- **Reasoning:** The gable roof is currently moderately insulated. Improving its insulation will enhance thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
- **Action:** Apply insulation on the gable roof, ensuring a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation issues.

#### **2. Upgrade the Ventilation System**
- **Suggestion:** Replace the ventilation system
- **Reasoning:** The current ventilation system (type D) may not be optimal for energy efficiency. Upgrading to a more efficient system can improve air quality and reduce energy loss.
- **Action:** Install a ventilation system that balances air supply and exhaust efficiently, considering the building's layout and occupancy.

#### **3. Optimize Window Glazing**
- **Suggestion:** Replace the glazing
- **Reasoning:** The windows in the bedrooms and living area have double glazing, which is good but may benefit from upgrading to triple glazing for better thermal performance.
- **Action:** Replace the double glazing with triple glazing in all windows, ensuring the window frames are in good condition and can support the new glazing.

#### **4. Enhance Roof Insulation**
- **Suggestion:** Insulate the flat roof
- **Reasoning:** The flat roof (extension) is well insulated, but adding an additional layer of insulation can further improve its thermal performance.
- **Action:** Apply an inverted roof structure with insulation on top of the roof covering to enhance its insulation properties.

#### **5. Consider Solar Panel Installation**
- **Suggestion:** Place PV-panels on the roof
- **Reasoning:** The building has potential for solar panel installation, which can generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.
- **Action:** Install photovoltaic panels on the roof, ensuring they are properly oriented and capture sufficient sunlight. Remove any asbestos present in the roof first.

#### **6. Evaluate Heating System**
- **Suggestion:** Place an emission system on low temperature
- **Reasoning:** The current heating system is a heat pump, which is energy-efficient. Ensuring the emission system is compatible with the supply and return temperatures of the heat pump can optimize performance.
- **Action:** Verify the compatibility of the emission system with the heat pump and make adjustments if necessary.

#### **7. Additional Considerations**
- **Monitoring and Maintenance:** Regularly monitor the performance of the new insulation and ventilation systems to ensure they operate efficiently.
- **Energy Audit:** Conduct periodic energy audits to track improvements in energy consumption and identify further optimization opportunities.

By following this plan, the building's energy efficiency will be significantly improved, leading to lower energy costs and a more comfortable living environment.
