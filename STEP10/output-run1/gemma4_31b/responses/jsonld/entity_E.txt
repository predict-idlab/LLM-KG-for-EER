### Proposed Renovation Path

The goal is to minimize heat loss through the building's shell. This is the most cost-effective way to reduce long-term operational costs.

Injecting insulation into the cavity is the most cost-effective and least disruptive method to significantly reduce wall heat loss.

Since the window frames are in a **Good State** (value 8), it is not cost-effective to replace the entire window. Instead, only the glazing should be replaced with double or triple glazing.

Improving the envelope and glazing seals the building, which can lead to moisture issues and poor air quality if ventilation is not addressed.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** The building currently uses **Ventilation System A** (natural). Upgrading to a more modern system (such as System C or D) ensures a healthy indoor climate without losing the thermal gains achieved in Phase 1.
