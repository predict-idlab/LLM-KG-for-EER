### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (1862, 136 m², EPC Score: 165)

#### Current State:
- **Building Type**: Detached (standalone structure)
- **Construction Year**: 1862 (likely pre-existing insulation standards)
- **EPC Score**: 165 (B Label) – already relatively efficient, but further improvements are possible.
- **Heating System**: Combination of radiators, electric convectors, and floor heating.
- **Insulation**:
  - **Roof**: Well-insulated (good condition).
  - **Walls**: All four walls (Front, Left, Rear, Right) are cavity walls and currently not insulated.
  - **Floor**: Floor above crawl space is uninsulated.
  - **Windows**: Single glazing with wooden frames (frames are in good condition but glazing is outdated).

#### Key Observations:
1. **High Potential for PV**: The building has confirmed potential for photovoltaic (PV) panels, making it a good candidate for renewable energy generation.
2. **Presence of Heat Grid**: No heat grid available, so alternatives like heat pumps are viable.
3. **Ventilation System**: Currently a basic ventilation system (System A), which may need upgrading after sealing the envelope.
4. **Historical Protection**: No historical protection constraints on walls, allowing for insulation upgrades.

#### Proposed Renovation Path:

##### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
The primary goal is to reduce heat loss through the building envelope, which is currently the weakest link in energy efficiency.

1. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
   - **Walls**: All four walls (Front, Left, Rear, Right) are cavity walls and uninsulated. Insulating these will significantly reduce heat loss.
   - **Method**: Inject insulation into the cavity. This is cost-effective and non-disruptive compared to exterior or interior insulation.
   - **Cost**: ~€22/m² (based on the ontology).

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - The floor above the crawl space is currently uninsulated. Adding insulation here will prevent heat loss to the crawl space.
   - **Method**: Install insulation on the underside of the floor.
   - **Cost**: ~€30/m².

3. **Replace Glazing**
   - The windows currently have single glazing, which is highly inefficient. Replacing this with double or triple glazing will improve thermal performance.
   - **Method**: Retrofit double or triple glazing into existing wooden frames (frames are in good condition).
   - **Cost**: ~€200/m².

##### Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade (Priority: Medium)
With a sealed envelope, the building's heat demand is now lower, allowing for a more efficient heating system.

4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - The current heating system includes electric convectors and radiators, which are inefficient. An air-water heat pump is a sustainable and efficient alternative.
   - **Method**: Install a heat pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Cost**: Fixed price of €8,000 + €35/m² (based on the ontology).

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - Before installing the heat pump, transition the existing radiators to low-temperature emissions to maximize the efficiency of the heat pump.
   - **Cost**: ~€100/m².

##### Phase 3: Renewable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
To offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and further improve the EPC label.

6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide renewable electricity for the heat pump and other appliances.
   - **Method**: Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Cost**: ~€275/m².

##### Phase 4: Ventilation Upgrade (Priority: Medium)
After sealing the building envelope, a more efficient ventilation system is necessary to maintain air quality.

7. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., System C or D) to ensure proper air exchange and moisture control.
   - **Cost**: Fixed price of €3,500 + €17/m².

#### Summary of Optimal Sequence:
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Wall Cavity** | Reduces heat loss through external walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Prevents heat loss to the crawl space. |
| **3** | **Replace Glazing** | Improves thermal performance of windows. |
| **4** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | Replaces inefficient heating systems with a sustainable alternative. |
| **5** | **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** | Maximizes heat pump efficiency. |
| **6** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Offsets electricity consumption and improves EPC label. |
| **7** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Ensures proper air quality in a sealed envelope. |

This plan follows the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** sequence, ensuring that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before upgrading heating systems and installing renewable energy sources. This approach maximizes energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective.
