### Renovation Plan for the Provided Building

#### Building Overview:
- **Type:** Attached building
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **EPC Score:** 262.0 (C Label)
- **Potential for PV:** True
- **Heat Grid Availability:** False
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve EPC
- **Current Issues:**
  - Uninsulated walls (Front and Rear)
  - Uninsulated floor above basement
  - Uninsulated flat roof
  - Moderately insulated gable roof
  - Double glazing (already in place)
  - Good state of window frames
  - Ventilation system type A (natural)

#### Renovation Strategy:
The strategy follows the "Insulate → Ventilate → Generate" approach, focusing on cost-effective measures that maximize energy efficiency.

#### Proposed Renovation Path:

1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior:**
   - **Action:** Insulate Wall_Front and Wall_Rear on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** These walls are full brick and not insulated. Exterior insulation is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges and improve the building's thermal envelope.
   - **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in heat loss through walls.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the FloorAboveBasement.
   - **Rationale:** The floor is currently not insulated, leading to heat loss to the basement. Insulating it will prevent cold drafts and improve thermal comfort.
   - **Expected Impact:** Improved thermal comfort and reduced heat loss.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the RoofExtension (flat roof).
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof is currently not insulated, and heat rises rapidly through flat sections. Insulating it will significantly reduce heat loss.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduced heat loss through the roof.

4. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., System C or D).
   - **Rationale:** With the building envelope sealed and insulated, a modern ventilation system is necessary to prevent moisture buildup and maintain air quality.
   - **Expected Impact:** Improved indoor air quality and comfort.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an Air-Water Heat Pump.
   - **Rationale:** The building has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid. An Air-Water Heat Pump is a sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing boiler.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduced energy consumption and improved heating efficiency.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Transition the current radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Before installing a heat pump, the current radiators should be adapted to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new generator.
   - **Expected Impact:** Improved efficiency of the new heating system.

7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduced reliance on grid electricity and lower energy costs.

#### Summary of Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
4. **Replace Ventilation System**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

This plan ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized first, followed by upgrades to the heating and ventilation systems, and finally the integration of renewable energy sources. This approach maximizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness while improving the overall EPC rating.
