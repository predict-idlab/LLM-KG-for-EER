### Renovation Plan for the Given Entity

**Building Summary:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached building (173 m²)
- **Construction Year:** 2017
- **Current EPC Score:** 87 (A Label)
- **Motivation:** To improve EPC (already excellent, but potential for optimization)
- **Key Elements:**
  - Well-insulated walls (Front, Left, Rear)
  - Well-insulated flat roof and floor above basement
  - Double-glazed windows (already in good condition)
  - Existing heat pump and ventilation system (Ventilation System D)

**Key Observations:**
1. The building is already well-insulated (ModeratelyInsulated to WellInsulated elements), so the primary focus should be on optimizing the existing systems.
2. The double-glazed windows are in good condition, so replacement is unnecessary.
3. The current EPC score is already excellent (87), but further improvements can be made through system optimizations.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

### Phase 1: System Optimization (Priority: High)
Since the building envelope is already well-insulated, the focus shifts to optimizing energy systems for efficiency and sustainability.

1. **Upgrade Ventilation System:**
   - The current **Ventilation System D** is efficient but could be fine-tuned for better energy recovery.
   - *Action:* **Replace Ventilation System** (e.g., upgrade to a more efficient model with higher heat recovery).

2. **Heat Pump Optimization:**
   - The existing heat pump is functional, but its efficiency can be improved.
   - *Action:* **Place Air-Water Heat Pump with Cooling** (if cooling is needed) or **Place Geothermal Heat Pump** (if feasible, though potential is "false" for this site).

3. **Solar Energy Integration:**
   - The building has PV panels, but their efficiency can be maximized.
   - *Action:* **Place Plug-and-Play PV Panels** (if additional panels are needed) or **Optimize Existing PV System** (e.g., cleaning, angle adjustment).

### Phase 2: Low-Temperature Emission (Priority: Medium)
To further improve the EPC score and energy efficiency, consider upgrading the emission system to operate at lower temperatures, which pairs well with heat pumps.

4. **Upgrade Emission System:**
   - *Action:* **Place Low-Temperature Emission System** (e.g., underfloor heating or low-temperature radiators) to work in tandem with the heat pump.

### Phase 3: Monitoring and Maintenance (Priority: Long-term)
To ensure sustained energy efficiency, regular maintenance and monitoring are essential.

5. **Monitoring System:**
   - *Action:* **Install Smart Energy Monitoring System** to track energy consumption and system performance in real time.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence:
1. **Upgrade Ventilation System** (for better heat recovery)
2. **Optimize Heat Pump** (e.g., place Air-Water Heat Pump with Cooling)
3. **Solar Energy Integration** (e.g., Plug-and-Play PV Panels)
4. **Low-Temperature Emission Upgrade** (for better heat pump efficiency)
5. **Smart Monitoring System** (for long-term efficiency tracking)

This plan ensures that the building remains energy-efficient while improving the EPC score further. The focus is on system optimization rather than structural changes, as the building envelope is already well-insulated.
