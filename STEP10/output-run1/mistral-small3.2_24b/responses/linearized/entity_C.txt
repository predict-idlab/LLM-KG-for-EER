### Analysis of the Building

The building is an attached house constructed in 2023 with the following key characteristics:
- **EPC Score**: 50 (A+ Label, excellent energy performance)
- **Insulation**: Most elements are already well-insulated (WellInsulated grade for walls, roof, and floor)
- **Windows**: Double-glazed with wooden frames in good condition
- **Heating System**: Electric convector and floor heating
- **Ventilation**: System D (mechanical supply and exhaust)
- **PV Potential**: No potential for photovoltaic panels
- **Geothermal Potential**: No potential for vertical or horizontal geothermal drilling
- **Heat Grid**: No presence of a heat grid

### Current State Assessment
The building is already in an excellent energy-efficient state (A+ EPC). The primary goal should be to maintain or potentially improve this level of efficiency while addressing any remaining optimization opportunities.

### Renovation Plan

Given the already high level of insulation and efficient systems, the renovation focus should be on:
1. **Maintaining System Efficiency**
2. **Addressing Potential Wear-and-Tear**
3. **Exploring Long-term Optimization**

#### Phase 1: System Maintenance and Optimization
1. **Regular Maintenance of Ventilation System D**
   - Ensure the mechanical supply and exhaust system is functioning optimally
   - Clean filters and ducts as needed

2. **Heating System Check-Up**
   - Inspect the electric convector and floor heating systems for optimal performance
   - Consider upgrading to more efficient models if necessary

#### Phase 2: Long-term Optimization
1. **Window Frame Refresh**
   - Although the wooden frames are in good condition, consider refreshing the frames to extend their lifespan and maintain thermal performance

2. **Roof Maintenance**
   - Inspect the roof underlayment and ensure it remains air- and watertight
   - Consider additional roof underlayment if signs of wear are detected

3. **Insulation Verification**
   - Periodically check the insulation of walls, roof, and floor to ensure no degradation over time

### Summary of Recommendations
| Action | Rationale |
|--------|-----------|
| **Regular Ventilation Maintenance** | Maintain optimal air quality and system efficiency |
| **Heating System Check-Up** | Ensure continued high energy performance |
| **Window Frame Refresh (Long-term)** | Extend lifespan and maintain thermal performance |

### Implementation Notes
- **Priority**: Immediate focus on maintenance of existing systems
- **Long-term**: Plan for periodic upgrades to maintain the excellent energy performance
- **Cost-Effectiveness**: Emphasize maintaining current systems rather than major renovations

This plan ensures the building retains its high energy efficiency while addressing potential future degradation in performance.
