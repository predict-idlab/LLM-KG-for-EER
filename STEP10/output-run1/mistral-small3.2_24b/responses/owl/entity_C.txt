### Renovation Plan:

The building is a relatively new attached building (2023) with a very good EPC score of 50 (A Label). The owner's primary motivation is to improve the EPC rating.

Given the already high EPC score and the owner's motivation, the focus should be on maintaining and slightly improving the energy performance.

Proposed Renovation Path:

Phase 1: Maintenance and Minor Upgrades (Priority: High)
1. **Insulate Flat Roof:** The `RoofExtension` is already well insulated, but ensuring its maintenance is crucial to prevent heat loss over time.
2. **Insulate Gable Roof:** The `RoofMain` is moderately insulated. Upgrading it to well-insulated status will further improve thermal retention.
3. **Maintain Existing Insulation:** Regularly check and maintain the insulation of `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, and `FloorAboveBasement` to ensure they remain well-insulated.

Phase 2: Heating System Optimization (Priority: Medium)
4. **Replace Ventilation System:** The current `VentilationSystem` (System D) is efficient, but upgrading to a more advanced system (e.g., System C) could enhance indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
5. **Consider Heat Pump:** If the owner is open to more significant changes, replacing the existing boiler with a **Air-Water Heat Pump** could further reduce energy consumption. However, this is optional given the current high EPC score.

Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Low)
6. **Evaluate PV Potential:** Although the current potential for PV is "false," reconsidering PV installation in the future could provide renewable energy for the building.

Summary of Sequence:
**Roof Maintenance** $\rightarrow$ **Ventilation Upgrade** $\rightarrow$ **Optional Heat Pump** $\rightarrow$ **Future PV Consideration**.
