To create an effective and cost-efficient renovation plan, we'll follow a "fabric first" approach, addressing the building envelope before upgrading heating systems or adding renewable energy sources.

### Step 1: Analyze the Building
- **Building Type**: Semi-detached house from 1951
- **Current EPC Score**: 388 (D Label)
- **Potential for PV**: True
- **Presence of Heat Grid**: False
- **Potential for Geothermal**: False
- **Owner's Motivation**: Improve EPC

### Step 2: Identify Key Issues
- **Walls**: All three walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`) are full brick and not insulated.
- **Roof**: The gable roof (`RoofMain`) is not insulated, while the flat roof (`RoofExtension`) is only moderately insulated.
- **Floor**: The floor above the basement (`FloorAboveBasement`) is not insulated.
- **Windows**: Windows have double glazing and are in good condition.
- **Ventilation System**: Currently uses Ventilation System A (natural).

### Step 3: Proposed Renovation Path
#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
   - **Why**: Since the walls are full brick and not cavity walls, exterior insulation is the most effective method.
   - **Suggestion**: Use `Insulate Wall On Exterior` (Order: 40).
   - **Considerations**: This will eliminate thermal bridges and significantly reduce heat loss.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Why**: The floor above the basement is currently not insulated, leading to heat loss to the basement.
   - **Suggestion**: Use `Insulate Floor Above Basement` (Order: 49).
   - **Considerations**: This will prevent cold drafts from the basement and improve overall comfort.

3. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Why**: The gable roof is currently not insulated, which is a significant source of heat loss.
   - **Suggestion**: Use `Insulate Gable Roof` (Order: 28).
   - **Considerations**: Condensation problems can occur without a vapour barrier, so proper installation is crucial.

#### Phase 2: Heating System Transition (Medium Priority)
4. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Why**: Upgrading from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., System C or D) is necessary to maintain air quality and reduce heat loss.
   - **Suggestion**: Use `Replace Ventilation System` (Order: 52).
   - **Considerations**: This ensures that the heat gained from new insulation is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Why**: With a sealed and insulated envelope, the building's heat demand is lower, making a heat pump a more efficient option.
   - **Suggestion**: Use `Place Air-Water Heatpump` (Order: 67).
   - **Considerations**: Since there is no potential for geothermal or a heat grid, an air-water heat pump is the most viable alternative.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Why**: Before installing the heat pump, the current radiators should be transitioned to low-temperature emissions to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new generator.
   - **Suggestion**: Use `Place Emission System On Low Temperature` (Order: 70).
   - **Considerations**: This step is crucial for optimizing the efficiency of the new heat pump.

#### Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-term Priority)
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Why**: The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Suggestion**: Use `Place PV-Panels On Roof` (Order: 85).
   - **Considerations**: Proper orientation and shading analysis should be conducted to ensure maximum efficiency.

### Step 4: Summary of Optimal Sequence
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
3. **Insulate Gable Roof**
4. **Replace Ventilation System**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

This sequence ensures that the building's energy efficiency is maximized in a cost-effective manner, aligning with the owner's motivation to improve the EPC.
