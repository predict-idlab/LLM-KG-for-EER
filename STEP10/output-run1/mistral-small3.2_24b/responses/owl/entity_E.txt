1. **Building Type**: Detached building (1862) with cavity walls.
2. **Current EPC**: 165 (D label).
3. **Windows**: Single-glazed with wooden frames in good condition.
4. **Heating System**: Outdated (radiators, floor heating, electric convectors).
5. **Potential for PV**: Yes.
6. **Heat Grid Availability**: No.

   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls.

2. **Replace Glazing**:
   - All windows have single glazing.
   - **Action**: Replace single glazing with double or triple glazing.
   - **Reason**: Single glazing leads to significant heat loss. Upgrading to double or triple glazing will improve thermal performance.

3. **Replace Ventilation System**:
   - Current ventilation system is type A (natural).
   - **Action**: Upgrade to a more efficient system (e.g., type C or D).
   - **Reason**: A more efficient ventilation system will ensure proper air exchange while minimizing heat loss.

4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**:
   - Current heating system is outdated and inefficient.
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Reason**: Heat pumps are more energy-efficient and can significantly reduce energy consumption.

5. **Place PV Panels on Roof**:
   - The building has potential for PV.
   - **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Reason**: PV panels can generate renewable electricity, further reducing the energy consumption and improving the EPC.
