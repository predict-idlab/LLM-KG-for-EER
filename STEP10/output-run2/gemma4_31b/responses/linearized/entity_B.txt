### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
The building has several critical areas of heat loss. Addressing these first ensures that any future heating system is sized correctly and operates at maximum efficiency.
1.  **Insulate Flat Roof:** The `RoofExtension` is currently not insulated. Since heat rises, this is a high-priority intervention to prevent thermal leakage.
2.  **Insulate Wall on Exterior:** Both the `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` are full brick and not insulated. Because they are full brick (not cavity walls), exterior insulation is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges and protect the structure.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The `FloorAboveBasement` is currently not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents cold drafts and reduces heat loss to the lower level.

#### Phase 2: Transition to Low-Temperature Heating (Priority: Medium)
With the thermal envelope sealed, the building's overall heat demand is reduced, making it possible to transition from a traditional boiler to a high-efficiency heat pump.
4.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** Before installing a new generator, the current radiator system should be upgraded to low-temperature emissions. This is essential to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of a heat pump.
5.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** As there is no potential for geothermal energy or a heat grid, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most sustainable and cost-effective replacement for the existing boiler.

#### Phase 3: Ventilation and Air Quality (Priority: Medium)
Increasing the insulation and sealing the building reduces natural air exchange. To prevent moisture buildup and maintain a healthy indoor climate, the ventilation must be upgraded.
6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from the current Ventilation System A (natural supply/exhaust) to a more controlled system (such as System C or D) to ensure air quality without losing the heat gains achieved in Phase 1.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
To offset the electricity required by the new heat pump and ventilation system, the building should move toward energy autonomy.
7.  **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will significantly lower operational costs and further improve the EPC label.
