### Renovation Plan:

#### **Building Information Summary:**
- **Type:** Attached building
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **EPC Score:** 262 (C Label)
- **Primary Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** No
- **Geothermal Potential:** False
- **Insulation Status:**
  - **Roof Main:** Moderately Insulated
  - **Roof Extension:** Not Insulated
  - **Floor Above Basement:** Not Insulated
  - **Walls (Front and Rear):** Not Insulated
- **Windows:**
  - **Bedrooms:** Double Glazing
  - **Living Area:** Double Glazing
- **Ventilation System:** Type A (Natural)
- **Emission System:** Radiator
- **Generation System:** Boiler

#### **Proposed Renovation Path:**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)**
1. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofExtension` which is currently not insulated.
   - **Rationale:** Heat rises, and insulating the flat roof will significantly reduce heat loss.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the basement and improves overall thermal comfort.

3. **Insulate Wall on Exterior:**
   - **Action:** Insulate `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** Both walls are full brick and not insulated. Exterior insulation is more effective than interior insulation for these walls.

**Phase 2: Window Performance (Priority: Medium)**
4. **Replace Glazing (if necessary):**
   - **Action:** Assess the current glazing. Since both bedroom and living area windows are already double-glazed, replacement is not necessary at this stage.

**Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition (Priority: Medium)**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing boiler with an **Air-Water Heat Pump**.
   - **Rationale:** The site has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid, making an air-water heat pump the most viable and efficient alternative.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Transition the current radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** This maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.

**Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV, which will provide renewable electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system.

**Phase 5: Air Quality and Ventilation (Priority: Medium)**
8. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (such as System C or D).
   - **Rationale:** Ensures that the heat gained from the new insulation is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage and maintains indoor air quality.

#### **Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Insulate Flat Roof**
2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
3. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**

This sequence ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system. The final step of installing PV panels ensures sustainable energy generation.
