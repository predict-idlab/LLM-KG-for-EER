### Renovation Plan Analysis

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached building constructed in 2017.
- **Current EPC Score:** 87 (A Label).
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve the EPC.
- **Key Elements:**
  - **Roofs:** Gable roof (moderately insulated), flat roof (well insulated).
  - **Walls:** Three external walls (Front, Rear, Left) are well insulated.
  - **Floors:** Floor above basement is well insulated.
  - **Windows:** Double-glazed windows in good condition.
  - **Heating System:** Heat pump.
  - **Ventilation System:** Type D.
  - **PV Potential:** False.

**Current Strengths:**
- The building already has good insulation on most elements (walls, floor, and flat roof).
- Double-glazed windows are in good condition.
- A modern heat pump is installed, which is highly efficient.

**Areas for Improvement:**
- The gable roof is only moderately insulated, which could be improved.
- The ventilation system is Type D, which might not be the most efficient for this building.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

1. **Insulate Gable Roof:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the insulation of the gable roof to well-insulated status.
   - **Rationale:** Improving the insulation of the gable roof will further reduce heat loss and improve overall energy efficiency.

2. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the ventilation system from Type D to a more efficient system (e.g., Type C).
   - **Rationale:** A more efficient ventilation system can reduce energy consumption and improve indoor air quality.

3. **Consider Additional Measures (Optional):**
   - **Place Sunshade:** If the building has large windows facing south, adding a sunshade can reduce solar heat gain in summer, which can be beneficial for cooling.
   - **Place Airconditioning:** If cooling is a concern, adding airconditioning can improve comfort during hot weather.

**Summary of Sequence and Rationale:**

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Gable Roof** | Reduces heat loss through the roof, improving overall energy efficiency. |
| **2** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Upgrades to a more efficient system to reduce energy consumption and improve indoor air quality. |
| **3** | **Optional: Place Sunshade** | Reduces solar heat gain in summer, beneficial for cooling. |
| **4** | **Optional: Place Airconditioning** | Improves comfort during hot weather. |

**Conclusion:**
The building is already in good condition with a high EPC score. The proposed renovations focus on further improving energy efficiency and indoor air quality. The optional measures can be considered based on specific needs and preferences.

This plan ensures that the building remains energy-efficient and comfortable while minimizing unnecessary costs.
