### Renovation Plan for Attached Building

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Attached (2023)
- **Net Floor Area:** 159.0 m²
- **EPC Score:** 50 (A Label)
- **Potential for PV:** False
- **Heat Grid:** Not Present
- **Construction Year:** 2023
- **Existing Systems:**
  - Electric Convector (SpaceHeater)
  - Boiler (Generation System)
  - Ventilation System D
  - Well-Insulated Walls (Front, Rear)
  - Well-Insulated Floor Above Basement
  - Well-Insulated Flat Roof
  - Well-Insulated Gable Roof (Moderately Insulated)
  - Double Glazing (Windows)
  - Wooden Window Frames

**Owner's Motivation:** Improve EPC (already excellent at A Label).

**Observations:**
1. **Excellent Thermal Envelope:** All walls, floors, and roofs are well-insulated, and windows are double-glazed.
2. **Outdated Heating System:** The building uses an electric convector, which is inefficient compared to modern heat pumps.
3. **High-Performance Ventilation:** Ventilation System D is already installed, ensuring good indoor air quality.
4. **No PV Potential:** The building cannot install solar panels due to lack of potential.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

**Phase 1: Optimize Heating System (Priority: High)**
1. **Replace Electric Convector with Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing electric convector with an **Air-Water Heat Pump**.
   - **Rationale:** Heat pumps are significantly more energy-efficient than electric convectors, reducing energy consumption while maintaining comfort.

2. **Upgrade Emission System to Low-Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Install low-temperature emissions (e.g., underfloor heating) to maximize the efficiency of the new heat pump.
   - **Rationale:** Low-temperature emissions work best with heat pumps, improving their Coefficient of Performance (COP).

**Phase 2: Improve Ventilation Efficiency (Priority: Medium)**
3. **Optimize Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Ensure the existing Ventilation System D is properly balanced and maintained.
   - **Rationale:** Proper ventilation is crucial for maintaining indoor air quality in a well-sealed, insulated building.

**Phase 3: Monitor and Maintain (Priority: Ongoing)**
4. **Regular Maintenance:**
   - **Action:** Schedule annual maintenance for the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Rationale:** Regular maintenance ensures optimal performance and longevity of the systems.

**Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Air-Water Heat Pump Installation**
2. **Low-Temperature Emission System Upgrade**
3. **Ventilation System Optimization**

**Cost-Effectiveness and Energy Efficiency:**
- The proposed plan focuses on upgrading the heating system to a more efficient technology (heat pump) while ensuring the building envelope remains well-sealed and insulated.
- No additional insulation or glazing is needed, as the building already meets high-performance standards.
- The lack of PV potential eliminates the need for solar panel installation, simplifying the renovation process.

This plan ensures the building maintains its excellent EPC rating while improving energy efficiency and comfort.
