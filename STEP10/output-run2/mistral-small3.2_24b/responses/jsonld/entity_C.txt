However, there is room for improvement, particularly in the heating system and potential integration of renewable energy sources.

### Proposed Renovation Path

1. **Phase 1: Heating System Optimization**
   - **Upgrade Emission System**: The current emission system includes electric convectors and floor heating. Transitioning to a low-temperature emission system will allow for the installation of a more efficient heat pump. This is crucial as the building currently has a reason to change the emission system.
   - **Install Air-Water Heat Pump**: Since the building has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid, an air-water heat pump is the most viable and efficient alternative to the current boiler. This will significantly reduce energy consumption and improve the EPC score.

2. **Phase 2: Window Performance Enhancement**
   - **Replace Glazing**: Although the windows are already double-glazed, upgrading to triple glazing can further reduce heat loss and improve thermal comfort. This is a medium-priority intervention as it builds upon the initial envelope improvements.

3. **Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation**
   - **Install PV Panels on Roof**: The building currently has no PV panels, but the ontology suggests there is potential for PV. Installing solar panels will offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label. This is a long-term investment that will pay off over time.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence
1. **Upgrade Emission System** $\rightarrow$ **Install Air-Water Heat Pump** $\rightarrow$ **Replace Glazing** $\rightarrow$ **Install PV Panels on Roof**.

This plan ensures that the building's energy efficiency is maximized while remaining cost-effective. The sequence follows the principle of addressing the most impactful interventions first, ensuring that each step builds upon the previous improvements.
