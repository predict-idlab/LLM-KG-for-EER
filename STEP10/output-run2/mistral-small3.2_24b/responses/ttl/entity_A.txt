### Renovation Plan

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First Approach)
The primary goal is to reduce heat loss by improving the building's thermal envelope.

1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The gable roof is currently not insulated, leading to significant heat loss. Insulating it will improve thermal retention.
   - **Action:** `insulate gable roof`

2. **Insulate Walls on Interior**
   - **Rationale:** All three external walls (Front, Rear, and Right) are full brick and not insulated. Insulating the walls on the interior is cost-effective and provides substantial thermal improvement.
   - **Action:** `insulate wall on interior`

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Rationale:** The floor above the basement is currently not insulated. Adding insulation here will prevent heat loss to the basement and improve overall comfort.
   - **Action:** `insulate floor above basement`

#### Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade
With a sealed envelope, the building's heat demand is significantly lower, allowing for the installation of a more efficient, low-temperature system.

4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Rationale:** Since geothermal potential is "false" and there is no heat grid, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing boiler.
   - **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Rationale:** Before installing a heat pump, the current radiators should be transitioned to low-temperature emissions to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new generator.
   - **Action:** `place emission system on low temperature`

#### Phase 3: Ventilation System Upgrade
Once the building is sealed and insulated, the air exchange rate decreases, making a modern ventilation system necessary to prevent moisture buildup and maintain air quality.

6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Rationale:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (such as System C or D). This ensures that the heat gained from the new insulation is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage.
   - **Action:** `replace ventilation system`

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label.

7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Action:** `place PV-panels on roof`
