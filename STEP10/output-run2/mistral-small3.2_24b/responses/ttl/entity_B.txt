### Renovation Plan for Attached Building (1832, EPC 262)

#### Key Observations:
1. **Building Type**: Attached (1832, Belgium, 212 m²).
2. **EPC**: 262 (C Label), motivation to improve.
3. **Thermal Envelope**:
   - **Roofs**: Flat roof (not insulated), gable roof (moderately insulated).
   - **Walls**: Full brick, not insulated (Front, Rear).
   - **Floors**: Floor above basement (not insulated).
4. **Windows**: Double-glazed, wooden frames (good condition).
5. **Systems**:
   - Radiator emission system.
   - Ventilation System A (natural).
6. **Potential**:
   - PV potential: **True**.
   - Geothermal potential: **False**.
   - Heat grid: **False**.

---

### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First)
**Goal**: Reduce heat loss through uninsulated elements.

1. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Action**: `insulate flat roof` (Ordering: 25).
   - **Rationale**: Flat roofs are major sources of heat loss; insulation will improve thermal performance significantly.

2. **Insulate Walls On Exterior**
   - **Action**: `insulate wall on exterior` (Ordering: 40).
   - **Rationale**: Full brick walls (Front, Rear) are not insulated. Exterior insulation avoids thermal bridges and is more effective than interior insulation for attached buildings.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action**: `insulate floor above basement` (Ordering: 49).
   - **Rationale**: Prevents cold drafts from the basement and improves overall comfort.

#### Phase 2: Window and Ventilation Upgrades
**Goal**: Improve air quality and thermal comfort.

4. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: `replace ventilation system` (Ordering: 52).
   - **Rationale**: Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to System D (mechanical supply/exhaust) to ensure controlled air exchange in a now-sealed envelope.

#### Phase 3: Heating System Transition
**Goal**: Optimize heating efficiency after envelope improvements.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: `place air-water heatpump` (Ordering: 67).
   - **Rationale**: Replaces the existing boiler with a more efficient, low-temperature system. Geothermal is not feasible, and there is no heat grid.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action**: `place emission system on low temperature` (Ordering: 70).
   - **Rationale**: Ensures the radiators work optimally with the new heat pump.

#### Phase 4: Renewable Energy Integration
**Goal**: Offset electricity consumption and further improve EPC.

7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: `place PV-panels on roof` (Ordering: 85).
   - **Rationale**: The building has PV potential. Solar panels will generate renewable electricity for the heat pump and ventilation system.

---

### Summary of Sequence and Rationale

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Flat Roof** | High heat loss through flat roofs; improves thermal performance. |
| **2** | **Insulate Wall On Exterior** | Eliminates thermal bridges; more effective than interior insulation. |
| **3** | **Insulate Floor Above Basement** | Prevents cold drafts from the basement. |
| **4** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Ensures controlled air exchange in a sealed envelope. |
| **5** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | More efficient than the existing boiler; compatible with low-temperature emissions. |
| **6** | **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** | Optimizes heat pump performance. |
| **7** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Offsets electricity consumption; leverages PV potential. |

---

### Notes:
- **Cost-Effectiveness**: The sequence prioritizes high-impact, low-cost measures first (e.g., insulation) before investing in new systems.
- **EPC Improvement**: This plan will significantly reduce the EPC score, potentially moving the building into the B or A label range.
- **Historical Protection**: None of the elements are historically protected, allowing for unrestricted renovations.

This plan aligns with the "Insulate → Ventilate → Generate" principle, ensuring long-term energy efficiency and cost savings.
