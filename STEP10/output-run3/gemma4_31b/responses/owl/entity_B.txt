### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
The building has several critical points of heat loss. Addressing these first ensures that any future heating system is sized correctly and operates at peak efficiency.

1.  **Insulate Flat Roof:** While the main gable roof is moderately insulated, the `RoofExtension` (flat roof) is currently not insulated. Since heat rises, this is a priority for reducing energy consumption.
2.  **Insulate Walls on Exterior:** Both the `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` are full brick and not insulated. As they are not cavity walls, **exterior insulation** is the most effective way to eliminate thermal bridges and significantly improve the EPC score.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The `FloorAboveBasement` is not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents heat loss to the basement and improves the thermal comfort of the ground floor.

#### Phase 2: High-Efficiency Heating Transition (Priority: Medium)
With the thermal envelope sealed, the building's heat demand is significantly reduced, allowing for a transition to a sustainable, low-temperature heating system.

4.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** The current system uses radiators. To maximize the efficiency (COP) of a modern heat pump, the emission system should be transitioned to low-temperature settings.
5.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since there is no heat grid available and the site has no potential for geothermal drilling or captation, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most efficient and sustainable replacement for the existing boiler.

#### Phase 3: Air Quality and Ventilation (Priority: Medium)
Improving the insulation of an 1832 building reduces natural air leakage. To prevent moisture buildup and maintain a healthy indoor climate, the ventilation must be upgraded.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more controlled system (such as System C or D) to ensure efficient air exchange without losing the heat gained from the new insulation.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
To further lower the EPC score and offset the electricity costs of the new heat pump and ventilation system.

7.  **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels is the final step to move the building toward energy independence.
