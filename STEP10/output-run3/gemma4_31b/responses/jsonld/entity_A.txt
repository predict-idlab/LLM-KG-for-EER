### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: Critical)
The building has significant heat loss through uninsulated structural elements. Addressing these first provides the most immediate and substantial reduction in energy consumption.

1.  **Insulate Gable Roof:** The main gable roof is currently not insulated. Since heat rises, this is the highest priority for reducing energy loss.
2.  **Insulate Walls on Exterior:** The front, rear, and right walls are full brick and not insulated. Because they are not cavity walls, **exterior insulation** is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges and protect the structure from moisture.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The floor above the basement is not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents cold drafts and reduces heat loss to the lower level.
*Note: Windows are already double-glazed and the wooden frames are in a "Good State," so replacing them is not recommended at this time to maintain cost-effectiveness.*

#### Phase 2: Transition to High-Efficiency Heating (Priority: High)
Once the heat demand is lowered via insulation, the building can transition from a traditional boiler to a sustainable, low-temperature system.

4.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** To maximize the efficiency (COP) of a heat pump, the existing radiators should be upgraded to low-temperature emission systems.
5.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since the site has no potential for geothermal energy and no access to a heat grid, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most efficient and sustainable replacement for the current boiler.

#### Phase 3: Air Quality and Ventilation (Priority: Medium)
As the building becomes more airtight through insulation and glazing, the natural air exchange rate decreases.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** The current Ventilation System A (natural) should be upgraded to a more controlled system (such as System C or D). This prevents moisture buildup and ensures a healthy indoor climate without losing the heat gained from Phase 1.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and ventilation system, the building should generate its own power.

7.  **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will significantly lower operational costs and further improve the EPC label.
