### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Refinement (Priority: Low-Medium)
The building envelope is almost entirely optimized, with one exception.
1.  **Enhance Main Pitched Roof Insulation:** While the flat roof and walls are "well insulated," the main pitched roof is described as having "moderate" insulation. Since heat rises, upgrading this to a "high" level of insulation is the most effective way to further lower the EPC score and reduce the load on the heat pump.

#### Phase 2: Energy Storage and Management (Priority: Medium)
Since the house already generates its own electricity (PV panels) and uses a high-efficiency heating system (Heat Pump), the next step is to optimize the *usage* of that energy.
2.  **Install a Battery Energy Storage System (BESS):** To maximize the return on the existing PV panels, adding a battery allows the homeowner to store excess solar energy generated during the day and use it to power the heat pump and Ventilation System D during the evening or night, reducing reliance on the grid.
3.  **Implement Smart Energy Management:** Installing a smart home energy management system (HEMS) to synchronize the heat pump's operation with peak solar production hours (thermal storage) will improve the overall operational efficiency.

#### Phase 3: Long-term Maintenance (Priority: Monitoring)
The windows were renewed in 2020 and the emission systems are in good condition. No immediate replacements are recommended as the cost-to-benefit ratio for upgrading double glazing to triple glazing in a house that is already this efficient is generally too low to be cost-effective.
