### Renovation Plan

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Attached Building
- **Year Built:** 1832
- **Current EPC Score:** 262 (C Label)
- **Primary Goal:** Improve EPC Label
- **Key Issues:**
  - Non-insulated walls (Front and Rear)
  - Non-insulated floor above basement
  - Non-insulated flat roof (RoofExtension)
  - Existing ventilation system (Type A)

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior (Priority: High)**
   - **Action:** Insulate both `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** These walls are full brick and not insulated, leading to significant heat loss. Exterior insulation is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges and improve the building's thermal envelope.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement (Priority: High)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** The floor is currently not insulated, leading to cold drafts from the basement. Insulating this floor will prevent heat loss and improve thermal comfort.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof (Priority: High)**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof is currently not insulated, and heat rises rapidly through flat sections. Insulating the roof will significantly reduce heat loss.

4. **Replace Ventilation System (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A to a more efficient system (such as System C or D).
   - **Rationale:** The existing ventilation system (Type A) is not sufficient to maintain air quality in a now-sealed envelope. Upgrading the ventilation system will ensure proper air exchange and prevent moisture buildup.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Action:** Install an Air-Water Heat Pump.
   - **Rationale:** The building has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid. An Air-Water Heat Pump is the most viable and efficient alternative to the existing boiler, providing sustainable heating.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Action:** Transition the current radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Before installing a heat pump, the current radiators should be transitioned to low-temperature emissions to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new generator.

7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof (Priority: Long-term)**
   - **Action:** Install solar panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system, further improving the EPC label.

**Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**
2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
4. **Upgrade Ventilation System**
5. **Install Air-Water Heat Pump**
6. **Transition to Low-Temperature Emissions**
7. **Install PV Panels**

This sequence ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system. It also focuses on cost-effective measures that provide the highest return on investment.
