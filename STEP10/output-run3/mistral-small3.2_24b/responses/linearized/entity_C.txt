### Renovation Plan

**Current State:**
- **Building Type:** Attached building
- **Construction Year:** 2023
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score:** 50
- **Potential for PV:** False
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** False
- **Insulation Status:**
  - **Floor Above Basement:** Well insulated
  - **Wall Front:** Well insulated
  - **Wall Rear:** Well insulated
  - **Roof Extension:** Well insulated
  - **Roof Main:** Moderately insulated
- **Ventilation System:** Ventilation System D
- **Heating System:** Electric Convector
- **Glazing:**
  - **Bedrooms:** Double glazing, changed during renovation
  - **Living Area:** Double glazing, changed during renovation
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve EPC

**Renovation Plan:**

1. **Insulate Roof Main:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the Roof Main to improve insulation grade from Moderately Insulated to Well Insulated.
   - **Rationale:** Enhances thermal performance and reduces heat loss through the roof.

2. **Replace Emission System:**
   - **Action:** Replace the current Electric Convector with a more energy-efficient system, such as a Heat Pump.
   - **Rationale:** Improves heating efficiency and reduces energy consumption.

3. **Install PV Panels:**
   - **Action:** Although the potential for PV is false, consider installing solar panels to offset electricity consumption.
   - **Rationale:** Reduces reliance on grid electricity and lowers energy costs.

**Summary of Sequence and Rationale:**

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Roof Main** | Enhances thermal performance and reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| **2** | **Replace Emission System** | Improves heating efficiency and reduces energy consumption. |

**Note:**
- Ensure that all renovations comply with local regulations and standards.
