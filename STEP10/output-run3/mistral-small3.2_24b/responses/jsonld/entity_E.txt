### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (Construction Year: 1862)

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Detached
- **Construction Year:** 1862
- **Current EPC Score:** 165 (B Label)
- **Owner's Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Key Elements:**
  - 4 Cavity Walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right) - Not Insulated
  - 1 Floor Above Crawl Space - Not Insulated
  - 2 Windows (Bedrooms, Living Area) - Single Glazing, Wooden Frames
  - 1 Gable Roof - Well Insulated
  - 1 Ventilation System (Type A)

**Renovation Priorities:**
1. **Envelope Sealing (High Priority):**
   - **Insulate Cavity Walls:** Since the walls are cavity walls, the most cost-effective method is to inject insulation into the cavity. This addresses a significant source of heat loss.
   - **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space:** Prevents heat loss to the crawl space and reduces drafts.

2. **Window Upgrades (Medium Priority):**
   - **Replace Glazing (Bedrooms and Living Area):** The single glazing in both windows is a major source of heat loss. Upgrading to double or triple glazing will significantly improve thermal performance.

3. **Heating System Transition (Long-term):**
   - **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A to a more efficient system (e.g., System C or D) to ensure proper air exchange and maintain indoor air quality.
   - **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since there is no potential for geothermal energy or a heat grid, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most viable and efficient alternative to the existing boiler.

4. **Sustainable Energy Generation (Optional):**
   - **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** If the roof has the right orientation and condition, installing solar panels can offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label.

**Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Insulate Cavity Walls** (High Priority)
2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** (High Priority)
3. **Replace Glazing** (Medium Priority)
4. **Replace Ventilation System** (Medium Priority)
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** (Long-term)
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof** (Optional)

**Summary of Recommendations:**

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Insulate Cavity Walls | Addresses significant heat loss through uninsulated cavity walls. |
| 2 | Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space | Prevents heat loss to the crawl space and reduces drafts. |
| 3 | Replace Glazing (Bedrooms and Living Area) | Upgrades from single to double/triple glazing to improve thermal performance. |
| 4 | Replace Ventilation System | Ensures proper air exchange and maintains indoor air quality. |
| 5 | Place Air-Water Heat Pump | Provides a sustainable and efficient heating solution tailored to the new lower heat demand. |
| 6 | Place PV-Panels on Roof | Offsets electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improves EPC label. |

This plan ensures a cost-effective path to improving the building's energy efficiency and EPC rating while maintaining the building's historical integrity and comfort.
