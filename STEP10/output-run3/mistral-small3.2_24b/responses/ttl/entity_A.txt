### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### Building Overview:
- **Type:** Semi-detached building
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **Current EPC Score:** 388 (D Label)
- **Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Potential for PV:** True
- **Heat Grid Presence:** False
- **Geothermal Potential:** False

#### Key Observations:
1. **Thermal Envelope:**
   - **Roofs:**
     - Flat roof (RoofExtension) is moderately insulated.
     - Gable roof (RoofMain) is not insulated.
   - **Walls:**
     - All external walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right) are full brick and not insulated.
   - **Floor:**
     - Floor above basement (FloorAboveBasement) is not insulated.

2. **Windows:**
   - All windows have double glazing and wooden frames in good condition.

3. **Heating and Ventilation:**
   - Current heating system includes radiators and floor heating.
   - Current ventilation system is Type A (natural).

4. **PV Potential:**
   - The building has potential for photovoltaic (PV) panel installation.

#### Proposed Renovation Path:

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First Approach)**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof (RoofMain).
   - **Rationale:** The gable roof is currently not insulated, making it a priority to prevent heat loss through the roof.
   - **Cost:** €60 per m².

2. **Insulate Walls on Exterior:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the exterior walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right).
   - **Rationale:** Exterior wall insulation is more effective for full brick walls and eliminates thermal bridges.
   - **Cost:** €200 per m².

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the floor above the basement.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the basement and improves overall thermal comfort.
   - **Cost:** €30 per m².

**Phase 2: Heating System Transition**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Rationale:** More energy-efficient and sustainable, especially with a well-insulated building envelope.
   - **Cost:** €8,000 (fixed) + €35 per m².

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the existing radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.
   - **Cost:** €100 per m².

**Phase 3: Ventilation Upgrade**
6. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A to System C or D.
   - **Rationale:** Ensures proper air exchange and maintains indoor air quality in a sealed envelope.
   - **Cost:** €3,500 (fixed) + €17 per m².

**Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** Generates renewable electricity to power the heat pump and other systems, further improving the EPC label.
   - **Cost:** €275 per m².

#### Summary of Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
2. **Insulate Exterior Walls**
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
4. **Install Air-Water Heat Pump**
5. **Upgrade to Low-Temperature Emission System**
6. **Replace Ventilation System**
7. **Install PV-Panels on Roof**

This plan follows the "Insulate → Ventilate → Generate" sequence, ensuring that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before upgrading heating and ventilation systems. This approach maximizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness while improving the EPC score.
