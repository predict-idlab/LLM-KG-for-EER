### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (1862, EPC Score: 165, B Label)

Given the building's characteristics and the goal to improve the EPC score, the renovation plan should follow a logical sequence that prioritizes cost-effective measures with the highest energy-saving potential. The plan will focus on the building envelope, heating systems, and renewable energy integration.

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric-First Approach)
1. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavities of the `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Rear`, and `Wall_Right`.
   - **Rationale**: These walls are cavity walls and not insulated. Cavity wall insulation is a cost-effective way to improve thermal performance.
   - **Ordering**: 34 (from the ontology).

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the `FloorAboveCrawlSpace`.
   - **Rationale**: Insulating this floor will reduce heat loss to the crawl space, improving overall comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Ordering**: 49 (from the ontology).

3. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action**: Replace the single glazing in `WindowGlazingBedrooms` and `WindowGlazingLivingArea` with double or triple glazing.
   - **Rationale**: Single glazing leads to significant heat loss. Upgrading to double or triple glazing will improve thermal performance.
   - **Ordering**: 43 (from the ontology).

#### Phase 2: Heating System Transition
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Rationale**: The current heating system includes electric convectors and radiators, which are less efficient. An air-water heat pump is a more sustainable and efficient alternative.
   - **Ordering**: 67 (from the ontology).

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the emission system to operate at lower temperatures.
   - **Rationale**: This will maximize the efficiency of the new heat pump.
   - **Ordering**: 70 (from the ontology).

#### Phase 3: Renewable Energy Integration
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the `RoofMain`.
   - **Rationale**: The building has potential for PV, and installing panels will provide renewable energy to power the heat pump and other electrical needs.
   - **Ordering**: 85 (from the ontology).
