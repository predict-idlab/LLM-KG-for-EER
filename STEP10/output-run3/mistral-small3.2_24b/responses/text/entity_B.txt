### Renovation Plan for an Attached House in Ghent, Belgium (Built in 1832)

**Current Situation:**
- **House Type:** Attached house, built in 1832.
- **Floor Area:** 212 m².
- **EPC Score:** 262.
- **Walls:** Solid brick walls (front and rear) uninsulated.
- **Roofs:** Pitched roof with moderate insulation, flat-roof extension uninsulated.
- **Floor:** Uninsulated above the basement.
- **Heating:** Boiler with radiators (in poor condition).
- **Ventilation:** Basic natural ventilation.
- **Windows:** Double-glazed wooden frames in good condition.
- **Potential for PV Panels:** Yes.
- **No district heating or geothermal potential.**

**Objective:**
Improve energy efficiency and EPC score in a cost-effective manner.

### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization ("Fabric First" Approach)
**Goal:** Reduce heat loss by insulating key areas.

1. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - Priority as heat rises; insulating the flat roof is highly effective.
   - Use rigid insulation boards or spray foam for best performance.

2. **Insulate Exterior Walls:**
   - Exterior wall insulation (EWI) is recommended for solid brick walls.
   - EWI improves thermal performance and eliminates thermal bridges.
   - Can be combined with a new exterior finish (render, cladding, etc.).

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - Install insulation (e.g., rigid foam boards) on the basement ceiling to reduce heat loss.

#### Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade
**Goal:** Replace inefficient boiler with a more sustainable solution.

4. **Replace Boiler with Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - Since geothermal is not an option, an air-water heat pump is the next best choice.
   - Ensure the heat pump is appropriately sized for the improved thermal envelope.

5. **Upgrade Radiators to Low-Temperature Systems:**
   - Replace or modify existing radiators to work efficiently with the heat pump.
   - Low-temperature systems improve the heat pump’s Coefficient of Performance (COP).

#### Phase 3: Ventilation Upgrade
**Goal:** Improve indoor air quality and maintain energy efficiency.

6. **Replace Natural Ventilation with Mechanical Ventilation (System C or D):**
   - System C (supply and extract with heat recovery) is ideal for sealed buildings.
   - Ensures proper air exchange without significant heat loss.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Optional but Recommended)
**Goal:** Reduce reliance on grid electricity and lower energy bills.

7. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels:**
   - Generate renewable electricity to power the heat pump and other systems.
   - Maximize solar potential by optimizing panel placement on the roof.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Flat Roof** | Reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| **2** | **Exterior Wall Insulation** | Eliminates thermal bridges and improves thermal performance. |
| **3** | **Insulate Floor Above Basement** | Reduces heat loss to the basement. |
| **4** | **Replace Boiler with Air-Water Heat Pump** | More efficient and sustainable heating solution. |
| **5** | **Upgrade Radiators to Low-Temperature Systems** | Optimizes heat pump performance. |
| **6** | **Replace Ventilation System (Natural → System C/D)** | Maintains air quality in a sealed building. |
| **7** | **Install PV Panels (Optional)** | Generates renewable electricity for the heat pump and other systems. |

This plan ensures a cost-effective, step-by-step approach to improving energy efficiency while maximizing long-term savings and comfort.
