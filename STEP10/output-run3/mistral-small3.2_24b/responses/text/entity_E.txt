1. **Insulate Cavity Walls**: Since all exterior walls are cavity walls and currently uninsulated, filling the cavities with insulation is a cost-effective way to improve thermal performance. This should be prioritized as it addresses a large surface area.
2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**: If the floor above the crawl space is not insulated, adding insulation here will reduce heat loss to the unconditioned space below. Ensure proper ventilation of the crawl space to prevent moisture issues.
3. **Insulate Flat Roof Extension**: If there is a flat roof extension that is not insulated, insulating it will help reduce heat loss through the roof.

4. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**: Upgrading from single to double or triple glazing will significantly reduce heat loss through the windows. Since the frames are in good condition, you can retain them and only replace the glazing.

7. **Replace Natural Ventilation with Mechanical Ventilation**: Upgrade from the basic natural ventilation system to a more efficient mechanical ventilation system (such as System C or D) to maintain indoor air quality and prevent moisture buildup in the now-sealed envelope.

**Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation**
8. **Install Photovoltaic Panels**: Leverage the potential for PV panel installation to generate renewable electricity. This will help offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system, further improving the EPC score.
