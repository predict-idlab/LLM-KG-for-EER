### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
The building has several critical areas of heat loss. Addressing these first ensures that any future heating system is sized correctly and operates at maximum efficiency.
1.  **Insulate Gable Roof:** The `RoofMain` is currently not insulated. Since heat rises, this is the most impactful first step to lower the energy score.
2.  **Insulate Walls on Exterior:** All three external walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, and `Wall_Right`) are full brick and not insulated. As they are not cavity walls, exterior insulation is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges and significantly reduce heat loss.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The `FloorAboveBasement` is not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents cold drafts and reduces energy loss to the basement.
*Note: Windows are already double-glazed and the frames are in a "Good State," so replacing them is not recommended at this stage to remain cost-effective.*

#### Phase 2: Heating and Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)
Once the building is sealed and insulated, the total heat demand will drop, allowing for a transition to a high-efficiency, low-temperature system.
4.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** Before installing a new generator, the existing radiators and air heating should be transitioned to low-temperature emissions. This is essential to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of a heat pump.
5.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since there is no potential for geothermal drilling and no available heat grid, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most sustainable and efficient replacement for the current boiler.

#### Phase 3: Ventilation and Air Quality (Priority: Medium)
Upgrading the envelope increases airtightness, which can lead to moisture issues if ventilation is not addressed.
6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (such as System C or D). This ensures healthy indoor air quality and prevents heat loss through uncontrolled air leakage.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and ventilation system, and to push the EPC label further toward A or B.
7.  **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide renewable energy to power the home's new electrical systems.
