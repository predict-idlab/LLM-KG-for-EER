### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
The goal is to eliminate heat leakage through the building's skin. Because the walls are cavity walls, we can use highly cost-effective injection methods.

1.  **Insulate Wall Cavity:** All four external walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`) are cavity walls and are currently not insulated. **Insulating the wall cavity** is the most cost-effective way to drastically reduce heat loss through the walls without altering the building's exterior appearance.
2.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The `FloorAboveCrawlSpace` is currently uninsulated. Adding insulation here prevents heat loss to the crawl space and eliminates cold drafts at the floor level.

#### Phase 2: Window Performance (Priority: High)
The building currently utilizes single glazing, which is a major source of thermal bridging.

3.  **Replace Glazing:** The window frames (`WoodenWindowFrame`) are reported to be in a "Good State." Therefore, instead of replacing the entire window unit, the most cost-effective move is to **replace the glazing** (upgrading from single to double or triple glazing). This maintains the existing frames while significantly improving the U-value.

#### Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition (Priority: Medium)
With the thermal envelope sealed, the building's heat demand is lowered, making it possible to transition from a traditional boiler to a sustainable system.

Increasing the airtightness of the building (via insulation and new glazing) necessitates a controlled ventilation strategy to prevent humidity buildup.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from the current Ventilation System A (natural) to a more modern system (such as System C or D). This ensures a healthy indoor climate and prevents the loss of heat through uncontrolled air leakage.
