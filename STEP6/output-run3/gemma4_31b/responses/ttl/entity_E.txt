1. **Insulate Wall Cavity:** All four external walls (Front, Left, Rear, Right) are identified as cavity walls and are currently not insulated. **Insulating the wall cavity** via injection is the most cost-effective and least disruptive method to drastically reduce heat loss through the walls.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The floor above the crawl space is currently not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents cold air infiltration from the crawl space into the living areas.
