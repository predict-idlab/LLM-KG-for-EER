### Renovation Plan

Given the building's characteristics, the following renovation plan is proposed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective:

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization
1. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Reasoning**: The flat roof is currently not insulated. Insulating the roof is crucial as heat rises, and uninsulated roofs lead to significant heat loss.
   - **Action**: Install insulation on the flat roof to improve thermal performance.

2. **Insulate Wall on Interior**
   - **Reasoning**: Both the front and rear walls are full brick and not insulated. Insulating the walls on the interior is more cost-effective than exterior insulation and provides substantial thermal retention.
   - **Action**: Add insulation to the interior surfaces of the front and rear walls.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Reasoning**: The floor above the basement is currently not insulated. Insulating this floor will prevent cold air from the basement from affecting the living areas.
   - **Action**: Install insulation on the floor above the basement.

#### Phase 2: Window Performance Enhancement
4. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Reasoning**: The windows currently have double glazing, which is better than single glazing but can still be improved. Replacing the glazing with triple glazing will further enhance thermal performance.
   - **Action**: Replace the existing double glazing with triple glazing to reduce heat loss through windows.

#### Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Reasoning**: The current generation system is a boiler. Transitioning to an air-water heat pump will significantly improve energy efficiency and reduce carbon emissions.
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler.

#### Phase 4: Air Quality and Ventilation
6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Reasoning**: The current ventilation system is system D, which is efficient but may not be optimized for the new thermal envelope. Upgrading to a more advanced system can improve air quality and comfort.
   - **Action**: Upgrade the ventilation system to a more efficient model to ensure proper air exchange and maintain indoor air quality.

### Summary of Sequence and Rationale

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | Insulate Flat Roof | Reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| **2** | Insulate Wall on Interior | Improves thermal retention of the walls. |
| **3** | Insulate Floor Above Basement | Prevents cold air from the basement affecting living areas. |
| **4** | Replace Glazing | Enhances window thermal performance. |
| **5** | Place Air-Water Heat Pump | Transitions to a more energy-efficient heating system. |
| **6** | Replace Ventilation System | Ensures proper air exchange and maintains indoor air quality. |

This plan follows a logical sequence that prioritizes thermal envelope improvements before upgrading heating systems and ventilation, ensuring a comprehensive and cost-effective approach to energy efficiency.
