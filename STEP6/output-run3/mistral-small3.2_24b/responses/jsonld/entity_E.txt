### Renovation Plan:

1. **Insulate Cavity Walls** (Wall_Front, Wall_Left, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right):
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls on the interior.
   - **Rationale**: All four walls are cavity walls and currently not insulated. Insulating them on the interior is more cost-effective than exterior insulation and significantly improves thermal performance.
   - **Estimated Cost**: 4 walls * 136.0 m² * €22/m² = €7,496
   - **Estimated Savings**: Significant reduction in heat loss through the walls.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**:
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space.
   - **Rationale**: The floor above the crawl space is currently not insulated, leading to heat loss to the unconditioned crawl space.
   - **Estimated Cost**: 136.0 m² * €30/m² = €4,080
   - **Estimated Savings**: Reduces heat loss to the crawl space, improving overall energy efficiency.

3. **Replace Glazing** (WindowsLivingArea, WindowsBedrooms):
   - **Action**: Replace the single glazing with double or triple glazing.
   - **Rationale**: All windows have single glazing, which leads to significant heat loss. Replacing the glazing will improve the thermal performance of the windows.
   - **Estimated Cost**: 2 sets of windows * 136.0 m² * €200/m² = €54,400
   - **Estimated Savings**: Significant reduction in heat loss through the windows.

4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**:
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Rationale**: The building has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid is present. An air-water heat pump is a sustainable and efficient alternative to the current boiler.
   - **Estimated Cost**: €8,000 (fixed price) + 136.0 m² * €35/m² = €12,760
   - **Estimated Savings**: Lower energy consumption and reduced carbon footprint.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**:
   - **Action**: Upgrade the current emission system to a low-temperature system.
   - **Rationale**: Before installing the heat pump, the current radiators should be transitioned to low-temperature emissions to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new generator.
   - **Estimated Cost**: 136.0 m² * €100/m² = €13,600
   - **Estimated Savings**: Improved efficiency of the new heat pump.

6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**:
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale**: The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Estimated Cost**: 136.0 m² * €275/m² = €37,400
   - **Estimated Savings**: Generation of renewable electricity, reducing reliance on the grid.
