### Renovation Plan Analysis

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached building
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **EPC Score:** 388 (D Label)
- **Current Heating System:** AirHeating with FloorHeating
- **Ventilation System:** VentilationSystemA (natural)
- **Insulation Status:**
  - RoofMain: NotInsulated
  - RoofExtension: ModeratelyInsulated
  - Wall_Front, Wall_Right, Wall_Rear: NotInsulated
  - FloorAboveBasement: NotInsulated
- **Windows:**
  - Double-glazed with wooden frames in good condition
- **Potential for PV:** True
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** False
- **Owner's Motivation:** ToImproveEPC

### Renovation Strategy

**Priority 1: Building Envelope Optimization**
The building has significant heat loss due to uninsulated walls, roof, and floor. Improving the thermal envelope is the most cost-effective way to reduce energy consumption and improve the EPC score.

1. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**
   - **Walls to Insulate:** Wall_Front, Wall_Right, Wall_Rear
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is the most effective method for full brick walls, eliminating thermal bridges and significantly improving thermal performance.
   - **Ordering:** High (Priority 40)

2. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The main gable roof (RoofMain) is currently uninsulated. Insulating it will reduce heat loss through the roof.
   - **Ordering:** Medium (Priority 28)

3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof extension (RoofExtension) is only moderately insulated. Upgrading to full insulation will further improve energy efficiency.
   - **Ordering:** Medium (Priority 25)

4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Rationale:** The floor above the basement is currently uninsulated. Insulating it will prevent cold drafts from the basement.
   - **Ordering:** Medium (Priority 49)

**Priority 2: Ventilation System Upgrade**
With the building envelope sealed, a more efficient ventilation system is needed to maintain air quality.

5. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Rationale:** Upgrading from VentilationSystemA (natural) to a more efficient system (such as System C or D) will ensure proper air exchange and prevent moisture buildup.
   - **Ordering:** Medium (Priority 52)

**Priority 3: Heating System Transition**
With the building envelope optimized, the heating system can be transitioned to a more efficient, low-temperature system.

6. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Rationale:** An air-water heat pump is the most sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing air heating system, especially since geothermal potential is limited and there is no heat grid.
   - **Ordering:** Medium (Priority 67)

7. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Rationale:** Before installing the heat pump, the current floor heating system should be transitioned to a low-temperature emission system to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.
   - **Ordering:** Medium (Priority 70)

**Priority 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label.

8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Ordering:** Medium (Priority 85)

### Summary of Optimal Sequence

1. **Insulate Wall on Exterior** (Wall_Front, Wall_Right, Wall_Rear)
2. **Insulate Gable Roof** (RoofMain)
3. **Insulate Flat Roof** (RoofExtension)
4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
5. **Replace Ventilation System** (Upgrade to System C or D)
6. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
7. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

This sequence ensures that the building's energy efficiency is maximized in a cost-effective manner, aligning with the owner's motivation to improve the EPC score.
