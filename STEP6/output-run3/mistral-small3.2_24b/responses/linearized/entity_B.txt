### **Step-by-Step Energy-Efficient Renovation Plan**

#### **Building Overview**
- **Construction Year:** 1832 (Old building)
- **Type:** Attached Building
- **EPC Score:** 262 (C Label)
- **Owner's Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Heat Grid Availability:** No
- **Geothermal Potential:** No
- **Ventilation System:** Type A (Natural)

#### **Key Issues**
1. **Thermal Envelope:**
   - **Walls:** `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` are not insulated.
   - **Floor Above Basement:** Not insulated.
   - **Roof Extension:** Not insulated.

2. **Windows:**
   - Double glazing but may need replacement due to age.

3. **Heating System:**
   - Current system: Radiator + Boiler.
   - Potential for upgrade to a heat pump.

4. **Ventilation:**
   - Type A is outdated and inefficient.

---

### **Proposed Renovation Path**

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
**Goal:** Reduce heat loss through walls, floors, and roofs.

1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
   - **Action:** Insulate `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is more effective for older buildings with non-cavity walls.
   - **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in heat loss.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the basement and improves overall comfort.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** Flat roofs lose heat rapidly; insulation will reduce heat loss.

#### **Phase 2: Window Upgrades**
**Goal:** Improve thermal performance of windows.

4. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action:** Replace all glazing with **triple glazing**.
   - **Rationale:** Double glazing is outdated for modern efficiency; triple glazing will further reduce heat loss.

#### **Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
**Goal:** Install a high-efficiency, low-carbon heating system.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Install an **Air-Water Heat Pump**.
   - **Rationale:** Sustainable, efficient, and suitable for the building’s size and location.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action:** Upgrade radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the efficiency of the heat pump.

#### **Phase 4: Ventilation Upgrade**
**Goal:** Improve air quality and energy efficiency.

7. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A to **Ventilation System C or D**.
   - **Rationale:** More efficient airflow control and heat recovery.

#### **Phase 5: Renewable Energy Integration**
**Goal:** Generate on-site renewable energy.

8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install **PV panels** on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** Utilizes the building’s PV potential to offset electricity consumption.

---

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
| **Sequence** | **Action** | **Rationale** |
|-------------|------------|-------------|
| **1** | **Insulate Exterior Walls** | Reduces heat loss through walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Basement** | Prevents cold drafts from basement. |
| **3** | **Insulate Flat Roof** | Reduces heat loss through roof. |
| **4** | **Replace Glazing** | Improves window thermal performance. |
| **5** | **Install Air-Water Heat Pump** | Sustainable heating solution. |
| **6** | **Upgrade to Low-Temp Emission** | Maximizes heat pump efficiency. |
| **7** | **Upgrade Ventilation System** | Improves air quality and efficiency. |
| **8** | **Install PV Panels** | Generates renewable energy. |

---

### **Final Recommendations**
- **Prioritize insulation upgrades** first to reduce heat loss.
- **Replace glazing** to improve window performance.
- **Install a heat pump** for sustainable heating.
- **Upgrade ventilation** for better air quality.
- **Add PV panels** to generate renewable energy.

This plan ensures **maximum energy efficiency** while remaining **cost-effective** by following the **"Insulate → Ventilate → Generate"** sequence.
