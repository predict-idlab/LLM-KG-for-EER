### Renovation Plan for the Provided Building

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached building
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **EPC Score:** 388 (D Label)
- **Current Issues:** Multiple uninsulated elements (walls, floor above basement, gable roof), outdated heating system
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** No
- **Geothermal Potential:** No

**Motivation:** To improve the EPC label.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First Approach)**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The gable roof is currently not insulated, which leads to significant heat loss. Insulating it will improve thermal retention.
   - **Action:** Install insulation on the interior of the gable roof, ensuring a vapor barrier is included to prevent condensation issues.

2. **Insulate Walls on Interior**
   - **Rationale:** All external walls (Front, Rear, and Right) are full brick and not insulated. Interior wall insulation is more cost-effective and provides substantial thermal retention.
   - **Action:** Add insulation to the interior surfaces of the walls, ensuring proper installation around sockets, pipes, and radiators.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Rationale:** The floor above the basement is currently not insulated, leading to cold drafts and heat loss.
   - **Action:** Install insulation on the floor to prevent heat loss to the basement.

4. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof is moderately insulated but can benefit from additional insulation to further reduce heat loss.
   - **Action:** Add insulation to the flat roof, ensuring proper installation and adherence to building codes.

**Phase 2: Window Performance**
5. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Rationale:** Although the windows are already double-glazed, replacing the glazing with triple glazing will further improve thermal performance and reduce heat loss.
   - **Action:** Replace the existing double glazing with triple glazing, ensuring the frames are in good condition to support the new glazing.

**Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition**
6. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Rationale:** With the building's thermal envelope optimized, the heat demand is significantly lower, making an air-water heat pump a sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing boiler.
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to provide heating and hot water, ensuring it is compatible with the existing emission system.

**Phase 4: Air Quality and Ventilation**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Rationale:** The current ventilation system (System A) is inefficient and does not provide adequate air exchange. Upgrading to a more efficient system will maintain indoor air quality in a now-sealed envelope.
   - **Action:** Replace the existing ventilation system with a more efficient system (such as System C or D), ensuring proper installation and integration with the new heating system.

**Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation**
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system, further improving the EPC label.
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof, ensuring proper orientation and adherence to building codes.

**Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
2. **Insulate Walls on Interior**
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
4. **Insulate Flat Roof**
5. **Replace Glazing**
6. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

This sequence ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before transitioning to a more efficient heating system and generating renewable energy, maximizing energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness.
