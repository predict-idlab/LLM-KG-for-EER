### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (Construction Year: 1862)

**Current EPC Score:** 165 (B Label)
**Building Type:** Detached
**Key Issues:**
- **Uninsulated Cavity Walls** (Front, Left, Rear, Right)
- **Single-Glazed Windows**
- **Ventilation System A** (Natural air supply and exhaust)
- **Old Electric Boiler and Heating System**
- **Potential for PV Panels**

**Objective:** Improve energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective.

---

### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)**
**Goal:** Reduce heat loss through uninsulated elements.

1. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
   - **Walls:** Front, Left, Rear, Right (Cavity Walls)
   - **Rationale:** Cavity wall insulation is cost-effective and minimizes disruption.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduces heat loss through walls.

2. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Windows:** All (Single-Glazed)
   - **Rationale:** Single glazing leads to significant heat loss. Upgrading to double glazing improves thermal performance.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduces heat loss through windows.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the crawl space.
   - **Expected Impact:** Improves comfort and reduces heat loss.

---

### **Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade (Priority: Medium)**
**Goal:** Transition to a more efficient heating system.

4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Rationale:** More energy-efficient than the current electric boiler.
   - **Expected Impact:** Lower energy consumption and improved EPC score.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Rationale:** Optimizes the performance of the new heat pump.
   - **Expected Impact:** Ensures efficient heat distribution.

---

### **Phase 3: Ventilation Upgrade (Priority: Medium)**
**Goal:** Improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency.

6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Current System:** Ventilation System A (Natural)
   - **Recommended System:** Ventilation System C or D (Mechanical)
   - **Rationale:** Mechanical ventilation improves air quality and reduces energy loss.
   - **Expected Impact:** Better indoor air quality and energy efficiency.

---

### **Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)**
**Goal:** Offset energy consumption with renewable energy.

7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The building has potential for PV panels.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduces reliance on grid electricity and lowers energy costs.

---

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Wall Cavity** | Reduces heat loss through walls. |
| **2** | **Replace Glazing** | Improves thermal performance of windows. |
| **3** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Prevents cold drafts from the crawl space. |
| **4** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | More energy-efficient heating system. |
| **5** | **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** | Optimizes heat pump performance. |
| **6** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Improves air quality and energy efficiency. |
| **7** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Offsets energy consumption with renewable energy. |

---

### **Expected Outcome**
By following this renovation plan, the building's EPC score is expected to improve significantly, leading to lower energy consumption, reduced energy costs, and improved thermal comfort. The plan prioritizes cost-effective measures first, ensuring long-term savings and sustainability.
