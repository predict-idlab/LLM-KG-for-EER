### Renovation Plan for Detached House in Ghent, Belgium

#### **Objective:**
Improve the energy performance certificate (EPC) score from 165 (likely an F or G label) to a higher rating through cost-effective, energy-efficient upgrades. The focus will be on maximizing thermal efficiency, optimizing heating systems, and integrating renewable energy sources.

#### **Proposed Renovation Path**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First Approach)**
The primary goal is to minimize heat loss by insulating key areas of the building envelope.

1. **Insulate Exterior Cavity Walls:**
   - The house has cavity walls on all four sides (Front, Rear, Left, Right) that are currently uninsulated.
   - Since these are cavity walls, **cavity wall insulation** is the most effective and cost-efficient solution.
   - This will significantly reduce heat loss through the walls.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space:**
   - The floor above the crawl space is currently not insulated.
   - Adding insulation here will prevent heat loss to the crawl space and improve comfort.
   - Consider using rigid insulation boards or blown-in insulation, depending on accessibility.

3. **Insulate Pitched Roof (if needed):**
   - The main roof is already well-insulated, so no further action is required here.

**Phase 2: Window Performance Upgrades**
The single-glazed windows are a major source of heat loss.

4. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing:**
   - Upgrading to **double or triple glazing** will drastically improve thermal performance.
   - Since the wooden frames are in good condition, they can be retained, reducing costs.

**Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
With a tighter thermal envelope, the heating demand will be lower, allowing for a more efficient heating system.

5. **Replace Boiler with an Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - Since geothermal energy is not suitable and there is no district heating, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the best alternative to the existing boiler.
   - This will significantly reduce energy consumption and lower operating costs.

6. **Upgrade Heating Emission Systems:**
   - Replace old radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors with **low-temperature emission systems** (e.g., modern radiators or underfloor heating designed for heat pumps).
   - This ensures optimal performance with the new heat pump.

**Phase 4: Ventilation System Upgrade**
Once the building is sealed, indoor air quality must be maintained.

7. **Replace Natural Ventilation with a Mechanical Ventilation System:**
   - Upgrade from natural ventilation to a **mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (System C or D)**.
   - This ensures proper air exchange while retaining heat, improving energy efficiency.

**Phase 5: Renewable Energy Integration**
To further reduce energy costs and improve the EPC score.

8. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels:**
   - The house has potential for PV installation.
   - PV panels will generate electricity to power the heat pump and other systems, reducing reliance on the grid.

#### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Cavity Walls** | Reduces heat loss through the largest surface area of the house. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Prevents heat loss to the crawl space and improves comfort. |
| **3** | **Replace Single Glazing with Double/Triple Glazing** | Reduces heat loss through windows. |
| **4** | **Install Air-Water Heat Pump** | Replaces the old boiler with a more efficient, low-carbon heating solution. |
| **5** | **Upgrade Heating Emission Systems** | Ensures compatibility with the heat pump for optimal performance. |
| **6** | **Upgrade Ventilation System** | Maintains indoor air quality in a sealed envelope. |
| **7** | **Install PV Panels** | Generates renewable energy to power the heat pump and reduce energy costs. |

This phased approach ensures that each upgrade builds on the previous one, maximizing energy efficiency while keeping costs manageable.
