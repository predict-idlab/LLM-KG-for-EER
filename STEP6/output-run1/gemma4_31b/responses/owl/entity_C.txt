### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Finalizing the Thermal Envelope (Priority: Low)
While the building is highly efficient, there is one remaining vulnerability in the building fabric.
1.  **Insulate Gable Roof:** The `RoofMain` (Gable Roof) is currently only "Moderately Insulated," whereas the walls and flat roof are "Well Insulated." Upgrading the gable roof insulation will eliminate the final significant point of thermal leakage and ensure a consistent thermal envelope.

#### Phase 2: Emission System Optimization (Priority: Medium)
The building currently uses `ElectricConvectors`, which are costly to operate and inefficient compared to modern alternatives.
2.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** To prepare for a high-efficiency heat pump, the emission system should be transitioned to low-temperature operation. This maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the future heat pump, ensuring the lowest possible operational cost.

#### Phase 3: Sustainable Generation Transition (Priority: High)
The final step is to replace the existing boiler with a sustainable energy source.
3.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since there is no potential for geothermal drilling/captation and no available heat grid, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most effective replacement. Combined with the already excellent insulation and the low-temperature emission upgrade, this will drastically reduce the building's primary energy consumption.
