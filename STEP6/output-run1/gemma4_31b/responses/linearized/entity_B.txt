### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
The building has significant heat loss through several non-insulated elements. Addressing these first provides the highest return on investment and the most immediate impact on the EPC score.
1. **Insulate Flat Roof:** While the main gable roof is moderately insulated, the `RoofExtension` (flat roof) is currently not insulated. Since heat rises, this is a critical priority.
2. **Insulate Walls on Exterior:** Both `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` are full brick and not insulated. As they are not cavity walls, exterior insulation is the most effective way to eliminate thermal bridges and protect the structure.
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The `FloorAboveBasement` is not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents heat loss to the ground and improves the thermal comfort of the lower living areas.
*Note: The windows are already double-glazed and the wooden frames are in a "Good State," so replacing them is not recommended at this time to maintain cost-effectiveness.*

#### Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)
Once the building is sealed, the total heat demand will be significantly lower, allowing for a transition to a high-efficiency, low-temperature system.
4. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** The current radiators should be upgraded to low-temperature emitters. This is a prerequisite for maximizing the efficiency (COP) of a heat pump.
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Given that the site has no potential for geothermal energy and no access to a heat grid, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing boiler.

#### Phase 3: Air Quality and Ventilation (Priority: Medium)
6. **Replace Ventilation System:** The building currently uses Ventilation System A (natural). After sealing the envelope with insulation, the air exchange rate will drop. Upgrading to a more modern system (such as System C or D) is necessary to prevent moisture buildup and maintain indoor air quality.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will generate the renewable electricity required to power the new heat pump and ventilation system, further lowering the EPC score and operational costs.
