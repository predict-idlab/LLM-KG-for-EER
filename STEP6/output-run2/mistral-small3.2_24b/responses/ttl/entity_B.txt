### Renovation Plan for the Provided Building

#### Building Overview:
- **Type:** Attached building
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **Current EPC Score:** 262.0 (C Label)
- **Current Issues:**
  - Non-insulated elements: Floor above basement, flat roof, walls (front and rear)
  - Moderately insulated gable roof
  - Double-glazed windows in good condition
  - Ventilation system type A (natural)
  - Radiator emission system
  - No heat grid available
  - Potential for PV panels

#### Renovation Goals:
1. **Improve EPC Label:** Primary motivation.
2. **Maximize Energy Efficiency:** Reduce heat loss and improve thermal comfort.
3. **Cost-Effectiveness:** Prioritize high-return investments.

#### Proposed Renovation Path:

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First Approach)**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior:**
   - **Action:** Insulate `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** Both walls are full brick and not insulated. Exterior insulation is more effective for non-cavity walls and eliminates thermal bridges.
   - **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in heat loss through walls.

2. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate `RoofExtension` (flat roof).
   - **Rationale:** Heat rises rapidly through flat sections, and insulation here is critical for reducing heat loss.
   - **Expected Impact:** Improved thermal performance of the roof.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the basement and improves overall thermal comfort.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduces heat loss to the basement.

**Phase 2: Ventilation System Upgrade**
4. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from `VentilationSystemA` (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., `VentilationSystemC` or `VentilationSystemD`).
   - **Rationale:** Natural ventilation is inefficient and can lead to uncontrolled air leakage. A mechanical system ensures better air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Expected Impact:** Improved indoor air quality and reduced energy loss.

**Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Rationale:** Geothermal potential is "false" and no heat grid is available. An air-water heat pump is a sustainable and efficient alternative.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduced energy consumption and lower carbon footprint.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Transition the current radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.
   - **Expected Impact:** Improved efficiency of the heating system.

**Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Solar panels will provide renewable electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Expected Impact:** Reduced reliance on grid electricity and lower energy costs.

#### Summary of Optimal Sequence:
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
2. **Insulate Flat Roof**
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
4. **Replace Ventilation System**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

#### Additional Considerations:
- **Historical Protection:** Ensure that any insulation or renovation work complies with historical preservation guidelines if applicable.
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** Prioritize actions with the highest return on investment, such as insulation and ventilation upgrades.
- **Long-Term Benefits:** The proposed sequence ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before transitioning to more efficient heating and ventilation systems.

This plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, tailored to the specific characteristics and needs of the building.
