### Renovation Plan for the Provided Building

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached building
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **Current EPC Score:** 388.0 (D Label)
- **Primary Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Key Issues:**
  - Uninsulated walls (Front, Rear, Right)
  - Uninsulated gable roof
  - Moderately insulated flat roof
  - Double-glazed windows in good condition
  - Potential for PV panels

**Renovation Strategy:**
The renovation will follow the **"Insulate → Ventilate → Generate"** principle, ensuring that the building envelope is optimized before upgrading heating systems.

---

### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)
**Objective:** Reduce heat loss through uninsulated elements.

1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
   - **Rationale:** The gable roof is currently uninsulated, leading to significant heat loss. Insulating it will improve thermal retention.
   - **Ordering:** 28
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Cost:** €60/m²

2. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**
   - **Action:** `insulate wall on exterior` for all three uninsulated walls (Front, Rear, Right).
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is more effective for full brick walls and eliminates thermal bridges.
   - **Ordering:** 40
   - **Nuisance Level:** High
   - **Cost:** €200/m²

3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Action:** `insulate flat roof`
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof is only moderately insulated. Upgrading insulation will further reduce heat loss.
   - **Ordering:** 25
   - **Nuisance Level:** High
   - **Cost:** €50/m²

4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action:** `insulate floor above basement`
   - **Rationale:** The floor above the basement is uninsulated, leading to cold drafts. Insulating it will improve comfort and energy efficiency.
   - **Ordering:** 49
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Cost:** €30/m²

---

### Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade (Medium Priority)
**Objective:** Transition to a more efficient heating system tailored to the sealed envelope.

5. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** `replace ventilation system`
   - **Rationale:** The current natural ventilation system (System A) is inefficient. Upgrading to a mechanical system (System C or D) will improve air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Ordering:** 52
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Cost:** €3,500 (fixed) + €17/m²

6. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`
   - **Rationale:** The site has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid. An air-water heat pump is the most sustainable and efficient alternative to the existing boiler.
   - **Ordering:** 67
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Cost:** €8,000 (fixed) + €35/m²

7. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action:** `place emission system on low temperature`
   - **Rationale:** Before installing a heat pump, the current radiators should be transitioned to low-temperature emissions to maximize the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new generator.
   - **Ordering:** 70
   - **Nuisance Level:** Moderate
   - **Cost:** €100/m²

---

### Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-term)
**Objective:** Offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label.

8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** `place PV-panels on roof`
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Ordering:** 85
   - **Nuisance Level:** Low
   - **Cost:** €275/m²

---

### Summary of Optimal Sequence
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Gable Roof** | Reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| **2** | **Insulate Wall on Exterior** | Eliminates thermal bridges and improves thermal retention. |
| **3** | **Insulate Flat Roof** | Further reduces heat loss through the flat roof. |
| **4** | **Insulate Floor Above Basement** | Prevents cold drafts from the basement. |
| **5** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Improves air quality in a sealed envelope. |
| **6** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | Transition to a sustainable heating system. |
| **7** | **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** | Maximizes the efficiency of the new heat pump. |
| **8** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Offsets the electricity consumption of the heat pump. |

This plan ensures that the building's energy efficiency is maximized in a cost-effective manner, improving the EPC label and reducing long-term operational costs.
