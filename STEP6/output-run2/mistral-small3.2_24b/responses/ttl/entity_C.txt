   - **Action**: Insulate Gable Roof.

2. **Upgrade Heating System**:

3. **Optimize Emission System**:
   - **Action**: Place Emission System on Low Temperature.
   - **Rationale**: Ensuring the emission system is compatible with the new heat pump will maximize its efficiency and performance.

4. **Maintain and Optimize PV Panels**:
   - **Action**: Ensure existing PV panels are functioning optimally.
   - **Rationale**: Given the presence of PV panels, maintaining their efficiency will help offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and other systems.
