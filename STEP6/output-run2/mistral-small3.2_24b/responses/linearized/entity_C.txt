### Renovation Plan

**Current Building Profile:**
- **Type:** Attached building (2023)
- **Net Floor Area:** 159.0 m²
- **EPC Score:** 50 (A Label)
- **Insulation Status:**
  - `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear`: Well-insulated
  - `FloorAboveBasement`: Well-insulated
  - `RoofExtension` (Flat Roof): Well-insulated
  - `RoofMain` (Gable Roof): Moderately insulated
- **Windows:**
  - Double-glazed with wooden frames in good condition
- **Heating System:**
  - Electric convector (floor heating)
- **PV Panels:** Already installed
- **Ventilation System:** Type D (mechanical supply and exhaust)
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve EPC

**Renovation Goals:**
1. **Maximize Energy Efficiency:** Further reduce heat loss and optimize heating performance.
2. **Cost-Effectiveness:** Prioritize interventions that offer the highest return on investment.
3. **Sustainability:** Maintain existing PV panels and transition to a more sustainable heating system.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

**Phase 1: Gable Roof Insulation**
- **Action:** **Insulate Gable Roof**
  - **Rationale:** The `RoofMain` is currently only moderately insulated. Upgrading to full insulation will reduce heat loss through the gable roof, which is a significant heat escape route.
  - **Priority:** High

**Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade**
- **Action:** **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
  - **Rationale:** The current electric convector is inefficient for space heating. An air-water heat pump will provide lower operational costs and align with the building's well-insulated envelope.
  - **Priority:** Medium

**Phase 3: Ventilation System Optimization**
- **Action:** **Replace Ventilation System**
  - **Rationale:** Since the building is already well-sealed, upgrading to a more efficient ventilation system (e.g., System D) will ensure optimal air quality without compromising energy efficiency.
  - **Priority:** Low

**Phase 4: Low-Temperature Emission System**
- **Action:** **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
  - **Rationale:** Before installing the heat pump, the existing emission system should be optimized for low-temperature operation to maximize the heat pump's Coefficient of Performance (COP).
  - **Priority:** Medium

**Phase 5: Roof Underlayment (Optional but Recommended)**
- **Action:** **Place Roof Underlayment**
  - **Rationale:** Ensures complete air- and water-tightness, further improving the building's thermal performance.
  - **Priority:** Low

**Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof** → **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** → **Replace Ventilation System** → **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** → **Place Roof Underlayment** (if needed).

**Rationale for Sequence:**
- **Insulate Gable Roof First:** Addresses the largest remaining thermal bridge (the moderately insulated gable roof).
- **Heat Pump Next:** Replaces the inefficient electric convector with a more sustainable and cost-effective heating solution.
- **Ventilation System Upgrade:** Ensures indoor air quality is maintained in a now-sealed envelope.
- **Low-Temperature Emission System:** Optimizes the heat pump's performance.
- **Roof Underlayment (Optional):** Provides additional protection and insulation if needed.

This plan ensures that the building's energy efficiency is maximized while remaining cost-effective and sustainable.
