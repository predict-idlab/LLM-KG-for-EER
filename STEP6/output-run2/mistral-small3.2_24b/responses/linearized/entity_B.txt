### Renovation Plan

**Building Overview:**
- **Type:** Attached Building
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **Net Floor Area:** 212 m²
- **Current EPC Score:** 262 (C Label)
- **Motivation:** To improve EPC
- **Insulation Status:**
  - **Roof Extension (Flat Roof):** Not Insulated
  - **Walls (Front, Rear):** Not Insulated (Full Brick)
  - **Floor Above Basement:** Not Insulated
- **Windows:**
  - **Glazing Type:** Double Glazing
  - **Frame Material:** Wood (Good State)
- **Heating System:** Boiler with Radiators
- **Ventilation System:** Type A (Natural)
- **PV Potential:** True
- **Heat Grid Availability:** False
- **Geothermal Potential:** False

### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls:**
   - **Action:** Insulate `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** These walls are full brick and not insulated. Exterior insulation is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges.
   - **Priority:** High

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the basement.
   - **Priority:** High

3. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** Heat rises rapidly through flat sections, making roof insulation a priority.
   - **Priority:** High

#### Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Transition
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Rationale:** Since geothermal potential is "false" and there is no heat grid, an air-water heat pump is the most sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing boiler.
   - **Priority:** Medium

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Transition the current radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new generator.
   - **Priority:** Medium

#### Phase 3: Sustainable Energy Generation
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install solar panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** Provides renewable electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Priority:** Long-term

### Summary of Optimal Sequence
1. **Exterior Wall Insulation**
2. **Floor Insulation**
3. **Flat Roof Insulation**
4. **Air-Water Heat Pump Installation**
5. **Low-Temp Emission Upgrade**
6. **PV Panels Installation**

### Additional Notes
- **Windows:** Already have double glazing and are in good condition, so replacement is not recommended at this stage to maintain cost-effectiveness.
- **Ventilation System:** Upgrade to a more efficient system (such as System C or D) after sealing the envelope to maintain indoor air quality.

This plan ensures a structured, cost-effective approach to improving the building's energy efficiency and EPC score.
