### Renovation Plan Analysis

**Building Context:**
- **Type:** Semi-detached house (2017 construction)
- **EPC Score:** 87 (B Label)
- **Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Key Elements:**
  - **Well-insulated walls** (exterior and left wall)
  - **Moderately insulated gable roof**
  - **Well-insulated floor above basement**
  - **Double-glazed windows** (no asbestos detected)
  - **Solar panels** (PVPanels) installed
  - **Heat pump** as the generation system
  - **Ventilation System D** (high efficiency)

**Current Strengths:**
1. **Modern Construction (2017):** The building already meets recent energy standards.
2. **Well-Insulated Envelope:** Exterior walls, left wall, and floor above basement are well-insulated.
3. **Double-Glazed Windows:** Minimal heat loss through windows.
4. **Heat Pump:** High-efficiency heating system.
5. **Ventilation System D:** Optimal indoor air quality management.

**Areas for Improvement:**
1. **Gable Roof Insulation:** Only moderately insulated.
2. **Flat Roof Insulation:** No information on insulation status (assumed to be well-insulated due to recent construction).
3. **Solar Panel Potential:** Current panels may not be optimized for maximum energy production.

### Proposed Renovation Path

**Phase 1: Roof Insulation Optimization**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Action:** Upgrade insulation from "Moderately Insulated" to "Well Insulated."
   - **Rationale:** The gable roof is the only remaining thermal bridge. Eliminating this will further reduce heat loss.

**Phase 2: Solar Energy Optimization**
2. **Check and Optimize PV-Panels**
   - **Action:** Assess the current solar panel orientation, angle, and shading. Optimize placement if necessary.
   - **Rationale:** Ensuring the panels are correctly positioned will maximize renewable energy generation, further reducing reliance on the heat pump.

**Phase 3: Long-Term Sustainability**
3. **Consider Battery Storage**
   - **Action:** Install a home battery system to store excess solar energy for nighttime use.
   - **Rationale:** This reduces grid dependence and maximizes self-consumption of renewable energy.

### Summary of Sequence and Rationale

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Gable Roof** | Eliminates the last significant thermal bridge. |
| **2** | **Optimize PV-Panels** | Maximizes renewable energy generation. |
| **3** | **Install Battery Storage** | Increases energy independence and reduces grid reliance. |

### Additional Notes
- **No Immediate Need for Window Replacement:** Windows are already double-glazed and in good condition.
- **No Need for Heating System Upgrade:** The heat pump is already a high-efficiency system.
- **Ventilation System is Optimal:** System D is already installed, ensuring good air quality.

This plan ensures that the building remains energy-efficient, cost-effective, and sustainable in the long term.
