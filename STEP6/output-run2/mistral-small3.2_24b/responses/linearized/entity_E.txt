### Step 3: Propose Renovation Path
#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization
1. **Insulate Walls On Exterior**:
   - All four walls are not insulated. Exterior insulation is preferred for cavity walls.
   - *Reason*: Reduces thermal bridges and improves overall thermal performance.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**:
   - Currently not insulated. Adding insulation prevents cold air infiltration.
   - *Reason*: Eliminates drafts and improves comfort.

3. **Replace Glazing**:
   - All windows have single glazing. Replacing with double or triple glazing improves thermal performance.
   - *Reason*: Reduces heat loss through windows.

#### Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**:
   - Since geothermal potential is "false" and no heat grid is present, an air-water heat pump is a viable alternative.
   - *Reason*: More efficient than electric convector and compatible with lower temperature emissions.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**:
   - Before installing the heat pump, ensure the emission system (floor heating) is compatible with low temperatures.
   - *Reason*: Maximizes the efficiency of the heat pump.

#### Phase 3: Ventilation Upgrade
6. **Replace Ventilation System**:
   - Upgrade from Type A (natural) to Type C or D (mechanical).
   - *Reason*: Ensures proper air exchange in a now-sealed envelope.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**:
   - Install solar panels to offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - *Reason*: Reduces reliance on grid electricity and improves sustainability.
