### Renovation Plan:

#### **Current State:**
- **EPC Score:** 87 (A Label)
- **Building Type:** Semi-Detached House
- **Construction Year:** 2017
- **Building Elements:**
  - **Roof:** Well insulated flat roof and moderately insulated gable roof.
  - **Walls:** All walls are well insulated.
  - **Windows:** Double glazing.
  - **Floor Above Basement:** Well insulated.
- **Motivation:** To improve the EPC rating.

#### **Recommendations:**
Given that the building is relatively new (2017) and already well-insulated, the primary focus should be on optimizing the existing systems and ensuring compliance with energy regulations.

1. **Ventilation System Upgrade (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Action:** Replace the existing ventilation system with a more efficient one.
   - **Rationale:** Even though the current ventilation system is well-maintained, upgrading it to a more efficient model can further improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency.

2. **Heating System Maintenance (Priority: High)**
   - **Action:** Regular maintenance of the existing heating system.
   - **Rationale:** Regular maintenance ensures the system operates at peak efficiency, reducing energy consumption and extending the system's lifespan.

3. **Energy Audit (Priority: High)**
   - **Action:** Conduct a thorough energy audit to identify any areas for improvement.
   - **Rationale:** An energy audit can reveal hidden inefficiencies or areas where energy savings can be achieved.

4. **Solar Panel Installation (Priority: Long-term)**
   - **Action:** Install solar panels if the potential is confirmed.
   - **Rationale:** Solar panels can significantly reduce the building's carbon footprint and energy costs over the long term.

5. **Monitoring and Feedback Systems (Priority: Medium)**
   - **Action:** Implement smart monitoring systems to track energy usage.
   - **Rationale:** Smart systems provide real-time feedback, helping occupants adjust their energy consumption habits.

#### **Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. **Energy Audit**
2. **Heating System Maintenance**
3. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
4. **Solar Panel Installation**

This plan ensures that the building remains energy-efficient and compliant with regulations while focusing on long-term sustainability.
