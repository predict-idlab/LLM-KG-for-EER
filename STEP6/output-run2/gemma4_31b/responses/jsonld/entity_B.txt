Increasing the airtightness of the building through insulation necessitates a better ventilation strategy to maintain indoor air quality and prevent humidity issues.

6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from the current Ventilation System A (natural) to a more controlled system (such as System C or D) to ensure a healthy living environment without losing the heat gains achieved in Phase 1.
