### Proposed Renovation Path

The windows are currently double-glazed and the frames are in a "Good State" (value 8), meaning a full window replacement is not cost-effective. However, the glazing itself can be improved.

2.  **Replace Glazing:** Upgrading from double glazing to **triple glazing** would further reduce heat loss through the windows. Given the modern construction and existing heat pump, this minimizes the energy required to maintain interior temperatures during winter.

#### Phase 3: Passive Cooling and Comfort (Low Priority/Long-term)
With a highly sealed and insulated envelope, the building may be prone to overheating during summer months, especially with the presence of PV panels and a heat pump.

3.  **Place Sunshade:** To maintain energy efficiency during the summer (reducing the need for active cooling/air conditioning), installing external sunshades is recommended. This is a cost-effective way to reduce solar gain.
