### Renovation Plan:

#### Current Situation:
- **Building Type:** Attached building
- **Construction Year:** 2023
- **EPC Score:** 50
- **Potential for PV:** False
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** False
- **Reason to Change Emission System:** True
- **Owner's Motivation:** Improve EPC

#### Key Observations:
1. **Building Envelope:**
   - The front and rear walls are well-insulated.
   - The floor above the basement is well-insulated.
   - The roof extension (flat roof) is well-insulated.
   - The roof main (gable roof) is moderately insulated.
   - Windows have double glazing and are in good condition.

2. **Heating System:**
   - The current emission system consists of electric convectors and floor heating.
   - There is a reason to change the emission system.

3. **Energy Generation:**
   - There are PV panels installed, but no information on their performance or potential is provided.

#### Proposed Renovation Path:

1. **Improve Roof Insulation:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof (RoofMain) to the same level as the flat roof (RoofExtension).
   - **Rationale:** Ensuring consistent insulation across all roof types will eliminate thermal bridges and improve overall thermal performance.

2. **Upgrade Heating System:**
   - **Action:** Place an air-water heat pump.
   - **Rationale:** Since there is no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid is present, an air-water heat pump is the most efficient and sustainable option to replace the existing electric convectors and floor heating.

3. **Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Consider upgrading the ventilation system to ensure proper air exchange and indoor air quality, especially after sealing the building envelope.
   - **Rationale:** While the current ventilation system (System D) is efficient, it might need adjustments to work optimally with the new heat pump.

#### Summary of Sequence and Rationale:
1. **Insulate the Gable Roof:** This will ensure uniform insulation across the entire roof area, reducing heat loss.
2. **Install an Air-Water Heat Pump:** This will replace the existing electric convectors and floor heating with a more efficient and sustainable heating solution.
3. **Review Ventilation System:** Ensure that the ventilation system is compatible with the new heating system to maintain indoor air quality.

This plan focuses on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, prioritizing actions that provide the highest return on investment and align with the owner's motivation to improve the EPC score.
