### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached Building (Example 3)

#### Building Overview
- **Type:** Semi-detached building (2017)
- **EPC Score:** 87 (A Label)
- **Net Floor Area:** 173.0 m²
- **Current Systems:**
  - Heat Pump (Generation System)
  - Ventilation System D
  - PV Panels (PVPanels)
- **Owner Motivation:** Improve EPC (already in good condition)

#### Existing Strengths
1. **Building Envelope:**
   - Well-insulated walls (Front, Rear, Left)
   - Well-insulated floor above basement
   - Well-insulated flat roof extension
   - Double-glazed windows with wooden frames in good condition

2. **Heating & Ventilation:**
   - Existing heat pump
   - Ventilation System D (already energy-efficient)

3. **Renewable Energy:**
   - Existing PV panels

#### Recommendations
The building is already in good condition with an A-label EPC, so major renovations are not strictly necessary. However, the owner may consider the following upgrades to further improve sustainability, comfort, and energy efficiency:

1. **Roof Insulation Upgrade:**
   - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof (RoofMain)
   - **Rationale:** The gable roof is currently only moderately insulated. Upgrading to well-insulated status will further reduce heat loss and improve thermal comfort.

2. **PV Panel Expansion:**
   - **Action:** Evaluate potential for additional PV panels (since current potential is false)
   - **Rationale:** Expanding the PV system can offset more of the building's electricity consumption, including that of the heat pump and ventilation system.

3. **Ventilation System Optimization:**
   - **Action:** Ensure optimal performance of the existing Ventilation System D
   - **Rationale:** Verify that the system is correctly balanced and that air quality is maintained at a high level.

#### Summary of Proposed Actions
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Gable Roof** | Improve thermal performance and reduce heat loss. |
| **2** | **Expand PV System** | Offset electricity consumption and increase self-sufficiency. |
| **3** | **Optimize Ventilation System** | Ensure indoor air quality and maintain energy efficiency. |

### Conclusion
The building is already in excellent condition with an A-label EPC. The proposed actions are optional and focus on further optimizing sustainability, comfort, and energy efficiency. The sequence follows a logical flow from building envelope improvements to system optimization and renewable energy expansion.
