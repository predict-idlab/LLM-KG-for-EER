The building is a detached building from 1862 with a B label (165). The owner wants to improve the EPC label. Since the building has a high historical value, the owner is likely to want to preserve the character of the building as much as possible.

### Renovation Plan:
The most important step is to insulate the building envelope. This will significantly reduce the energy demand of the building and improve the EPC label. The most cost-effective way to insulate the building envelope is to insulate the walls on the exterior.

1. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**: All four external walls are cavity walls and not insulated. Since they are cavity walls, exterior insulation is the most cost-effective method to eliminate thermal bridges. This will significantly reduce the energy demand of the building and improve the EPC label.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**: The floor above crawl space is currently not insulated. Adding insulation here prevents cold drafts from the crawl space.

3. **Insulate Gable Roof**: The gable roof is currently not insulated. This is a priority as heat rises rapidly through gable sections.

4. **Replace Glazing**: All glazing should be replaced, as all windows still have single glazing. Adding double glazing would drastically improve the thermal performance.

5. **Place Air-Water Heatpump**: Since the site has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid, an Air-Water Heatpump is the most viable and efficient alternative to the current boiler.

6. **Place PV-panels on roof**: The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.

7. **Replace Ventilation System**: Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (such as System C or D). This ensures that the heat gained from the new insulation is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage.

### Summary of Sequence and Rationale

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulation (Exterior Wall $\rightarrow$ Floor $\rightarrow$ Gable Roof)** | Reduces the total energy demand of the house. |
| **2** | **Glazing Replacement** | Reduce the remaining thermal heat losses. |
| **3** | **Heat Pump** | Switches to a sustainable energy source tailored to the new lower heat demand. |
| **4** | **Ventilation Upgrade** | Maintains indoor air quality in a now-sealed envelope. |
| **5** | **PV Panels** | Offsets the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and ventilation system. |
