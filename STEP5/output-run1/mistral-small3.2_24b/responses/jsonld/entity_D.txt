### Renovation Plan for the Building
Based on the provided building information, the following renovation plan is proposed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective:

#### Current Status:
- **Building Type:** Semi-detached house
- **Construction Year:** 2017
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC):** 87 (A label)
- **Current Heating System:** Heat pump
- **Ventilation System:** Ventilation System D
- **Roof:** Gable roof (moderately insulated) and flat roof (well insulated)
- **Walls:** All walls (Front, Rear, Left) are well insulated
- **Floors:** Floor above basement is well insulated
- **Windows:** All windows are double-glazed and in good condition
- **Potential for PV:** No
- **Reason to Change Emission System:** No

#### Proposed Renovation Path:
Since the building already has a good EPC rating (A label) and the building envelope is well insulated, the focus should be on maintaining and optimizing the current systems. The primary goal is to ensure the longevity and efficiency of the existing infrastructure.

1. **Maintenance and Optimization of Existing Systems:**
   - **Maintain the Heat Pump:** Ensure regular maintenance of the heat pump to keep it operating at peak efficiency. This includes checking refrigerant levels, cleaning filters, and inspecting the system for any wear and tear.
   - **Optimize the Ventilation System:** Ensure that the ventilation system is operating efficiently. This may include regular cleaning of ducts and filters, and possibly upgrading to a more efficient model if necessary.

2. **Monitoring and Upgrading as Needed:**
   - **Monitor Energy Consumption:** Regularly monitor the energy consumption of the building to identify any areas where efficiency can be improved.
   - **Upgrade as Needed:** If any components of the heating or ventilation systems start to fail or become less efficient, consider upgrading to more energy-efficient models.

#### Summary of Sequence and Rationale:
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Maintain the Heat Pump** | Ensure the current efficient heating system remains at peak performance. |
| **2** | **Optimize the Ventilation System** | Maintain indoor air quality and ensure the system is operating efficiently. |
| **3** | **Monitor Energy Consumption** | Identify areas for potential efficiency improvements. |
| **4** | **Upgrade as Needed** | Replace any failing or inefficient components with more energy-efficient models. |

This plan ensures that the building remains energy-efficient and cost-effective to operate, while minimizing the need for major renovations. Regular maintenance and monitoring will help extend the lifespan of the existing systems and ensure they continue to perform optimally.
