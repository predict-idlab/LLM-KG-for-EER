### Renovation Plan

#### **Building Overview:**
- **Building Type:** Semi-detached
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **Current EPC Score:** 388 (D Label)
- **Owner's Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Heat Grid Presence:** No
- **Geothermal Potential:** No

#### **Key Issues:**
1. **Thermal Envelope:**
   - **Roof (Flat):** Moderately Insulated
   - **Walls (Front, Rear, Right):** Not Insulated
   - **Floor Above Basement:** Not Insulated
   - **Windows:** Double-Glazed (Good State)

2. **Heating System:**
   - **Current System:** Boiler
   - **Reason to Change:** Yes
   - **Potential for Upgrade:** Air-Water Heat Pump

3. **Ventilation System:**
   - **Current System:** Type A (Natural)
   - **Potential for Upgrade:** Type C or D (Mechanical)

4. **Energy Generation:**
   - **Potential for PV:** Yes

#### **Recommended Renovation Path:**
The renovation should follow the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** sequence to maximize energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness.

##### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior:**
   - **Action:** Exterior wall insulation for `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, and `Wall_Right`.
   - **Rationale:** Eliminates thermal bridges and reduces heat loss significantly.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Add insulation to `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the basement.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Add insulation to `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** Heat rises rapidly through flat sections, making roof insulation a priority.

##### **Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Transition (Medium Priority)**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an Air-Water Heat Pump to replace the existing boiler.
   - **Rationale:** More energy-efficient and suitable for the lower heat demand after insulation.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade the current radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the Coefficient of Performance (COP) of the new heat pump.

##### **Phase 3: Ventilation System Upgrade (Medium Priority)**
6. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from `VentilationSystemA` to `VentilationSystemC` or `VentilationSystemD`.
   - **Rationale:** Ensures proper air exchange in a sealed envelope.

##### **Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-term Priority)**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install solar panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** Provides renewable electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system.

#### **Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
**Exterior Wall Insulation** $\rightarrow$ **Floor Insulation** $\rightarrow$ **Flat Roof Insulation** $\rightarrow$ **Air-Water Heat Pump** $\rightarrow$ **Low-Temp Emission Upgrade** $\rightarrow$ **Ventilation Upgrade** $\rightarrow$ **PV Panels**.

This plan ensures a systematic approach to improving the building's energy efficiency while considering cost-effectiveness and the owner's motivation.
