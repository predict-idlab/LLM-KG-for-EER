### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (1862, 136 m², EPC 165)

**Objective:** Improve energy efficiency and EPC label cost-effectively through a phased approach.

---

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
**Goal:** Minimize heat loss through the building envelope.

1. **Insulate Wall Cavity (Front, Left, Rear)**
   - *Rationale:* All walls are cavity walls with no insulation. Cavity wall insulation is cost-effective and reduces thermal bridges.
   - *Priority:* High (immediate impact on heat retention).

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - *Rationale:* The crawl space floor is uninsulated, leading to cold drafts. Insulation here improves comfort and reduces heat loss.
   - *Priority:* Medium.

3. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - *Rationale:* The roof is well-insulated, but gable sections may have gaps. Adding insulation here completes the thermal envelope.
   - *Priority:* Low (secondary to walls and floor).

---

#### **Phase 2: Window Performance**
**Goal:** Reduce heat loss through single-glazed windows.

4. **Replace Glazing (All Windows)**
   - *Rationale:* Single glazing leads to significant heat loss. Upgrading to double or triple glazing will improve thermal performance.
   - *Priority:* High (critical for comfort and efficiency).

---

#### **Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
**Goal:** Replace inefficient electric convector with a low-temperature, high-efficiency system.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - *Rationale:* The current boiler is inefficient. An air-water heat pump is sustainable and suitable for the building’s new lower heat demand.
   - *Priority:* Medium.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - *Rationale:* Prepares the system for the heat pump by ensuring compatibility with low-temperature operation.
   - *Priority:* Medium.

---

#### **Phase 4: Ventilation & Air Quality**
**Goal:** Ensure proper air exchange in a sealed envelope.

7. **Replace Ventilation System (A → C or D)**
   - *Rationale:* The current natural ventilation (System A) is inadequate for a sealed envelope. Upgrading to System C or D ensures controlled air exchange.
   - *Priority:* Low (post-insulation).

---

#### **Phase 5: Renewable Energy Generation**
**Goal:** Offset electricity consumption and improve EPC label.

8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - *Rationale:* The building has confirmed PV potential. Installing solar panels will provide renewable electricity for the heat pump and reduce grid dependency.
   - *Priority:* Low (long-term investment).

---

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1** | **Insulate Wall Cavity** | Reduces heat loss through walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Eliminates cold drafts. |
| **3** | **Replace Glazing** | Reduces heat loss through windows. |
| **4** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | Replaces inefficient boiler. |
| **5** | **Place Low-Temperature Emission System** | Prepares for heat pump. |
| **6** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Ensures air quality in sealed envelope. |
| **7** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Provides renewable electricity. |

This plan follows the **"Insulate → Ventilate → Generate"** principle, ensuring cost-effective energy efficiency improvements.
