### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (1862)

**Current EPC Score:** 165 (B Label)
**Primary Goal:** Improve EPC and energy efficiency in a cost-effective manner.

#### Phase 1: Insulation and Thermal Envelope (High Priority)
1. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
   - **Action:** Insulate the cavities of `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Rear`, and `Wall_Right` (all cavity walls).
   - **Rationale:** Cavity wall insulation is cost-effective and significantly reduces heat loss through external walls.
   - **Note:** Ensure the cavity is suitable for insulation (check for contamination, adequate width, etc.).

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - **Action:** Add insulation to `FloorAboveCrawlSpace`.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents heat loss to the unconditioned crawl space below.

#### Phase 2: Window Upgrades (Medium Priority)
3. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action:** Upgrade `WindowGlazingBedrooms` and `WindowGlazingLivingArea` from single to double or triple glazing.
   - **Rationale:** Single glazing contributes to high heat loss. Double glazing will improve thermal performance.

#### Phase 3: Heating System Transition (Medium Priority)
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler (`Generation_System`).
   - **Rationale:** More energy-efficient than the current system, especially with a well-insulated envelope.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action:** Adapt the emission system (radiators, floor heating) to operate at lower temperatures.
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the efficiency of the heat pump.

#### Phase 4: Ventilation and Air Quality (Low Priority)
6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from `VentilationSystemA` (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., `VentilationSystemC` or `D`).
   - **Rationale:** Ensures proper air exchange in a now-sealed envelope.

#### Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation (Optional)
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the gable roof (`RoofMain`).
   - **Rationale:** Offsets the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.

### Summary of Sequence and Rationale
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Wall Cavity** | Reduces heat loss through external walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Prevents heat loss to the crawl space. |
| **3** | **Replace Glazing** | Improves thermal performance of windows. |
| **4** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | More efficient heating with a lower heat demand. |
| **5** | **Low-Temperature Emission Upgrade** | Maximizes heat pump efficiency. |
| **6** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Maintains air quality in a sealed envelope. |
| **7** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Generates renewable energy for the heat pump. |

### Notes:
- **Ordering:** Follow the sequence to maximize cost-effectiveness and energy savings.
- **Permits:** Check local regulations for any required permits, especially for historical buildings.
- **Asbestos:** Ensure no asbestos is present before renovations, particularly when working on the roof or walls.
