### Renovation Plan for the Attached Building (1832, 212 m², EPC 262)

#### **1. Insulate Thermal Envelope (Priority: High)**
- **Insulate Flat Roof (RoofExtension)**
  - Currently uninsulated, which leads to significant heat loss.
  - **Action:** Insulate the flat roof to reduce upward heat transfer.
  - **Rationale:** Flat roofs lose a lot of heat due to convection; insulation here is cost-effective.

- **Insulate Wall on Exterior (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear)**
  - Both walls are full brick and uninsulated.
  - **Action:** Apply exterior insulation to eliminate thermal bridges.
  - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is more effective than interior insulation for historic buildings.

- **Insulate Floor Above Basement (FloorAboveBasement)**
  - Currently uninsulated.
  - **Action:** Add insulation to prevent cold air infiltration from the basement.
  - **Rationale:** Prevents drafts and improves overall thermal comfort.

#### **2. Upgrade Heating System (Priority: Medium)**
- **Place Air-Water Heat Pump (Replace Boiler)**
  - The current boiler is inefficient.
  - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump to reduce energy consumption.
  - **Rationale:** Heat pumps are more efficient than boilers, especially in well-insulated buildings.

- **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
  - **Action:** Upgrade the radiators to low-temperature emissions.
  - **Rationale:** Improves the efficiency of the heat pump.

#### **3. Improve Ventilation (Priority: Medium)**
- **Replace Ventilation System (VentilationSystemA → System D)**
  - The current natural ventilation system is inefficient.
  - **Action:** Upgrade to a mechanical ventilation system (e.g., System D).
  - **Rationale:** Ensures proper air exchange without heat loss.

#### **4. Add Renewable Energy (Priority: Long-term)**
- **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
  - The building has potential for PV.
  - **Action:** Install solar panels to offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump.
  - **Rationale:** Reduces reliance on grid electricity and lowers energy costs.

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
1. **Insulate Flat Roof**
2. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
6. **Replace Ventilation System**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**

This plan follows the **"Insulate → Ventilate → Generate"** principle, ensuring that the building is optimized for energy efficiency before transitioning to renewable energy sources. The sequence maximizes cost-effectiveness while improving the EPC label.
