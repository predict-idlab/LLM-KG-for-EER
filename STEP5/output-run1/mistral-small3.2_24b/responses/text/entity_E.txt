Given the specific characteristics of your 1862 detached house in Ghent, Belgium, with an EPC score of 165 and cavity walls, here’s a tailored, cost-effective renovation plan prioritizing energy efficiency:

### **Proposed Renovation Path**

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric-First Approach)**
1. **Insulate Cavity Walls (Exterior or Interior)**
   - Since the exterior walls are cavity walls, **external wall insulation (EWI)** is highly recommended to avoid reducing interior space and to improve thermal performance. Alternatively, interior insulation can be considered if EWI is not feasible.
   - **Rationale:** Cavity wall insulation is one of the most cost-effective ways to reduce heat loss in older homes.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - If the floor above the crawl space is not insulated, adding insulation (e.g., rigid foam boards or mineral wool) will prevent heat loss to the unconditioned crawl space.
   - **Rationale:** Cold floors contribute to discomfort and energy loss.

3. **Upgrade Roof Insulation (If Needed)**
   - Although the pitched roof is already well-insulated, check for any gaps or areas needing reinforcement, especially around eaves and chimneys.
   - **Rationale:** Ensures no unintended heat loss through the roof.

#### **Phase 2: Window Upgrades**
4. **Replace Single Glazing with Double or Triple Glazing**
   - Since the windows have single glazing, upgrading to **double glazing (or triple if budget allows)** will significantly reduce heat loss.
   - **Rationale:** Single glazing is a major source of heat loss in older homes.

#### **Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
5. **Install Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - Replace the old boiler with an **air-water heat pump**, which is efficient and suitable for detached houses.
   - **Rationale:** Heat pumps are more energy-efficient than traditional boilers, especially in well-insulated homes.

6. **Upgrade Emission Systems for Low-Temperature Heating**
   - Ensure radiators and underfloor heating are compatible with low-temperature heating to maximize heat pump efficiency.
   - **Rationale:** Optimizes heat pump performance and reduces energy consumption.

#### **Phase 4: Ventilation & Air Quality**
7. **Upgrade to Mechanical Ventilation with Heat Recovery (MVHR)**
   - Replace the basic natural ventilation system with **MVHR (System C or D)** to maintain indoor air quality while retaining heat.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents moisture buildup and improves energy efficiency in a sealed envelope.

#### **Phase 5: Renewable Energy Generation (Optional but Recommended)**
8. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - Given the potential for PV installation, adding solar panels will offset electricity costs and further improve the EPC score.
   - **Rationale:** Generates clean energy to power the heat pump and reduce reliance on the grid.

| **Sequence** | **Action** | **Rationale** |
