### Renovation Plan

**Objective:**
To improve the EPC label of the building from **C (Score 50)** to **A+**, ensuring energy efficiency while maintaining cost-effectiveness. The renovation will follow a structured, multi-phase approach to optimize thermal performance and heating systems.

---

### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric-First Approach)**
The primary goal is to **minimize heat loss** through the building envelope. This phase focuses on the most cost-effective improvements to reduce energy demand before upgrading heating systems.

1. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** The roof is currently not insulated, leading to significant heat loss. Insulating it will reduce thermal bridges and improve overall thermal performance.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Reduces heat loss through the roof, improving the overall energy efficiency of the building.

2. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** Both walls are full brick and currently not insulated. Exterior insulation is more cost-effective than cavity insulation and provides better thermal retention.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Reduces heat loss through the walls, improving thermal comfort and energy efficiency.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action:** Insulate the `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** The floor above the basement is currently not insulated, leading to cold drafts. Insulating it will eliminate this heat loss pathway.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Improves thermal comfort in the living areas and reduces overall energy demand.

---

### **Phase 2: Window Performance Enhancement**
The next step is to **enhance the performance of the windows**, as they are currently a significant source of heat loss.

4. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action:** Replace the glazing on all windows (both `WindowsLivingArea` and `WindowsBedrooms`).
   - **Rationale:** The windows currently have **double glazing**, but replacing them with **triple glazing** will further improve thermal performance and reduce heat loss.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Significantly reduces heat loss through the windows, improving thermal comfort and energy efficiency.

---

### **Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition**
With a **sealed and insulated thermal envelope**, the building is now ready for a **high-efficiency heating system** that operates at lower temperatures.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Install an **Air-Water Heat Pump** as the primary heating source.
   - **Rationale:** The site has **no potential for geothermal energy** and **no heat grid**, making an Air-Water Heat Pump the most sustainable and efficient option. It is tailored to the lower heat demand of the newly insulated building.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Provides sustainable, efficient heating that reduces energy consumption and improves the EPC label.

---

### **Phase 4: Air Quality and Ventilation**
Once the building is **sealed and insulated**, the air exchange rate decreases, making a **modern ventilation system** necessary to maintain air quality.

6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from the current **Ventilation System D** to a more efficient system (e.g., System C or System B).
   - **Rationale:** The current system may not be sufficient to maintain proper air quality in a now-sealed envelope. Upgrading ensures that the heat gained from insulation is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Maintains indoor air quality and ensures proper ventilation without compromising thermal performance.

---

### **Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation**
To **offset the electricity consumption** of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label, renewable energy generation is recommended.

7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install **PV-Panels** on the `RoofMain` or `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** The building has **confirmed potential for PV**. Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Reduces reliance on grid electricity, lowers energy costs, and contributes to a higher EPC label.

---

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
The renovation plan follows a **structured, phased approach** to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective:

1. **Insulate Thermal Envelope** (Roof, Walls, Floor)
2. **Enhance Window Performance** (Replace Glazing)
3. **Transition to High-Efficiency Heating** (Air-Water Heat Pump)
4. **Upgrade Ventilation System** (Maintain Air Quality)
5. **Generate Renewable Energy** (PV-Panels)

This sequence ensures that the building's thermal performance is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for oversized and more expensive HVAC equipment.

By following this plan, the building's **EPC label will improve from C to A+**, ensuring **energy efficiency, cost-effectiveness, and long-term sustainability**.
