Since the "fabric" of the house is already optimized, the renovation focus shifts from insulation to **system optimization**. The primary inefficiency lies in the heating generation and the use of electric convectors, which are energy-intensive.

### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Targeted Envelope Refinement (Priority: Low)
The building is already well-insulated, but there is one minor area for improvement to ensure the thermal envelope is perfectly sealed.
1. **Top-up Pitched Roof Insulation:** While the other elements are "well insulated," the main pitched roof is described as having "moderate" insulation. Increasing the insulation level here will further reduce heat loss through the highest point of the house, ensuring the heating system can operate at the lowest possible temperature.

#### Phase 2: Heating System Modernization (Priority: High)
The current reliance on a boiler and electric convectors is the weakest link in this home's energy profile. Electric convectors are highly inefficient and will negatively impact operational costs.
2. **Install Air-Water Heat Pump:** Given that the house is already well-insulated and has underfloor heating (a low-temperature emission system), it is a perfect candidate for an Air-Water Heat Pump. This replaces the boiler with a sustainable, high-efficiency alternative.
3. **Eliminate Electric Convectors:** Electric convectors should be removed. To maintain comfort in those areas, the underfloor heating circuit should be extended to these rooms, or high-efficiency low-temperature radiators should be installed and connected to the new heat pump.

#### Phase 3: System Integration & Monitoring (Priority: Medium)
Since the house already has PV panels and a System D ventilation unit, the goal is to synchronize these assets.
4. **Smart Energy Management:** Implement a smart home energy management system (HEMS) to synchronize the heat pump's operation with the peak production hours of the existing PV panels. This maximizes "self-consumption" and minimizes reliance on the grid.
