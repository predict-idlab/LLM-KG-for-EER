Based on the provided data, here is the recommended strategic plan for the renovation and improvement of the property.

### **Overview of Current Status**
The property currently has a mix of efficiency levels. While some elements are in place, the primary goal is to transition from a standard setup to a high-efficiency, sustainable model. The focus is on reducing energy consumption, improving thermal comfort, and lowering the carbon footprint.

---

### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope & Insulation (The "Passive" Layer)**
*Goal: Stop energy loss and maintain internal temperatures with minimal mechanical effort.*

1.  **Wall & Roof Insulation:** Prioritize the insulation of the attic and external walls. This is the most cost-effective way to reduce heating and cooling costs.
2.  **Window Upgrades:** Replace single-pane or outdated windows with double or triple-glazing (Low-E coating) to prevent thermal bridging.
3.  **Air Sealing:** Seal gaps around doors, windows, and utility penetrations to eliminate drafts (infiltration).

### **Phase 2: HVAC & Energy Systems (The "Active" Layer)**
*Goal: Replace inefficient, fossil-fuel-based systems with high-efficiency electric alternatives.*

1.  **Heat Pump Installation:** Replace traditional boilers/furnaces with an **Air-Source Heat Pump (ASHP)** for terms of heating and cooling. This significantly reduces CO2 emissions.
2.  **Ventilation with Heat Recovery (MVHR):** Install a Mechanical Ventilation with Heat Recovery system. This ensures a constant supply of fresh air while recovering heat from the exhausted air.
3.  **Smart Thermostats:** Implement zoned heating and smart controls to ensure energy is only used in occupied rooms.

### **Phase 3: Renewable Energy Integration**
*Goal: Transition to net-zero energy production.*

1.  **Solar PV Array:** Install rooftop solar panels to generate electricity for the heat pump and general household use.
2.  **Battery Storage:** Add a battery storage system (e.g., Lithium-ion) to store excess solar energy generated during the day for use at night.
3.  **EV Charging Station:** Install a Level 2 electric vehicle charger to integrate the home's energy system with transport.

---

### **Summary of Priority & Impact**

| Priority | Action | Impact | Primary Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High** | Insulation & Sealing | Very High | Immediate reduction in energy bills |
| **Medium** | Heat Pump & MVHR | High | Decarbonization & Air Quality |
| **Low** | Solar PV & Battery | Medium | Energy Independence |

### **Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for Success**
* **U-Value Reduction:** Lowering the thermal transmittance of walls and windows.
* **Energy Use Intensity (EUI):** Reduction in kWh per square meter per year.
* **Carbon Footprint:** Reduction in annual tonnes of CO2 equivalent.
