### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached Building (1951, EPC Score: 388)

#### Key Observations:
- **Thermal Envelope Issues**:
  - All external walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Right`, `Wall_Rear`) are **not insulated**.
  - `RoofMain` (Gable Roof) and `RoofExtension` (Flat Roof) have **poor insulation** (one is "Not Insulated," the other "Moderately Insulated").
  - `FloorAboveBasement` is **not insulated**.
- **Windows**:
  - Double-glazed windows with wooden frames in good condition.
  - No urgent need for replacement (cost-effective to retain).
- **Heating & Emission**:
  - Current system: **Boiler + Radiators**.
  - Ventilation: **System A (natural)**, which is inefficient for a sealed envelope.
- **Potential for PV**: **True** (roof suitable for solar panels).
- **Owner's Goal**: Improve EPC (currently D Label).

---

#### **Proposed Renovation Path**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
1. **Insulate Exterior Walls**
   - *Action*: Insulate `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Right`, and `Wall_Rear` **on the exterior**.
   - *Rationale*: Full brick walls cannot be cavity-insulated. Exterior insulation provides the highest thermal performance and eliminates thermal bridges.
   - *Cost-Effectiveness*: Lower nuisance level than interior insulation (no relocation of sockets/pipes).

2. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - *Action*: Insulate `RoofMain` (gable roof).
   - *Rationale*: The gable roof has **no insulation** and is a major heat loss area.
   - *Note*: Install vapour barrier on the warm side to prevent condensation.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - *Action*: Insulate `RoofExtension` (flat roof).
   - *Rationale*: The flat roof is **moderately insulated** but needs improvement to match the new thermal envelope standards.
   - *Note*: Opt for an **inverted roof structure** (insulation on top of roof covering).

4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - *Action*: Insulate `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - *Rationale*: Eliminates cold drafts from the basement and improves overall comfort.

---

**Phase 2: Heating System Transition**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - *Action*: Replace the boiler with an **Air-Water Heat Pump**.
   - *Rationale*:
     - Geothermal potential is **false**; heat grid is **not present**.
     - Heat pumps are **highly efficient** for sealed, insulated buildings.
   - *Note*: Requires a **low-temperature emission system** (see next step).

6. **Place Low-Temperature Emission**
   - *Action*: Upgrade radiators to **low-temperature emissions** (e.g., underfloor heating or low-temp radiators).
   - *Rationale*: Maximizes the **Coefficient of Performance (COP)** of the heat pump.

---

**Phase 3: Ventilation Upgrade**
7. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - *Action*: Upgrade from **System A (natural)** to **System C or D (mechanical)**.
   - *Rationale*:
     - A sealed envelope reduces natural air exchange, necessitating a **controlled ventilation system**.
     - System C/D ensures **air quality** while minimizing heat loss.

---

**Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - *Action*: Install **PV panels** on the roof.
   - *Rationale*:
     - The building has **confirmed PV potential**.
     - Solar energy offsets the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.

---

#### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
1. **Exterior Wall Insulation** → **Gable Roof Insulation** → **Flat Roof Insulation** → **Floor Insulation**
2. **Air-Water Heat Pump** → **Low-Temperature Emission Upgrade**
3. **Ventilation System Replacement**
4. **PV Panel Installation**

This sequence follows the **"Insulate → Ventilate → Generate"** principle, ensuring energy efficiency while maintaining cost-effectiveness.
