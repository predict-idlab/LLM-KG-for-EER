### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (1862, 136m², EPC: 165)

**Objective:** Improve EPC rating by optimizing thermal performance and transitioning to a sustainable heating system.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric-First Approach)**
1. **Insulate Wall Cavity** (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Left, Wall_Right)
   - *Rationale:* All walls are cavity walls with "Not Insulated" grade. Insulating the cavity is cost-effective and eliminates thermal bridges.
   - *Note:* Verify structural suitability and contamination before injection.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - *Rationale:* Prevents cold air infiltration from the crawl space, reducing heat loss.

3. **Insulate Attic Floor**
   - *Rationale:* Minimizes heat loss through the roof, as heat naturally rises.

**Phase 2: Window Upgrades**
4. **Replace Glazing** (WindowsLivingArea, WindowsBedrooms)
   - *Rationale:* Single glazing is inefficient. Replace with double or triple glazing to reduce heat loss through windows.

**Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - *Rationale:* Replaces the existing boiler with a more energy-efficient system. Suitable for the building's size and lack of geothermal potential.

6. **Place Low-Temperature Emission System**
   - *Rationale:* Ensures compatibility with the new heat pump, maximizing efficiency.

**Phase 4: Ventilation Upgrade**
7. **Replace Ventilation System** (VentilationSystemA → VentilationSystemB or C)
   - *Rationale:* Improves air quality and maintains indoor comfort after sealing the envelope.

**Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation**
8. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - *Rationale:* Offsets electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.

**Summary of Optimal Sequence:**
1. Insulate Wall Cavity → 2. Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space → 3. Insulate Attic Floor → 4. Replace Glazing → 5. Place Air-Water Heat Pump → 6. Place Low-Temperature Emission → 7. Replace Ventilation System → 8. Place PV-Panels on Roof.

**Cost-Effectiveness Notes:**
- Prioritize cavity wall insulation due to low cost and high impact.
- Glazing replacement is necessary but should be done after insulation to avoid cold bridges.
- PV panels are a long-term investment to reduce energy costs and improve EPC.

**Permit Requirements:**
- Insulation work and ventilation system replacement may require permits depending on local regulations.

**Next Steps:**
- Conduct a detailed energy audit to confirm insulation feasibility and identify asbestos risks.
- Consult with a certified installer for heat pump sizing and system integration.

This plan adheres to the "Insulate → Ventilate → Generate" principle and aligns with the building's historical context and current EPC rating.
