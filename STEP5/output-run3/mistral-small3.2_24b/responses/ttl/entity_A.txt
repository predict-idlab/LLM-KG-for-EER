### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### Building Overview
- **Type:** Semi-detached building
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **Current EPC Score:** 388.0 (D Label)
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** No
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve EPC

#### Key Issues
1. **Insulation:** Multiple elements are not insulated (walls, floor above basement, gable roof).
2. **Ventilation:** Current system is type A (natural), which is less efficient.
3. **Heating System:** Current boiler and emission system may not be energy-efficient.
4. **PV Potential:** Available, but not utilized.

#### Proposed Renovation Path

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric First Approach)**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior:**
   - **Elements:** `Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is more effective for full brick walls and eliminates thermal bridges.
   - **Cost:** High (€200/m²), but long-term savings on heating.

2. **Insulate Gable Roof:**
   - **Element:** `RoofMain`
   - **Rationale:** Currently not insulated; critical to prevent heat loss through the roof.
   - **Cost:** €60/m².

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Element:** `FloorAboveBasement`
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold drafts from the basement.
   - **Cost:** €30/m².

**Phase 2: Window Performance (If Needed)**
- **Current Status:** Double glazing, good condition.
- **Action:** No immediate replacement needed; maintain and ensure seals are intact.

**Phase 3: Heating and Emission System Transition**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Rationale:** Replaces the existing boiler with a more efficient system.
   - **Cost:** €8,000 (fixed) + €35/m².

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Rationale:** Optimizes the heat pump's performance.
   - **Cost:** €100/m².

**Phase 4: Ventilation Upgrade**
6. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Current System:** Type A (natural)
   - **Upgrade To:** Type C or D (mechanical)
   - **Rationale:** Ensures better air quality and efficiency.
   - **Cost:** €3,500 (fixed) + €17/m².

**Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Rationale:** Utilizes the building's PV potential to offset electricity consumption.
   - **Cost:** €275/m².

#### Summary of Optimal Sequence
1. **Exterior Wall Insulation** → 2. **Gable Roof Insulation** → 3. **Floor Insulation** → 4. **Air-Water Heat Pump** → 5. **Low-Temp Emission Upgrade** → 6. **Ventilation Upgrade** → 7. **PV Panels**.

#### Rationale for Sequence
- **Fabric First Approach:** Addressing the thermal envelope first reduces the overall heat demand, making subsequent upgrades more effective.
- **Heating System Upgrade:** Transitioning to a heat pump after insulation ensures the system is appropriately sized.
- **Ventilation and PV:** Final steps to ensure long-term energy efficiency and sustainability.

This plan prioritizes cost-effective measures that provide the highest return on investment while significantly improving the building's energy performance.
