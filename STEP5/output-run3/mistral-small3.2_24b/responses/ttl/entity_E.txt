### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (Construction Year: 1862, EPC Score: 165)

Given the building's characteristics (detached, old construction, poor insulation, single glazing, and an existing electric convector and radiator system), the renovation plan focuses on improving thermal performance, reducing energy consumption, and enhancing the overall energy efficiency of the property.

#### **Phase 1: Insulation and Thermal Envelope Optimization**
1. **Insulate Wall Cavity**
   - **Action**: Insulate the cavity walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right).
   - **Rationale**: The walls are cavity walls and not insulated. Injecting insulation into the cavity is a cost-effective way to improve thermal performance without significant structural changes.
   - **Cost**: €22/m².

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the crawl space.
   - **Rationale**: Uninsulated floors can lead to significant heat loss. Insulating this floor will improve comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Cost**: €30/m².

3. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action**: Replace single glazing with double glazing.
   - **Rationale**: Single glazing has poor thermal and acoustic properties. Upgrading to double glazing will significantly reduce heat loss through windows.
   - **Cost**: €200/m².

#### **Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade**
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump.
   - **Rationale**: The current heating system (electric convector and radiator) is inefficient. An air-water heat pump is a sustainable and efficient alternative, especially after improving the building's thermal envelope.
   - **Cost**: €8,000 (fixed) + €35/m².

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the emission system to operate at lower temperatures.
   - **Rationale**: Ensures compatibility with the new heat pump and maximizes its efficiency.
   - **Cost**: €100/m².

#### **Phase 3: Ventilation and Air Quality**
6. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action**: Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., System C or D).
   - **Rationale**: Improves indoor air quality and ensures proper ventilation in a now-sealed envelope.
   - **Cost**: €3,500 (fixed) + €17/m².

#### **Phase 4: Renewable Energy Integration**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale**: The building has potential for PV. Generating solar energy will offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and other appliances.
   - **Cost**: €275/m².

### **Summary of Sequence and Rationale**

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Wall Cavity** | Reduces heat loss through external walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Prevents heat loss to the crawl space. |
| **3** | **Replace Glazing** | Improves thermal performance of windows. |
| **4** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | Replaces inefficient heating system with a sustainable alternative. |
| **5** | **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** | Ensures compatibility with the heat pump. |
| **6** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Maintains indoor air quality in a sealed envelope. |
| **7** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Generates renewable energy to power the heat pump and other systems. |

### **Expected Outcomes**
- **Improved EPC Label**: The combination of insulation, efficient heating, and renewable energy generation will significantly improve the EPC score.
- **Reduced Energy Consumption**: Lower heat loss and more efficient systems will reduce energy bills.
- **Enhanced Comfort**: Better insulation and controlled ventilation will improve thermal comfort.

This plan ensures a cost-effective and practical approach to maximizing energy efficiency while respecting the building's constraints and historical context.
