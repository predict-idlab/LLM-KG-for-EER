### Renovation Plan

**Property Details:**
- **Type:** Attached building (pre-1900)
- **EPC Label:** C (262 kWh/m²)
- **Primary Motivation:** Improve EPC label
- **Key Issues:**
  - Non-insulated exterior walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`)
  - Non-insulated floor above basement (`FloorAboveBasement`)
  - Non-insulated flat roof (`RoofExtension`)
  - Current ventilation system is `VentilationSystemA` (natural)
  - Double-glazed windows in good condition
  - Potential for PV generation (`true`)
  - No heat grid available (`false`)

**Renovation Strategy:**
To maximize energy efficiency and improve the EPC label in a cost-effective manner, the renovation must follow the **"Insulate → Ventilate → Generate"** sequence. This ensures that the building's thermal envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems, preventing the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system.

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)**
The primary goal here is to stop heat leakage. Since the building is an attached house with several uninsulated elements, this phase offers the highest return on investment.

1. **Insulate Wall On Exterior (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`):**
   - Both walls are full brick and not insulated.
   - Exterior insulation is recommended to eliminate thermal bridges and improve thermal performance.
   - Expected to have a significant impact on the EPC label.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement (`FloorAboveBasement`):**
   - Currently not insulated.
   - Adding insulation here eliminates "cold feet" and reduces heat loss to the basement.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof (`RoofExtension`):**
   - Currently not insulated.
   - Insulating the flat roof is one of the most effective ways to lower energy consumption, especially since heat rises rapidly through flat sections.

**Phase 2: Window Performance (Priority: Medium)**
The window performance is already relatively good (double-glazed windows in good condition), so no immediate action is recommended for the windows.

**Phase 3: High-Efficiency Heating Transition (Priority: Medium)**
With a tight thermal envelope, the building can now operate on lower temperatures, allowing for a transition away from the existing boiler.

4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - Since the site has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most viable and efficient alternative to the current boiler.
   - This will significantly improve the EPC label and reduce energy consumption.

**Phase 4: Air Quality and Ventilation (Priority: Medium)**
Once the building is sealed and insulated, the air exchange rate decreases, making a modern ventilation system necessary to prevent moisture buildup and maintain air quality.

5. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - Upgrade from `VentilationSystemA` (natural) to a more efficient system (such as `VentilationSystemC` or `VentilationSystemD`).
   - This ensures that the heat gained from the new insulation is not lost through uncontrolled air leakage.

**Phase 5: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)**
To offset the electricity consumption of the new heat pump and further improve the EPC label, consider installing PV panels.

6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - The building has confirmed potential for PV.
   - Installing solar panels will provide the renewable electricity needed to power the heat pump and ventilation system, further improving the EPC label.

**Summary of Sequence and Rationale:**

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulation (Exterior Walls $\rightarrow$ Floor $\rightarrow$ Flat Roof)** | Reduces the total energy demand of the house. |
| **2** | **Heat Pump Replacement** | Switches to a sustainable energy source tailored to the new lower heat demand. |
| **3** | **Ventilation Upgrade** | Maintains indoor air quality in a now-sealed envelope. |
| **4** | **PV Panels** | Offsets the electricity consumption of the heat pump and further improves the EPC label. |

**Additional Notes:**
- The historical protection status of the building should be verified before proceeding with any insulation work.
- The state of the emission system (`EmissionSystem`) should be assessed to ensure compatibility with the new heat pump.
- The renovation plan is designed to be cost-effective while significantly improving the EPC label and energy efficiency of the building.
