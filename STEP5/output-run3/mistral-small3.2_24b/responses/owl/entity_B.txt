### Renovation Plan for the Attached Building

**Building Details:**
- **Type:** Attached Building
- **Construction Year:** 1832
- **EPC Score:** 262 (C Label)
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** No
- **Reason to Change Emission System:** Yes
- **Owner's Motivation:** To improve EPC

**Key Observations:**
1. **Thermal Envelope:**
   - `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` are full brick and not insulated.
   - `FloorAboveBasement` is not insulated.
   - `RoofExtension` is not insulated.
   - `RoofMain` is moderately insulated.

2. **Windows:**
   - All windows have double glazing and are in good condition. No replacement needed at this stage.

3. **Heating System:**
   - Current system is a radiator-based emission system with a boiler.
   - There is a reason to change the emission system.

4. **Ventilation:**
   - Current ventilation system is type A (natural ventilation).

**Proposed Renovation Path:**

### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior:**
   - **Action:** Insulate `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on the exterior.
   - **Rationale:** These walls are full brick and not insulated. Exterior insulation is more effective than interior insulation for thermal performance.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement:**
   - **Action:** Insulate `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents cold air from the basement from entering the living spaces.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof:**
   - **Action:** Insulate `RoofExtension`.
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof is currently not insulated, leading to significant heat loss.

### Phase 2: Heating System Transition (Medium Priority)
4. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:**
   - **Action:** Install an Air-Water Heat Pump.
   - **Rationale:** The building has no potential for geothermal energy and no heat grid. An Air-Water Heat Pump is the most efficient and sustainable alternative to the current boiler.

5. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:**
   - **Action:** Transition the current radiators to low-temperature emissions.
   - **Rationale:** Ensures compatibility with the new heat pump and maximizes its efficiency.

### Phase 3: Ventilation Upgrade (Low Priority)
6. **Replace Ventilation System:**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A to Ventilation System D.
   - **Rationale:** Improves indoor air quality and ensures proper ventilation in a now-sealed envelope.

### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-term)
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof:**
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Solar panels will provide renewable electricity to power the heat pump and other systems.

### Summary of Sequence and Rationale

| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Insulate Walls on Exterior | Reduces heat loss through external walls. |
| 2 | Insulate Floor Above Basement | Prevents cold air from entering the living spaces. |
| 3 | Insulate Flat Roof | Prevents heat loss through the roof. |
| 4 | Place Air-Water Heat Pump | Replaces the old boiler with a more efficient and sustainable system. |
| 5 | Place Emission System on Low Temperature | Ensures compatibility with the new heat pump. |
| 6 | Replace Ventilation System | Improves indoor air quality and ensures proper ventilation. |
| 7 | Place PV-Panels on Roof | Provides renewable electricity to power the heat pump and other systems. |

This plan follows the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** sequence, ensuring that the building envelope is optimized before installing high-efficiency heating systems. This approach prevents the need for an oversized and more expensive HVAC system and maximizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness.
