Since the wooden frames are in a **Good State (Value: 8)**, it is more cost-effective to **replace the glazing** (upgrading to double or triple glazing) rather than replacing the entire window units.

#### Phase 2: HVAC & Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)
Once the building is sealed, the total heat load is reduced, allowing for the installation of a smaller, more efficient heating system.

The final phase ensures a healthy indoor environment and offsets the electricity used by the new heat pump.
