### Renovation Plan for Attached Building (1832)

**Current State:**
- **EPC Score:** 262 (C Label)
- **Building Type:** Attached (1832)
- **Key Issues:**
  - Non-insulated walls (Front, Rear)
  - Non-insulated floor above basement
  - Non-insulated flat roof
  - Double-glazed windows in good condition
  - Existing boiler and radiator system
  - Potential for PV

**Objective:** Improve EPC score through cost-effective measures.

---

### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
   - *Action:* Insulate `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` on exterior.
   - *Rationale:* Full brick walls are not cavity walls; exterior insulation is most effective.
   - *Cost-Effectiveness:* High (reduces heat loss significantly).

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - *Action:* Add insulation to `FloorAboveBasement`.
   - *Rationale:* Eliminates cold drafts from basement.
   - *Cost-Effectiveness:* High.

3. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - *Action:* Insulate `RoofExtension`.
   - *Rationale:* Flat roofs lose heat rapidly; insulation reduces energy demand.
   - *Cost-Effectiveness:* High.

---

### **Phase 2: Ventilation Upgrade**
4. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - *Action:* Upgrade from `VentilationSystemA` (natural) to `VentilationSystemC` or `VentilationSystemD`.
   - *Rationale:* Sealed envelope requires controlled ventilation to prevent moisture buildup.
   - *Cost-Effectiveness:* Medium.

---

### **Phase 3: Heating System Transition**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - *Action:* Replace boiler with an Air-Water Heat Pump.
   - *Rationale:* Geothermal potential is "false," and no heat grid is available.
   - *Cost-Effectiveness:* Medium (long-term energy savings).

---

### **Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - *Action:* Install solar panels on the roof.
   - *Rationale:* Building has confirmed PV potential; offsets electricity consumption.
   - *Cost-Effectiveness:* Medium (long-term savings).

---

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Walls (Exterior)** | Reduces heat loss through non-insulated walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Basement** | Eliminates cold drafts from basement. |
| **3** | **Insulate Flat Roof** | Reduces heat loss through the flat roof. |
| **4** | **Replace Ventilation System** | Ensures controlled air exchange in a sealed envelope. |
| **5** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | Replaces inefficient boiler with a sustainable alternative. |
| **6** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Offsets electricity consumption of the heat pump. |

### **Notes:**
- **Windows:** Double-glazed and in good condition; replacement is not recommended.
- **Historical Protection:** None reported on walls.
- **EPC Improvement:** Expected to significantly improve the EPC score (targeting B or A label).

This plan follows a **"Fabric First"** approach, ensuring the building envelope is optimized before upgrading the heating system. The sequence maximizes cost-effectiveness while achieving long-term energy efficiency.
