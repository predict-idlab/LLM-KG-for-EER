### Renovation Plan for Detached Building (EPC Score: 165, B Label)

**Building Characteristics:**
- **Year Built:** 1862 (Detached)
- **EPC Score:** 165 (B Label)
- **Owner's Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Current State:**
  - **Walls:** All four walls are cavity walls, not insulated.
  - **Roof:** Well-insulated (gable roof).
  - **Floor:** Floor above crawl space is not insulated.
  - **Windows:** Single-glazed with wooden frames (in good state).
  - **Ventilation:** System A (natural).
  - **Heating:** Electric convector, radiators, and floor heating.
  - **PV Potential:** Yes
  - **Geothermal Potential:** No

**Renovation Strategy:**
The building has significant heat loss through uninsulated cavity walls, single-glazed windows, and the floor above the crawl space. The priority is to **reduce heat loss first** before upgrading the heating system. Since the owner wants to improve the EPC, we will follow the **"Insulate $\rightarrow$ Ventilate $\rightarrow$ Generate"** sequence.

---

### **Proposed Renovation Path**

#### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)**
1. **Insulate Wall Cavity (All Four Walls)**
   - **Action:** Inject insulation into the cavity walls (Front, Rear, Left, Right).
   - **Rationale:** Cavity wall insulation is cost-effective and eliminates thermal bridges.
   - **Expected Impact:** Significant reduction in heat loss.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**
   - **Action:** Add insulation to the floor above the crawl space.
   - **Rationale:** Prevents heat loss to the crawl space and improves thermal comfort.

3. **Replace Glazing (All Windows)**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from single-glazed to double-glazed windows.
   - **Rationale:** Single glazing leads to significant heat loss; double glazing improves thermal performance without replacing frames.

#### **Phase 2: Ventilation System Upgrade (Medium Priority)**
4. **Replace Ventilation System (System A → System C)**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from natural ventilation (System A) to mechanical exhaust ventilation (System C).
   - **Rationale:** Ensures controlled air exchange in a now-sealed envelope.

#### **Phase 3: Heating System Transition (Medium Priority)**
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Replace the electric convector and boiler with an air-water heat pump.
   - **Rationale:** More energy-efficient than electric heating, especially in a well-insulated building.

#### **Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Long-Term Priority)**
6. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install solar panels on the roof.
   - **Rationale:** Offsets electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.

---

### **Summary of Optimal Sequence**
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Cavity Walls** | Reduces heat loss through walls. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space** | Eliminates cold drafts from below. |
| **3** | **Replace Glazing** | Improves window thermal performance. |
| **4** | **Upgrade Ventilation (A → C)** | Maintains air quality in a sealed envelope. |
| **5** | **Install Air-Water Heat Pump** | More efficient heating for a low-demand building. |
| **6** | **Install PV Panels** | Generates renewable electricity for the heat pump. |

This plan ensures **cost-effectiveness** by prioritizing insulation (highest ROI) and **energy efficiency** by transitioning to sustainable heating and PV generation.
