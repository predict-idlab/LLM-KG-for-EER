### Renovation Plan:

#### Current Situation:
- **Building Type:** Attached (1963)
- **EPC Score:** 50 (A+ Label)
- **Motivation:** Improve EPC
- **Thermal Performance:**
  - **Roof Main:** Moderately Insulated (Gable)
  - **Roof Extension:** Well Insulated (Flat)
  - **Walls:** Front (Well Insulated, Full Brick), Rear (Well Insulated, Full Brick)
  - **Floor Above Basement:** Well Insulated
- **Windows:** Double-Glazed (Living Area & Bedrooms)
- **Heating System:** Electric Convector
- **Ventilation System:** Type D
- **PV Panels Installed:** Yes
- **Geothermal Potential:** False
- **Heat Grid Availability:** False

#### Recommendations:

1. **Replace Glazing (High Priority)**
   - **Action:** Upgrade to Triple Glazing
   - **Rationale:** Although double glazing is already installed, replacing it with triple glazing will further reduce heat loss and improve thermal comfort.
   - **Impact:** High energy savings, improved comfort.

2. **Replace Ventilation System (Medium Priority)**
   - **Action:** Upgrade to Ventilation System C (or D if ductwork is feasible)
   - **Rationale:** The current ventilation system (Type D) may not be optimal for the building’s needs. A more efficient system will improve air quality and reduce energy loss.
   - **Impact:** Moderate energy savings, better indoor air quality.

3. **Replace Emission System (Low Priority)**
   - **Action:** Transition to Low-Temperature Emission System
   - **Rationale:** Since the building already has a low EPC score (A+), further optimization can be achieved by improving heat distribution efficiency.
   - **Impact:** Minor energy savings, improved comfort.

#### Summary of Sequence:
| Priority | Action | Rationale |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| **1**    | **Replace Glazing** | Maximize thermal performance of windows. |
| **2**    | **Upgrade Ventilation System** | Improve air quality and energy efficiency. |
| **3**    | **Low-Temperature Emission System** | Fine-tune heating distribution for optimal comfort. |

#### Next Steps:
- **Consult a Specialist:** For detailed assessments on glazing replacement and ventilation system upgrades.
- **Budgeting:** Estimate costs for glazing replacement and ventilation system upgrades.
- **Permit Check:** Ensure compliance with local regulations for ventilation system changes.

This plan ensures a balanced approach between cost-effectiveness and energy efficiency while maintaining the building’s high EPC rating.
