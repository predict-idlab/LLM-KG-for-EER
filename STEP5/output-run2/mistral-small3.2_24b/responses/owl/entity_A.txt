### Renovation Plan for the Provided Entity:

#### **Current Building Context**
- **Building Type:** Semi-detached house (1951)
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score:** 388 (E Label)
- **Primary Motivation:** Improve EPC rating
- **Heating System:** Existing boiler with radiators
- **Potential for PV:** True
- **Geothermal Potential:** False
- **Heat Grid Availability:** False
- **Building Envelope:**
  - Walls (Front, Rear, Right): Full brick, not insulated
  - Roof (Gable): Not insulated
  - Roof (Flat): Moderately insulated
  - Floor Above Basement: Not insulated
  - Windows: Double-glazed, wooden frames in good condition

#### **Proposed Renovation Sequence**
1. **Insulate Walls on Exterior**
   - **Action:** Exterior wall insulation for all three walls (Front, Rear, Right)
   - **Rationale:** Exterior insulation is more effective for full brick walls and prevents thermal bridges. This is the highest priority to reduce heat loss.

2. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action:** Insulate the floor above the basement
   - **Rationale:** Eliminates heat loss to the basement and prevents cold air infiltration.

3. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof (currently uninsulated)
   - **Rationale:** Reduces heat loss through the roof, which is a major source of energy loss in older homes.

4. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Action:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more efficient system (e.g., System D)
   - **Rationale:** Modern ventilation ensures controlled air exchange, reducing energy loss while maintaining indoor air quality.

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action:** Install an Air-Water Heat Pump as the primary heating source
   - **Rationale:** Replaces the existing boiler with a more efficient, low-carbon heating solution tailored to the reduced heat demand after insulation.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action:** Transition radiators to a low-temperature emission system
   - **Rationale:** Maximizes the efficiency of the new heat pump by operating at lower flow temperatures.

7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action:** Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof
   - **Rationale:** Generates renewable electricity to power the heat pump and other electrical loads, further improving the EPC rating.

#### **Summary of Sequence and Rationale**
| **Sequence** | **Action** | **Rationale** |
|--------------|-----------------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| **1** | Insulate Walls on Exterior | Reduces heat loss through uninsulated walls. |
| **2** | Insulate Floor Above Basement | Prevents cold air infiltration and heat loss to the basement. |
| **3** | Insulate Gable Roof | Reduces heat loss through the roof. |
| **4** | Replace Ventilation System | Ensures controlled air exchange and reduces energy loss. |
| **5** | Place Air-Water Heat Pump | Provides efficient heating with lower carbon emissions. |
| **6** | Place Emission System on Low Temperature | Optimizes the heat pump’s efficiency. |
| **7** | Place PV-Panels on Roof | Generates renewable electricity to power the heat pump and other loads. |

#### **Implementation Notes**
- **Cost-Effectiveness:** The sequence prioritizes actions with the highest return on investment, starting with the building envelope.
- **Sustainability:** The final steps focus on transitioning to renewable energy sources (PV panels) and highly efficient heating systems (heat pump).
- **Compatibility:** The suggested sequence ensures that each step logically follows the previous one to maximize efficiency and minimize disruptions.

This plan follows a **"Fabric-First"** approach, ensuring that the building envelope is optimized before transitioning to high-efficiency heating systems. This strategy is critical for achieving long-term energy savings and improving the EPC rating.
