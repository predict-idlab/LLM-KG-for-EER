### Renovation Plan for Attached House in Ghent, Belgium (Built in 1832)

#### **Current EPC Score:** 262
#### **Priority:** Improve energy efficiency and EPC score in a cost-effective manner.

#### **Proposed Renovation Path**

**Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Fabric-First Approach)**
1. **Insulate Flat Roof Extension**
   - **Rationale:** The flat roof is a significant source of heat loss. Insulating it will reduce energy consumption effectively.
2. **Insulate Exterior Walls (Front and Rear)**
   - **Rationale:** Solid brick walls are poorly insulated. Exterior insulation is recommended to eliminate thermal bridges and improve thermal performance.
3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Rationale:** Uninsulated floors lead to heat loss to the basement. Insulating this area will improve comfort and reduce energy demand.

**Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade**
4. **Replace Boiler with Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Rationale:** With an improved thermal envelope, a heat pump will be more efficient and sustainable than the current boiler.
5. **Upgrade to Low-Temperature Emission System**
   - **Rationale:** Ensures the heat pump operates at peak efficiency by transitioning radiators to a low-temperature system.

**Phase 3: Ventilation Upgrade**
6. **Replace Natural Ventilation with Mechanical Ventilation (System C or D)**
   - **Rationale:** A sealed envelope requires controlled ventilation to maintain air quality and prevent moisture buildup.

**Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Optional but Recommended)**
7. **Install Photovoltaic (PV) Panels**
   - **Rationale:** Generates renewable electricity to power the heat pump and ventilation system, further improving energy efficiency.

| **Sequence** | **Action** | **Rationale** |
