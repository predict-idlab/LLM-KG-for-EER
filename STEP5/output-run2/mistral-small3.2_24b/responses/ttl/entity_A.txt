### Renovation Plan for the Given Building

#### **Building Overview**
- **Type:** Semi-detached
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **Current EPC Score:** 388 (D Label)
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Heat Grid Availability:** No
- **Geothermal Potential:** No (Horizontal or Vertical)
- **Motivation:** Improve EPC

#### **Key Issues**
1. **Uninsulated Elements:** Walls (Front, Rear, Right), Floor Above Basement, Gable Roof
2. **Moderately Insulated Flat Roof**
3. **Outdated Ventilation System (Type A)**
4. **Double Glazing (Good, but could be upgraded)**
5. **No Heat Pump or Renewable Energy Integration**

#### **Proposed Renovation Path**
The renovation should follow a **"Fabric-First"** approach, focusing on improving the building envelope before upgrading heating systems. This ensures energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness.

##### **Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The gable roof is currently uninsulated, leading to significant heat loss. Insulating it will improve thermal retention.
   - **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
   - **Cost:** ~€60/m²

2. **Insulate Walls on Interior**
   - **Rationale:** All three external walls (Front, Rear, Right) are full brick and not insulated. Interior insulation is cost-effective and minimizes thermal bridges.
   - **Action:** `insulate wall on interior`
   - **Cost:** ~€150/m²

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Rationale:** The floor above the basement is uninsulated, leading to cold drafts and heat loss.
   - **Action:** `insulate floor above basement`
   - **Cost:** ~€30/m²

##### **Phase 2: Heating System Upgrade**
4. **Replace Ventilation System**
   - **Rationale:** The current ventilation system (Type A) is inefficient. Upgrading to a mechanical system (Type D) ensures better air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Action:** `replace ventilation system`
   - **Cost:** ~€3,500 (fixed) + €17/m²

5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Rationale:** The current boiler is outdated. An air-water heat pump is a sustainable and efficient alternative, especially with the improved insulation.
   - **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`
   - **Cost:** ~€8,000 (fixed) + €35/m²

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Rationale:** To maximize the efficiency of the heat pump, the emission system should be optimized for low-temperature operation.
   - **Action:** `place emission system on low temperature`
   - **Cost:** ~€100/m²

##### **Phase 3: Renewable Energy Integration**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Rationale:** The building has potential for PV, which can offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system.
   - **Action:** `place PV-panels on roof`
   - **Cost:** ~€275/m²

#### **Summary of Sequence and Rationale**
| **Sequence** | **Action**                          | **Rationale**                                                                                     |
|--------------|--------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1            | Insulate Gable Roof                  | Eliminate heat loss through the roof.                                                              |
| 2            | Insulate Walls on Interior           | Minimize thermal bridges and improve overall insulation.                                             |
| 3            | Insulate Floor Above Basement         | Reduce cold drafts and heat loss to the basement.                                                  |
| 4            | Replace Ventilation System            | Improve air quality and energy efficiency.                                                          |
| 5            | Place Air-Water Heat Pump             | Transition to a sustainable and efficient heating system.                                           |
| 6            | Place Emission System on Low Temperature | Optimize heat pump performance.                                                                   |
| 7            | Place PV-Panels on Roof               | Generate renewable energy to power the heat pump and ventilation system.                          |

#### **Expected Outcomes**
- **Improved EPC Label:** From D to B or higher.
- **Reduced Energy Consumption:** Lower heating costs and environmental impact.
- **Enhanced Comfort:** Better insulation and ventilation lead to a more comfortable living environment.

This plan ensures a **cost-effective** and **energy-efficient** renovation while adhering to the **fabric-first** principle.
