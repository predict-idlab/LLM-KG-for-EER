### Renovation Plan for the Given Building
The proposed renovation plan aims to maximize energy efficiency while maintaining cost-effectiveness. The building is a semi-detached house from 1951 with an EPC score of 388 (D Label). The primary motivation for renovation is to improve the EPC rating.

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (High Priority)
1. **Insulate Flat Roof**
   - **Action**: Insulate the flat roof (`RoofExtension`) to prevent heat loss through the roof.
   - **Rationale**: Flat roofs are particularly prone to heat loss, and insulating them can significantly reduce energy consumption.

2. **Insulate Wall On Exterior**
   - **Action**: Insulate the exterior walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Rear`, `Wall_Right`) on the outside to eliminate thermal bridges and improve thermal performance.
   - **Rationale**: Exterior wall insulation is more effective than interior insulation for full brick walls, as it minimizes heat loss through the structural elements.

3. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the basement (`FloorAboveBasement`) to prevent cold drafts and reduce heat loss to unconditioned spaces.
   - **Rationale**: Insulating the floor above the basement helps maintain a comfortable indoor temperature and reduces energy consumption.

#### Phase 2: Window Performance Enhancement
4. **Replace Glazing**
   - **Action**: Replace the glazing in all windows (`WindowsLivingArea`, `WindowsBedrooms`) with double or triple glazing to improve thermal performance.
   - **Rationale**: Replacing single glazing with double or triple glazing significantly reduces heat loss through windows, which are a major source of energy loss.

#### Phase 3: Heating System Transition
5. **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**
   - **Action**: Install an air-water heat pump to replace the existing boiler (`Generation_System`).
   - **Rationale**: Air-water heat pumps are highly efficient and can significantly reduce energy consumption for space heating and domestic hot water.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the existing radiators (`EmissionSystem`) to low-temperature emissions to maximize the efficiency of the new heat pump.
   - **Rationale**: Low-temperature emissions allow the heat pump to operate at higher Coefficient of Performance (COP), reducing energy consumption.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**
   - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable electricity.
   - **Rationale**: PV panels can offset the electricity consumption of the heat pump and ventilation system, further improving the EPC rating.

### Summary of Optimal Sequence
| Sequence | Action | Rationale |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | **Insulate Walls on Exterior** | Reduces heat loss through the building envelope. |
| **2** | **Insulate Floor Above Basement** | Prevents cold drafts and reduces heat loss to unconditioned spaces. |
| **3** | **Insulate Flat Roof** | Prevents heat loss through the roof. |
| **4** | **Replace Glazing** | Reduces heat loss through windows. |
| **5** | **Place Air-Water Heat Pump** | Replaces the existing boiler with a more efficient heating system. |
| **6** | **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** | Maximizes the efficiency of the new heat pump. |
| **7** | **Place PV-Panels on Roof** | Generates renewable electricity to offset energy consumption. |

This plan follows the "Insulate → Ventilate → Generate" sequence, ensuring that the building envelope is optimized before upgrading heating systems and installing renewable energy sources. This approach maximizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness.
