The building has several critical areas of heat loss. Addressing these first provides the most significant reduction in energy consumption.
1.  **Insulate Flat Roof:** The `RoofExtension` is currently not insulated. Since heat rises, this is a priority intervention to prevent significant thermal loss.
2.  **Insulate Walls on Exterior:** Both `Wall_Front` and `Wall_Rear` are full brick and not insulated. Because they are not cavity walls, **exterior insulation** is the most effective method to eliminate thermal bridges and improve the EPC score.
3.  **Insulate Floor Above Basement:** The `FloorAboveBasement` is currently not insulated. Adding insulation here reduces heat loss to the basement and improves ground-floor thermal comfort.

Once the building is sealed, the total heat demand is lowered, allowing for a transition to high-efficiency, low-temperature systems.
4.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** The current radiators should be upgraded or replaced with low-temperature emitters. This is a prerequisite for maximizing the efficiency (COP) of a heat pump.

Increasing the airtightness of the building through insulation necessitates a ventilation upgrade to prevent humidity buildup and maintain a healthy indoor climate.
6.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrade from Ventilation System A (natural) to a more controlled system (such as System C or D) to ensure efficient air exchange without losing the heat gains achieved in Phase 1.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
To further lower the EPC score and offset the electricity costs of the new heat pump and ventilation system.
