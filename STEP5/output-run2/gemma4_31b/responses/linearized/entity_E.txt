### Proposed Renovation Path

#### Phase 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Priority: High)
The building has significant heat loss through its walls and windows. Since the roof is already "Well Insulated," the focus shifts to the vertical surfaces and glazing.

1.  **Insulate Wall Cavity:** All four external walls (`Wall_Front`, `Wall_Left`, `Wall_Right`, and `Wall_Rear`) are identified as `CavityWall` and are currently `NotInsulated`. Because these are cavity walls and have no historical protection, **insulating the wall cavity** is the most cost-effective and least disruptive method to drastically reduce heat loss.
2.  **Replace Glazing:** Both the bedrooms and living area currently have `SingleGlazing`. This is a major source of thermal leakage. Upgrading to double or triple glazing will significantly improve the EPC score and increase indoor thermal comfort.
    *   *Note: The wooden frames are in "Good State," so replacing only the glazing (rather than the entire window) is the more cost-effective choice.*

#### Phase 2: Heating & Emission System Transition (Priority: Medium)
The building has a reason to change its emission system and currently relies on a boiler and a mix of radiators, floor heating, and electric convectors.

3.  **Place Emission System on Low Temperature:** To prepare for a high-efficiency heat pump, the current emission systems should be transitioned to low-temperature operation. This ensures the new generator can operate at its maximum efficiency (COP).
4.  **Place Air-Water Heat Pump:** Since the site has no potential for geothermal drilling/captation and no access to a heat grid, an **Air-Water Heat Pump** is the most sustainable and efficient replacement for the existing boiler.

#### Phase 3: Air Quality and Ventilation (Priority: Medium)
The building currently uses `VentilationSystemA` (natural ventilation). Once the walls and windows are sealed and insulated, the natural air exchange rate will drop.

5.  **Replace Ventilation System:** Upgrading to a more controlled system (such as System C or D) is necessary to prevent moisture buildup and ensure a healthy indoor air quality in the now-tightened thermal envelope.

#### Phase 4: Sustainable Energy Generation (Priority: Long-term)
To further lower the EPC score and offset the electricity costs of the new heat pump.

6.  **Place PV-Panels on Roof:** The building has confirmed potential for PV. Installing solar panels on the roof will provide renewable energy to power the new energy-efficient installations.
