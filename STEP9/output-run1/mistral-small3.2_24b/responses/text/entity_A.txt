To create a concise and actionable renovation plan for your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium, we'll focus on maximizing energy efficiency while keeping costs manageable. Here's a step-by-step approach:

### 1. **Insulation Upgrades**
   - **Exterior Walls**: Insulate the solid brick walls with external wall insulation (EWI) or internal wall insulation (IWI). EWI is generally more effective and doesn't reduce interior space, but it may require planning permission. IWI is less disruptive but reduces room size.
   - **Pitched Roof**: Insulate the uninsulated pitched roof with loft insulation. This is a cost-effective measure with high impact.
   - **Flat-Roof Extension**: Although it has moderate insulation, consider topping up to the current standards.
   - **Floor Above Basement**: Insulate the uninsulated floor to prevent heat loss through the basement.

### 2. **Heating System Upgrade**
   - Replace the old boiler with a modern, condensing boiler for better efficiency.
   - Consider switching to a heat pump system, which is more energy-efficient. However, this might be more costly upfront but could save money in the long run.
   - Replace the old radiators, underfloor heating, and air heating systems with new, efficient models. Ensure they are compatible with the new boiler or heat pump.

### 3. **Ventilation System**
   - Upgrade the basic natural ventilation system to a mechanical ventilation system with heat recovery (MVHR). This will improve indoor air quality and reduce heat loss.

### 4. **Windows and Doors**
   - Although the windows have double glazing, consider upgrading to triple glazing for better insulation, especially if the frames are in good condition.
   - Ensure all doors are well-sealed and insulated.

### 5. **Photovoltaic Panels**
   - Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the roof to generate renewable energy. This will reduce your electricity bills and potentially earn you feed-in tariffs.

### 6. **Energy-Efficient Lighting and Appliances**
   - Replace all lighting with LED bulbs.
   - Upgrade to energy-efficient appliances if any are old or inefficient.

### 7. **Smart Home Systems**
   - Install smart thermostats and controls to optimize heating and cooling, reducing energy waste.

### Optimal Sequence of Renovations
1. **Insulation**: Start with the easiest and most cost-effective insulation upgrades, such as loft insulation and floor insulation.
2. **Heating System**: Replace the boiler and heating systems next, as this will have a significant impact on energy efficiency.
3. **Ventilation**: Upgrade the ventilation system to ensure good air quality and heat recovery.
4. **Windows and Doors**: Upgrade windows and doors for better insulation.
5. **Photovoltaic Panels**: Install PV panels to generate renewable energy.
6. **Smart Home Systems**: Finally, install smart systems to optimize energy use.

### Cost-Effectiveness and Energy Efficiency
- Prioritize measures with the highest cost-to-benefit ratio, such as insulation and heating system upgrades.
- Consider government grants or subsidies available for energy-efficient renovations in Belgium.

This plan should significantly improve your home's energy performance certificate and reduce long-term operational costs.
