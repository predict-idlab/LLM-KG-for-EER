Given the information provided, here's a concise and actionable renovation plan tailored to your semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium. The plan focuses on maximizing energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, considering the optimal sequence of renovations:

### 1. **Improve Roof Insulation (Main Pitched Roof)**
   - **Action**: Upgrade the insulation of the main pitched roof to match the well-insulated flat-roof extension.
   - **Rationale**: The pitched roof has only moderate insulation, and improving it will significantly reduce heat loss, especially since roofs are major sources of heat loss in pitched-roof houses.
   - **Benefit**: Enhanced thermal performance, leading to lower heating demands and improved EPC score.

### 2. **Upgrade Ventilation System to Heat Recovery (Ventilation System D to HRV)**
   - **Action**: Replace the existing ventilation system with a Heat Recovery Ventilation (HRV) system.
   - **Rationale**: HRV systems preheat incoming fresh air with the heat from exhaust air, reducing the need for additional heating. This is particularly effective in well-insulated homes like yours.
   - **Benefit**: Improved indoor air quality, reduced heating costs, and a better EPC score.

### 3. **Optimize Heat Pump Performance**
   - **Action**: Conduct a professional assessment of the heat pump to ensure it is operating at peak efficiency. Consider upgrading the control system or adding smart thermostats for better temperature regulation.
   - **Rationale**: Even though the heat pump is in good condition, optimizing its performance can lead to further energy savings.
   - **Benefit**: More efficient heating, lower energy consumption, and potential cost savings.

### 4. **Enhance Air Sealing**
   - **Action**: Conduct an air tightness test and seal any gaps or leaks around windows, doors, and other openings.
   - **Rationale**: Air leaks can significantly reduce the effectiveness of insulation and heating systems.
   - **Benefit**: Improved overall energy efficiency and comfort.

### 5. **Consider Secondary Glazing for Windows (If Needed)**
   - **Action**: Evaluate the performance of the existing double-glazed windows. If heat loss through windows is still a concern, consider adding secondary glazing to the most critical windows.
   - **Rationale**: Secondary glazing can further reduce heat loss without replacing the existing windows.
   - **Benefit**: Enhanced insulation and potential improvement in the EPC score.

### 6. **Monitor and Optimize Photovoltaic (PV) System**
   - **Action**: Ensure the PV system is performing optimally by cleaning the panels and checking the inverter and other components.
   - **Rationale**: Regular maintenance can maximize the energy output of the PV system.
   - **Benefit**: Increased self-consumption of renewable energy, leading to lower electricity costs.

### 7. **Consider Smart Home Integration**
   - **Action**: Install smart home devices such as smart thermostats, sensors, and controls to monitor and manage energy usage more effectively.
   - **Rationale**: Smart home technology can help optimize energy consumption and provide insights into energy usage patterns.
   - **Benefit**: Improved energy management and potential cost savings.

### Optimal Sequence:
1. Improve roof insulation.
2. Upgrade ventilation system to HRV.
3. Optimize heat pump performance.
4. Enhance air sealing.
5. Consider secondary glazing (if needed).
6. Monitor and optimize PV system.
7. Consider smart home integration.

This plan prioritizes upgrades that provide the most significant energy savings and EPC score improvements first, ensuring cost-effective and practical renovations.
