Given the information about your house in Ghent, Belgium, here’s a step-by-step renovation plan to improve energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective. The plan prioritizes upgrades that offer the highest energy savings and long-term operational benefits, considering the current condition of your home.

### Step 1: Replace the Heating System
**Action:** Replace the old boiler and heat emission systems (underfloor heating and electric convectors) with a more efficient system.
**Options:**
- **Heat Pump:** Given the underfloor heating system already in place, an air-source heat pump (ASHP) could be a good fit. It’s efficient and works well with underfloor heating. Although the site isn’t suitable for geothermal, an ASHP can still be viable.
- **Modern Gas Boiler:** If a heat pump isn’t feasible, opt for a high-efficiency condensing boiler. Pair it with new underfloor heating or radiators for better performance.

**Why First?**
A modern heating system will immediately reduce energy consumption and improve the Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) score.

### Step 2: Upgrade Roof Insulation (Main Pitched Roof)
**Action:** Improve insulation in the main pitched roof, which currently has a moderate level of insulation.
**Options:**
- Add additional insulation (e.g., mineral wool or rigid foam boards) to reach the recommended U-value (e.g., 0.18 W/m²K or lower).
- Consider installing a vapor barrier if not already present to prevent condensation.

**Why Next?**
Roofs are significant sources of heat loss. Upgrading insulation here will have a noticeable impact on energy efficiency.

### Step 3: Optimize Ventilation System
**Action:** Ensure the ventilation system (System D) is operating efficiently.
**Options:**
- Service the system to clean ducts and filters.
- Upgrade to a more efficient heat recovery ventilation (HRV) system if possible, though this may be more costly.

**Why Next?**
Efficient ventilation reduces heat loss and improves indoor air quality, contributing to overall energy savings.

### Step 4: Enhance Window Performance (If Needed)
**Action:** Assess the performance of the double-glazed windows.
**Options:**
- If the windows are drafty or not performing well, consider upgrading to triple-glazed windows with low-emissivity (Low-E) coatings and argon or krypton gas fill.
- Ensure all windows are properly sealed to prevent air leaks.

**Why Next?**
Windows are another major source of heat loss. Upgrading them will further improve the EPC score, though this is lower priority given the recent 2024 renovation.

### Step 5: Monitor and Optimize Photovoltaic (PV) System
**Action:** Ensure the PV panels are performing optimally.
**Options:**
- Check for any shading issues or maintenance needs.
- Consider adding a battery storage system to store excess energy for use during peak hours or outages.

**Why Next?**
Maximizing the use of your existing PV system will reduce reliance on grid electricity, further lowering energy costs and improving sustainability.

### Step 6: Additional Measures (If Budget Allows)
**Action:** Consider other cost-effective upgrades.
**Options:**
- **Smart Thermostats:** Install programmable or smart thermostats to optimize heating schedules.
- **Insulate Hot Water Pipes:** Reduce heat loss from hot water distribution.
- **Draft Proofing:** Seal gaps around doors, windows, and other openings to prevent air leaks.

**Why Last?**
These are smaller upgrades that can be done after the major systems are addressed. They provide incremental improvements to energy efficiency.

### Summary of Renovation Plan:
1. Replace the old boiler and heat emission systems with a heat pump or high-efficiency boiler.
2. Upgrade insulation in the main pitched roof.
3. Optimize the ventilation system.
4. Assess and potentially upgrade windows if needed.
5. Monitor and optimize the PV system.
6. Implement additional measures like smart thermostats, pipe insulation, and draft proofing.

This plan prioritizes the most impactful upgrades first, ensuring cost-effective improvements to your home’s energy performance.
