### Renovation Plan for Detached Building

**Objective:** Improve energy efficiency and comfort, with a focus on EPC improvement.

**Building Details:**
- Detached building with cavity walls (4 walls, not insulated).
- Gable roof (well insulated).
- 2 spaces: living area and bedrooms.
- Single glazed windows.
- Electric convector heating system.
- No heat grid available.
- No potential for geothermal drilling.
- No potential for PV installation.
- EPC score: 165 kWh/m²/year.

**Key Recommendations:**

1. **Insulate Walls (High Priority):**
   - **Action:** Insulate cavity walls on the interior (ordering 37).
   - **Reason:** Walls are currently not insulated (NotInsulated).
   - **Expected Outcome:** Improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Note:** Insulating on the interior is less disruptive than exterior insulation.

2. **Replace Glazing (High Priority):**
   - **Action:** Replace single glazing with double or triple glazing (ordering 43).
   - **Reason:** Single glazing has poor thermal and acoustic properties.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Significantly reduce heat loss through windows and improve comfort.

3. **Consider Ventilation System Upgrade (Medium Priority):**
   - **Action:** Replace ventilation system with a more efficient system (ordering 52).
   - **Reason:** Current system may not be adequate for maintaining good indoor air quality.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Improve indoor air quality and potentially reduce energy consumption if combined with heat recovery.

4. **Heating System Upgrade (Medium Priority):**
   - **Action:** Consider replacing electric convector with a heat pump (e.g., air-water heat pump with cooling, ordering 73).
   - **Reason:** Electric heating is generally less energy-efficient than heat pumps.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Lower energy consumption and potentially lower energy bills.

5. **Roof Maintenance (Low Priority):**
   - **Action:** Ensure roof underlayment is properly installed (ordering 16).
   - **Reason:** Proper underlayment prevents water infiltration and extends roof lifespan.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Protect roof structure and prevent future issues.

**Implementation Sequence:**
1. Insulate walls (interior)
2. Replace glazing
3. Upgrade ventilation system
4. Upgrade heating system
5. Roof maintenance

**Notes:**
- Prioritize actions that address the building's largest sources of energy loss (walls and windows).
- Consider combining actions where possible to minimize disruption (e.g., wall insulation and glazing replacement).
- Verify local regulations and permits for any structural changes.
- Consult with professionals for technical specifications and installation.

This plan focuses on practical, cost-effective upgrades that will maximize energy efficiency improvements while respecting the building's existing characteristics.
