### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached Building

Given the information provided, here is a step-by-step renovation plan that prioritizes energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness while considering the optimal sequence for maximizing benefits:

#### 1. **Insulation Upgrades**
   - **Insulate Gable Roof**: The gable roof is currently moderately insulated. Improving insulation will enhance thermal comfort and reduce heat loss.
     - **Action**: Insulate the gable roof with appropriate materials, ensuring a vapor barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation.
     - **Cost**: Approximately €60 per m².
   - **Insulate Flat Roof**: The flat roof is well insulated, but if there is potential for improvement, consider upgrading the insulation.
     - **Action**: Opt for an inverted roof structure to place insulation on top of the roof covering.
     - **Cost**: Approximately €50 per m².

#### 2. **Window and Glazing Upgrades**
   - **Replace Glazing**: The windows in both the bedrooms and living area have double glazing. If the glazing is old or inefficient, consider replacing it with triple glazing for better insulation.
     - **Action**: Replace the glazing with triple glazing to improve thermal performance.
     - **Cost**: Approximately €200 per m².

#### 3. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
   - **Replace Ventilation System**: The current ventilation system is type D, which is mechanical for both supply and return. Consider upgrading to a more efficient system if needed.
     - **Action**: Replace the ventilation system with a more efficient model, ensuring proper ductwork and grilles are installed.
     - **Cost**: Fixed cost of €3,500 plus €17 per m².

#### 4. **Renewable Energy Integration**
   - **Place PV-Panels on Roof**: Given the potential for solar energy, consider installing photovoltaic panels on the roof.
     - **Action**: Install PV-panels on the roof, ensuring they are properly oriented and not shaded by buildings, chimneys, or trees.
     - **Cost**: Approximately €275 per m².

#### 5. **Additional Considerations**
   - **Heat Pump Installation**: If the current heating system is inefficient, consider installing a heat pump for space heating and hot water.
     - **Action**: Place an air-water heat pump or a ground source heat pump, depending on the site's potential.
     - **Cost**: Varies based on the type of heat pump and installation complexity.
   - **Sun Shade Installation**: To reduce solar gain and improve cooling efficiency, consider installing sun shades.
     - **Action**: Place outdoor blinds or other effective sun shades.
     - **Cost**: Fixed cost of €750.

### Optimal Sequence
1. **Insulation Upgrades**: Start with insulating the gable and flat roofs to improve the building's thermal envelope.
2. **Window and Glazing Upgrades**: Replace or upgrade glazing to enhance insulation and reduce heat loss through windows.
3. **Ventilation System Upgrade**: Ensure proper ventilation to maintain indoor air quality and comfort.
4. **Renewable Energy Integration**: Install PV-panels to generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.
5. **Heat Pump Installation**: Upgrade the heating system to a more efficient heat pump if necessary.
6. **Sun Shade Installation**: Finally, install sun shades to control solar gain and improve cooling efficiency.

This plan ensures that each renovation step builds on the previous one, maximizing the benefits of each upgrade and creating a more energy-efficient and cost-effective home.
