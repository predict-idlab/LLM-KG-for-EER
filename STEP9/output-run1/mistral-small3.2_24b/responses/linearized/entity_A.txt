   - **Place PV-Panels on Roof**: Given the building's potential for PV (true), installing solar panels on the roof will generate renewable energy and reduce electricity costs.

   - **Connect to Heat Grid**: If feasible, connecting to a heat grid can provide a stable and efficient heat source.

- **High Priority**: Insulate roofs and walls, replace glazing, and upgrade the ventilation system. These measures will have the most significant impact on energy efficiency.

2. **Insulate Roofs and Walls**: Hire a professional to insulate the roofs and walls according to local building codes.
