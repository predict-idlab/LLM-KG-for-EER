### Renovation Plan for the Semi-Detached Building (http://example.com/Building)

#### 1. **Insulation and Roofing**
- **Replace Flat Roof (http://example.com/RoofExtension)**
  - **Action**: Replace the existing flat roof.
  - **Reasoning**: The current flat roof is moderately insulated, but replacing it will improve energy efficiency.
  - **Suggestion**: `replace flat roof` (Ordering: 10)

- **Insulate Gable Roof (http://example.com/RoofMain)**
  - **Action**: Insulate the gable roof.
  - **Reasoning**: The gable roof is not insulated, and insulating it will enhance thermal performance.
  - **Suggestion**: `insulate gable roof` (Ordering: 28)

#### 2. **Window and Glazing Upgrades**
- **Replace Glazing (http://example.com/WindowsBedrooms & http://example.com/WindowsLivingArea)**
  - **Action**: Replace the glazing in the bedrooms and living area windows.
  - **Reasoning**: The current glazing is double glazing, which can be upgraded to triple glazing for better insulation.
  - **Suggestion**: `replace glazing` (Ordering: 43)

#### 3. **Wall Insulation**
- **Insulate Wall on Exterior (http://example.com/Wall_Front, http://example.com/Wall_Right, http://example.com/Wall_Rear)**
  - **Action**: Insulate the exterior walls.
  - **Reasoning**: The walls are not insulated, and exterior insulation will significantly improve the building's thermal efficiency.
  - **Suggestion**: `insulate wall on exterior` (Ordering: 40)

#### 4. **Heating System Upgrade**
- **Place Air-Water Heatpump (http://example.com/EmissionSystem)**
  - **Action**: Replace the current emission system with an air-water heat pump.
  - **Reasoning**: The current emission system is outdated, and an air-water heat pump will provide more efficient heating.
  - **Suggestion**: `place air-water heatpump` (Ordering: 67)

- **Place Emission System on Low Temperature (http://example.com/EmissionSystem)**
  - **Action**: Upgrade the emission system to operate at low temperatures.
  - **Reasoning**: A low-temperature emission system is more energy-efficient and compatible with the new heat pump.
  - **Suggestion**: `place emission system on low temperature` (Ordering: 70)

#### 5. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
- **Replace Ventilation System (http://example.com/VentilationSystem)**
  - **Action**: Replace the existing ventilation system.
  - **Reasoning**: The current ventilation system is type A, which can be upgraded to a more efficient system.
  - **Suggestion**: `replace ventilation system` (Ordering: 52)

#### 6. **Renewable Energy Integration**
- **Place PV-Panels on Roof (http://example.com/RoofMain)**
  - **Action**: Install photovoltaic (PV) panels on the gable roof.
  - **Reasoning**: The building has potential for PV installation, and the gable roof is suitable for PV panels.
  - **Suggestion**: `place PV-panels on roof` (Ordering: 85)

#### 7. **Additional Recommendations**
- **Place Sunshade (http://example.com/WindowsLivingArea & http://example.com/WindowsBedrooms)**
  - **Action**: Install sunshades on the windows.
  - **Reasoning**: Sunshades can reduce solar heat gain and improve comfort.
  - **Suggestion**: `place sunshade` (Ordering: 79)
