### **Renovation Plan for Building (http://example.com/Building)**

**Objective:**
Improve the EPC-value of the dwelling (as indicated by the owner's motivation).

**Current Status:**
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC):** 262 kWh/m²year (F label)
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Potential for Geothermal Drilling:** No
- **Presence of Heat Grid:** No
- **Insulation Status:**
  - Flat Roof (http://example.com/RoofExtension): Not Insulated
  - Gable Roof (http://example.com/RoofMain): Moderately Insulated
  - Walls (http://example.com/Wall_Front, http://example.com/Wall_Rear): Not Insulated
  - Floor Above Basement (http://example.com/FloorAboveBasement): Not Insulated
- **Ventilation System:** Ventilation System A (natural air supply and exhaust)
- **Emission System:** Radiator (http://example.com/EmissionSystem)

**Recommended Actions:**

1. **Insulate the Flat Roof (http://example.com/RoofExtension)**
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate flat roof
   - **Priority:** High
   - **Reason:** The flat roof is currently not insulated, which significantly impacts the building's energy efficiency.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Improved thermal comfort and reduced energy consumption.

2. **Insulate the Walls (http://example.com/Wall_Front, http://example.com/Wall_Rear)**
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate wall on exterior or insulate wall on interior
   - **Priority:** High
   - **Reason:** The walls are not insulated, contributing to heat loss.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Enhanced thermal performance and lower heating costs.

3. **Insulate the Floor Above Basement (http://example.com/FloorAboveBasement)**
   - **Suggestion:** Insulate floor above basement
   - **Priority:** Medium
   - **Reason:** The floor above the basement is not insulated, leading to potential heat loss.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Better energy efficiency and comfort.

4. **Replace the Ventilation System (http://example.com/VentilationSystem)**
   - **Suggestion:** Replace ventilation system
   - **Priority:** Medium
   - **Reason:** The current ventilation system (Ventilation System A) may not be the most energy-efficient option.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Improved air quality and potential energy savings.

5. **Install PV Panels**
   - **Suggestion:** Place PV-panels on roof or place PV-panels in garden
   - **Priority:** Medium
   - **Reason:** The building has potential for PV installation, which can significantly reduce energy costs and improve sustainability.
   - **Expected Outcome:** Reduced reliance on grid electricity and lower carbon footprint.

6. **Replace the Emission System (http://example.com/EmissionSystem)**
   - **Suggestion:** Place heatpumpboiler or place air-water heatpump
   - **Priority:** High
   - **Reason:** The current emission system (radiator) may not be the most efficient for heating the building.
   - **Expected Outcome:** More efficient heating and potential cost savings.

**Sequence of Actions:**
1. Insulate the flat roof.
2. Insulate the walls.
3. Insulate the floor above the basement.
4. Replace the ventilation system.
5. Install PV panels.
6. Replace the emission system.

**Additional Considerations:**
- **Permits and Regulations:** Check local regulations for any necessary permits, especially for structural changes like insulation and PV panel installation.
- **Professional Assistance:** Consult with architects or contractors for expert advice, especially for wall insulation and emission system replacement.
- **Budget and Timeline:** Develop a detailed budget and timeline for each action, considering the potential costs and the expected return on investment.

This plan aims to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, prioritizing actions that offer the most significant improvements to the building's EPC-value.
