Based on the provided ontology and building data, here is the proposed renovation plan.

### **Building Analysis**
The building is a very modern **Attached Building (constructed in 2023)** with an excellent current energy performance (**EPC score of 50**, which corresponds to an **A label**). Most elements are already "Well Insulated," including the walls and the floor above the basement. 

However, there are two primary areas for improvement to maximize long-term energy efficiency and reduce operational costs:
1.  **Thermal Envelope:** The main gable roof is only "Moderately Insulated."
2.  **Heating Systems:** The building currently relies on **Electric Convectors** (which are inefficient and expensive to operate) and a standard **Boiler**.

---

### **Proposed Renovation Path**

To maximize efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the renovation should follow the "envelope-first" principle: improve the shell to reduce demand before upgrading the systems that meet that demand.

#### **Step 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization**
*   **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
*   **Reasoning:** The `RoofMain` is the only major building element not yet "Well Insulated." Upgrading this from moderately insulated to well insulated will reduce heat loss through the top of the house, ensuring the heating system doesn't have to work as hard.
*   **Benefit:** Low nuisance level and high impact on maintaining a stable internal temperature.

#### **Step 2: Domestic Hot Water Upgrade**
*   **Action:** `place heatpumpboiler`
*   **Reasoning:** The building currently has a standard `Boiler`. Replacing this with a heat pump boiler significantly reduces the electricity required to heat water by extracting heat from the ambient air.
*   **Benefit:** Direct reduction in energy consumption for water heating with a moderate installation nuisance.

#### **Step 3: Space Heating System Transition**
*   **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`
*   **Reasoning:** The current `ElectricConvector` system is one of the least efficient ways to heat a home. Since the site has **no potential** for geothermal drilling (vertical or horizontal) and **no heat grid** available, an Air-Water Heat Pump is the most efficient sustainable alternative.
*   **Benefit:** Drastically lowers the energy score and operational costs compared to electric resistance heating.

---

### **Summary of the Optimal Sequence**

| Sequence | Renovation Action | Target Element | Priority | Impact |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1** | `insulate gable roof` | RoofMain | High | Reduces heat loss (Demand) |
| **2** | `place heatpumpboiler` | Generation_System | Medium | Efficient water heating |
| **3** | `place air-water heatpump` | EmissionSystem | High | Efficient space heating |

### **Cost-Effectiveness & Constraints Note**
*   **Excluded Actions:** `place PV-panels` and `place geothermal heatpump` were excluded because the site data explicitly states there is **no potential** for PV or geothermal drilling.
*   **Ventilation:** No action is needed for the ventilation system as it is already a `VentilationSystemD` (the most advanced type in the ontology).
*   **Windows:** While triple glazing is an option, the current double glazing in a 2023 build is already highly efficient; the ROI on replacing them would be negligible compared to the heating system upgrade.
