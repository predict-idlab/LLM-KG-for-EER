### Proposed Renovation Path

Given that the building is already highly efficient, the goal is to move from "A" toward "A+" through targeted, low-impact upgrades rather than expensive overhauls.

*   **Action:** **Insulate Gable Roof**
*   **Reasoning:** The `RoofMain` is the only structural element not yet "well insulated." Increasing the insulation grade of the gable roof will reduce heat loss in winter and heat gain in summer, directly lowering the energy score.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** This is more cost-effective than replacing the roof entirely, as the roof is already in a "Good State."
*   **Ordering:** According to the ontology, this is a primary step (Ordering: 28).

#### Step 2: Passive Cooling & Comfort (Priority: Medium)
*   **Action:** **Place Sunshades**
*   **Reasoning:** With an A-label home, the building is likely very airtight. This can lead to overheating during summer months. Installing sunshades is a practical, low-cost way to reduce the cooling load on the heat pump, thereby reducing operational energy consumption.
*   **Cost-Effectiveness:** This is a low-fixed-cost intervention that prevents the need for higher-energy active cooling (like air conditioning).
