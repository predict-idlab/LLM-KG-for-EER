*The final step is to reduce operational costs and carbon footprint.*

### Summary of the Optimal Sequence & Justification

| Sequence | Action | Justification |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Envelope** | Roof $\rightarrow$ Walls $\rightarrow$ Floor | Stops heat leakage first. This allows for a smaller, cheaper heating system in the next phase. |
| **2. Ventilation** | Replace Ventilation System | Prevents moisture build-up caused by the increased airtightness of the new insulation. |
| **3. Heating** | Low-Temp Emission $\rightarrow$ Heat Pump | Ensures the heat pump operates at maximum efficiency (COP) by lowering the required flow temperature. |
| **4. Energy** | PV Panels | Reduces the cost of electricity needed to run the heat pump and improves the final EPC label. |

**Cost-Effectiveness Note:** By insulating first, the owner avoids over-investing in a heating system that would be oversized for a well-insulated home. This sequence ensures the highest possible jump in EPC rating per euro spent.
