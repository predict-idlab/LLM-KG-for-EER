### 1. Building Analysis
The building is a modern **Attached Building (constructed in 2023)** with an already excellent energy profile (**EPC score of 50**, which falls into the **A/A+ label** category). Most elements—including the flat roof, walls, and floor above the basement—are already "Well Insulated."

However, there are two primary areas for improvement:
*   **Thermal Envelope:** The main gable roof is only "Moderately Insulated," representing the weakest point in the building's shell.
*   **Systems:** The building uses **Electric Convectors**, which are energy-inefficient. The entity information explicitly states there is a **reason to change the emission system**.

---

### 2. Proposed Renovation Path
To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the sequence follows the "Envelope First, Systems Second" principle.

#### Step 1: Optimize the Thermal Envelope
Before upgrading the heating system, we must ensure the building retains as much heat as possible to allow for a smaller, more efficient heat pump.
*   **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
    *   **Reasoning:** This is the only major element not yet "Well Insulated." Improving this reduces the overall heating load.
    *   **Cost/Nuisance:** Low nuisance level; highly cost-effective compared to wall or floor renovations.
*   **Action:** `replace glazing` (Upgrade to Triple Glazing)
    *   **Reasoning:** The building currently has double glazing. To push the EPC score even lower (closer to A+), upgrading to triple glazing is the most practical step.

#### Step 2: Upgrade the Generation & Emission Systems
With the envelope optimized, we address the inefficient electric heating.
*   **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`
    *   **Reasoning:** The building already has **Floor Heating**, which is a low-temperature system. An air-water heat pump is the most efficient pairing for floor heating.
    *   **Constraint Check:** Vertical and horizontal geothermal options were ruled out based on the site's lack of potential (`potentialForVerticalGeothermalDrilling: false`).
*   **Action:** `place heatpumpboiler`
    *   **Reasoning:** To replace traditional boiler functions for domestic hot water with a high-efficiency electric heat pump system.

#### Step 3: Final Efficiency Tweaks
*   **Action:** `place sunshade`
    *   **Reasoning:** To prevent overheating in the summer (reducing the need for active cooling) and maintain a stable indoor climate.
