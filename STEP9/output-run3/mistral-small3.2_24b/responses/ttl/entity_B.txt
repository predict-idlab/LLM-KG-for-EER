### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### 1. **Initial Assessment**
   - **Current Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score**: 262.0 (C label)
   - **Building Type**: Attached, constructed in 1832
   - **Key Areas for Improvement**: Roofs, Walls, Floors, Windows, Ventilation System, Emission System
   - **Owner's Motivation**: To improve EPC

#### 2. **Prioritized Renovation Actions**
   - **High Impact, Low Nuisance**:
     1. **Insulate Gable Roof**: Moderately insulated, high impact on thermal performance.
     2. **Insulate Attic Floor**: Not insulated, critical for heat retention.
     3. **Insulate Wall Cavity**: Not insulated, cost-effective and low nuisance.
     4. **Insulate Floor Above Basement**: Not insulated, important for overall building efficiency.
     5. **Insulate Flat Roof**: Not insulated, replace if necessary.

   - **Moderate Impact, Moderate Nuisance**:
     1. **Replace Ventilation System**: Current system is type A (natural air supply and exhaust), consider upgrading to a more efficient system (e.g., type D for mechanical supply and exhaust).
     2. **Replace Glazing**: Current glazing is double, consider upgrading to triple glazing for better insulation.

   - **High Impact, High Nuisance**:
     1. **Insulate Wall on Exterior**: High cost but significant impact on thermal performance.
     2. **Replace Windows**: High cost but improves both thermal and acoustic performance.

   - **Optional Upgrades**:
     1. **Place PV-Panels on Roof**: High potential for PV, consider if budget allows.
     2. **Place Heat Pump**: Consider if budget allows and if the emission system is compatible.

#### 3. **Step-by-Step Implementation Plan**
   1. **Phase 1: Roof and Attic Insulation**
      - **Action**: Insulate gable roof and attic floor.
      - **Rationale**: Immediate impact on heat retention and energy efficiency.
      - **Estimated Cost**: Moderate.

   2. **Phase 2: Wall Insulation**
      - **Action**: Insulate wall cavity and consider exterior wall insulation if budget permits.
      - **Rationale**: Walls are a major source of heat loss.
      - **Estimated Cost**: Moderate to high.

   3. **Phase 3: Floor Insulation**
      - **Action**: Insulate floor above basement.
      - **Rationale**: Ensures comprehensive thermal envelope.
      - **Estimated Cost**: Moderate.

   4. **Phase 4: Window Upgrades**
      - **Action**: Replace glazing and consider replacing entire windows if frames are old.
      - **Rationale**: Improves thermal and acoustic performance.
      - **Estimated Cost**: High.

   5. **Phase 5: Ventilation System Upgrade**
      - **Action**: Replace ventilation system to a more efficient type (e.g., type D).
      - **Rationale**: Enhances indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
      - **Estimated Cost**: Moderate.

   6. **Phase 6: Optional Upgrades**
      - **Action**: Install PV-panels on roof and consider a heat pump if emission system is compatible.
      - **Rationale**: Further reduces energy consumption and costs.
      - **Estimated Cost**: High.

#### 4. **Budget and Timeline**
   - **Total Estimated Cost**: Varies based on phases implemented.
   - **Timeline**: Phased approach allows for budgeting and prioritization over time.

#### 5. **Additional Considerations**
   - **Permits**: Check local regulations for permits, especially for exterior wall insulation and roof replacements.
   - **Professional Consultation**: Engage architects or contractors for complex tasks like exterior wall insulation and window replacement.
   - **Energy Audits**: Conduct regular energy audits to monitor progress and adjust the plan as needed.

This plan ensures a structured approach to improving energy efficiency while being mindful of cost and practicality.
