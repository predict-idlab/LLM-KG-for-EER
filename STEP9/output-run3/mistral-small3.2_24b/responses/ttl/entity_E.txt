### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

Given the building's characteristics and the goal to improve the EPC (Energy Performance Certificate) score, here's a step-by-step renovation plan that maximizes energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective:

#### 1. **Insulation Upgrades**
   - **Insulate Wall Cavity**: Start with the cavity walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Left, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right). This is cost-effective (€22/m²) and has a low nuisance level.
   - **Insulate Attic Floor**: Since the attic floor is not mentioned, but the roof (RoofMain) is well-insulated, this step ensures comprehensive thermal performance.
   - **Insulate Floor Above Crawl Space**: This step is crucial for reducing heat loss through the floor (€30/m²).

#### 2. **Window and Glazing Upgrades**
   - **Replace Glazing**: Upgrade from single glazing to double or triple glazing to improve thermal performance. This is a high-impact upgrade but also high in cost (€200/m²).
   - **Replace Windows**: If the window frames are in good condition, consider only replacing the glazing. If frames need replacement, opt for energy-efficient frames with a thermal break.

#### 3. **Roof and Ventilation System**
   - **Replace Ventilation System**: Upgrade from Ventilation System A to a more efficient system (e.g., Ventilation System C or D) to improve air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Place Roof Underlayment**: Ensure the roof is properly sealed to prevent heat loss and improve overall insulation.

#### 4. **Heating System Upgrades**
   - **Place Heat Pump Boiler**: Replace the existing boiler with a heat pump boiler to reduce energy consumption for water heating.
   - **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**: Optimize the heating system to operate at lower temperatures, improving efficiency.

#### 5. **Renewable Energy Integration**
   - **Place PV-Panels on Roof**: Install photovoltaic panels on the roof to generate renewable energy, reducing reliance on the grid.
   - **Place Plug and Play PV-Panels**: Consider plug-and-play solar panels for easier installation and immediate energy savings.

#### 6. **Final Touches**
   - **Place Sunshade**: Install sunshades to reduce solar heat gain in summer, improving comfort and reducing cooling needs.
   - **Place Airconditioning**: If needed, install an air conditioning system to maintain comfortable indoor temperatures.
