### Analysis of the Building and Prioritization of Renovations

Given the building's characteristics and the goal of improving its energy performance, here's a step-by-step renovation plan that prioritizes cost-effective and energy-efficient upgrades:

1. **Remove Asbestos (if present)**:
   - **Action**: Remove asbestos from the flat roof (RoofExtension) and gable roof (RoofMain) if it is present.
   - **Reason**: Asbestos removal is a safety precaution and may be required before other renovations.
   - **Cost**: ~€40/m² for flat roof, ~€45/m² for gable roof.

2. **Insulate the Roofs**:
   - **Action**: Insulate the flat roof (RoofExtension) and gable roof (RoofMain).
   - **Reason**: Roofs are significant sources of heat loss. Insulating them will improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
   - **Cost**: ~€50/m² for flat roof, ~€60/m² for gable roof.

3. **Insulate the Walls**:
   - **Action**: Insulate the walls (Wall_Front, Wall_Rear, Wall_Right) on the interior or exterior.
   - **Reason**: Walls are another major source of heat loss. Interior insulation is less disruptive but may require relocating sockets and pipes.
   - **Cost**: ~€150/m² for interior insulation, ~€200/m² for exterior insulation.

4. **Insulate the Floor Above Basement**:
   - **Action**: Insulate the floor above the basement (FloorAboveBasement).
   - **Reason**: Basements can be cold, and insulating the floor above will reduce heat loss.
   - **Cost**: ~€30/m².

5. **Replace the Ventilation System**:
   - **Action**: Replace the ventilation system (VentilationSystem) with a more efficient system (e.g., VentilationSystemC or D).
   - **Reason**: Upgrading the ventilation system can improve indoor air quality and energy efficiency.
   - **Cost**: ~€3,500 (fixed) + €17/m².

6. **Upgrade the Heating System**:
   - **Action**: Consider upgrading the emission system to a low-temperature system or replacing the boiler with a heat pump boiler.
   - **Reason**: Low-temperature systems are more energy-efficient and can work well with heat pumps.
   - **Cost**: ~€4,000 for a heat pump boiler.

7. **Install Solar Panels**:
   - **Action**: Place PV-panels on the roof (RoofMain) or in the garden.
   - **Reason**: Solar panels can generate renewable energy, reducing the building's carbon footprint and energy costs.
   - **Cost**: ~€275/m² for roof installation, ~€275/m² for garden installation.

8. **Additional Measures**:
   - **Action**: Replace glazing or windows if they are old or inefficient.
   - **Reason**: Upgrading windows can improve insulation and reduce heat loss.
   - **Cost**: ~€200/m² for glazing, ~€800/m² for windows.

### Optimized Sequence of Renovations

1. **Asbestos Removal** (if applicable)
2. **Roof Insulation** (flat and gable)
3. **Wall Insulation** (interior or exterior)
4. **Floor Insulation** (above basement)
5. **Ventilation System Upgrade**
6. **Heating System Upgrade** (low-temperature emission or heat pump boiler)
7. **Solar Panel Installation** (roof or garden)
8. **Window/Glazing Replacement** (if needed)

### Cost-Benefit Considerations

- **Prioritize Insulation**: Insulating roofs, walls, and floors will have the most significant impact on reducing heat loss and improving energy efficiency.
- **Consider Heating System Upgrades**: Upgrading to a heat pump boiler or low-temperature emission system can further enhance energy efficiency.
- **Solar Panels**: Installing solar panels can provide long-term energy savings and reduce the building's environmental impact.

### Final Recommendation

The most cost-effective and energy-efficient renovations for this building are insulating the roofs, walls, and floors, upgrading the ventilation and heating systems, and installing solar panels. These upgrades will significantly improve the building's energy performance and thermal comfort.
