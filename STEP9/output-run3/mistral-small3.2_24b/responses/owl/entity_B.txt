### Renovation Plan

   - **Insulate Gable Roof**: The gable roof is moderately insulated. Insulating it further will enhance thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.

2. **Insulate Walls**
   - **Insulate Wall on Interior**: The front and rear walls are not insulated. Insulating them from the interior will improve the building's thermal performance without significantly altering the exterior appearance.
   - **Insulate Wall on Exterior**: If the interior insulation is not feasible, consider exterior insulation. This is more disruptive but can be more effective in some cases.

3. **Improve Windows**
   - **Replace Glazing**: The current double glazing is adequate, but replacing it with triple glazing can further improve insulation. This is particularly important for the bedrooms and living area.

4. **Insulate Floor**
   - **Insulate Floor Above Basement**: The floor above the basement is not insulated. Insulating it will help maintain a comfortable temperature in the living spaces above.

5. **Ventilation System**
   - **Replace Ventilation System**: Upgrading from a Ventilation System A to a more efficient system (e.g., Ventilation System B or C) can improve air quality and energy efficiency.

6. **Heating System**
   - **Place Emission System on Low Temperature**: Install a low-temperature emission system to work with the new insulation measures. This can include underfloor heating or radiators designed for low-temperature operation.
   - **Connect to Heat Grid**: If available, connecting to a heat grid can provide a sustainable and efficient heating solution.

7. **Renewable Energy**
   - **Place PV-Panels on Roof**: Given the potential for PV, installing solar panels on the roof can generate renewable energy, reducing the building's carbon footprint and energy costs.
   - **Place PV-Panels in Garden**: If roof space is limited, consider installing panels in the garden.

8. **Additional Measures**
   - **Place Sunshade**: Install sunshades to reduce solar heat gain in the summer, improving thermal comfort and reducing cooling needs.
   - **Place Air-Water Heat Pump**: Consider installing an air-water heat pump for heating and possibly cooling, depending on the climate and specific needs.
