### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

**Objective:**
Improve the energy efficiency of the semi-detached building (constructed in 1951) to achieve a better Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) label, focusing on practical and cost-effective upgrades.

**Current State:**
- **Building Type:** Semi-detached
- **Construction Year:** 1951
- **EPC Label:** E (388 kWh/m²year)
- **Insulation:** Poor (NotInsulated for most elements)
- **Heating System:** Air heating with radiators and floor heating
- **Ventilation System:** Type A (natural air supply and exhaust)
- **Windows:** Wooden frames with double glazing
- **Roof:** Main gable roof (NotInsulated), extension flat roof (ModeratelyInsulated)
- **Walls:** Full brick walls (NotInsulated)
- **Floor:** Above basement (NotInsulated)
- **Potential for PV:** Yes
- **Heat Grid:** Not available

**Renovation Priorities (Ordering):**
1. **Insulate Attic Floor** (Ordering: 31)
   - **Reason:** Quick and cost-effective way to improve thermal comfort and reduce heat loss.
   - **Action:** Insulate the attic floor with walkable insulation, ensuring a vapour barrier is installed.
   - **Cost:** ~€60/m² (PER-M2)

2. **Insulate Wall Cavity** (Ordering: 34)
   - **Reason:** Improves thermal performance of the walls.
   - **Action:** Inject insulation into the cavity walls after inspection.
   - **Cost:** ~€22/m² (PER-M2)

3. **Insulate Gable Roof** (Ordering: 28)
   - **Reason:** Reduces heat loss through the roof.
   - **Action:** Insulate the gable roof with proper vapour barrier installation.
   - **Cost:** ~€60/m² (PER-M2)

4. **Insulate Flat Roof (Extension)** (Ordering: 25)
   - **Reason:** The flat roof extension is already moderately insulated; further insulation can improve performance.
   - **Action:** Add insulation to the flat roof, ensuring proper vapour barrier installation.
   - **Cost:** ~€50/m² (PER-M2)

5. **Replace Glazing** (Ordering: 43)
   - **Reason:** Upgrade to triple glazing for better thermal and acoustic performance.
   - **Action:** Replace existing double glazing with triple glazing, ensuring window frames are in good condition.
   - **Cost:** ~€200/m² (PER-M2)

6. **Place PV Panels on Roof** (Ordering: 85)
   - **Reason:** Utilize the potential for PV to generate renewable energy.
   - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof, ensuring proper orientation and shading considerations.
   - **Cost:** ~€275/m² (PER-M2)

7. **Place Air-Water Heatpump** (Ordering: 67)
   - **Reason:** Replace the existing air heating system with a more efficient heat pump.
   - **Action:** Install an air-water heat pump with proper integration into the existing emission system.
   - **Cost:** ~€8,000 (Fixed Price)

**Additional Considerations:**
- **Ventilation System:** The existing ventilation system (Type A) is adequate, but ensure proper grilles are installed if replacing windows.
- **Asbestos:** Check for asbestos in roof structures before any insulation work.
- **Permits:** Ensure necessary permits are obtained for roof work and PV panel installation.

**Expected Outcomes:**
- Improved thermal comfort and reduced energy consumption.
- Better EPC label (target: C or B).
- Lower energy costs and increased property value.

**Note:** Always consult with professionals (architects, contractors) to ensure proper execution of the renovation work.
