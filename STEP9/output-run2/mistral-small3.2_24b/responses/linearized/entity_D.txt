### Renovation Plan for Semi-Detached Building

#### **1. Initial Assessment**
- **Current State**:
  - The building is a semi-detached structure built in 2017.
  - The energy performance certificate (EPC) score is 87, indicating a need for improvement.
  - The owner's primary motivation is to improve the EPC value.
  - The building has well-insulated walls (front, rear, left) but lacks insulation in certain areas (roof extension, attic floor).
  - The roof is in good condition but not optimized for energy efficiency.
  - No potential for photovoltaic (PV) panels or geothermal heat pumps due to site constraints.

#### **2. Priority Renovations**
Given the ontology and suggestion list, the following actions are recommended for energy efficiency and cost-effectiveness:

**a. Roof Insulation**
- **Action**: Insulate the flat roof (RoofExtension).
  - **Justification**: The roof extension is currently well-insulated, but further insulation can enhance thermal performance.
  - **Ordering**: High priority due to significant heat loss through roofs.
  - **Cost**: PER-M2 (exact cost depends on material and labor).

- **Action**: Insulate the gable roof (RoofMain).
  - **Justification**: The gable roof is moderately insulated; improving insulation will reduce heat loss.
  - **Ordering**: High priority.
  - **Cost**: PER-M2.

**b. Attic Floor Insulation**
- **Action**: Insulate the attic floor.
  - **Justification**: The attic floor is a critical area for heat loss; insulation will improve thermal comfort and energy efficiency.
  - **Ordering**: High priority.
  - **Cost**: PER-M2.

**c. Window Glazing Upgrade**
- **Action**: Replace glazing on windows in the living area and bedrooms.
  - **Justification**: Double glazing is already in place, but upgrading to triple glazing or improving the quality of double glazing can further reduce heat loss.
  - **Ordering**: Medium priority.
  - **Cost**: PER-M2.

**d. Wall Cavity Insulation**
- **Action**: Insulate wall cavities if applicable.
  - **Justification**: Ensures comprehensive insulation, especially if the walls are cavity walls.
  - **Ordering**: Medium priority.
  - **Cost**: PER-M2.

**e. Ventilation System Upgrade**
- **Action**: Replace the ventilation system (VentilationSystemD).
  - **Justification**: Improving ventilation efficiency can reduce energy consumption for heating and cooling.
  - **Ordering**: Medium priority.
  - **Cost**: PER-M2 or fixed price (€3500).

**f. Emission System Optimization**
- **Action**: Place the emission system on low temperature.
  - **Justification**: Optimizes heating efficiency and reduces energy consumption.
  - **Ordering**: Medium priority.
  - **Cost**: PER-M2.

**g. Additional Considerations**
- **Heat Pump Boiler**: Evaluate the feasibility of installing a heat pump boiler if the current heating system is inefficient.
- **Sun Shades**: Consider installing sun shades to reduce solar heat gain in summer, improving cooling efficiency.

#### **3. Implementation Sequence**
1. **Roof Insulation** (flat and gable roof).
2. **Attic Floor Insulation**.
3. **Window Glazing Upgrade**.
4. **Wall Cavity Insulation** (if applicable).
5. **Ventilation System Upgrade**.
6. **Emission System Optimization**.

#### **4. Cost-Effectiveness and Energy Efficiency**
- **Cost-Effectiveness**: Prioritize actions with high energy savings and low installation costs (e.g., roof and attic floor insulation).
- **Energy Efficiency**: Focus on reducing heat loss through the building envelope (roof, walls, windows) and optimizing the heating system.

#### **5. Long-Term Benefits**
- **Improved EPC Score**: Achieve a better energy performance certificate, increasing the property's value and appeal.
- **Reduced Energy Consumption**: Lower heating costs and improved thermal comfort.
- **Sustainability**: Contribute to environmental goals by reducing the carbon footprint of the building.

This renovation plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, tailored to the specific characteristics of the semi-detached building.
