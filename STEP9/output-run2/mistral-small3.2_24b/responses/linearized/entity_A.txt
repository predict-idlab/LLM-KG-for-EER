### Step-by-Step Renovation Plan

#### 1. **Assessment of Current State**
   - **Building Type**: Semi-detached (1951)
   - **Current EPC Score**: 388 (D label)
   - **Motivation**: To improve EPC
   - **Potential for PV**: Yes
   - **Heat Grid Availability**: No
   - **Geothermal Potential**: No (horizontal or vertical)

#### 2. **Key Issues Identified**
   - **Insulation**: Most walls and roof are not insulated (NotInsulated grade).
   - **Emission System**: Current system (Radiator + FloorHeating) may not be efficient.
   - **Windows**: Double glazing, but frames may need inspection for thermal breaks.
   - **Roof**: Moderately insulated (RoofExtension), but main roof (RoofMain) is not insulated.

#### 3. **Prioritization of Renovations**
   - **High Impact, Low Nuisance**:
     - **Insulate Wall Cavity**: Low nuisance, high impact on thermal performance.
     - **Insulate Attic Floor**: Low nuisance, improves thermal comfort.
   - **Medium Impact, Medium Nuisance**:
     - **Replace Ventilation System**: Upgrade to a more efficient system (e.g., VentilationSystemC).
     - **Insulate Wall Interior**: Improves thermal comfort but requires relocation of fixtures.
   - **Low Impact, High Nuisance**:
     - **Replace Roof**: High nuisance but necessary for long-term energy efficiency.

#### 5. **Expected Outcomes**
   - **Energy Efficiency**: Significant reduction in EPC score, likely moving from D to C or B label.
   - **Thermal Comfort**: Improved insulation reduces heat loss and drafts.
   - **Cost Savings**: Lower energy bills due to improved heating efficiency.

#### 6. **Next Steps**
   - **Consult Professionals**: Engage architects or contractors for detailed planning and execution.
   - **Permit Check**: Ensure compliance with local regulations, especially for roof replacements.
   - **Budgeting**: Allocate funds based on prioritized phases.

This plan ensures a systematic approach to renovations, balancing immediate needs with long-term benefits while minimizing disruption.
