### Step-by-Step Renovation Plan

**Objective:** Improve the energy efficiency of the attached building while keeping the renovations cost-effective and practical.

#### 1. **Assessment and Prioritization**
   - **Current State:** The building has an energy score of 50 (EPC label E) and is moderately insulated.
   - **Motivation:** The owner wants to improve the EPC value.
   - **Constraints:** The building is located in Belgium, has no heat grid nearby, and has potential for neither vertical nor horizontal geothermal drilling.
   - **Key Areas to Address:** Roofs, walls, windows, insulation, heating, and ventilation systems.

#### 2. **Immediate Actions (High Priority)**
   - **Insulate the Roofs:**
     - **Action:** Insulate the flat roof (RoofExtension) and gable roof (RoofMain).
     - **Reason:** Roofs are significant sources of heat loss. Insulating them will improve thermal comfort and reduce energy consumption.
     - **Suggestion:** Use `insulate flat roof` and `insulate gable roof` from the suggestion list.
   - **Improve Window Insulation:**
     - **Action:** Replace the glazing on the windows in the living area and bedrooms.
     - **Reason:** Double glazing (current type) can be upgraded to triple glazing for better insulation.
     - **Suggestion:** Use `replace glazing` from the suggestion list.
   - **Upgrade Heating System:**
     - **Action:** Replace the current emission system (ElectricConvector) with a more efficient system.
     - **Reason:** The current system is inefficient and contributes to high energy consumption.
     - **Suggestion:** Consider `place air-water heatpump` or `place heatpumpboiler` from the suggestion list.

#### 3. **Medium Priority Actions**
   - **Wall Insulation:**
     - **Action:** Insulate the walls on the interior or exterior, depending on feasibility and cost.
     - **Reason:** Walls are another major source of heat loss. Insulating them will further improve energy efficiency.
     - **Suggestion:** Use `insulate wall on interior` or `insulate wall on exterior` from the suggestion list.
   - **Ventilation System:**
     - **Action:** Replace the current ventilation system (VentilationSystemD) with a more efficient one.
     - **Reason:** Improving ventilation can enhance indoor air quality and reduce energy loss.
     - **Suggestion:** Use `replace ventilation system` from the suggestion list.

#### 4. **Long-Term Actions (Low Priority)**
   - **Renewable Energy Integration:**
     - **Action:** Install PV panels on the roof.
     - **Reason:** Generating renewable energy on-site can further reduce energy costs and carbon footprint.
     - **Suggestion:** Use `place PV-panels on roof` from the suggestion list.
   - **Additional Measures:**
     - **Action:** Consider placing a sunshade to reduce solar gain in summer.
     - **Reason:** This can help maintain a comfortable indoor temperature without excessive cooling.
     - **Suggestion:** Use `place sunshade` from the suggestion list.

#### 5. **Implementation Sequence**
   - **Phase 1:** Insulate roofs and replace glazing.
   - **Phase 2:** Upgrade heating system and insulate walls.
   - **Phase 3:** Replace ventilation system and install PV panels.

#### 6. **Cost and Benefit Analysis**
   - **Immediate Actions:** High impact on energy efficiency with moderate cost.
   - **Medium Priority Actions:** Balanced cost and benefit, improving overall efficiency.
   - **Long-Term Actions:** Higher initial cost but long-term savings and sustainability benefits.
