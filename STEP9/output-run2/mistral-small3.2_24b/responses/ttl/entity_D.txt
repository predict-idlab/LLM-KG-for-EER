### Renovation Plan for Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness

#### **1. Assess Current State**
- **Building Type**: Semi-detached (shares a wall with another house).
- **Construction Year**: 2017.
- **Energy Performance Certificate (EPC) Score**: 87.0 (good but can be improved).
- **Insulation**: Well-insulated walls, moderately insulated gable roof, well-insulated flat roof.
- **Windows**: Double glazing, wooden frames in good condition.
- **Heating System**: Heat pump.
- **Ventilation System**: Ventilation system D (mechanical supply and exhaust).
- **Solar Potential**: No PV panels installed; potential for PV is false (likely due to shading or orientation).
- **Heat Grid**: Not present.
- **Owner’s Motivation**: To improve EPC.

#### **2. Prioritize Renovations Based on Energy Efficiency and Cost-Effectiveness**
Given the building’s current state, the following renovations are recommended in order of priority:

##### **A. Roof and Attic Improvements**
1. **Insulate Gable Roof** (Ordering: 28)
   - **Action**: Insulate the gable roof to improve thermal performance.
   - **Cost**: €60/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Notes**: Install a vapour barrier on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation.

2. **Insulate Attic Floor** (Ordering: 31)
   - **Action**: Insulate the attic floor to reduce heat loss.
   - **Cost**: €60/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Notes**: Ensure a vapour barrier is installed to prevent moisture issues.

##### **B. Window and Glazing Upgrades**
3. **Replace Glazing** (Ordering: 43)
   - **Action**: Upgrade to triple glazing if possible, or ensure double glazing is in good condition.
   - **Cost**: €200/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: High (requires careful installation).
   - **Notes**: Check that window frames are straight and strong enough to support new glazing.

##### **C. Wall Insulation**
4. **Insulate Wall on Exterior** (Ordering: 40)
   - **Action**: Consider external wall insulation if internal insulation is not feasible.
   - **Cost**: €200/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: High (requires permits if thickness exceeds 26 cm or building line is overrun).
   - **Notes**: Ensure compatibility with the building line and council guidelines.

##### **D. Ventilation and Heating System**
5. **Replace Ventilation System** (Ordering: 52)
   - **Action**: Upgrade to a more efficient ventilation system if needed.
   - **Cost**: €3,500 (fixed) + €17/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.
   - **Notes**: Ensure ducts for supply and return are properly installed.

6. **Place Emission System on Low Temperature** (Ordering: 70)
   - **Action**: Optimize the emission system to work at lower temperatures for better efficiency.
   - **Cost**: €100/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Moderate.
   - **Notes**: Ensure compatibility with the existing heat pump.

##### **E. Solar Panel Installation**
7. **Place PV-Panels on Roof** (Ordering: 85)
   - **Action**: Install PV panels on the roof if shading and orientation allow.
   - **Cost**: €275/m².
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Notes**: Check the 'Zonnekaart' for optimal placement and ensure no asbestos is present in the roof.

##### **F. Additional Considerations**
8. **Place Sunshade** (Ordering: 79)
   - **Action**: Install outdoor blinds to reduce solar gain in summer.
   - **Cost**: €750 (fixed).
   - **Nuisance Level**: Low.
   - **Notes**: Outdoor blinds are more efficient than indoor blinds.

#### **3. Implementation Sequence**
1. **Roof and Attic Insulation** (Insulate gable roof and attic floor).
2. **Window Glazing Upgrades** (Replace or ensure optimal glazing).
3. **Exterior Wall Insulation** (If feasible and permitted).
4. **Ventilation and Heating System Upgrades** (Replace ventilation system and optimize emission system).
5. **Solar Panel Installation** (If feasible).
6. **Sunshade Installation** (For summer comfort).

#### **4. Expected Outcomes**
- **Improved EPC Score**: Higher energy efficiency leading to a better EPC rating.
- **Reduced Energy Consumption**: Lower heating costs due to better insulation and efficient systems.
- **Increased Comfort**: Better thermal comfort and indoor air quality.
- **Cost Savings**: Long-term savings on energy bills and potential increase in property value.

This plan is designed to maximize energy efficiency while being cost-effective, prioritizing actions that provide the highest return on investment.
