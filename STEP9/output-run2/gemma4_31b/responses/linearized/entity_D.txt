### Building Analysis
The building is a modern semi-detached house (constructed in 2017) that already exhibits a very high level of energy efficiency, with an **EPC score of 87** (A-label). 

**Current State Summary:**
*   **Thermal Envelope:** The walls (Front, Left, and Rear), the floor above the basement, and the flat roof extension are all **Well Insulated**. The windows are double-glazed with wooden frames in good condition.
*   **Systems:** The home is already equipped with a **Heat Pump** for generation and a **Ventilation System D** (the most advanced type listed, featuring mechanical supply and exhaust).
*   **Renewables:** PV panels are already installed.
*   **Constraints:** There is no potential for geothermal drilling (vertical or horizontal) and no available heat grid.

### Proposed Renovation Path
Given that the building is already highly optimized, the goal is to target the only remaining "moderate" thermal weakness to further lower the EPC score and maximize long-term operational performance without incurring unnecessary costs.

#### Step 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization
The only element not rated as "Well Insulated" is the **RoofMain (Gable Roof)**, which is currently **Moderately Insulated**.
*   **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
*   **Reasoning:** Improving the insulation of the gable roof is the most cost-effective way to reduce heat loss in a building where the walls and floors are already optimized. This directly supports the owner's motivation to improve the EPC.
*   **Point of Attention:** Ensure a vapour barrier is installed on the warm side of the insulation to prevent condensation.

#### Step 2: Strategic Evaluation (Optional/Long-term)
Because the building is already at an "A" label, further structural interventions (like replacing windows for triple glazing) would yield diminishing returns on investment. 
*   **Recommendation:** Since the building is from 2017 and the EPC is already 87, I advise **against** replacing windows or adding interior/exterior wall insulation, as these elements are already "Well Insulated."
