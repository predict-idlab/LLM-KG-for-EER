### Building Analysis
The building is exceptionally modern (constructed in 2023) and already highly efficient (EPC score of 50, which is an **A label**). Most elements—including the walls, flat roof, and floor above the basement—are already "Well Insulated." The ventilation system (Type D) is also top-tier.

However, there are three specific areas for optimization:
1.  **The Gable Roof:** Currently only "Moderately Insulated."
2.  **The Heating System:** The building uses Electric Convectors (which are energy-inefficient) and the owner has explicitly indicated a reason to change the emission system.
3.  **Glazing:** Currently double-glazed; upgrading to triple-glazing would further lower the EPC score.

---

### Proposed Renovation Path
To maximize energy efficiency while remaining cost-effective, the renovations should follow the "Envelope First, Systems Second" logic, adhering to the `ordering` values defined in the ontology.

#### Step 1: Thermal Envelope Optimization (Low-Hanging Fruit)
Before upgrading the heating system, we must ensure the building retains as much heat as possible to allow for a smaller, more efficient heat pump.
*   **Action:** `insulate gable roof`
*   **Reasoning:** This is the only part of the envelope not yet "Well Insulated." Improving this reduces the overall heating load.
*   **Ordering:** 28 (Execute early).

#### Step 2: Glazing Upgrade
*   **Action:** `replace glazing`
*   **Reasoning:** Since the building is new (2023) and the wooden frames are in "Good State," replacing the entire window is not cost-effective. Replacing only the glass (upgrading from double to triple glazing) is the most cost-effective way to further reduce heat loss through the windows.
*   **Ordering:** 43.

#### Step 3: High-Efficiency Heat Generation
With the envelope optimized, we address the inefficient electric convectors.
*   **Action:** `place air-water heatpump`
*   **Reasoning:** The building already has floor heating (a low-temperature emission system), making an air-water heat pump the most efficient and logical choice. Geothermal options are ruled out as the site data indicates no potential for vertical or horizontal drilling.
*   **Ordering:** 67.

#### Step 4: Domestic Hot Water Optimization
*   **Action:** `place heatpumpboiler`
*   **Reasoning:** To move away from inefficient electric heating for water, a dedicated heat pump boiler should be installed to complement the air-water system.
*   **Ordering:** 76.
