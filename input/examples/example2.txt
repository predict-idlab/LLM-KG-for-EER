I live in an attached house in Ghent, Belgium, which was built in 1963. The house has a net floor area of 149 m², and I am considering renovations because I would like to improve its energy performance certificate (EPC), which currently has a score of 278. The house is not historically protected.

The exterior walls at the front and rear of the house are solid brick walls and are currently not insulated. The main roof is a pitched roof with a good level of insulation, while the flat-roof extension is not insulated. Both roofs are generally in good condition. The floor above the basement is also uninsulated.

For heating, the house relies on a boiler connected to a combination of radiators, underfloor heating, and air heating systems. These heat emission systems are no longer in good condition, and I would like to replace them. The house is equipped with a ventilation system D.

The windows in both the living area and the bedrooms have wooden frames that are in good condition and are fitted with double glazing.

There is no district heating network available in the area, and the site is not suitable for geothermal energy systems. However, the house does have potential for the installation of photovoltaic panels.
