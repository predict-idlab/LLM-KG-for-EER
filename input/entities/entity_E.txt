I live in a detached house in Ghent, Belgium, which was built in 1862. The house has a net floor area of 136 m², and I am considering renovations because I would like to improve its energy performance certificate, which currently has a score of 165. The house is not historically protected.

The exterior walls at the front, rear, left side, and right side of the house are cavity walls and are currently not insulated. The main roof is a pitched roof with a good level of insulation. The floor above the crawl space is not protected but I do not know whether it is insulated.

For heating, the house relies on a boiler connected to a combination of radiators, underfloor heating, air heating systems, and electric convectors. These heat emission systems are no longer in good condition, and I would like to replace them. The house is also equipped with a basic natural ventilation system.

The windows in both the living area and the bedrooms have wooden frames that are in good condition and are fitted with single glazing.

There is no district heating network available in the area, and the site is not suitable for geothermal energy systems. However, the house does have potential for the installation of photovoltaic panels.
