I live in a semi-detached house in Ghent, Belgium, which was built in 2017. The house has a net floor area of 173 m², and I am considering renovations because I would like to improve its energy performance certificate, which currently has a score of 87. The house is not historically protected.

The exterior walls at the front, rear, and left side of the house are solid brick walls and are well insulated. The main pitched roof has a moderate level of insulation, while the flat-roof extension is well insulated. The roofs are generally in good condition. The floor above the basement is also well insulated.

The house is heated by a heat pump. The heat emission system is in good condition, and there is currently no reason to replace it. The house is equipped with a ventilation system D.

The windows in both the living area and the bedrooms have wooden frames that are in good condition and are fitted with double glazing. The glazing was renewed during a renovation that was completed in 2020.

There is no district heating network available in the area, and the site is not suitable for geothermal energy systems. The house already has photovoltaic panels installed.
